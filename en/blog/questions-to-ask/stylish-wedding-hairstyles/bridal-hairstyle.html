Three trendy bridal hairstyles

Just like for clothes, trends come and go for wedding hairstyles. Whether you are looking for hairstyles for long hair or hairstyles for short hair, find out what are the three most trendy and latest hairstyles that every bride-to-be is looking for. As dedicated fairies, we have also sprinkled a few hair tips to help you pick the best wedding hair accessories that will suit your bridal hairstyle. From flower crowns to hairpins, embellish your wedding hair with delicate flowery adornments to be the prettiest when walking down the aisle!

A low bun for a vintage vibe

For a winter wedding or a summer wedding, buns have always been center stage when it comes to wedding hair ideas. They are perfect when you have thick hair to avoid the heat for example. Yet they also suit thin hair as they can help create some volume. Having your hair up in a chignon will give you a glamorous and elegant wedding vibe and will delicately highlight the shape of your face.

While the high bun has had its time to shine, it’s the low bun that’s in vogue at the moment. Style your hair back in a messy bun for a more modern vibe or stick to the classic ballerina bun for an even more vintage wedding style. Either way, secure it with a clear elastic and bobby pins to keep your wedding day hair in place.

Updo hairstyles offer quite a lot of options when it comes to accessorizing them. For a classy look, adorn your messy updo with a comb or a double comb to add elegance to your wedding hairstyle. It will subtly highlight the shape of the bun whilst adding texture and volume to your hairdo.

Aria wide comb

For a romantic wedding, you could also adorn your beautiful hair styled in an updo with a wedding veil. Add a sparkly wedding dress to that and you got yourself a modern-day Disney princess look. Hair pins are also a great option as you can place them anywhere you’d like in your hair updo. Scattered in your bridal hair, they will bring a touch of colour to your wedding attire. Browse through our website to discover which hair accessories will be best to adorn your wedding bun.

A braided hairstyle for a bohemian look

Boho or bohemian hairstyles are really popular at the moment, for a daily look but also for weddings. They give off that artsy, poetic vibe that is so unique. Braided hairstyles are your go-to to turn into a romantic bohemian fairy. They work well for wedding hairstyles for long hair but are also very flattering on shoulder length hair. The great advantage of braids is that they exist in many shapes and forms. Upgrade your classic French braid and switch it for a waterfall braid, a side braid, or a fishtail braid for a more intricate braided hairstyle. If you like classic hairstyles, you could also go for a braided bun or a crown braid which bring a more vintage vibe.

Braid your hair loosely, wrapping a strand of hair around the elastic for a cute hairstyle. Braiding your hair will also avoid them from being tousled at the end of the day which is quite important when you know you will be taking pictures all day long on your wedding-day.

Ornella hairpin

If you’d like to have your bridal hair cascading in your back but still want a braided hairstyle, ask your hairstylist for a half up half down hairstyle. Braid two locks of hair starting from the front of your hairline towards the back of your head and pinning them at the back to keep them in place. This will create an enchanting hairstyle that will wow the groom at the altar.

If you’re wondering which hair accessories to wear with these braided hairstyles, hairpins are your best friends. Versatile and dainty, you can pin them anywhere you want in your braid. They will embellish any kind of braid. Loosely curled hair, a few braided strands of hair, a flowy gown and delicate flowery hair pins and you are guaranteed to have the most beautiful wedding.

Loose hair for a natural hairstyle

Looking for a natural, beachy hairstyle? Keep your hair down and accessorize your hairstyle. Nothing beats wavy hair flowing in the breeze, a natural bridal makeup, a flower crown and a backless dress for a beach wedding. Although weddings tend to be the occasion to create sophisticated bridal hairstyles, natural, flawless looks are very trendy at the moment and we’re seeing more and more brides wearing their hair down at their wedding.

Depending on your haircut and your bridal hairstyle, there are a few things you can do to make this hairstyle more unique. You can use some texturizing hairspray to make your hair more voluminous and give them a bit more texture. This kind of hairsprays can also create a messy beachy vibe which is the secret to obtain a hairstyle that looks both natural and dazzling. If you have straight hair, you can also curl your hair in loose or bouncy curls with a curling-iron.

Adorn your hair with a beautiful headband decorated with preserved fresh flowers to enhance that bohemian vibe. A jeweled tiara ornated with Swarovski crystals will also glamorize your hairstyle and give you a feminine and dramatic style.

Calypso crown

Hair clips are also a great option to adorn your hairstyle if you are wearing your hair down. You could take one strand of hair from each side of your face and clip them together at the back of your head to delicately frame your face. You could also take multiple locks of hair and place them at the back of your head, fixing them into place with the hair clips like the model in the picture. Style your hair with a side part and place a hair clip on one side for an asymmetrical look.

Harper hair clip

Whether you are more of a flawless hair down bride, a modern-day bohemian fairy or you prefer a vintage low bun, choose the hairstyle that will make you spend the best wedding. These three hairstyles are only a selection of some of the trendiest hairstyles. You could also style your hair in a pulled-back sleek ponytail or a half bun for example.

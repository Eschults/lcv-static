Floral wedding crowns: which style to choose?

Colorful ones, white ones, ones with one type of flowers or several ones, flower headbands are very trendy and exist in many forms and styles. This article aims at helping you choose the right style of wedding tiara that will match your wedding gown and your decorations on your wedding-day. It is also a great opportunity to discover a selection of our flowery bridal headpieces.

For a vintage wedding: a white floral headpiece

If you are having a vintage wedding, pick a flower crown among our vintage-inspired white flower hair accessories. These will be perfect to give you a chic and elegant immaculate bridal look.

Wendy

Composed of preserved hydrangea, ruscus, munni and freshwater pearls, this handmade head band can be worn towards the front like a classic flower crown, or towards the back like a double comb. Its white flowers will look exquisite worn with an ivory embroidered wedding gown. This headwear can be worn on its own or associated with a piece of jewellery such as simple pearl earrings for example. Veils can also be combined with flower crowns to enhance the vintage vibe of your wedding attire.

Eudora

Made of hydrangea and baby’s breath, this beautiful flower headband is embellished with a white plumetis birdcage veil therefore accentuating the vintage-style aspect of this adornment. Worn with a long white satin or organza gown, this flower headband will bring elegance to your beautiful wedding.

Eudora crown

For a boho-chic wedding: a colorful flowery tiara

Whether you are having a floral wedding or not, colorful bridal hair accessories are the ideal choice for a bohemian bridal style. Less dainty than the previous white flower headpieces, these flowery tiaras are still very delicate and feminine.

Margareth

With its English roses, hydrangeas, gypsophila and fern, this head wreath epitomizes bohemian romanticism. Styling your bridal hair in an updo will enable you to wear it like a bridal hair comb, while wavy or braided hairstyles will look beautiful with this flowery tiara worn towards the front.

Margareth crown

Sierra

Adorned with powder pink, purple and ivory hydrangeas, pink gypsophila, eucalyptus, roses, pink bougainvillea and red and pink daisies, this colorful hair accessory will make you look dazzling to walk down the aisle. Opt for a loose hairstyle to make your hair flowers stand out more. This boho hairband will embellish any wedding dress. Associate it with a matching colorful bouquet to add the finishing touch to your wedding style.

Sierra crown

Ornate your wedding hair with this beautiful wreath to look like a modern-day fairy on your wedding-day. Accented with foliage, these types of wedding accessories are ideal to bring a touch of color and of greenery to your wedding outfit while keeping an elegant and delicate style.

For an original wedding: an intricate and eccentric wreath

If discreet or classic is not what you had in mind, and you want your bridal accessories to bring a sparkle of bling to the party, here is a selection of our most dramatic tiaras that will leave the groom speechless.

Sirena

Combining our Sirena tiara with our Nerina comb, you will for sure look exquisite on the big day with our Sirena set. The hair jewelry of this jeweled bridal headpiece will delicately sit on your forehead while its large white roses will crown your head to make you look like a queen.

Olympia

With its large gardenias, ruscus leaves and gypsophila, this floral headband is a glamorous halo of white flowers that will guarantee you an elegant bridal look. Wear it on loose wavy hair with a couture wedding dress to make the most of this dramatic hair band.

Olympia crown

Electre

Made with gold plated ruscus and encrusted with freshwater pearls and crystal pearls, this intricate bridal tiara gives off a Grecian goddess vibe that will bring a sparkly and unique look to the bride. You can wear it towards the back to adorn a bun or a half-up half-down hairstyle or towards the front on loose hair.

Electre crown

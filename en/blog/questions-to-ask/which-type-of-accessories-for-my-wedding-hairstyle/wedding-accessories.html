Top 10 of our trendiest wedding accessories

While wedding dresses are hard to choose given the number of options a bride-to-be has, it is also the case for bridal hair accessories. Wedding veils used to be the only way of adorning your hair for weddings but now, there are many alternatives for the embellishment of your bridal style. From our double combs to our hairpins, checkout this selection of 10 of our trendiest handmade wedding accessories to help you create the perfect bridal attire for your wedding-day.

For a bohemian chic wedding: our California dreaming collection

With its hippie-chic inspiration, our California dreaming collection is ideal to bring a bohemian vibe to your wedding hair. It is also a great option to add a pop of color to a vintage-inspired wedding gown. With a beautiful mix of vivid and pastel colors, this collection can be a great source of inspiration to create the floral decorations for your wedding venue.

The Amaya double comb

With its ruscus, pink and white gypsophila, powdered pink hydrangea, phalaris, nigella, wild peach roses, and lagurus, this wedding hair comb is ideal to ornate an updo secured with bobby pins. Mounted on gold plated combs, each petal and flower brings its own shade of color and a touch of delicacy to this hair accessory. Pastel colored but accented with a few more vivid elements, this bridal jewelry will beautifully enhance an ivory or champagne flowy wedding dress.

Amaya double comb

The Hazel double comb

While hair combs are usually pictured as a rigid structure, this boho hair jewelry is quite unique as it can either be worn towards the back like a classic bridal hair comb or towards the front, sitting on your forehead, more like a bridal tiara. Decorated with a gold-plated chain and stamps as well as terracotta pearls, this sparkling adornment will make you look dazzling and graceful for your perfect wedding. Worn on a loose hairstyle, assort your hair comb with a piece of jewelry that matches its bohemian vibe like our pear-shaped Pasadena earrings to sparkle even more.

Hazel double comb

The Harper hair clip

More sober and discreet than the rest of our wedding accessories, the Harper hair clip is composed of one powder pink hydrangea flower mounted on a brass crocodile structure. Easy to place in your bridal hair, this barrette is ideal to add even more elegance as well as a blush of color to your hairstyle. If you want a more dramatic wedding hairstyle, complete your hair clip with a birdcage veil or any of our powder pink headbands for example.

To look like a Bridgerton: our English roses collection

Want to look like a Bridgerton and have a vintage wedding that makes you look like you are part of a noble family? Get the lace out, an embroidered vintage-style wedding dress, a cascading bouquet, a couture veil, and one of our English roses collection wedding accessories and you’ll be walking down the aisle thinking you’re Daphne Bridgerton and Simon is waiting for you at the altar.

The Ophelia crown

Composed of preserved English roses, eucalyptus, ivy leaves, wild berries, hydrangea & gypsophila, this exquisite flower crown is among the most romantic of our wedding hair accessories. Let your hair loose with delicate curls or style them in a half up half down hairstyle and place the floral wreath gently on your wedding hair to brighten up your attire for the big day. For an elegant wedding with a Bridgerton vibe, opt for an organza dress with puffed sleeves and tie our matching Ophelia wrist corsage to your wrist with its satin ribbon.

Ophelia Crown

The Maddie set

Combining a double comb and a set of hair pins, the Maddie set stands out thanks to the immaculate white of its English roses which is enhanced by the eucalyptus that brings contrast to these hair accessories. Quite delicate, these wedding accessories will look gorgeous with a simple white satin dress or an embroidered one. This set is quite versatile so it can suit many hairstyles: you could place the comb towards the front like a tiara and place each hairpin around the back of your head so that the set forms a sort of flower crown for example. Another option would be to style your hair in some sort of braid or low ponytail, place the comb at the front and pin the hairpins randomly in the braid or in the ponytail. You can also only use the comb and give one hair pin to each bridesmaid and flower-girl so that everyone matches.

The Maddie set

The Diana veil

Whether you prefer them chapel length, cathedral length or birdcage, bridal veils always have a dramatic effect. Quite unique, this tulle bridal veil is embellished with English roses and hydrangeas that have been sewn by hand. A very special item that would help you become the Queen’s diamond, right? The chapel length of this wedding veil will enhance your silhouette while its delicate flowers will add texture and color to your wedding gown.

Diana veil

For the groom: the Ophelia boutonniere

Whether you want a matching bride and groom outfit or not, the bride is not the only one to beautify her wedding outfit with wedding accessories, the groom can have his own Bridgerton moment too! With its preserved English roses, wild berries & pepper berries, ivy leaves and hydrangeas, our Ophelia boutonniere will bring a pop of color to your man’s suit and will enhance it no matter its shade.

For a dramatic mermaid effect: our L’Odyssee collection

With its gold plated shells, cultured pearls, and wildflowers, our L’Odyssee collection is here to bring out the mermaid in you. Whether you are opting for a white wedding, a dramatic winter wedding or a beach wedding, put on a white lace wedding gown, pick your finest bridal jewellery, and walk down the aisle with some mermaid attitude.

The Sirena set

Combining our Sirena flower headband with our Nerina comb, this ensemble will make you look straight out of a fairytale. This glamorous jeweled headband is perfect for a unique wedding look and will especially suit simple sober gowns like satin ones for example. If you want your bridesmaids to match your wedding accessories, our Sirena wrist corsage is what you are looking for.

Sirena Set

The Poseïdon tiara

With gilded shells encrusted with freshwater pearls and decorated with preserved umbellifer, this wedding tiara is as sparkly as a crystal tiara adorned with Swarovski crystals. Beaded tiaras like this one can be worn with a simple gown for a classic-chic look or more sophisticated, embroidered ones. Either way, you will look the most gorgeous for your beautiful wedding.

Poseidon Tiara

The Anemone earrings

Each adorned with a white pearl, these dangling earrings will add the finishing-touch to your fairy wedding attire. Dainty and delicate, these wedding accessories bring a twist to more traditional wedding earrings. Assort them with a bracelet decorated with beads to complete your jewellery set.

Anemone earrings

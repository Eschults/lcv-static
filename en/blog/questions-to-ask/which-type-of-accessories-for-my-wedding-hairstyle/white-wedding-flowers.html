Wedding inspiration: a romantic-chic wedding with floral accessories

No matter your wedding theme, using flowers to decorate your wedding venue but also to ornate your wedding attire on your wedding-day is always a good idea. Of course, there’s your floral wedding bouquet that you will carry when walking down the aisle but there are also many flowery accessories to choose from. From floral pieces of jewelry like earrings to flowery hair accessories like crowns or combs, discover our handmade delicate accessories that will add the final touch to your beautiful wedding.

A flower crown for a real princess vibe

If you’ve been to weddings lately, you’ve certainly seen the bride or her bridesmaids wearing a flower crown. Whether they are made of colorful blooming flowers or adorned with some greenery, flowery wreaths are very trendy at the moment. With your wedding hair delicately and loosely cascading down your back or styled in a half braid, you will look like a poetic fairy.

Our Wendy crown, composed of hydrangea, ruscus, munni and pearls, is perfect for an all white vintage wedding. With its preserved white flowers, this wreath can be worn like a crown towards the front or towards the back like a double comb. For an even more vintage look, opt for a cascading bouquet made of beautiful flowers and ask your wedding florist to create white centerpieces and other flowery arrangements. It would also be perfect for a winter wedding as the immaculate white of the tiara will evoke the wintery white of the snow.

Wendy

A set of hairpins for a sophisticated hairstyle

If white wedding flowers are not your cup of tea but you still love a very romantic wedding: red, red-rose, and light pink roses are the solution! The bright colors of our Margareth hairpins are perfect to avoid a monochromatic accessory while still being harmonious. Made with beautiful blooms of English roses, hydrangea, fern and gypsophila, these hair pins can be pinned in a bun, a bubble braid, or a half updo.

Depending on your bridal style and your wedding colors, you can choose to match your wedding decorations with your hair accessory. You could make your bridal bouquet with gorgeous garden roses, have a small roses bouquet for your bridesmaids and even have your flower-girl throw rose petals down the aisle when you will be walking towards the altar on the big day.

Set of 6 Margareth hairpins

A double comb for an elegant look

Although they are not as versatile as hairpins, double combs are the ideal hair accessories to add elegance to any hairstyle. Depending on your wedding style and color palette, we have a wide collection of combs and double combs to help you plan the most perfect wedding. From all-white flowery double combs to sparkly ones with Swarovski crystals, discover our many hair accessories that will make you feel the prettiest for your elegant wedding.

Adorned with ruscus, pastel pink and white gypsophila, powdered pink hydrangea, Phalaris, nigella, wild peach roses, and lagurus, the pale coral and terracotta tones of our Amaya double comb will enhance any wedding gown. With its different textures, it is also a great way to inspire which types of flowers you will pick for your wedding centerpieces and floral arrangements. Arranging your beautiful pale-pink seasonal flowers in terracotta vases will be ideal to match the color-scheme of your bridal hair accessory.

Amaya double comb

A pair of earrings to frame your face

Ideal for a spring wedding or a summer wedding, our Delilah hoop earrings are made with beautiful daisies that will make you look like a sunny queen on your wedding photos. Just like the rest of our floral accessories, these earrings are made with fresh flowers that have been naturally stabilized to last a lifetime.

Though they are not your usual wedding accessory, these earrings are perfect for a modern wedding or to add a bohemian touch to your wedding dress.

Delilah earrings

A wrist corsage to match the groom’s boutonniere

More traditional, wrist corsages can be worn by the bride to match the groom’s boutonniere and even the bridesmaid bouquets. Made with hydrangea, eucalyptus, and Limonium, our Amelia wrist corsage gets attached with a delicate ivory satin ribbon. It is ideal to incorporate a real touch of foliage to a white wedding or to be assorted with the decorations of a green wedding or a rustic wedding. Add a hand tied bouquet made with white roses or any white flower with foliage and your wedding attire is complete!

Amela wrist corsage

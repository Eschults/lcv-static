Our five accessories for beautiful wedding hairstyles with your hair down

You are a bride-to-be, a bridesmaid or a wedding guest and you have decided to wear your hair down at the wedding but don’t know how to accessorize your hairstyle? Well, first of all, congrats on picking your wedding hairstyle, that’s a difficult choice. As to the hair accessories, you’ve knocked on the right door!

At Les Couronnes de Victoire, we are specialised in flowery hair accessories that are handmade with naturally preserved flowers. All our accessories, from our combs to our hair pins, are eternal and will be handed down from generation to generation. As accessory experts (and wedding experts), we have put together our top 5 accessories for wedding hairstyles with your hair down.

A simple but timeless choice: hair clips

When having your hair down, some accessories might be more flattering than others depending on your face shape, your type of hair or your haircut. Hair clips are a safe choice as they suit any type of hair length and hairstyles: short hairstyles, long hairstyles, shoulder length hairstyles, curly hairstyles, thick hair, … If you are going for a simple wedding hairstyle, you could style your hair in a side part and simply clip one or two barrettes on one side. You could also take two locks of hair from the front and secure them at the back of your head with a hair clip.

Marjorie hair clip

The model in the picture is wearing our Marjorie hair clip, a jewel barrette made with three Swarovski crystals and a multitude of crystal stones that will make any wedding hairstyle glamorous and elegant.

A classic with a twist: flowery hair pins

If you are looking for cute hairstyles and easy hairstyles for your wedding day hair, hair pins are your go-to. Our flowery hair pins are not your usual bobby pins: pinning them on the side of your hair just like barrettes will immediately bring that romantic wedding vibe to any hairstyle. These hair accessories will make you the prettiest whether you have textured hair, wavy hair, or straight hair. They will also keep your hair out of your face so that you can spend the best wedding without worrying about your hair getting in the way.

Joana hairpins

Composed of preserved flowers, golden leaves and flowers, and pearls, our Joana hairpins will add that subtle romantic and bohemian look to your hair down hairstyle.

A statement accessory: flower crowns

If you prefer to style your hair down with a statement accessory, a flower crown is what you need. They are the perfect hair accessory whether you are having a winter wedding, a summer wedding, a vintage wedding or even a beach wedding. They are also a great addition to any wedding hairstyles for short hair. Simply wear your natural hair down, put on the crown and you are ready to go! Our flower crowns are ideal to wear with loosely curled hair, wavy hair or even with bouncy curls.

Sirena set

Here, the model is wearing the Sirena set on loose curls for that feminine, bohemian effect. If you have straight hair, grab your curling-iron, take a strand of hair, and wrap it loosely around the curling-iron, hold it a few seconds and repeat this operation until all your hair are curled. When you’re done, gently brush your hair with your fingers to loosen the curls a bit more. You could also opt for a sleek hairstyle to bring that Hollywood look and simply brush your hair back, setting them with some hairspray for a classy and glamorous effect.

A vintage one: veils

For the vintage wedding lovers, veils will beautifully complement your bridal hairstyle if you are wearing your hair down. Delicate and poetic, our veils are the perfect hair accessory for an elegant wedding. They are also very versatile and wonderfully adorn all wedding hairstyles whether you have long hair, short hair, wavy, curly, or straight hair.

Adele veil

Our Adele veil, embroidered with shiny flakes, will delicately beautify your wedding gown. With its sparkly effect, you are sure to impress the groom when walking down the aisle.

A unique and versatile accessory: combs and double combs

If you are still hesitant about having your hair down but don’t want your hair up, a half-up half-down bridal hairstyle or bridesmaid hairstyle is a great option. They are very trendy amongst wedding hairstyles for long hair and if you lack inspiration, you can find many hairstyle ideas on our website. For example, you could go for a half updo with a classic chignon or a braided bun and have the rest of your hair down.

Styling your perfect wedding hairstyle with a comb or a double comb will bring elegance to your bridal hair. They are also a great accessory if you want to make your hairstyle more voluminous.

Rochelle Set

The model in the picture is wearing our Rochelle set composed of our Rochelle comb and our Ezili comb which brings a boho modern-day mermaid effect. If you want to recreate this hairstyle, take a piece of hair from one side just above your ear, leaving locks of hair framing your face at the front. Divide the hair into two sections and braid the strands of hair towards the back of your head.  Once you’re done, secure the braid with an elastic and pin it across the back of your head. Gently place the comb on the braid and there you have it: an easy and romantic beautifully adorned hairstyle.

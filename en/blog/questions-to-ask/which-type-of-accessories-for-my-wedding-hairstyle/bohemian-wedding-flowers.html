5 accessories for a boho-chic bride

Bohemian weddings are very trendy at the moment and every aspect of a wedding has its boho variation: bohemian wedding flowers, bohemian color-scheme, a bohemian gown, boho petals on the floor to walk down the aisle, bohemian cakes, etc. If you’ve opted for a bohemian wedding style for your wedding and need a few wedding ideas, let us help you with your wedding accessories. From flower crowns to earrings, our delicate handmade adornments will perfectly complete your wedding attire. Here are five accessories that will embellish all the boho-chic brides for their big day.

The timeless one: a flower crown

Wearing flowers to ornate your hair is always a great idea for a boho-chic bride. Peonies, hydrangeas, roses: all flowers constitute beautiful adornments for weddings. Ideal for a spring wedding or a summer wedding, our beautiful flower crowns epitomize the bohemian style. Opt for a pale tone wreath with a delicate flowy champagne satin dress to enhance your boho-chic style.

Made with pink and ivory gypsophila, stabilized wild roses and powder pink hydrangeas, our Nora flower crown will look wonderful worn with your hair loose. Each light pink pastel bloom will add a blush of color to your bridal hair and will brighten up your face.

If you are planning a floral wedding, ask your wedding florist for pale-pink summer flowers to create the wedding arrangements that will decorate the aisle, the altar, and your wedding venue.

Nora

The most versatile: a set of hairpins

If bohemian flowery wreaths are not your style, wearing a set of hairpins with bohemian wedding flowers may be the option you are looking for. Subtle and versatile, hairpins can be placed in a bun, a braided hairstyle, or a half up half down hairstyle for a romantic wedding.

Adorned with white roses, white gypsophila, and stabilized eucalyptus, our Suzanne hair pins set is perfect to add a pinch of greenery to an all white wedding. For a modern wedding, wear these white flowers hairpins in a half ponytail to create a delicate hairstyle. For a white wedding, match your bridal bouquet and your floral arrangements with beautiful flowers such as white garden roses.

Suzanne hair pins set

The vintage one: a flowy veil

Wedding planning is not an easy task so picking a theme will help you in every choice you will have to make. If you choose to organize a vintage wedding, you might want to select floral accessories that will match your wedding theme and what’s more vintage than a flowy veil?

Embellished with roses, daisies and hydrangeas, our tulle Carmel veil is very unique and will enable you to create a vintage look with a twist. With its dazzling pinks and peach, yellow and coral tones, this blooming veil is perfect for a beach wedding and is also ideal to add a real touch of color to a winter wedding. Ask your local florist for wildflowers to create your wedding decorations such as your centerpieces. You can also use the same wedding colors as your veil for your wedding bouquet and even your bridesmaid bouquet.

Carmel veil

The trendy one: a double comb

Placed in a messy bun, a sophisticated bun, a half up half down updo or worn as a crown, double combs will add definition and delicacy to any hairstyle. Our double combs, just like our flower crowns, veils, hair pins and hair clips are made with fresh flowers that have been naturally preserved to make them eternal.

Made with eucalyptus, thistle, bears’ ear, stoebe and roses mounted on a gold plated structure, our Garance Double Comb is the ideal accessory to add greenery and elegance to your hair on your wedding-day. Unique and romantic, this comb will be perfect to complete your bohemian wedding attire. Wear it with a flowy ivory dress with frills for an even more boho-chic style.

Ask your florist to add foliage to your wedding flowers to create your beautiful wedding flower arrangements that will match your hair adornment.

Garance Double Comb

The most subtle: delicate earrings

If you are looking for a more subtle way to accessorize your boho-chic attire with bohemian wedding flowers, our flowery earrings are your go-to. Earrings are the perfect accessory to wear if you have a sophisticated wedding dress so that you avoid a look that seems too busy. They will look gorgeous worn with your hair loosely curled cascading down your back and a backless dress.

Adorned with baby breath and fern, pearls and Swarovski crystals, our Fleur earrings will add different tones and textures to your bridal style to make you the prettiest bride for your perfect wedding. Add a cascading bouquet with white and pink tones and a bit foliage to enhance your boho-chic look.

Fleur earrings

Whether you wish to adorn your bridal look with seasonal flowers, daisies, pink roses, red roses, or freesia, at Les Couronnes de Victoire, we have everything you need to create a boho-chic hairstyle with delicate bohemian wedding flowers. If you want a matching bride and groom style, pick a boutonniere for the groom that will match your boho-chic vibe. From different types of flowers to different color palettes, we have an option for every bride so come and meet us at our flagship store in Paris to discover our large collection of wedding accessories!

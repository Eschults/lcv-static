Something timeless: Our 10 iconic bridal pieces for your wedding

There’s an old rhyme: Something Old, Something New, Something Borrowed, Something Blue. The four “somethings” every bride needs to wear or carry on their wedding day. The things that are said to bring good luck to the bride and the marriage. While the age-old superstition originated from England, it’s become a sort of tradition that has transcended cultures, countries, and time all over the world. Superstitions aside, we do agree that this ritual adds a bit of romance to your special day, but we think beyond something old, new, borrowed, and blue, there is one “something” that every bride must have on her wedding day: something timeless.

Here at Les Couronnes De Victoire, there’s nothing we love more than weddings and beautiful moments. Our crowns, combs, earrings, and bridal hair pieces are made of genuine, natural flowers that have been preserved using a completely natural biological process that sets them while keeping their petals soft, smooth, and real. The idea was to have the flowers frozen in time, making our bridal hair pieces and accessories something you can have for the rest of your life. If you're looking for a special accessory to match your special occasion, here are some of our favorite iconic pieces to have and to wear—from floral earrings to bridal hair pieces—for your wedding.

The Sirena Set

The Sirena set is the centerpiece of the “L’Odyssée” collection. This set is one of our most iconic bridal hair pieces. Featuring a gold-plated chain, labradorite stones, freshwater pearls, gold metal combs, preserved hydrangea, roses, as well as preserved gypsophila, lagurus, ruscus, nigella, pampas, and angel hair, it is a beautiful piece to wear for your wedding. Have a beach wedding? Combine it with one of the best-selling hair bridal pieces, the Nerina comb, for that modern-day mermaid look.

The Sirena Set

Ilana Double Comb

Double the style, double the fun? The Ilana Double Comb is one of our bridal hair pieces you can wear in two chic ways. First, on top of the forehead (which you can pair with our Stella flower tiaras) or the back as a complement to a loose bun. Crafted with preserved baby breath and hydrangea, gold plated leaves, flowers and chain, it’s a showstopping piece that transcends seasons: wear for a bohemian-themed or classic winter occasion.

Ilana double comb

Valentine Hair Clip

What better way to accessorize on your wedding day than with bridal hair pieces inspired by love itself? Named after the patron saint and day of love, our Valentine hair clip is an iconic piece for the modern bride who wants to make a bold statement not just in love, but also in style. The bow accessory features French Plumetis, polyamide tulle, a gold plated hair clip—a couture-inspired item that will instantly elevate any look.

Valentine Hair Clip

Elvira Double Comb

Aside from the Ilana Double Comb, one of the most versatile bridal hair pieces in our collection is the Elvira Double Comb. Adorned with four large, preserved gardenia blooms, it is the perfect piece to add sophistication to your hairstyle—tuck your hair to the side, for a minimal yet polished look or go for a low bun for that effortlessly elegant vibe. We also love how you can also wear just around your hairline for that Frida Kahlo-inspired look.

Elvira double comb

Nami Crown

Featuring powder pink preserved hydrangea flowers, oats and other small preserved wildflowers, get even more in touch with your romantic side with one of our iconic bridal hair pieces inspired by our Jeanne Crown. We are talking about our Nami crown, which emphasizes softness and purity through its soft tones nude, pink, and white tones that make up its delicate floral halo. 

Nami Crown

Josephine Hairpin

Sometimes less is more. If you are looking for something a little more on the minimalist side, then the Josephine hair pin, created with just a piece of a mini white gypsophila, is for you. Handcrafted in our Parisian atelier, it is one of our iconic bridal hair pieces that could subtly but surely add a hint of sophistication to any bridal look, from bohemian to tradition to beach-themed style. created with just the perfect piece of a mini white gypsophila. 

Josephine hairpin

Gaïa

Gaia is the goddess of the Earth. And true to its meaning, the Gaia crown is an ideal piece for any bride looking for bridal hair pieces with a more laid-back, down-to-earth feel with its preserved baby’s breath garland you can either twist in a braid, wrap around a bun, or wear as a crown.

Gaia Crown

Charlie Earrings

The Charlie earrings are for a bold bride who loves a touch modern to her look. The pieces are designed with preserved white hydrangeas flowers and small pearls. Though a pair, the earrings are of different lengths, giving it that contemporary appeal to it. You can also wear it as a pair, or just one.

Charlie earrings

Chiara Hairpins

A Swarovski crystal softly rests on preserved white and golden Baby’s Breath flowers for our Chiara Hairpins. The result is a minimal yet sophisticated pin a bride can pin to the side of their hair. Whether you want to wear your hair in a chignon or ponytail, the Chiara Hairpin adds that subtle polish to any look.

Chiara Hair Pin

Naomi Earrings

Looking for something that would go well with most of our bridal hair pieces? The Naomi earrings are a classic pair that blends the bohemian-chic vibe with classic elegance in perfect fashion. Designed with white hydrangea flowers and Swarovski white opal crystals, pearly freshwater pearls, and a gold-plated ear post, the dangling pair is an effortless addition to any bridal theme, whether your wedding is set on the beach, the countryside, or a chateau.

Naomi earrings

There you have it, our 10 iconic bridal pieces for your wedding. All our accessories are made of real flowers from our Parisian workshop. Although everything is handmade in France, our looks are also available in other parts of the world such as Spain, Italy, Belgium, Austria, UK, Emirates, China, Singapore, Canada, and the US. We hope you find the perfect piece for your special. In the meantime, check out our website www.lescouronnesdevictoire.com to discover more.

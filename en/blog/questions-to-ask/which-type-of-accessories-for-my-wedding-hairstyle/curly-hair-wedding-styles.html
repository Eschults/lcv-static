Which accessories for curly hair wedding

Nowadays, wedding hair accessories are a must-have that guarantee beautiful wedding hairstyles. Veils are obviously still very trendy, but other types of accessories are becoming more and more prevalent.

Depending on your type of hair and on your hair length, some accessories might fit you more. If you have naturally curly hair or have decided to get a curly hairstyle for your wedding, this one is for you: from our flower crowns to our combs, here is our selection of accessories for curly hair wedding styles.

Flower crowns

Whether you have short hair, long hair, or shoulder length hair, our flower crowns are always flattering and instantly add that feminine fairy-like vibe to any kind of hairstyle. There is really no secret as to why the flower crown is our flagship hair accessory: it goes with any type of hair. Tight curls, loose curls, bouncy curls, or textured hair: every bridal with curly hairstyles will feel confident and beautiful in these hair jewels when walking down the aisle.

Clémentine

Far from being a classic headband, our flower crowns are handmade with naturally preserved flower to offer you a timeless and eternal accessory. They will also be your best ally if you like easy hairstyles. You could simply let your hair down just like the model in the picture above and place the tiara on your head. No need for a hairstylist and if your bridal hair gets messy, it will simply give off that bohemian vibe.

Talia

As you can see, our flower crowns are gorgeous on bob hairstyles. If you want to style this type of haircut, you could simply get your hair parted in a side part for a poetic asymmetrical look. If you have thin hair, our flower crowns will make your hair more voluminous. If, on the contrary, you have thick hair and don’t want to keep your hair down, our flowery headbands also embellish braided hairstyles or hairstyles with buns.

Hair clips

For effortless, flawless, cute hairstyles, hair clips are what you need. Delicately placed on one side or keeping two locks of hair together at the back of your head, they are a wonderful accessory for a romantic wedding. They are also a great adornment for bridesmaid hairstyles. They will be a great addition if you decide to style your natural curls in a bun or a braid.

For hairstyles for short hair, you could simply keep your hair down and place the hair clip on one side. For a minimalist look, you could go for our Swarovski crystals barrettes that are delicate and subtle. If you prefer statement accessories, our Naomi hair clip will definitely be a great choice as it will bring a glamorous touch to your wedding hair.

Naomi hair clip

Combs

If you are a bride-to-be who is planning a vintage wedding, combs are the perfect hair adornments. For wedding hairstyles for long hair, they are perfectly suited if you want to have your hair up.

You could go for updo hairstyles such as a low bun, a messy bun, a braided updo or a crown braid. Your curled hair will bring volume to such a hairdo. It will be a gorgeous hairstyle for an elegant wedding. If you have a backless gown, styling your hair in a bun is a great hair idea for a delicate romantic look. You could also opt for a half up half down bridal hairstyle. If you want some wedding hairstyles inspirations and ideas for using combs and double combs, simply browse through our website, it’s a treasure trove of information for wedding hairstyles and hair accessories.

If you fear your hair will get tousled during your wedding-day, simply use some hairspray to keep your updo in place and then gently attach the comb to the top of the bun for an airy bohemian touch.

Bérénice double comb

Hair pins

Hair pins are gracious, delicate hair accessories that will subtly embellish any hairstyle for a beautiful boho look. They beautifully enhance braided hairstyles such as a fishtail braid, a side braid, a French braid, or a braided ponytail. But if you want to put your curly hair in the spotlight, a half up half down hairdo is what you’re looking for.

Ornella hairpin

Just like the model in the picture above, an easy hairstyle would be to take two locks of hair from the front of your face – one on each side – and to twist them towards the back of your head. If you want, you could secure them with a clear elastic or bobby-pins. Simply pin the hair pins in your twisted strands of hair, with no specific pattern for a modern-day fairy look. If your hair is naturally curly, simply let the rest of your hair down; but if you have straight hair and want that beachy wavy hair vibe, the curling-iron is your best friend!

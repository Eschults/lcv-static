Our iconic wedding hair pieces

Looking for bridal hair accessories to adorn your wedding attire? Depending on your bridal style, whether your wedding dress is champagne or ivory, or if you are more of a pink roses or white flowers kind of girl, you could opt for vintage-inspired wedding-jewellery or maybe boho hair accessories. From flowery earrings to goddess crowns, discover our five most iconic wedding accessories.

Our Maria earrings

No need for intricate accessories, rhinestones, or glitter to make you the most beautiful bride-to-be: with their gold-plated metallic base, our handcrafted Maria earrings are a timeless piece of bridal jewelry. Their delicate hydrangea petals will give you the white flower look you are looking for. This type of earring can be worn with a chignon or with your hair down. Regardless of the hairstyle, you could ornate your beautiful hair with a hair clip, any type of combs or a birdcage veil to add even more elegance to your bridal attire.

Maria earrings

Our Jeanne crown

Composed of baby’s breath, tatarica, eucalyptus, heather and hydrangea flowers, this iconic wedding hair piece will give you the most graceful look to walk down the aisle on your big day. The pastel tones of this wreath associated with delicate green leaves are the ideal duo for a bohemian look. This floral headband will look beautiful on wavy hair. If you are planning a floral wedding, you could match the floral centrepieces and your bouquet with the flowers of this wedding headband. Subtle and delicate, you can also wear this flower crown at your bridal party. Our flowery headbands are also great wedding hair accessories for bridesmaids.

Jeanne crown

Our Yasmine earrings

These eye catching hand gilded earrings are the hair accessory you are looking for if you like glamorous, dazzling styles. If you want your earrings to be the statement accessory of your bridal look, opt for a minimalist hairstyle, wearing your hair in a low bun, making sure to hide the hair tie with a lock of hair. Whether you are wearing a tulle, an organza, or a satin wedding gown, these earrings will elegantly light up your face to give you a fresh and flawless look for your beautiful wedding.

For a sophisticated look, style your hair in a retro bun, the Yasmine earrings will bring a dramatic touch of glamour to your wedding attire. For even more sparkle, you could add a matching bridal headpiece such as our Yasmine bridal hair comb or our Yasmine hair clip. Our Yasmine boutonniere will complete the groom’s attire for an even more coordinated wedding style.

Yasmine earrings

Our Ondine crown

Another one of our iconic wedding hair pieces: our Ondine crown. Made of preserved white and golden ruscus leaves, this delicate tiara will make you look like a Grecian goddess making it the ideal hair accessory to add a twist to a vintage wedding. You can wear this bridal tiara like a real crown towards the front of your head to subtly frame your forehead, or you can wear it towards the back, styling your hair in a braided updo or a low chignon with the bridal headband delicately sitting on your updo like a halo. With its sparkling gold-leaf details and its white accents, this adornment acts both as a piece of jewellery and as a hair accessory. This gold leaves head band will look exquisite worn with a long flowy wedding dress, making your stand out on your wedding-day.

Ondine crown

Our Poema earrings

These hammered gold-plated earrings, adorned with preserved gypsophila and umbellifer, are part of our iconic “L’Odyssée” collection. Decorated with small white pearls, these earrings will embellish all hairstyles. Dainty and unique, these beautiful earrings are the perfect accessory for an elegant bridal look. Wear your hair in a messy bun – securing it with bobby-pins – and an embroidered wedding gown for a mermaid look. You could also opt for a bohemian braid or simply let your hair down, loosely curled. This would be the perfect look for a beach wedding.

If you want your bridal hairstyle to stand out more, associate your Poema earrings with another accessory with beads such as a hairpin, a barrette, or a wedding tiara. Browse through our website to find other beaded wedding hair accessories to match with your Poema earrings.

Poema earrings

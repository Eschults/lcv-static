Bride and bridesmaid hairstyles: How to match your bridal accessory to your bridesmaid

Matching looks adds sophistication to the bridal party. It also gives the entourage that unified and harmonious look, whether it's with a dress or hair accessories such as wedding crowns or hair clips. But aside from aesthetic reasons, did you know that the idea of matching looks for bridesmaids and groomsmen has a traditional origin? Back in ancient Rome, it was believed that evil spirits often come to crash weddings with not very good intentions. Yes, that’s right, mainly to break up the happy couple! To counter such a situation from happening, the bridesmaids and groomsmen would wear the same looks like the bride and the groom to confuse the uninvited guests, who are set on creating havoc on a special day. With the spirits not knowing who the real bride and groom are, this would give the couple enough time to actually finish their vows before the spirits even figure out who is who.

These days, the bridal party wears matching looks by design. And it’s usually based on a wedding theme. This is why deciding on a theme is one of the most important checklists for a wedding. Having a theme doesn’t just make the lives of brides easier with preparation, but also creates a state of harmony for everyone who is part of the special celebration.

One way to create matching looks for your bridal party is through matching the bridal accessory to the bridesmaid hairstyles. Whether you choose to wear a low chignon or a sleek bun to your party’s wavy loose curls (or vice versa), you can always create that sense of harmony through various hairpieces, from a crown to a hair clip to elegant hair combs. Here are some ideas:

A garden party

Combining romance and elegance, it’s no surprise that a garden wedding is one of the most searched wedding terms around. Even if you are not having your wedding in a garden location, you can still add a bit of that flower power to your special day through floral elements added to the bride and bridesmaid hairstyles. Choose from different types of flowers, from roses to baby’s breath to hydrangea, and make them match!

Season’s blooms

While we love matching looks, getting that harmonious look doesn’t always need to mean wearing the same exact accessories. If you are going for the floral look, similar to the one mentioned above, another way you can do this is to have different types of flowers but from the same season.

Going for a theme or trend

A theme or a trend is a creative, and perhaps subtle, way to match bride and bridesmaid hairstyles. You can go for hairpieces that are trending. For example, pearls are in at the moment. The bridal party can have a hint of pearl to their bridesmaid hairstyles. They don’t have to be similar in style but one could be wearing a hair clip with a pearl on it, while another bridesmaid wears her pearls as a crown.

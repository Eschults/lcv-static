Our wedding flower ideas

When it comes to organizing weddings and more specifically picking wedding flowers, you can either define your wedding theme and wedding colors and choose your seasonal flowers according to these; or, you can be inspired by your wedding centerpieces and floral arrangements to define your color palette and general theme. All flowers are beautiful, but not all of them will suit your bridal style. To help you pick the flowers that will be placed down the aisle for the big day, here is a selection of wedding bouquets that will embellish your wedding attire.

A romantic bouquet

Composed of pastel pink, old pink and dark red English roses, ivy leaves, pastel pink hydrangea and wild berries, our Ophelia bouquet is ideal to bring a blush of color to your ivory gown. Its beautiful flowers (especially its pink roses) are perfect for a romantic wedding while the ivy leaves add a real touch of greenery. If you don’t have centerpiece ideas but like this bucolic-romantic style, ask your florist to use garden roses or English roses to create your floral wedding decorations. A pink and red wrist corsage will enable you to harmonize your bridesmaids and flower-girl style with your own while the groom and his groomsmen can opt for a matching boutonniere with a single bloom of rose for example.

Ophelia bouquet

A vibrant colorful bouquet

While bouquets with some foliage and pastel tones are usually favored by the bride for a modern wedding, beautiful blooms with vibrant colors are a great option especially if you want to avoid monochromatic wedding bouquets. A colorful bouquet will match the festive spirit of a summer wedding but it can also brighten up the decorations of a winter wedding or replicate the orange tones of autumn if you’re having a fall wedding. For a spring wedding, why not opt for fragrant tulip floral centerpieces? Wedding florists always enjoy creating a colorful flower arrangement that stands out on wedding photos.

A pastel tone bouquet

If your dream wedding is an elegant wedding with pale colors and pink tones, bridal bouquets with pastel tones such as our Eugenia bouquet are what you are looking for. Made of hydrangea, baby’s breath and English roses tied together with a satin ribbon, this wedding bouquet looks like a hand tied bouquet and will delicately adorn your wedding gown. Decorate your wedding venue with different types of pale pink flowers to create your flower centerpieces and flower arrangements.

Eugenia bouquet

A classic bouquet

White roses (or any type of white flowers) accented with eucalyptus, ivy leaves or other types of foliage: that’s the secret for a beautiful bouquet that will enhance the bride’s outfit for a vintage wedding or a white wedding. These types of bouquets are ideal for a garden wedding or to incorporate some greenery to an all white wedding. If you want to accentuate the vintage vibe of your bridal bouquet, ask your local florist for a cascading one. Assorted wreaths will beautifully adorn your bridal hair and your bridesmaids’ hair while your future husband can opt for a boutonniere that matches the green and white style of your bouquet for a matching bride and groom look.

Naïs bouquet

A rustic bouquet

If you are more of a country wedding bride-to-be or if you want a bouquet with a bohemian wedding style, opt for a rustic bouquet like our Nami bouquet. With different textures and wildflowers, these bouquets really convey the rustic wedding feeling but can also match the vibe of a beach wedding. No matter the scenery, a rustic bouquet will make you look the prettiest for your beautiful wedding. Composed of umbelliferous, gypsophila, lagurus, ruscus, oat, pampas, fern, nigella, palm flower and roses, our Nami bouquet is a great starting point for wedding flower ideas for a rustic wedding. You can use it as the main source of inspiration to create your wedding flower arrangements placed in transparent or terracotta vases and even replicate smaller versions of this bouquet as bridesmaid bouquets.

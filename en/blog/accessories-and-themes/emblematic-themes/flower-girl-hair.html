How to accessorize your flower girl hair?

During weddings, flower girls always bring that “oooh so cute” moment that warms the heart of the wedding guests. Just imagine little girls in their cute dress, a sparkle in their eyes, walking proudly down the aisle and throwing petals before your big entrance on your wedding-day. See? We can almost hear you think “oooh so cute”. Now picture them with flowery crowns or headbands. Even cuter, right? The internet is full of inspirations for hairstyles for girls and our mission is to help you find the perfect hair accessory to adorn little girls’ hair.

A classic hair accessory: the headband

Headbands are super-cute wedding hair accessories to enhance girls’ hairstyles. It is an ornament that suits all haircuts: short hair, medium-length hair, as well as long hair. Whether the little girls have thick hair or fine hair, you could simply let their hair down and place the headband on their head, tying it in a pretty knot if you’ve picked a headband with a ribbon.

Clémence headband

Our Clémence headband is a flower headband that can fit all heads thanks to its elastic embroidered with a golden edging. Made of preserved white hydrangea flowers, this hair jewel is soft and delicate and will had a romantic twist to the little girls’ hairstyles.

Izia

Our Izia tiara is not your usual tiara. The classic headband accessory has been revisited with naturally preserved flowers to add a bohemian touch to wedding hairstyles. Girls can wear it with their hair down and a side part or, just like in the picture, if they have bangs already framing their face, they could tie their hair in a low ponytail with a cute hair tie.

A versatile accessory: the hair clips

Hair clips are also classic hair accessories and are very versatile. For easy hairstyles, simply brush the hair in a middle part or a side part and place the hair barrette on one side. For hairstyles for short hair, you could also place two hair clips on one side or one on each side for a retro vibe. For hairstyles for long hair, there are many hairstyles to choose from. The little girl’s hair could be styled in a bun such as a classic chignon, a braided updo or a ballerina bun for a voluminous hairstyle. Then all you need to do is pin the hair clip on one side of the updo. Braiding the girl’s hair in a fishtail braid, a crown braid, a Dutch braid, a waterfall braid, or a French braid and then pinning multiple hair clips in the braids will add a bohemian touch to the hairstyle. Buns and braids also prevent the hair from getting tousled. Although, to be honest, whatever the hairstyle, little girls will for sure get their hair dishevelled by the end of the wedding.If you lack inspiration regarding your flower girl hairstyle, you can find many hair ideas and tutorials online. But in the meantime, here are two hair ideas adorned with floral hair clips:

Lou Hair clip

In this picture, the classic chignon has been revisited in a messy bun. Simply create a bun as you would usually do, securing it with an elastic. Gently pull a few strands of hair to make it messy, pinning bobby-pins in as you go to keep it in place. Once you are satisfied with the shape of the bun, place the hair clip on one side on the upper part of the chignon. This hairstyle will add a vintage but trendy vibe to your flower girl’s hair.

Elisa Hair clip

This hairstyle is also an easy one: braid your little princess’ hair in two thin braids, one on each side, towards the back of the head. Secure them together with bobby-pins. Let the rest of her hair down, loosely curled for a boho effect. Place the Elisa hair clip on one of the braids to add a subtle pinch of elegance. To get the full effect, your flower girl could wear our Elisa wrist corsage that perfectly matches the hair clip.

A princess accessory: the flower crown

Flower crowns are the must-have hair accessory for modern-day princesses. Ours combine delicate preserved flowers with subtle leaves with a satin ribbon at the back to fit any head size, from babies to teenagers. We offer a wide range of flower crowns with different floral associations which means you could always find the perfect crown to match your wedding bouquet.

Any wedding hairstyle for your cute flower girls will be embellished with this hair adornment. Whether they are wearing their hair up, down, half up half down, curled or straight, our handmade flower crowns will bring a poetic fairy-like effect to the little girls who will for sure be the cutest and prettiest at the wedding.

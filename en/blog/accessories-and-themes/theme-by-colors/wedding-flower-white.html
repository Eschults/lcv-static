Wedding inspiration: our floral creations for a total white look

When we think about planning a wedding, most people think about the place, the food, and the music. Yet, choosing your flowers is a key ingredient for a beautiful wedding, especially if you want a floral wedding.

Wildflowers, tulips, orchids, garden roses, freesia, gerbera, peonies, or even succulents: the choice is not easy. A great idea would be to plan your wedding bouquet and your floral centerpieces according to your flowery hair accessories. If you are not keen on a colorful color-scheme, opt for monochromatic all-white bridal bouquets, accessories, and wedding flower arrangements. Whites are timeless and versatile. A spring wedding, a beach wedding, a rustic wedding, a summer wedding, an autumn wedding, a winter wedding: an all-white flowery decoration suits any wedding style. Discover our selection of wedding accessories such as our hair clips and headbands to help you find the immaculate all-white bridal style that will make you stand out on wedding photos.

The Ariane hair clip

Composed of a single gardenia bloom, our Ariane hair clip is unique and eternal, just like all our flowery accessories as they are naturally preserved to look like fresh flowers forever. For a natural, flawless bride look, pin the hair clip on one side with your hair loose. For a vintage wedding, style your wedding hair in a classic chignon or a sophisticated updo and pin the hair clip in it.

If you want seasonal flowers and wish to match your hair accessory with your wedding bouquet and your floral decorations, our Ariane hair clip is perfect for a summer wedding as gardenias blooming season spans from May to September.

Ariane hair clip

The Elene earrings

Made with stabilized hydrangea blossoms and freshwater pearls, the Elene earrings are the perfect flowery piece of jewelleryfor a minimalist yet feminine and delicate all white look. They will look wonderful with a long satin wedding gown and a cascading bouquet for a vintage-inspired style.

If you want to add a blush of color to your wedding reception, you could ask your local florist for blue, pink, or green hydrangeas and place them in white or ivory vases. The wedding centerpieces will still match your accessory as it would be the same species of flower and the white of the vases will enhance the vintage style of your perfect wedding.

Elene earrings

The Panarea headband

Embellished with a white satin ribbon embroidered with white hydrangea petals, our Panarea headband can be worn on loosely curled hair to be the prettiest when walking down the aisle. The satin of this wreath enables you to incorporate different textures to your wedding attire to give more depth to your all-white look.

Use the bohemian vibe of this head band to inspire the wedding centerpiece of your modern wedding and create wreaths of pastel flowers to decorate your tables, leaving the stems to add a touch of greenery to your decoration.

Panarea headband

The Blanche hair pins set

Adorned with gardenias and roses, this set of three white hair pins is the ideal hair ornament for a romantic walk down the aisle on your big day. Very versatile, you can pin each barrette anywhere you’d like in your beautiful hair. For a romantic hairstyle, create a braided bun and place each hair pin in the bun without following a specific pattern. For a more classic elegant wedding, style your hair in a low bun, fixing the hair pins around it just like in the picture below.

Roses are really easy to play with for your wedding decoration: ask your wedding florist for white roses in your beautiful bouquet, add pale tones like baby pink roses to your floral arrangement and sprinkle a few rose petals on the tables or even on your wedding cakes and you will have the most romantic wedding scenery. The groom could also match your white roses theme with a white rose boutonniere such as our Giulia boutonniere.

Blanche hair pins set

The Lily double comb

Don’t be fooled by its name, our Lily double comb is not made of lilies but is decorated with white English roses, everything you need to make you feel like a British queen. Its beautiful flowers will enhance any type of bun such as a braided bun, a low bun or a French twist. Its immaculate color is ideal for a white wedding. Assorted with a long white dress and a pearl necklace, this comb will bring a touch of elegance on your wedding-day.

Lily double comb

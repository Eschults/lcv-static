Wedding hair pins: your allies for wedding hairstyles

You’ve tried every headwear on Earth, every crown, comb, tiara, hair clip, headband and barrette but still cannot find the hair accessory that will match your bridal jewellery, leave the groom speechless, and make you feel dazzling and confident on your wedding-day?

From boho to vintage-inspired, here is a selection of our delicate hair pins. These hair accessories are very versatile and suit all hairstyles.

50 shades of pink for a romantic look

Whether you are planning on having a floral wedding or not, our pink and red wedding hair pins are a must-have for every romantic lover out there. Is there anything that screams love more than red and pink roses? A graceful hairstyle, a smooth skin, a touch of mascara, a hint of blush, a pink nude lip, red rose flowers in your hair and a sparkle in your eyes and you’ll look like you just stepped out of Pride and Prejudice.

Set of 8 Ella hairpins

Composed of preserved English roses and eucalyptus leaves, our Ella hairpins can be worn with any hairstyle. Combine them with our Gemma hairpins for a gorgeous mix of pink and red. Though the bridal hairstyle in the picture looks a bit intricate, you can recreate it very easily. Simply take one strand of hair on each side of your face and loosely braid them towards the back of your head. Secure the braids together with bobby-pins and curl the rest of your hair with a curling-iron. Pin the roses in your hair anywhere you’d like.

To complete your wedding attire, match your bouquet with the colours of the flower hairpins. You could even give red and pink rose petals to your flower-girls for a harmonious walk down the aisle.

Set of 6 Margareth hairpins

With preserved English roses, hydrangea, fern, and gypsophila, our Margareth hairpins have very vibrant colours that are balanced out by pastel tones. This creates a gorgeous pop of colour that will make you stand out on your big day. They will also delicately contrast with the ivory-white of your wedding dress.

Here, the model has her hair styled in a low bubble ponytail. If you wish to recreate this hairstyle for your wedding-day, simply tie your hair at the back of your head in a low ponytail, wrapping one strand of hair around the hair tie to hide it. Take two hair ties and place one around halfway through the ponytail, and the second one towards the end. Take two strands of hair from the front out of the ponytail so that they delicately frame your forehead and add a matching flower crown on your bridal hair for a more dramatic effect.

Our vibrant wedding hair pins are also wonderful for your bridesmaids or even for your flower-girl.

White flowers and green foliage for a bucolic style

If romantic glamour is not your cup of tea, maybe you should go for a more retro, vintage-style hair accessory. The combination of white and green creates a pastoral look, perfect for a rustic, country-style wedding but also for a poetic fairy-themed wedding.

Set of 5 Pimprenelle hairpins

Embellished with eucalyptus leaves, ruscus, stoebe, baby breath and hydrangea flowers, and adorned with pearls, our handmade Pimprenelle hairpins bring a touch of delicacy and a soupçon of poetry. They are the perfect wedding accessories for an elegant wedding. They will look lovely with your hair up in a chignon, secured with an elastic (and a bobby-pin if needed), with a tulle backless gown to delicately show your neck and back. If you have thin hair or shoulder-length hair, you can add a hairpiece for a more voluminous bun. Then, simply place each hair pin around the top of the updo in the shape of a wreath. If you have a veil, you could also use these hair pins to fix the veil on your wedding hair.

Going all-white for a classic hairstyle with a twist

If you prefer classic styles over shiny rhinestones, colourful flowers, or pastoral vibes, sticking to white and ivory wedding hair accessories is a wonderful solution to create a vintage but unique wedding hairstyle.

Set of 5 Daïna hairpins

Made with a gold-plated metal structure, freshwater pearls, and preserved umbellifer, our Daïna hairpins are both delicate and glamorous. They will enable the embellishment of wavy, curly, or straight hair, styled in a low ponytail or a braid for a bohemian effect. No need for sparkly bridal accessories to make you look like a queen, the dainty white pearl details of these hairpins will make you feel like a Grecian goddess when walking down the aisle in your airy wedding gown. Complete the look with our shell Nerina earrings for a full out-of-the-sea modern-day mermaid effect.

Set of 6 Ombelline hairpins

Adorned with preserved baby breath and ruscus and beads, our Ombelline hairpins are the allies of the elegant bridal who is planning a vintage wedding. Gently placed on a sophisticated or braided updo, these beaded bridal hair accessories will make you look graceful and full of elegance. Add vintage pearl earrings and a pearl necklace to complete this princely look.

If none of these styles suit you, you will find many more options on our online store: from hair pins with gold leaves to baby blue flowers, you’re sure to find the perfect hair pin for your beautiful hair. If you prefer glitter and sparkling hair accessories, you could opt for our hair clips encrusted with Swarovski crystals. Stop by our flagship store in Paris to discover our beautiful tiaras, hair combs, flower crowns, and wedding veils. It could even be part of your bridal party for a unique and very special moment with your bridesmaids.

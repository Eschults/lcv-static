Help: how to accessorize wedding hairstyles for thin hair?

The big day is coming and you’re still having the same struggle: you have thin hair, but you would love to have thicker hair and are wondering how to accessorize your wedding hairstyle.

First of all, rest assured: people with thick hair want thin hair just like people with straight hair want curly hair and people with curly hair are still obsessed with straightening them. Second, at Les Couronnes de Victoire, we have accessories for any style: from our hair pins to our flower crowns, we’ve got you covered. So, if you are looking for wedding hairstyles for thin hair, here are our top five hair accessories to make you the most beautiful bride the world has even seen.

Hair clips

Hair clips are a classic but timeless choice. They are the perfect fit for wedding hairstyles for thin hair as they subtly add a new dimension to your bridal hairstyle. Our flowery hair barrettes bring a romantic touch to any wedding hairstyle as well as a natural pop of colour. They also offer different options: you could part your wedding hair and clip your delicate hair clip on one side, or you could take two locks of hair back and secure them into place with the hair clip just like the model in the picture. And there you have it: a simple but cute hairstyle that will beautifully complete your bridal look and will make you the prettiest.

Lorella hair clip

Hair pins

Accessorizing your bridal hair with hair pins will also bring texture to fine hair. Just like our hair clips, they are perfect for a bohemian wedding, a romantic wedding, an elegant wedding or even a beach wedding. If you are wondering which wedding hairstyles for thin hair they would suit best, you could opt for braids. A fishtail braid, a loose braid, a French braid, a side braid, a rope twisted braid, a waterfall braid, a crown braid, a braided ponytail: any type of braid will be enhanced by our flowery hair pins. These types of hairstyles adorned with hair pins are very flattering on thin hair as they bring even more delicacy.

Set of 5 Daïna hairpins

Combs

If you prefer a more classic, elegant, retro, or vintage look, you could never go wrong with a bun adorned with a luxurious hair comb or double comb. Buns are amongst the best wedding hairstyles for thin hair as they immediately have a volumizing effect. If you want a more voluminous updo, you can always ask your hairstylist to add a hairpiece.

Combs are the perfect addition to buns and go with both formal hairstyles such as a sleek bun, a low bun, or a French twist but also with modern buns such as a braided bun or a messy bun. They will also enhance the feminine vibe that is already given by the chignon. Having your hair pulled-back in an updo hairstyle will also prevent your wedding day hair from getting messy or tousled.

Calice wide comb

Crowns

Our beautiful flower crowns are also the perfect hair accessory to bring volume to your wedding hairstyle. Made with preserved flowers just like the rest of our hair accessories they turn any hairdo into a poetic dazzling and glamorous hairstyle, immediately showing that you are the queen of the day.

They are also very versatile and complement all wedding hairstyles for thin hair, whether you have your hair down, your hair up or your hair back. They also beautify any type of hair. Yet, if you have straight hair and would like textured hair, you could ask your stylist, or your bridesmaids, to use texturizing hairspray. If you don’t have naturally curly hair, you could also curl them into loose curls or use your curling-iron to get a wavy look. Styling your loosely curled hair with a crown will complete your boho romantic look.

Now, we don’t wear crowns everyday but what better day to wear a crown than on your wedding-day? Our flower crowns will give you the boost you need to walk down the aisle with confidence.

Sirena crown

Hats

Last but not least, hats are also a great option to accessorize wedding hairstyles for thin hair. Of course, they are less conventional, but your wedding can be the occasion to be a bit adventurous with your style. Again, just like our other accessories, they are perfect to style thin hair as they add volume and structure to your hairstyle. They also work on long hair, medium-length hair, and short hair.

If you are unsure about matching a hat with your gown, hats are the perfect hair accessory for your Sunday morning wedding brunch. Just imagine waking up on a beautiful and sunny Sunday morning, finally married, ecstatic but also quite tired after all the dancing and celebrating. Don’t you think you’d wish you had planned an easy hairstyle to make you look flawless? Our Azalais boater hat will do just that: it is an easy to throw on statement accessory with a classy, chic but simple vibe.

Azalais boater hat

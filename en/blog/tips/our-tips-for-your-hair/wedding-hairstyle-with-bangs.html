Our inspirations for wedding hairstyles with bangs

Whether you are planning a bohemian wedding, a romantic wedding, a relaxed beach wedding ora more classic vintage wedding, finding the best wedding hairstyle is always a bit tricky as a bride-to-be and if you have a fringe, it can be even trickier. Especially when the internet is flooded with wedding hairstyles with bangs inspirations and tutorials, it can feel overwhelming. Luckily, at Les Couronnes de Victoire, we’re here to help.

Find out our inspirations for wedding hairstyles with bangs as well as our favourite accessories to style them with such as our beautiful flower crowns.

Olympia crown

 

Opt for a natural style: wear your hair down

When we hear wedding hairstyles, we often picture buns, braids, sleek ponytails, or fancy hairstyles. We usually don’t imagine ourselves with our day-to-day hairstyle on our big day. Yet, natural hairstyles are amongst the trendiest wedding hairstyles with bangs at the moment so wearing your hair down could be a great option to look flawless, glamorous and feminine.

It is a bridal hairstyle that goes with any type of hair whether they are straight, curly, frizzy, or wavy, as having bangs is already bringing structure to your hair and face. It also goes with any type of hair length: short hair, medium-length or long hair. If you are worried about your natural bridal hair not having enough of a wedding vibe, don’t worry: there’s nothing hair accessories can’t do.

Our flower crowns are your best allies to bring volume and elegance to your hairstyle. They will also add structure and texture and subtly highlight your bangs. The model in the picture is wearing our beautiful Camila flower crown with bangs and her natural straight hair down. If you don’t have natural curls and want even more voluminous hair, you can always ask your hairstylist or one of your bridesmaids to curl them into loose curls.

Camila flower crown

If you prefer a more classic hair accessory, veils are you go-to. Just like crowns, they add volume to your hairstyle. If you are having a winter wedding or a fall wedding, veils will also bring a poetic movement which is perfect for your beautiful wedding pictures. If you would like a vintage accessory with a twist, our Carmel veil is THE hair accessory you need. Subtly adorned with our stabilized coloured flowers, this veil will add an airy, romantic and elegant touch to both your wedding hair and your wedding dress.

Carmel veil

Braided hair don’t care

Braids have always been very popular when it comes to picking a hairstyle to walk down the aisle and wedding hairstyles with bangs are no exception. Depending on the type of braids you choose to get, braiding your beautiful hair could bring a retro, bohemian, elegant or beachy vibe to your hairstyle. In any case, having your hair back in a braid means you won’t have to worry about your hair being all over the place.

Amaya hair clips set

If you are lacking inspiration, Pinterest is your best friend for braided hairstyles: crown braid, waterfall braid, side braid, fishtail braid, French braid and even more. There are many ways to braid your hair, from easy hairstyles to more complex ones, so you will for sure find the one that will perfectly complete your wedding look.

To decorate your braided hair, hairpins are the perfect hair accessory as they are versatile so they can delicately adorn any type of braids. In the picture, you can see our elegant Ornella hairpins on a loose fishtail braid.

Ornella hairpin

If you wish to recreate this look, simply divide your hair into two sections, using a comb to be more precise. Starting from the outside part of the left section, take a thin strand of hair and place it in the right section. Repeat this operation on the right section and alternate between the two sections until all your hair are braided. Secure the end of your fishtail braid with a hair tie. Gently pull on each part of the braid to make it loose and messy. If you fear your bridal hairstyle will fall apart during the day and end up tousled, use a bit of good quality hairspray to fix everything into place. Pin your lovely hairpins in different places, not necessarily following a pattern, and there you have it: a gorgeous wedding-day hairstyle with your bangs framing your face.

A timeless option: the bun

The famous wedding bun or chignon doesn’t need to be introduced anymore. It is a timeless hairstyle that has been declined in many versions over the years to adapt to different trends. Buns also work very well for wedding hairstyles with bangs. If you have thick hair, they are especially great for those sunny but hot summer weddings as you will be able to enjoy breezes of fresh air on your neck with your hair up.

If you are going for a traditional, vintage or classic themed wedding you could choose to wear your hair pulled back in a low bun, a sleek bun, a high bun, a French twist, a ballerina bun or a braided bun. Yet, an updo can also be a great option if you prefer a boho, romantic or beachy look. You could simply style your hair in a messy bun or have one part of your hair in a bun and two locks of hair loosely curled at the front on each side of your bangs. Or you can go the opposite way, having your hair half up half down and with the upper part of your hair in a top-knot and the lower part loosely curled. If you wish to accessorize your wedding bun, combs and double combs will delicately ornate any type of bun and add a new dimension to your hairstyle.

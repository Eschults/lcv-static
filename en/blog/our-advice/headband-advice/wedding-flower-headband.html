How about a wedding flower headband for the big day?

At Les Couronnes de Victoire, we are specialised in handcrafted hair accessories and our flower headbands are a favourite among hair jewels. Whether you are planning a floral wedding or not, there is a flowery wedding tiara for everyone.

Some of them give off a retro vibe while others bring more of a bohemian or vintage touch. No matter the style, flower crowns are very trendy. So, if you want to add a flowery adornment to your bridal hairstyle, here is a selection of our flagship headbands that will make you the most beautiful bride.

The Victoria crown

Made of preserved eucalyptus, gypsophila, and ruscus, our Victoria crown is exquisite and delicate. This floral head wreath will add a subtle touch of colour to your wedding hair. Very versatile, it can suit any hairstyle. It will beautifully adorn an updo, such as a vintage-style bun or a more modern, messy bun. You could even opt for a braided bun to enhance the romantic vibe of your hairstyle. Simply secure your chignon with a clear elastic and bobby pins and gently place your graceful bridal headband on your head.

Victoria

The Flora headband

With preserved roses, eucalyptus leaves, fern and white Limonium flowers, the Flora headband is perfect for a romantic pastel-themed wedding. The association of pastel pink and delicate green foliage is a classic among bridal hair accessories. Whether you are wearing your hair down or styling them in a chignon, this wreath is a lovely adornment that will guarantee the embellishment of your wedding hairstyle. Its satin ribbon will enable you to adjust it to perfection. For a harmonious style, you could match the petals that your flower girls will be throwing when walking down the aisle on your wedding-day with the pink colour of your headband.

Flora

The Aïda crown

Embellished with gilded garden roses, rice flowers, hydrangeas and baby’s breath, this bridal tiara will make you feel and look like a Grecian goddess. With its gold-leaf details, it will sit on your head like a gold halo. Mounted on combs, you can wear this bridal headpiece like a real crown, or at the back of your head over a low bun or a vintage chignon. This magnificent handmade piece of jewellery with gold leaves will add a glamorous vibe to your bridal hair. Our crowns, such as the Aïda crown, can also be used to secure veils.

Aïda

The Rochelle tiara

Adorned with small, gilded coral branches, the Rochelle tiara is a delicate hair accessory that will bring glamour, elegance, and delicacy to your wedding hair. With its subtle and unique design, this headband is to be worn with your hair down, loosely curled for a fairy-like effect. Its gold coral details will be the perfect addition to your hairstyle for a bohemian beach wedding. This delicate filigree hair jewellery will go wonderfully with a vintage-inspired flowy wedding dress.

Rochelle

The Sirena Set

Combining our Sirena tiara with our Nerina comb, the Sirena Set is a statement wedding headpiece. With its gold-plated chain, shell and sun, freshwater pearls, gold metal combs, preserved flowers, this dazzling bohemian tiara will beautifully ornate any haircut. Whether you have short hair, long hair, or shoulder-length hair, its white beads and gilded details will delicately sit on your forehead for a dramatic effect. Wear it with your hair down, slightly curled and an ivory or champagne wedding gown to sublimate your wedding attire. It will be a gorgeous adornment for a winter wedding to give off that wintery goddess-from-the-North vibe. For a summer wedding, this beaded flower headband will make you look like a modern-day mermaid.

Sirena Set

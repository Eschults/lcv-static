Fall in love with our floral wedding earrings

They say flowers breathe beauty into the world. This rings so true for us here at Les Couronnes de Victoire. As you probably already know, flowers have always been a passion of ours. We believe that nothing complements the beauty of a woman more than the soft and ever-so-subtle touch of the season’s changing blooms. You name it, whether it is through a crown of hydrangea and baby's breath, a pretty pair of earrings made of English roses, or an elegant hair comb accessory adorned with mini gypsophila, we truly think that flowers possess this natural power to bring freshness, beauty, sophistication, and class to any style,  especially if it’s for a very special event such as the celebration of love and union of two people: a wedding! A flower, after all, is the symbol of not only purity, but also of a fresh new beginning.

 

Blossoming love

That said, we are truly proud that our floral wedding earrings are made from real organic flowers. From conception to design to completion, all our accessories are handcrafted in the Les Couronnes de Victoire workshop in Paris, France. This makes every item a unique piece, each with its own beautiful and original story. Our designers and artisans, in fact, take utmost care to carefully choose the flowers and design down to the smallest details for every jewelry. It is our wish to reveal the regal, the royal, and the magnificent that lie inside everyone through our collections. That’s why we are inviting all of you to come visit our Parisian boutique and discover the blossoms that await. Explore our collection of exquisite jewelry, which you can choose to wear in the day or in night. Our floral pieces also don't just bloom in spring, but we have a selection of floral styles you can wear for every season, and for every occasion. And if you’re looking for floral wedding earrings to wear, we have every style to match every bride (and bridesmaids!) and theme—whether it’s a bohemian wedding, a beach wedding, a winter wedding, or a woodland-themed soiree, we have every style that will surely add elegance and freshness to your whole look.

 

Our colorful Monica earrings is perfect for the bohemian bride

 

Everyone knows that a beautifully handcrafted earring is just the right piece to elevate an everyday look to something sophisticated. It’s that added oomph that certainly makes all the difference! A hoop earring or a simple stud is just about what it takes to bring an entire ensemble together. Not to mention that it also illuminates and frames the face, which is key to enhancing one's natural beauty. So we say don’t be afraid to give in to the beauty of our blooming floral wedding earrings. They are preserved, which explains their longevity and incredible resistance to time, and are handcrafted in our Parisian workshop by our dedicated and creative artisans, so there is no doubt that every piece is simply one-of-a-kind.

 

If you want to brighten up your best day, may we suggest our Ruby earrings? Adorned with preserved English roses and beautiful deep red Swarovski crystals, this style definitely brings shine and femininity to your wedding look. Small branches gilded with fine gold sit side by side scattered delicate flowers of baby's breath and crystals.  

Ruby earrings

 

Meanwhile, we also have the Doris earrings. These earrings are all about character, and it is perfect for any bride who wants to show a bit of fun and personality with loops mounted on sleepers for pierced ears (but they can be mounted on clips as well by request). 

Doris earrings

 

As for the Marais earrings, which have been inspired by our classic Agata earrings, shades of pink, deep red, and burgundy red hydrangea flowers intermingle with a 24-carat gold plated chain to illuminate one’s beauty. Our little tip? Wear it with your hair up and see it add a pinch of pretty to your whole ensemble. 

Marais earrings

 

Finally, from our collaboration with the Belgian wedding dress group Rembo Styling and Marylise, the Arizona earrings are all you need for that carefree feeling to your bohemian-themed wedding dress. It is adorned with black roses and hydrangeas, which makes that perfect addition to natural hairstyles worn loosely or in a boho bun. While it may look rather intricate, Arizona earrings are very light and easy to wear, and as with the other styles can also be mounted on a clip structure by request.

Arizona earrings

 

We have more styles in our online shop and brick-and-mortar boutique in Paris. And because we know that your wedding day is special, we want you to look and feel your very best. That’s why to get the look of your dreams, we suggest matching your floral wedding earrings to the theme of your wedding. We have an array of styles, from timeless classics to trendy and modern designs to pieces inspired by our very own designer Lauranne Favre. Check out our shop so you don’t miss out on the latest updates.

 

Flowers at its finest

At LCV, we are committed to creating the most beautiful designs made with only the finest flowers. Like our diverse flora, our collections are inspired by the diverse beauty of women all over the world and our range is designed to suit every age, every taste, and every style. While our home is Paris, our jewelry can also be found in other parts of France, as well as in Germany, Canada, Italy, Spain, Austria, the UK, the US, China, Japan, and Singapore. If you want to see our accessories, get to experience them in real life, and just try them on, you can make an appointment at our flagship store in Paris located at 230 rue Saint Martin in the 3rd arrondissement to get tips from one of our little fairies from Monday to Saturday, from 10:30 am to 7:30 pm or online. Our in-person appointments last about 45 minutes (or 30 minutes for a virtual appointment) during which you can try out different hairstyles with our accessories, discuss with our little fairies about your dream wedding, and possible personalization requests for you and your entourage. 

 

We really look forward to seeing you and to being part of your special day!

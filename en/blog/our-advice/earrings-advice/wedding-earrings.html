Must have: our iconic flowery hoop earrings for all your occasions

At Les Couronnes de Victoire, we are specialized in handcrafted flowery hair accessories that are naturally preserved to last eternally. From tiaras to hairpins, we have a wide variety of hair adornments. However, you should also checkout our trendy, feminine and delicate jewellery collection. For a start, here is a selection of five flowery hoop earrings for all your festive events but also for your everyday life.

Our Catalina earrings for a colorful look

Each Catalina hoop earring is composed of powder pink hydrangeas, pink and ivory gypsophila, achile, pastel purple Limonium and yellow acrolimnium on a gold-plated structure. These hook earrings can be worn on a day-to-day basis to add glamour and flowers to a summery outfit with a flowy dress for example.

They can also help you create a glamorous bridal look. As they are long earrings and statement earrings, they are best worn with a simple wedding gown and a delicate hairstyle without any other piece of bridal jewelry. On a bride, these flower earrings would work perfectly with their hair styled in a knot to let them dangle freely.

With the textured aspect given by the different types of flowers, our Catalina dangle earrings would also look gorgeous as a bridesmaid jewelry, especially if the colors of the wedding flowers match their pink and yellow tones.

Catalina earrings

Our Pasadena earrings for a unique piece of jewellery

These vintage-inspired pear-shaped earrings bring a twist to traditional hoop earrings. With their glass beads, gold plated chain and stabilized pink gypsophila, these beautiful earrings are the perfect wedding earrings for a vintage wedding. They can be worn on their own with a sophisticated hairstyle or, as they are very dainty, they can be assorted with a simple gold pendant necklace. If you want to stand out more on your wedding photos, you could adorn your bridal hair with any hair accessory that would match the gold-plated and delicate pink flowers of these earrings.

The glass beads that embellish each earring could also add a sparkle effect to any special occasion: a baptism, a Communion, a wedding or even a birthday party. They will for sure add a touch of elegance to your outfit and hairstyle while bringing a bohemian vibe.

Pasadena earrings

Our Liliana earrings for a romantic style

The lilac and purple tones of these romantic pearl earrings are perfect to brighten up your daily outfits with a poetic touch. Composed of a gold plated base hammered creole, gypsophila, glass and freshwater pearls, these shimmering earrings will embellish any autumnal outfit. As the rest of our dazzling flowery jewelry collection, their fresh flowers have been naturally stabilized to last until infinity and beyond. Thus, they constitute the ideal gift to give for a birthday for someone that is special to you.

Our Lilian earrings can also be worn as wedding earrings with a cultured freshwater pearl jewelry set such as a pearl bracelet and a pearl necklace. Worn with a long champagne or ivory wedding gown, the white pearl details of the bracelet and necklace will bring a vintage vibe to your outfit whilst the lilac and purple of the earrings will be the original touch that will make your wedding attire so unique.

Liliana earrings

Our Gigi earrings for an all-white look

Part of our Coachella collection, the Gigi sparkling hoops can be worn for any occasion. On a day-to-day basis, these gold earrings will add a touch of freshness to any outfit thanks to their white hydrangea flowers and gypsophila that dangle like little charms. Our handmade Gigi dangling earrings are the ideal finishing-touch for a summer white party in a garden. With your face framed with each earring creating a gold-tone halo, they can also be worn for a bridal party as the white of these earrings will foreshadow your white bridal outfit.

As wedding earrings, these Gigi earrings constitute a great alternative to more traditional pearl earring sets or beaded earrings that are usually picked as wedding jewelry. Light and aery, they will sublimate your wedding style and add a boho-chic vibe to it.

Gigi earrings

Our Iris earrings for a touch of greenery

Accented with mauve and white flowers, these vintage earrings will look exquisite with a vintage-style couture wedding gown like a simple but elegant silky dress encrusted with sparkly rhinestones on the upper part.

Perfect as bridal earrings, they can also be worn by bridesmaids or wedding guests for a romantic and glamorous look. Embellished with preserved baby breath and Limonium and mounted on a golden frame, they will help you create a fairytale vibe.

Iris earrings

Our most beautiful wedding earrings with delicate pearls!

Looking for the perfect gems to create your wedding jewelry set of dreams? If you love glamour and vintage, opting for some pearl jewelry is always a great idea. More than simple stud earrings, discover our selection of bridal earrings with pearls.

Dainty earrings for the romantics

Marianne earrings

Combining hydrangeas, iridescent freshwater pearls and a pear shaped gold plated structure, these hook earrings will delicately frame your face to make you feel like the most beautiful bride. To create your jewelry set, you can add a minimalist gold necklace as well as a gold bracelet to these pearl drop earrings. If you like a pearl and diamond style, why not opt for a necklace with a delicate diamond pendant?

Marianne earrings

Wendy earrings

Composed of white pearls and glass beans and accented with limonium flowers, these hoop earrings have been handmade to last for eternity and be kept in the family as a precious heirloom. Each white pearl brings a delicate shimmer to the gold-tone of these beautiful earrings. Associate them with a pearl bracelet to add the finishing touch to your pearl bridal look.

Wendy earrings

Bastille earrings

Delicate and original, these pearl stud earrings with heart-shaped pearls are a great alternative to more classic pearl studs. The little hearts of these 24 carat gold earrings will dangle at your ears like little charms. A dainty freshwater pearl necklace will elegantly finish your bridal attire.

Bastille earrings

Calypso earrings

Adorned with hydrangea and gypsophila, these freshwater pearl earrings will enhance any ivory wedding gown with a sparkle of elegance. Designed for pierced ears, they can also be made as clip-ons on demand. Chic and dainty, these dangling earrings will look exquisite with a pearl ring and a pendant necklace.

Statement earrings for the divas

Want to bring more bling to your bridal jewelry collection and try something different from classic studs, button earrings or a pearl pendant? Here is a selection of statement earrings that will for sure make you feel like the queen of the day (and of the night).

Poséïdon earrings

With their gold shell encrusted with freshwater pearls as well as their hoop adorned with hydrangea and gypsophila flowers, these pearl earrings will make you look glamorous and flawless for the big day. As statement pieces, you can wear them on their own or, if you want an even more dramatic bridal look, you can combine them with our matching Poséïdon comb for example.

Poséïdon earrings

Montorgueil earrings

Inspired by autumn, these fashion earrings have a hint of baroque in them, just the perfect amount to make you look dazzling on your wedding-day. With your face framed by their yellow-gold leaves and freshwater pearls, you will be sure to wow the groom and the guests when walking down the aisle. Each earring being quite eccentric, no need for a necklace or a bracelet; but if you really want to, a simple pearl necklace will beautifully compliment these sparkling drop earrings.

A twist on pearls: glass beads

If pearls are not your cup of tea, glass beads might be what you are looking for. More modern and discreet, a glass bead is still as chic and elegant as a traditional freshwater pearl.

Marisa earrings

Looking like a shimmering halo, these gold stud earrings are adorned with glass beads that are as shiny as Swarovski crystals. Quite dainty, you can wear them on their own but also with a flower crown, a gold hair comb, a barrette or any other bridal hair accessory. If you want even more glass beads, our Claire chandelier earrings are a great option to consider.

Pasadena earrings

These gold flower studs with a pear-shaped structure adorned with glass beads and pink gypsophila are the perfect piece of jewelry to bring a touch of color to your bridal attire. These glittering beaded teardrop earrings will embellish any wedding gown, especially the ones with a bohemian style.

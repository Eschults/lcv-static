Our sparkling wedding pieces of jewellery to shine bright on your big day

Whether they prefer silver jewellery or gold jewellery, picking a jewellery set for the big day is an important choice for a bride. Wedding rings are a great starting point to get an idea of what would marvelously complete your diamond engagement ring. Your wedding theme will also help you choose accessories that will give off the energy you’re looking for. If you like shiny accessories, here is a selection of some of our most sparkly bridal accessories from earrings to double combs.

The Scarlett earrings

With their gold plated structure, their crystal stones and their grey and pink transparent glass beads, these dangle earrings will make you look radiant and exquisite on your wedding-day. They will beautifully complement a simple diamond ring. If you want to add another element to your bridal jewellery, a simple sparkling wedding necklace will be ideal.

The Montorgueil earrings

These gorgeous vintage-inspired gold plated clips of 24 carats 3 microns adorned with freshwater pearls bring a bold twist to the more traditional white pearl earrings that are usually worn at weddings. If you want to walk down the aisle the vintage way, wear a long ivory gown and complete your bridal earrings and gold ring with a pearl bracelet and a pearl necklace. If a complete pearl jewelry set is not your cup of tea, you can complete your jewelry collection with very simple gold plated pieces like a pendant necklace for example.

Montorgueil earrings

The Claire earrings

These transparent crystal tones silver earrings will dangle at your ears like little charms and sparkle during the whole wedding to make you look like a star. With each earring looking like a vintage-style chandelier, these wedding earrings will fit in any jewelry set as long as your wedding band is made of silver too and even then, if your perfect ring is golden, you could totally go for a mix and match look. As part of your wedding jewelry, a rhinestone necklace would give off the same sparkly energy as these very unique earrings.

The Pasadena earrings

Teardrop or pear-shaped, our Pasadena earrings bring a twist to the traditional hoop earrings. Compared to the previous ones, these are coloured and will therefore bring a touch of colour and light to a pale-toned bride-to-be for example. Very trendy with their bohemian style, you can wear them with a bracelet and a necklace which also have a touch of pastel colour to have a harmonious set of accessories on your wedding-day. Dainty and delicate, these earrings can be part of your bridal jewelry but can also be worn by a bridesmaid or a wedding guest.

Pasadena earrings

The Poseïdon tiara

Place this beautiful headpiece in your bridal hair to instantly look like a shiny ocean queen. The freshwater pearl effect of this handmade tiara will be perfect to complete a vintage wedding-ring, a pearl bridal necklace and an accumulation of multiple thin bracelets. A champagne wedding dress will be wonderfully brightened up by this golden bridal jewelry set.

Poseïdon tiara

The Stella tiara

Adorned with preserved baby breath flowers, Swarovski crystals, and pearls, this beautiful, beaded tiara will add elegance to your wedding attire making you look like a dazzling princess, enough to leave the groom speechless. If you want to complete your bridal set with more gold jewelry, simple drop earrings and a gold necklace will form the perfect set. Like other wedding accessories such as flower crowns, tiaras can be associated with veils to add a more traditional aspect to your bridal style.

Stella tiara

The Ilana double comb

Composed of preserved baby breath and hydrangea, and gold plated leaves, flowers and chain, our Ilana double comb will sit delicately on your head like a halo. Quite versatile, this type of hair combs can be worn like a tiara towards the front or towards the back like a more classic comb. If the wedding jewelry set you have in mind for your perfect wedding is dainty and subtle, avoid a statement necklace and opt for a simple gold plated chain or one with small beads.

Ilana double comb

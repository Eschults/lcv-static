Our iconic floral wedding crowns for a bucolic and romantic wedding

While veils are still very popular for weddings and wearing a hair clip, a comb, or a hair comb is very trendy, floral crowns are among a bride-to-be favorite bridal accessories. Depending on your bridal gown, you might opt for a specific style or specific colors to be sure to feel the most beautiful when walking to the altar. Worn on their own or with a wedding veil, discover our selection of flowery wedding crowns that will look especially great for a bucolic and romantic wedding.

Our Faustine crown

Made of a golden brass wire adorned with peach and powder pink hydrangeas, mini green and pink gypsophila and yellow achillea, our Faustine crown is ideal for a bohemian beach wedding. This type of headpieces can suit any type of gown whether it is a sober satin wedding dress or a more sophisticated embroidered one. This wedding accessory can be worn with several hairstyles such as on loose hair, with a crown braid, a fringe or any type of bun. This delicate hairband will look especially dazzling on gold hair or copper hair with a champagne or ivory dress for your perfect wedding.

Faustine

Our Wendy crown

Embellished with hydrangea, ruscus, munni and pearls, this bridal tiara is mounted on gold plated combs to enable the bride to wear it either towards the front like a bridal headband or towards the back like a double comb. For a majestic look, the type that can be worn by princesses at a royal wedding, wear it with a flowy organza or tulle wedding gown that will make you look aery for the big day.

For a vintage wedding, opt for a loose hairstyle and an all-white look with some white bridal jewellery like dainty beaded earrings for example. Wearing your wedding headband like a diadem and enhancing it with assorted bridal jewelry will make you look even more like royalty. This all white style would also be perfect for a winter wedding. Assort it with our Wendy sash to brighten up your bridal look. For a bride and groom matching vibe, our Wendy boutonniere will be ideal to ornate your husband’s suit.

Wendy

Our Ornella crown

Adorned with small preserved gypsophila, tatarian honeysuckle, white roses and eucalyptus leaves, our Ornella crown is the ideal flower crown to create a glamorous and intricate look to walk down the aisle with a sparkle in your eyes on your wedding-day. To make the most of this wreath, ask your stylist for a vintage-style updo secured with bobby pins and place the oval shape of this ornament like vine around your chignon to complete your bridal style.

White and green bridal hair accessories like this one are ideal to ornate the bride’s hair for a romantic wedding as adding some foliage definitely brings a bucolic vibe to any hairstyle. Quite sophisticated, this bridal headpiece will look wonderful with a couture wedding gown with a long train.

Ornella

Our Flora crown

With its eucalyptus leaves, fern and white limonium flowers accented with roses, our Flora crown epitomizes romanticism. The exquisite light pink roses of this floral headband will embellish any vintage-inspired wedding look. For a very princess fairy vibe, opt for an organza wedding dress, a cascading bouquet adorned with foliage and place your handmade hair accessory over a sophisticated bun. If you want to enhance the vintage vibe of your bridal look, don’t forget that wedding crowns can be assorted with wedding veils.

As all our flowery hair accessories have been naturally preserved to last for eternity, you can keep your headpiece as an heirloom after your wedding and even lend it or give it to your bridesmaid for her future wedding as her “something borrowed”.

Flora

Our Jeanne crown

Looking for a delicate but dazzling flower headband to bring elegance to your boho bridal attire? Our subtle Jeanne wedding tiara will sit on your bridal hair like a halo of flowers to create a flawless look for your beautiful wedding. Again, you can complete this adornment with a veil to add a vintage touch to your hairstyle. Made with baby’s breath, tatarica, eucalyptus, heather and hydrangea, this headband can serve as an inspiration for your other wedding decorations such as your floral arrangements or even to design your wedding invitations.

Enhance your style with sober jewelry like bead earrings or a delicate white pearl bracelet as the final touch to your wedding attire. If you want to match your bridesmaid’s style, add our Jeanne wrist corsage as part of your bridesmaid jewelry set. Our Jeanne mini comb will also be ideal to adorn your lovely flower-girl hair.

Jeanne

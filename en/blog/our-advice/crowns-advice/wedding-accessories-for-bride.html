Our 5 iconic crowns to say “I do”

Veils, combs, earrings, hair pins, bracelets, necklaces, handbags, hats, … picking accessories for weddings is often quite difficult. Not to mention buying delicate lingerie, composing your bouquet, deciding if you want to wear a shawl or not and, most importantly, making a choice between all the beautiful wedding gowns that exist. Lucky for you, our little fairies are here to help you! If you have decided to have hair accessories to complete your wedding attire, Les Couronnes de Victoire is the place to be. In this article, you will find out more about our wonderful handmade crowns that will add the finishing-touch to your big day.

The Camila crown

Adorned with yellow and peach roses, pink wild roses, eucalyptus, purple Limonium, red carnations and pink bougainvillea, this flower crown tied with a satin ribbon will guarantee the embellishment of all the brides, no matter your bridal style. Whether you are planning a floral wedding or not, a flowery bridal headband is always a great idea to add a touch of nature and, in this case, a blush of color to your bridal hair.

Among all the wedding accessories for bride, a wedding headband is always a great choice to bring elegance to your wedding-day hair. Worn on loose hair, our colorful flowery tiaras are ideal to create a bohemian summery look.

Camila Crown

The Stella crown

With its gold-plated structure, baby breath flowers, Swarovski crystals that sparkle like rhinestones, and white pearl details, our vintage-inspired crystal tiara will make you look and feel like a real-life princess for your elegant wedding. If you are planning a vintage wedding, you can arrange your hair in an updo, wrapping a strand of hair around it to hide the hair tie and place the sparkly bridal jewelry on your hair to look even more royal.

Worn on loosely curled hair with an organza wedding gown, our dainty rhinestone and beaded hairband will give you a dazzling fairy vibe when walking down the aisle.

Stella Crown

The Eudora crown

Composed of a white embroidered tulle birdcage veil, preserved hydrangea and baby’s breath, this headband can be worn as a crown or bent in half like a wedding tiara to create a unique look. Though this bridal headpiece can be worn with many hairstyles, wear it with your hair down to have a glamorous and graceful style.

This bridal tiara will wonderfully ornate your wedding attire, especially if you have an embroidered couture like ivory gown that will match the intricate vibe of this hair accessory.

Eudora Crown

The Daisy crown

White flowers and delicate foliage always create exquisite bridal accessories that sit on your bridal hair like a green halo accented with immaculate flowers. Our Daisy floral headband is no exception. Embellished with jasmine flowers, eucalyptus leaves, fern, and Limonium flowers and mounted on gold plated combs, this boho flower headband is among the most fairy like wedding accessories for bride.

Like the rest of our flowery hair accessories, this flower headband is everlasting as its flowers have been preserved through a natural process to make them look like fresh flowers for eternity. As it is mounted on combs, depending on your hairstyle, it can be worn at the front like a classic crown or at the back like a hair comb. Add matching accessories for your bridesmaids to create a stunning group of fairies for your beautiful wedding.

Daisy Crown

The Sirena set

Part of our “L’Odyssée” collection of bridal hair accessories, our Sirena set combines our Sirena tiara with our Nerina comb. This fascinating adornment will sit wonderfully on your forehead, creating a real-life mermaid look for your wedding hair. With its sparkling queen of the ocean effect, this is the perfect accessory for a beach wedding.

Composed of a gold-plated chain, labradorite stones, gold-plated shell and sun, freshwater pearls, gold metal combs, hydrangea and roses flowers, gypsophila, lagurus, ruscus, nigella, pampas and angel hair, this jeweled tiara is among the most sophisticated wedding accessories for bride in our collections. Worn on loose hair (loosely curled or straight), this wreath will look gorgeous with a long flowy champagne or white satin wedding dress.

Sirena Set

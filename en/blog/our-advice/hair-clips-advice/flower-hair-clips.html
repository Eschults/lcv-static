Discover our flower hair clips

Are you looking for a graceful hair jewellery for your wedding, your best friend’s wedding, or simply looking for a way to twist up your daily looks? Say no more, we’ve got you covered.

Our flower hair clips can work both as bridal hair accessories and as day-to-day ornaments. Whether you are more of a daisy flower girl or a rose flower girl, our delicate hair barrettes will be the perfect addition to embellish your beautiful hair.

Hair clips for a bride-to-be

As a bride-to-be, we can easily feel overwhelmed by all the hair accessory possibilities: veils, combs, double combs, hair pins, flower crowns, tiaras, headbands … That’s a lot! It can be especially hard to pick hair jewelleries if you are not yet decided on your bridal hairstyle. If that’s the case, hair clips are the solution you’re looking for. They are very versatile and can suit any haircut and hairstyle. Even if you decide to change your wedding hairstyle last minute, you will always be able to ornate your wedding hair with a hair clip or a set of flower hair clips.

Whether you prefer a gold-leaf barrette, a sparkly one or a retro hair clip, we have a large of collection of flower hair clips for your bridal hair. You could even match your hair clip with the flowers of your bouquet.

Margot hairclip

Composed of baby breath and hydrangea flowers, and pearls, our dainty Margot hairclip is a vintage-inspired hair clip that will guarantee the embellishment of your hairstyle. If vintage-style is not what you are looking for, you can wear it with our Swarovski crystal Chrystal hairclip for a more fashion-forward look. Whether you are wearing an organza gown, a tulle one or a satin one, the combination of these two hair clips will give a subtle shine to your wedding attire.

You can wear the Margot hairclip with your hair down, simply parted in a side part, placing the hair accessory on one side for a poetic hairstyle.

Set of Calypso hair clips

With their gold-plated structure, freshwater pearls, and preserved hortensia and gypsophila, our Calypso hair clips will be the perfect bridal hair accessory for a bohemian look or a vintage wedding. If you are planning on having your hair up, you will be able to clip these beaded hairclips as you wish thanks to their metallic alligator structure.

These ornaments will be perfect to adorn any style of bun such as a classic chignon, a braided bun, a French twist or even a half bun. You can combine this set of flower hair clips with an ivory or champagne flower crown or veil for an even more elegant hairstyle. What’s better than wearing flowers in full blossom to mark the start of something new?

Hair clips for a bridesmaid

Flower hair barrettes are also wonderful accessories for bridesmaids. As white or ivory is usually for the bride, we have a large collection of colourful flower hair clips from pastel pinks to more vibrant colours. You can also have the same hair clips as the bride with her wearing a veil, a comb, a tiara or a crown to stand out more.

Set of 6 Amaya hair clips

Our handmade Amaya hair clips are adorned with old pink and peach wild roses and powder pink hydrangea. These pink flower alligator-clips are ideal to wear with your hair back in a braid or an updo. These hair accessories with light pink flowers in bloom can be assorted with the Amaya earrings or wrist corsage to complete your bohemian look. They will give you a very harmonious and dazzling style if you are wearing an old pink or a terracotta dress.

Hair clips for your everyday look

If you are neither a bride-to-be nor a bridesmaid, hairclips are still very trendy for other occasions such as a bridal party, a birthday, a baptism or a communion. These hair accessories can also be worn in your day-to-day life to twist things up a bit.

Inès & Rose hair clip set

With its fuchsia pink Swarovski crystals, the jewelled Inès hair clip is perfect to break with the boho style of the flowery Rose hair clip. Simply pin two strands of hair at the back of your head, placing the hair clips one above the other to create a bohemian-rock look. The sparkling rhinestones will add just enough shimmer or sparkle while the delicate flowers will bring a sprinkle of delicacy.

Winnie hair clip

If you enjoy a pop of colour every now and then, our Winnie hair clip, embellished with preserved hydrangea flowers, immortals, gypsophila and field flowers, will brighten up your hair as much as your day.

Whether you have fine hair, thick hair, straight hair or curly hair, this hair barrette will be gorgeous on a low ponytail secured with a clear hair tie or simply placed on one side with your hair loose.

If you are not a flowery girl, we also have many other types of hair clips: rhinestone ones, pearl ones, ones with a velvet or tulle ribbon tied in a bow, a butterfly, a shiny star, a moon, some with a sparkling effect, …

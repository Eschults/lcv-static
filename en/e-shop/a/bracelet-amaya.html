Amaya wrist corsage

Wrist corsage Eternal Preserved flowers
32€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Amaya bracelet, as delicate as the crown that bears the same name, will give your witnesses the flowery touch to match your accessories on the big day. In powdery tones and composed of an assortment of delicate flowers, it will be the perfect gift to offer to your witnesses as a souvenir for your wedding day.

  • Composition: ruscus, pink gypsophila, powder pink hydrangea, phalaris, nigella, wild peach roses, lagurus and bougainvillea stabilised
  • Size: unisize
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this wrist corsage are eternal
  • Storage instructions: keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 days from order

California Dreaming is our great novelty, a collection inspired by the bohemian, hippy, countryside, glamorous and romantic 70’s. We offer you handmade, original, fancy, elegant, customizable, delicate and refined accessories, to be worn as a set or alone for all occasions and for infinite looks

The Amaya bracelet is a pretty fantasy bracelet, decorated with ruscus, precious pink gypsophila, powder pink hydrangea, phalaris, nigella, wild peach roses, lagurus and stabilised bougainvillea, a cameo of pink, making it a fashion accessory for your bridesmaids, delicate but discreet. Mounted on a satin thread, the bracelet is adjustable and can be personalised according to the size of your bridesmaid’s wrist. Its flowers are minimalist and very delicate, making it a timeless bracelet for a wedding jewel.

For a rustic and minimalist wedding, this bracelet made of small flowers will be the perfect, ideal gift to sublimate the wrists of your witnesses and bridesmaids, the wedding bracelet is an ode to femininity in the air of time. It can be worn as a set with the Amaya earrings and the comb of the same line, to create a lace effect on your hair. You can also decide to dress up your man’s suit with the matching buttonhole.

At Les Couronnes de Victoire, we handcraft a vast collection of floral accessory jewellery, including pendant necklaces, bracelets/sleeves, flower crowns, barrettes/knots/elastics, hoop earrings and dangling earrings (gold and silver plated), sleeper earrings, stud earrings, rhinestone and glitter earrings, flower earrings, pearl earrings, multicoloured earrings, geometric earrings, asymmetric earrings, crystal earrings, twisted earrings with elegant flowers, openwork earrings or matching earrings… Everything is done to offer you beautiful, chic and sophisticated gifts. For men, we offer buttonholes to wear as sets with your crowns or earrings. If you can’t come to Paris, don’t worry: for your orders, all our accessories are customizable, allowing you to decide the colour of the metal (gold, silver) or the colour of the petals (purple, blue, turquoise, coral), to add rhinestones, glass crystal, pearls, mother-of-pearl to shine on the big day. Our precious pairs of feminine and original earrings include various shapes (cubic, oval/losange, round) and are adjustable: their clasps can be mounted as clips for non-pierced ears.

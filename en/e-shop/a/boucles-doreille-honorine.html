Honorine earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
100€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Discover our floral creoles designed for the holidays with Louise Misha, adorned with hydrangea and gypsophila stabilized and hand-gilded you will shine until the end of the night! Pair them with an elegant blouse for a festive dinner or a dress with nice volume for a dance party. It is the perfect jewel to sublimate your party outfits or to be the most beautiful to go dancing.

  • Composition: preserved gold hydrangea flowers and gypsophila, gold-plated structure
  • Size: 4 cm (or 5 cm on request)
  • Shelf life: the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions: keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 days from order

From the capsule imagined with the pretty house Louise Misha are born the Honorine earrings, a pair of earrings that will illuminate your winter and summer looks. This collection of original accessories has been designed to sublimate little girls and their mother, with glamorous pieces in golden tones. A great way to shine for the holidays and create matching mother-daughter sets.

Honorine earrings are mounted on gold-plated brass hoop earrings. The sleeper clasps make these earrings pretty bohemian earrings. The earrings are adorned with hydrangea and gypsophila stabilized and hand-gilded. No matter what your hairstyle, these fancy earrings will add a sophisticated touch to your outfit. For women who don’t have pierced ears, it’s entirely possible to mount Honorine earrings on clips. At Les Couronnes de Victoire, we want all women to be able to wear earrings! Put away your discreet earrings and dare to wear them to dress up your head! If you’re looking for a gift for your sweetheart, fiancée, sister or friend, this is the perfect gift! Elegant and timeless, these creole earrings can be worn for the holidays but also for all occasions. They will express your femininity and accessorize a simple look and sublimate your head.

Let yourself be tempted by the Honorine earrings, our new winter collection for an ultra shiny trendy look! For the holidays, dare to use bright and shiny ornaments such as sequins, crystals, gold and silver to shine as gold earrings!

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a French company that makes hair accessories with stabilized flowers. Our creations are for women, men and children! For all occasions, we sublimate your hair and outfits with our flowery creations, but also with precious stones, pearly pearls, crystals, sequins and other ornaments for a beautiful setting! Whether for you or for a birthday gift for example, fall for our women’s accessories and in particular our earrings set with stabilized flowers and hand-gilded. Find on our e-shop, our vast collection of original earrings, we are sure that you will find a model which will please you! We also make other sophisticated and bohemian costume jewelry such as bracelets or rhinestone hairclip.

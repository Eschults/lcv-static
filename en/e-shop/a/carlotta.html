Carlotta

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
190€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Inspired by our muse for this “Dance with me” collection, the Carlotta crown represents the perfect blend of prima ballerina grace and the character of a strong iconic piece. With its tiara shape, become the queen of your big day.

  • Composition : white preserved hydrangea and gardenia, gold metal structure
  • Size: one size
  • Shelf life: eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

The Carlotta crown is from our beautiful new “Dance with me” collection. Inspired by our muse, this radiant piece of women’s jewelry will brighten up your outfit while affirming your bohemian, joyful, glamorous and romantic personality. Its sumptuous immaculate white will make you the queen of the day. Our hair accessories will sublimate you and accessorize your wedding outfit. Full of refinement, the crowns set with flowers like the Carlotta crown, can be worn for all occasions. Unlike a crown set with rhinestones, pearls, crystals or other precious stones… Wearing flowers is a beautiful combination of jewelry and fantasy. The eternal flowers that make up this costume jewelry are a true ode to love and femininity… Because it is adorned with flowers, you will be able to wear your ornament for a wedding as well as for a pretty occasion. As precious as a gold jewel, a flower crown is a timeless jewel. It will make you shine for pretty occasions. And to bring you even more shine, let yourself be tempted by earrings.

For a bohemian wedding, a refined flower crown or a golden jewel such as our Electre flower crown with princely allure, can be proposed to you. A more delicate hair accessory, composed of small flowers, white flowers, crystals, fresh water pearls or fine stones could be proposed to you according to the style you wish to adopt for your wedding. If you are hesitating for your head jewels and do not know for which of these accessories to choose to bring the final touch to your outfit, we are waiting for you at Les Couronnes de Victoire. Les Couronnes de Victoire is a company that makes fashion accessories from stabilized flowers for weddings, parties and everyday wear. Our jewelry and accessories include bun picks, earrings, bracelets, tiaras, a veil, hats, headbands, barrettes, combs and of course flower crowns. Our accessories are not only for women who are getting married, we have accessories that can be worn by bridesmaids, bridal party, groom… All accessories are customizable, you can add or remove ornaments of types: white flowers, rhinestones and sequins, white pearls, gemstones, crystals…It all depends on your theme: chic, glamorous, fancy, hippie and the colors of your wedding or party: purple, silver, gold. If you don’t know how to wear and position these accessories in your hair you can follow us on social networks to discover our tutorials. You will find them with long hair, short hair, with a braid, a bun, or even on a semi-attached.

Melrose earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
85€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our Melrose earrings are inspired by Californian sweetness with their delicate pink marcela flowers, not unlike our iconic Blanca earrings. They are the discreet but flowery touch that will go with your most beautiful hairstyles. These earrings can be mounted on clips on request.

  • Composition: gold-plated structure, stabilised pink marcela and powder pink gypsophila
  • Size: 1,5 inches
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions : keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order

California Dreaming is our brand new wedding collection! It is a perfect combination of the pop and colourful Californian universe and the hippie, bohemian and country wedding inspirations of the 70s. This new collection is bright, with accessories in pastel tones and warm colours. Here are the very feminine and elegant Melrose earrings. You are not dreaming, they are our Blanca earrings revisited in soft Californian colours. Adorned with delicate pink marcela and powder pink gypsophila, our hoop earrings will accessorize, sublimate and dress up your outfits. Melrose is a pair of sober and discreet earrings. The setting of the hoop earrings is gold-plated but you can ask for a personalization by choosing a silver-coloured ring instead of gold. It is also possible to replace the earrings with ear chips and clips, if you don’t have pierced ears. With Melrose, fantasy, glamour and femininity will be on duty. To enable you to find the accessory that perfectly matches your personality, customisation is also practiced in terms of the colours of the flowers and ornaments that you can add or remove as you wish on our models. Indeed, it is possible to add pearls, diamonds, crystals, sequins, a cultured pearl, precious silver or crystal stones, rhinestones, mother-of-pearl and other ornaments. Or, you can change the colour to turquoise, coral, green or blue flowers… All occasions are good to wear Melrose in the evening as well as during the day, they will be your ally. If you don’t like hoop earrings, no problem, California Dreaming has dangling earrings. Let yourself be tempted by our women’s jewellery to offer or to give as a gift. A pair of earrings can be a nice gift for a birthday present for example. Or simply treat yourself and add these original earrings to your extensive collection of earrings in your jewellery box.

Les Couronnes De Victoire makes accessories and jewellery for life’s special occasions with stabilised flowers. Come and meet us at 230 rue Saint Martin, 75003 Paris.

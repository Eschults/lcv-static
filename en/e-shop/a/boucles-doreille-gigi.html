Gigi earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
95€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

One of the highlights of our Coachella collection, the Gigi hoop earrings are inspired by festivals and the hippie wave of the 1960s. These earrings will add a touch of style to a country or summer outfit with their dangling chain and delicate bouquets of hydrangea and gypsophila flowers.

  • Composition: gold-plated structure and chain, preserved hydrangea flowers and white gypsophila
  • Size: 6cm
  • Shelf life: the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

The Gigi earrings are not dangling earrings but a pair of hoop earrings, part of our new hippie bohemian collection: Coachella. They are fancy earrings, set with discreet flowers, which will allow you to wear them for any occasion as well as for a special moment. Accessorise yourself with flowery ornaments to bring a fresh and cheerful touch to your summer outfit.

Sober, they are the earrings that will perfectly dress up your outfit, like a piece of jewellery made of fine stones and precious stones. These original earrings will be your perfect allies in elegance, bohemia and glamour. The circles, symbols of femininity, will make the golden frame a structure that will please among women’s jewellery.

Ideal gift to offer, the pairs of earrings are original and always form an authentic set. You will be sure to make a nice gift for a loved one’s birthday or one of the most beautiful and original gifts for an engagement party.

For women, the 6cm diameter can be very slightly oversized and very sophisticated. The white hydrangea flowers will brighten up your face and bring light to your summer style. So you can give yourself a gift and wear fancy jewellery earrings to shine all summer long or forever. Let yourself be tempted.

Whether they are children’s earrings, flea earrings, pearl earrings, diamond earrings, gold earrings, feather earrings or flower earrings… Jewellery earrings for women always sublimate the look of a lady.

So, very trendy, flowers make you shine as well as rhinestones, sequins and white pearls. Full of love, they are an easy jewel to match with a chain or bracelets.

Fans of festivals, let yourself be tempted and fall for our Gigi earrings.

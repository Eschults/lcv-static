Alix Hair clip

Hairpin Eternal Preserved flowers
25€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Composed of delicate preserved dark pink hydrangea flowers and small touches of gypsophila and eucalyptus, the Alix hair clip is ideal to adorn a little girl’s hair for a wedding, baptism or communion ceremony. Very easy to fix on very fine hair using its crocodile system, this little hair clip will be the perfect accessory for a little girl from 1 year old to teenager.

  • Composition : preserved dark pink hydrangea, pink gypsophila and eucalyptus, bronze hair clip structure
  • Size: 4cm
  • Shelf-life: the flowers in this hair clip are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

The Alix hair clip is the flowery jewel which will come to sublimate your little stars. Composed of Dark Pink Hydrangea, Gypsophila and preserved Eucalyptus, it is an eternal head jewel that will accompany your little girl for all occasions until she is older. It comes in one size and is adjustable to the head circumference with the ribbon which, once tied, forms a sublime knot to accessorize our sublime little flowers. All the delicacy of this floral head accessory lies in the softness of the colours of its pretty flowers. Powdered tones, touches of pink and green always form a beautiful combination and set of colours.

Its pink colour gives it a glamorous and bohemian touch. If your princess is romantic, she will be delighted. It is an ideal accessory for autumn-winter as well as spring-summer. She can then wear it for a birthday, wedding, communion or baptism. If it’s a baptism ceremony, your little girl can accompany her head jewellery with a pretty summer dress or other children’s outfits. Beautiful dresses are not the only children’s clothing for little girls. You can also find ceremonial outfits such as cotton outfits, satin tops, polka dot trousers, pretty long-sleeved jumpers or flounced skirts. If it’s cold, coats and waistcoats in soft materials are perfect for little girls’ outfits.

To wear the hairclip and dress the little girl, no need to have watched a tutorial or a fashion show, let your heart and your softness speak for itself. Ponytail, loose hair, buns… The most important thing in a child’s world is creativity. For a christening and for all special occasions, fantasy is the main element of a little girl’s outfit.

She can of course choose to wear her accessory every day by matching it with other colourful accessories from this new collection that will dress her up. Made in white or bright colours, the wristbands, hairbands and flower crowns of the Les Couronens de Victoire children’s collection will make your little heart look like a little fairy.

Join us at 230 rue Saint-Martin to try all the hairstyles on your little girls’ christening outfits or other beautiful occasions.

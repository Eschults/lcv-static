Comb Eternal Preserved flowers

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our wide comb Ginevra is adorned with sumptuous floral ornaments to gracefully enhance a bridal bun or half-up hairstyle. With its pearls and golden leaves, it becomes the perfect accessory to transform you, for a day, into a beautiful Italian nymph.

  • Composition: Stabilized hydrangea with a pink gradient, pearls, flowers, and leaves gilded with fine gold.
  • Size : 8 cm

Discover our Sienna comb from the Renaissance collection! Designed with stabilized hydrangeas in two different shades of pink: pale pink and antique pink, stabilized gypsophila and stabilized roses. This hair comb embodies elegance and brilliance, and will also add a touch of refinement, a new “twist” to your hairstyle.

Our Sienna hair comb is ideal for enhancing the floral note in your hair. The fragile petals of stabilized flowers provide a pleasant sensation on the scalp, offering gentle application for even the thickest hair. Feel free to style hair, gently detangle curls and comb with Sienna for an elegant look.

This handmade toothed comb is much more than just a styling accessory, it’s an integral part of your hair ritual. Use it to evenly distribute flyaways, guaranteeing clean, shiny hair. For perfect application, or even stronger hair, don’t forget to massage your scalp in the shower to stimulate blood circulation.

Our team of talented designers created this comb with the hope that it would be fun to use and suitable for all hair types, whether short, long, dry, thick, fine, curly, or even frizzy. Its stainless steel trim and fine teeth ensure a comfortable grip and magnificent hair transformation, whether for a country wedding, a sophisticated soiree, or simply for your normal days.

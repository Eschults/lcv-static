Sierra

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
255€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Were you dreaming of it? Here is the Sierra crown, an elegant, colourful and delicate mix of all our favourite flowers. This crown of multiple nuances will make you shine on your big day and give character to your look. In a vintage and bohemian spirit, this is the crown that will seduce with its enchanting tones.

  • Composition: powder pink, purple and ivory hydrangeas, pink gypsophila, eucalyptus, roses, pink bougainvillea and stabilised red and pink daisies
  • Size: unisize. Can be adjusted with the ribbon on the back
  • Shelf life: the flowers in this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Dreaming of a hippie style wedding with pretty flower crowns on your loose hair? It’s time to discover the new Les Couronnes de Victoire collection, California Dreaming, which offers every imaginable ornamental accessory: a romantic, country-style flower crown for your hair, a flowery comb to slip into a bohemian braid or a beautiful glamorous bun, hair clips for women, earrings to enhance your ears and boutonnieres for men. Hair jewelry has become a real fashion accessory for women in recent seasons, and even more so for spring-summer! This 70’s inspired collection will accessorize and perfect your bridal or bridesmaid look.

The Sierra flower crown is an ideal wedding accessory ornament to create the perfect match between your wedding dress and your long hair (why not even on short hair)! This bohemian hair jewel will delicately complement your bridal hairstyle. Leave a few strands of hair in the front of your head and place the jewel crown on your head. The Sierra crown is a hair accessory made of natural flowers of powder pink, purple and ivory hydrangea, pink gypsophila, eucalyptus, roses, pink bougainvillea and stabilized red and pink daisies. Pair it with your wedding hairstyle: ponytail, braid (or braids!), low bun or high bun and even side-hair! Anything is possible with this sublime hair accessory.

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a brand of handmade fantasy hair accessories that will adorn the most beautiful moments of your life such as a wedding, a baptism or a communion! We make jewelry and accessories of a sophisticated design: flowers, sequins, rhinestones, crystals to wear for all occasions! If you need a tuto or new hairstyle ideas for your wedding, come and see us at 230 rue Saint Martin in Paris to get precious advice!

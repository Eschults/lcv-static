Electre Crown

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
205€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Dress up in a golden halo with our Electra crown decorated with delicately hand gilded preserved ruscus and a multitude of small pearls and crystals. Wear it on the front like a diadem to look like a modern-day mermaid, or on the back to adorn a low bun or bohemian braid.

  • Composition : preserved ruscus and hand gilded, freshwater and transparent crystals pearls
  • Size: unisize
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 8 working days from the date of order

Discover our Electre crown imagined in the L’Odyssée collection, a selection of bridal accessories, influenced by the sea and the heavens. Our Electre crown is inspired by the princess from Greek mythology who bears her name. Through this collection you will find jewellery that will make you look like a modern-day mermaid. Our golden crown will be ideal for your wedding. It will look great on any hairstyle to enhance your wedding dress. Its golden colour, thanks to its preserved ruscus leaves, will give a bohemian touch to your wedding. The arrangement of its foliage makes it possible to dress the whole hair like a real ornament. Decorated with delicate pearls: fresh water and transparent, this flower wreath has all the romanticism that a bride-to-be can dream of for her hair accessory. These pearls are the ivory touch to your crown.

How to do your bridal hairstyle? Before asking yourself this question, you need to think about your wedding theme. Choose the dominant colours you want, the moods and decorative inspirations… This is what will be decisive as well as the period of your wedding. Although our accessories are not subject to the same laws of nature as seasonal flowers. It goes without saying that the season affects the desires in the choice of colours for example. If you decide to add a bohemian chic touch to your country style, our head jewellery is made for you. Wedding hairstyle: what ideas with this pretty floral crown? For a bohemian wedding you can let go of your hair whether you have long or short hair. Our delicate hair accessory will match your hairstyle. Also, you can choose to tie your hair in braids: large single braid, side braid or half braids, the choice is yours. This hairstyle will definitely give you a real mermaid silhouette. Our Electre crown is designed for our brides but if you are a bridesmaid and you are part of the procession, you can very well add a floral touch to your hairstyle (braided or not) for weddings or other ceremonies: headband, hair clip, comb… all the flowers that compose it will be able to sublimate your hairstyle without overshadowing the bride. For this, we advise you to take a look at the Electre range. Even these gentlemen, the future bride and groom, are entitled to their ornament with a buttonhole, matching the Electre wreath, to add a country feel.

If you prefer more discreet flower crowns with a predominance of white flowers and soft tones, we advise you to take a look at our Eurona, Calypso or Nami flower crowns also from the L’Odyssée collection.

About our house Les Couronnes de Victoire. We accompany you in your wedding preparations to make the handmade accessory that will come up on your hairstyle to sublimate you to the end on your wedding day. All our hair bridal accessories are made from real fresh flowers that are transformed into stabilised flowers using 100% natural process to preserve all the brilliance of the natural flowers and their petals. No arrangements use artificial flowers or dried flowers. All our jewellery is hand-made, a true know-how of floral art that makes our pieces unique. We can also make custom-made and personalised crowns or even combs, buckles or bun picks. Les Couronnes de Victoire is the essential floral accessory for all weddings for a romantic touch.

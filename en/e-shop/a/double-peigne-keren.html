Our Keren double comb with subtle golden tones would be perfect for brides with loose or tied hair. Live the Indian summer with sweetness with this model made of preserved wild roses, hydrangea flowers, lagurus, gypsophila. The delicate detail ? The pretty pampas flowers to bring a bohemian touch.

  • Composition: wild roses, golden hydrangea flowers, lagurus, gypsophila and stabilized pampas flowers

  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Let yourself be seduced by our new Golden Hour collection. This romantic and luminous collection is inspired by the end of summer evenings, where the warm lights of the setting sun intermingle to create beautiful colors. Romanticism, light and poetry are the key words of this capsule. The Keren double comb is a combination of delicate white roses and bright golden hydrangeas. This piece with multiple shades will make you shine for your big day and will bring a glamorous side to your wedding dress. You can wear it to your spring-summer wedding as well as your fall-winter wedding and even for the holidays.

Composed of white roses, ivory and gold hydrangea flowers, ecru gypsophila, lagurus and ruscus, this beautiful comb is the perfect wedding accessory to enhance your outfit. Indeed, the comb is the essential floral accessory for weddings. It allows you to perfect your pretty hairstyle and dress up your look with a country touch. It will bring a sophisticated and bohemian touch to your hair, reminding the warm light of the Indian summer. The gold color on the Keren comb brings a chic and elegant touch while remaining discreet. Long hair, short hair, curly hair, you decided to tie your hair for your wedding. This comb can be placed on a low bun, a loose bun or a banana bun leaving a few strands sticking out. Very handy, it can also be placed on the side of a high bun or on a side-hair for an asymmetrical finish. If you are a bohemian bride, then the Keren comb can be worn with a loose braid, a spike braid or a braid. You want to show off your hair on your wedding day ? Then it is possible to use the Keren comb on loose hair on the front of your head, like a crown or on a semi-choker. You want to wear a veil ? This comb can be added to a bridal veil, and why not on a ponytail. This comb with delicate colors can be worn for your wedding but also for all special occasions. It will also suit bridesmaids looking for a head jewel to enhance their hairstyle and bring the final touch.

This collection consists of a selection of hair accessories for brides-to-be, bridesmaids, guests, or simply for women looking for a piece of head jewelry or a pair of earrings for any occasion such as a christening, a communion or simply for every day.

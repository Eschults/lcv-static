Jacqueline wide comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
148€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Discover our brand new wide comb from our collection with Olive + Piper ! It is composed of a delicate gardenia flower associated with rosebuds and small stabilized flowers. The whole forming an elegant and romantic accessory that will dress up your bridal hairstyle and sublimate it.

  • Composition : gardenia, white and powder pink roses, powder pink hydrangea, ruscus and mini ivory gypsophila stabilized
  • Size: 8 cm
  • Shelf life: the flowers that make up this comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions: keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 days from the order

Feeling like blooming or embellishing your hair with a fancy, flowery hair accessory? The Jacqueline wide comb, adorned with gardenia, hydrangea, gypsophila and white and powder pink roses, is announcing the arrival of spring and summer. Pastel, this hair accessory will sublimate your lengths with a romantic bouquet of white flowers to bring freshness to your hairstyle. Our floral arrangements are made from stabilized flowers. Hence, forget about artificial flowers and dried flowers, wearing our Jacqueline comb and touching its petals will give you the impression of wearing satin and touching velvet. These seasonal flowers are natural flowers, fresh flowers that have been stabilized by a natural process to make them last forever.

Any hairstyle is possible, wear it over a bun or over a braid or even ponytail for a glamorous look. Prefer it in a half-tie, between two fine knotted braids, for a country, hippie or bohemian wedding. The large comb is the essential accessory for those who wish to decorate and accessorize their wedding hairstyle with a mini bouquet of flowers. The ruscus leaf brings lightness and geometry while the gardenia, as well as the hydrangea and roses, will be a reminder of your wedding bouquet or the wedding flowers chosen for your decoration.

Les Couronnes de Victoire handcraft your headpieces and ornaments for your bridal hairstyle, for the bridesmaids or the guests. Eternal, our colorful flowers can be carried over for any occasion, so think about using our hair accessories and hair jewelry for Valentine’s Day, a communion, a baptism or a birthday! From flower crowns, pins / picks, combs, hats, to hair clips, tiara headbands, bibi, earrings, all accessories can be worn as ornaments on any type of hair: short hair, long hair, thin or thick. Don’t hesitate to make an appointment in our boutique to have your hair styled and try on these original wedding accessories. Our consultants will be more than happy to help you find your hair accessories for your wedding or other ceremonies.

Marguerite hair clip

Hairpin Eternal Preserved flowers
25€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Like a mini colorful bouquet, the Marguerite hair clip combines hydrangea, gypsophila, glixia, and eucalyptus. Its crocodile clip attachment is very convenient for sliding into the hair of all little girls. Wear them layered for a truly bucolic result. This playful piece was crafted in collaboration with Louise Misha.

  • Composition : Stabilized mauve hydrangea, stabilized mauve, yellow, and violet gypsophila, stabilized eucalyptus, and dried glixia.
  • Size : 3 cm
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order date

The Margherite hair clip from Louise Misha and Couronnes de Victoire is a hair accessory designed for both children and women, aimed at adding a touch of colorful whimsy to any hairstyle. Despite its modest size, this hair clip is a true gem to enhance your hair.

Crafted with stabilized mauve hydrangeas, stabilized mauve, yellow, and violet gypsophilas, as well as stabilized eucalyptus leaves and dried glixias, the Margherite hair clip offers a harmonious blend of colors and textures, perfect for brightening up any hairstyle.

Its gold-plated fine structure adds an element of elegance and glamour, while its barrette shape ensures a secure and comfortable hold in the hair, whether it’s long, short, loose, or styled in a ponytail or bun.

Whether it’s for a special occasion like a wedding, a party, or just to add a touch of enchantment to your everyday look, the Margherite hair clip is the ideal accessory to style your hair with flair and originality.

The stunning stabilized flowers are carefully attached to a gold pin, ensuring a solid hold without damaging your hair. Their beauty is accentuated, while the small-sized design adds a touch of lightness. This hair accessory can also be worn as a dazzling headpiece. Handcrafted with high-quality materials, this stabilized flower hair clip is a unique and precious piece for all lovers of hair accessories. Don’t miss the opportunity to perfect your look with style! With this trendy hair accessory, you’re guaranteed to shine forever. Let your creativity flow by using it to secure your bun, braids, bangs, rebellious strands, or even to accessorize your headbands, ribbons, scarves, and turbans, turning your hairstyles into true works of art.

Our brand, Couronnes de Victoire, strives to create head jewelry related to wedding themes such as hair clips, bun pins, headbands, combs, flower crowns, hairpins, and a selection of accessories for girls like rings, earrings, necklaces, and more. Whatever your hair type, we’ll provide you with information on coloring, and proper styling for women, to perfect your glamorous looks to perfection.

Lavinia earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
125€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Lavinia earrings, composed of hydrangea flowers and pearls, will give you a princess-like posture. Airy and light, these dangling earrings form the perfect accessory for your wedding. You can pair them with our Judith hairpin set for a very chic and mystical look !

  • Composition : Stabilized hydrangea, pearls, ear stem in gold-plated silver.
  • Size : 6 cm
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

The Lavinia earrings from the brand new Renaissance collection by Les Couronnes de Victoire embody absolute elegance. These dangling earrings gracefully enhance feminine earlobes, revealing a perfect balance between sophistication and refinement. Delicately arranged white stabilized hydrangea petals create a captivating play of contrasts, elevated by a pearl that completes the ensemble. The silver-gilt ear post, meticulously crafted in a round shape, is adorned with gilded details, imparting an exclusive touch to this sumptuous jewel. Each pair of earrings from the Renaissance collection is a work of art in its own right, customizable to reflect the tastes and personality of the wearer. The options are vast, from yellow gold to white gold, encompassing varied hues and exquisite pearls. These pieces can be set with sparkling cultured pearls or dazzling crystals, offering endless diversity. Les Couronnes de Victoire go beyond the Lavinia earrings; the brand offers a wide range of equally splendid accessories. From hoops to studs, from sleepers to clips, from pendants to rings, each piece is designed with the same precision and attention to detail. Whether it’s a dazzling set for a glamorous evening or discreet earrings for everyday wear, Les Couronnes de Victoire offers creations for every occasion. Meticulous craftsmanship and a myriad of choices allow every woman to find her ideal jewel, one that will enhance her earlobe and add a touch of femininity to her hairstyle and overall look. Thus, these Lavinia earrings, like other treasures in the collection, transcend mere accessories to become an expression of personal style, history, and timeless sophistication. Les Couronnes de Victoire, with their collection of dazzling jewels, continue to write the story of jewelry by offering unique pieces, precious jewels to cherish and pass on, true witnesses of unparalleled elegance.

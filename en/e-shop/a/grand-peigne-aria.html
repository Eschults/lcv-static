Aria wide comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
95€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Made of stabilised flowers with a bohemian inspiration, the Aria wide comb will be perfect for the bride who would like to have a colourful, floral touch to her hairstyle. Thanks to its size, the wide comb can be placed into an elegant or loose bun or into a braid for a boho-chic bridal look.

  • Composition: ivory, pink and burgundy gypsophila, nigella, white hydrangea and stabilised oats
  • Size: 3 inches
  • Shelf life: the flowers in this comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

California Dreaming is here, the new collection of wedding jewelry for brides-to-be from Les Couronnes de Victoire with hippie and bohemian inspirations! If you like little flowers in your hair, you’ll love our jewelry and accessories for your romantic wedding. In this selection of accessories in acid colors you will find flower crowns, earrings to wear on all occasions, combs for a sophisticated bridal hairstyle, ornamental buttonholes for future husbands or best men, a pretty handmade flowered hat, a sumptuous bridal veil adorned with pretty colored flowers, hair clips to slide easily into your mane… All of our bridal hair accessories are made to match your wedding dress, whether it is satin or lace!

The wide Aria comb is a marvelous piece of hair jewelry made of gypsophila, nigella, hydrangea and oatmeal in pink tones mounted on a gold-plated comb. Become the French Lana Del Rey by slipping this women’s hair comb to adorn your long or medium length hair. In terms of hairstyles, treat yourself! Braid (or braids), bridal bun (chic high bun or sophisticated low bun), ponytail or why not a semi-attached. A beautiful floral hair accessory is the perfect finishing touch to your most beautiful hairstyles.

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a French brand of wedding hair accessories: the rural flower crown, the glamorous princess tiara, the hair clip ( bow / sequins / small flowers / pearls / rhinestones), the bridegroom’s boutonniere, the little child’s flower crown, the simple or ornamented veils, the bride-to-be’s bouquet …. All our hair accessories and jewelry for women are customizable: you can change the flowers and colors (purple, ivory, pink, orange, gold, silver …). Come and meet us at 230 rue Saint-Martin with or without an appointment to try our little wonders.

Aurelia

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
275€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

How can one not love the Aurélia Crown and its delicate arrangement? Wear this piece with elegance, like a muse straight out of the painting “La Primavera”! Whether in an updo or with loose hair, it complements all hairstyles to shine on your wedding day.

  • Composition : Gypsophila and stabilized golden ruscus, flowers, and leaves gilded with fine gold
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

The Aurélia crown from the Renaissance collection, delicate and sophisticated, evokes the very essence of a romantic, rustic, or bohemian wedding. Crafted with a golden headband structure, it captures the essence of flowers and foliage gilded with fine gold, uniting elegance and freshness in an extraordinary accessory. For the bride seeking an extraordinary floral accessory to enhance her hairstyle, the Aurélia crown is a unique piece. With its golden stabilized gypsophila and ruscus, it embodies the purity and delicacy of white flowers, evoking both tradition and originality.

This crown, perfect for your wedding or that of your loved ones, harmonizes with romantic and rustic bouquets, offering a bohemian floral touch to brides and bridesmaids. Adorned with a satin ribbon, it pairs beautifully with wedding dresses, whether long and flowing or short and delicate. Whether for a delicately adorned chignon, a braided hairstyle, or free-flowing locks, this floral crown adapts to all hairstyles. The stabilized foliage and flowers ensure enduring freshness, while the flowers gilded with fine gold add a touch of elegance and refinement.

At the heart of this floral crown, artificial floral compositions blend with natural elements like stabilized flowers, creating a perfect harmony between the ephemeral and the enduring. Whether you opt for natural or stabilized flowers, this golden floral crown adds a touch of refinement to your style. It coordinates perfectly with other floral elements of your wedding, from bridal bouquets to venue decorations, including table centerpieces.

By ordering this lovely crown, you’re choosing a handmade accessory crafted delicately by a floral artisan. It allows you to reveal your personality and style, whether it’s bohemian chic, minimalist, or romantic. Whether for a secular ceremony, communion, baptism, or any other special occasion, the Aurélia crown remains the perfect accessory to enhance your look with delicate flowers, pastel tones, and a rare touch of golden elegance.

Victoire’s crowns offer a wide range of wedding accessories made from stabilized flowers, including women’s or children’s crowns, flower earrings—be they dangling or hoops—combs, mini-combs, double combs, men’s boutonnières, and even bouquets. Feel free to explore all these treasures at our boutique !

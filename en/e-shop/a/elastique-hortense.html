Hortense hair band

Pony cuff Eternal Preserved flowers

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

A hair band, but make it chic ! Our piece features a delicate golden flower to elevate all your hairstyles. A true hair jewel for those who don’t overlook any detail. Low ponytail, bun, braid: it’s the girly and perfect accessory to slip into your bag. The Hortense hair tie was designed in collaboration with Marie, the creator of Scrunchie is Back fancy accessories.

  • Composition : Hair band with a flower plated in 24-carat gold, 3 microns
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Discover the Hortense hair band, a black elastic adorned with a 24-carat gold-plated flower from the new Scrunchies is Back collection in collaboration with Les Couronnes de Victoire. This hair accessory is the perfect element to add a touch of glamour and elegance to your hairstyle, whether it’s for a casual day or a special occasion.

Made with high-quality materials, this elastic features a delicate gold-plated flower, giving it a unique and sophisticated charm. Its classic black color makes it versatile and easy to match with all kinds of outfits.

Whether you have long, short, curly, or straight hair, this elastic will perfectly adapt to your locks. It’s ideal for creating elegant buns, sophisticated ponytails, or simply for securing your strands with ease.

The elasticity of this hair tie allows for a firm hold while preserving the comfort of your hair. It’s strong enough to keep your hairstyles in place throughout the day, while being gentle enough to avoid damaging your hair.

Whether you’re a bride looking for a chic accessory for your wedding hairstyle or simply a woman wanting to add a touch of elegance to your daily look, this elastic hair tie will be your ideal ally.

Don’t miss the opportunity to add this jewel to your collection of hair accessories. With its gold-plated flower and comfortable elasticity, it will quickly become a staple in your haircare routine.

Come and discover our poetic universe through our various collections. You’ll find different hair accessories and head jewelry: tiaras, flower crowns, double combs, bun pins adorned with petals or small flowers, floral boutonnieres, ribbon bracelets, hats, headbands, veils, and more.

We also offer jewelry and hair accessories for all occasions: colorful and original earrings, ear clips, hair ties, barrettes set with Swarovski crystals, and necklaces. Our Les Couronnes de Victoire ornaments are created from real natural flowers and not artificial ones.

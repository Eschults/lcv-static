Coline

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
59€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our Coline crown effortlessly slips into any hairstyle thanks to its satin ribbon. With its gentle composition of pastel flowers, it’s perfect for adorning all little heads ! Pair it with our Amaryllis women’s crown to create adorable matching mother-daughter looks. This bucolic piece was crafted in collaboration with Louise Misha.

  • Composition : Stabilized mauve hydrangea, stabilized mauve, yellow, and violet gypsophila, stabilized eucalyptus, and dried glixia.
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order date

Immerse yourself in romantic sweetness with our Coline flower crown, an exquisite creation from our collaboration with the brand Louise Misha. This flower crown for baby girls is an ideal hair accessory for a variety of special events, including weddings.

The Coline crown is beautifully crafted with mauve hydrangea flowers, mauve, yellow, and violet gypsophilas, as well as eucalyptus and glixias, creating a delicate and romantic ambiance. Whether for a flower girl at a wedding or simply to add a touch of elegance to a lovely hairstyle, this flower crown is perfect for all occasions, whether it’s a baptism, a wedding, or a communion.

Hair fashion accessories for girls are essential for completing your party outfit, and the bohemian Coline crown is no exception. Its romantic design and floral details make it a versatile fashion accessory that can be worn with different hairstyles, whether it’s for a wedding, a party, or just a special day.

For those looking to tie their style together with elegance and glamor, the handmade Coline flower crown is a perfect and essential accessory. Add a touch of romance to your look with this exquisite hair accessory, and let yourself be captivated by its timeless charm for all special occasions.

To perfect your look with a final touch, explore our selection of accessories at Les Couronnes de Victoire. In addition to the sophisticated Coline flower crown, we offer a wide range of accessories, jewelry, and hair accessories to cater to all styles and preferences.

For fans of minimalist style, opt for our delicate and discreet head jewelry that adds a subtle touch to your hairstyle. If you prefer more colorful accessories, our range of small flowers and multicolored hair accessories is perfect for adding a touch of cheerfulness to your look. Whatever your preferences, our hair accessories are available in a variety of colors and styles to complement your outfit with elegance and originality.

Looking to accessorize, enhance, and tame your wedding hairstyle or your overall look? The jewelry house Les Couronnes de Victoire offers a wide range of natural flower compositions such as floral barrettes, flower crowns for the bride-to-be, glitter hair clips, earrings adorned with hydrangeas, etc. All accessories are meticulously crafted by our team of artisans with love, not only for your rustic wedding but also for all special occasions!

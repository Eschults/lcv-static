Our Nymphea hair clip is practical for instantly enhancing your everyday hairstyles! A speckled clip that will surely make an impression… You can pair it with the Nymphea hair clip in size S to create a layered look ! This hair clip was designed in collaboration with Marie, the creator of Scrunchie is Back fancy accessories.

  • Composition : The hair clip is made from cellulose acetate, a biodegradable material derived from cotton flower.
  • Size : 9 cm
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Introducing the brand new large Nymphea hair clip. A transparent crab clip adorned with hints of orange and peach, from the collaboration between Scrunchies is Back and Les Couronnes de Victoire. Carefully crafted from cellulose acetate, a biodegradable material derived from cotton flowers, this crab clip is both eco-friendly and trendy.

This hair accessory is both practical and aesthetic. Its transparent color with orange and peach nuances gives it an elegant and modern look, perfect for enhancing your hairstyle on a daily basis or for special occasions.

Thanks to its crab shape, this clip offers an excellent grip and ensures optimal hold for your hair, whether you’re creating a sophisticated bun, an elegant ponytail, or simply securing your strands with ease.

The generous dimensions of this crab clip allow it to grasp a large quantity of hair, making it a versatile accessory suitable for all hair types, whether thick, thin, curly, or straight.

Its refined design and orange and peach touches add a subtle pop of color to your hairstyle, for a trendy and original look.

Don’t miss out on this essential accessory from the new Scrunchies is Back and Les Couronnes de Victoire collection. With its cellulose acetate construction and unique design, this crab clip will charm you with its style and practicality in your daily life.

Since 2018, Scrunchie is Back has become the pioneering brand in hair accessories, continuously expanding its range: scrunchies, headbands, barrettes, bows, clips… always with the same requirements: creating comfortable, durable accessories locally crafted.

Les Couronnes de Victoire, on the other hand, designs wedding accessories and hair accessories for life’s beautiful moments such as ceremonies. It was necessary to combine our different backgrounds and expertise to give birth to this beautiful collection!

Our keywords are crafting, personalizing, creating, and making our brides beautiful. For your wedding, it’s with us that you’ll find your bridal accessory and our iconic accessory: the flower crown. So which one will you choose? A beautiful crown, bun pins, combs, hairpins, barrettes, a delicate tiara, headbands, hats, earrings? We even make boutonnieres for men! We have a wide range of accessories with customizable compositions and ornaments (crystals, glitter, rhinestone beads) and flowers (white flowers, pink flowers, green flowers, etc.). Know that it’s possible to add your favorite ornament to your accessory upon request. Wearing the flower crown or one of our other wedding accessories will add a touch of glamour to your outfits.

Dublin Bouquet

Bouquet Eternal Preserved flowers
118€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Discover our “Dublin” bridal bouquet, a romantic composition thanks to its stabilized roses and generous eucalyptus that form a flowery halo ideal for saying “I do”. A bouquet all in volume which will accompany you for the most beautiful day of your life and for all the years to come. Indeed, after your wedding you can keep it as long as you want, at home!

  • Composition: white mini gypsophila, pink rice flower, eucalyptus, parvis and powder pink English roses stabilized, beige linen ribbon
  • Size : approximately 42 cm in height and 18 cm in diameter
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

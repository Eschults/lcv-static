Poséïdon Wide Comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
245€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Adorn your hair with golden shells and pearls with our large Poséïdon comb. This comb is ideally worn on a half tail or a low bun. Don’t hesitate to combine it with our Poseidon earrings to give your outfit even more style.

  • Composition : gold plated metal shells, freshwater pearls, preserved hydrangea and gypsophila flowers, gold plated metal comb
  • Size: around 13 cm
  • Shelf life: the flowers that make up this wide comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 8 working days from the date of order

The Poseidon Wide Comb takes its name from the god of the sea and the oceans. It belongs to L’Odyssée collection. A collection highlighting marine poetry and its universe. The teeth of the comb are a poetic reference to the trident, symbol of the Greek god. The arrangement of the flowers adorning the accessory is reminiscent of the shape of a crown. Sumptuous with its golden leaves, the hair comb is ideal to wear for all types of hairstyles of the bride. It is also possible to accessorize it with barrettes, clips and other head jewellery. Situated between a glamorous and bohemian style, your golden hair accessory will perfectly adorn your half ponytail, your bridal bun or even your loose hair. To beautifully style your bridal bun and add a romantic touch or head jewellery, we have created several Pinterest selections for you to find your hairstyle inspirations, or ideas for combinations: how to wear a mini comb with a tiara for example. It is important to know that today, dressed accessories such as the large Poseidon comb are becoming more and more popular. They can be worn on the day as well as for parties, for evening hairstyles and for all occasions, to bring a touch of fantasy to your daily hairstyle. You will show a special elegance by wearing your hair tied up. For the bohemian bride, in the same collection, you will find earrings, bracelets, bridal jewellery, flower crowns, tiaras… for all hairstyles. Whether they are adorned with crystals or pearly pearls, our delicately perfumed creations are a blend of sublime materials and craftsmanship recognised by our brides. The bridal hair accessories can be complementary or worn separately, as worn by your bridesmaids. The sophisticated object for the bridesmaid of honour is often the hair clip. It can be worn as a main accessory or to add the finishing touch to their hairstyle. Nowadays, hair clips are a great choice for special occasions and are suitable for curly, frizzy or straight hair. For a minimalist style, you will find our Electre, Tallulah, Sirena, Calypso and Nami mini combs. Conversely, in the country spirit, for bridal hairstyles, you will find our crowns and other jewellery and accessories. The essential accessory is the earrings, which you will find under the sign of different gods and mermaids in our L’ Odyssée collection. For bohemian, sophisticated or romantic women, Les Couronnes de Victoire think and create the hair jewellery of your dreams. All the accessories, from bun pins to hair jewellery are made for a wedding that will exceed your expectations. That you feel as beautiful as you look is our priority.

Whether you have long or short hair, whether you want to make braids or a loose cut… From a barrette to a flower crown, the categories proposed in the Les Couronnes de Victoire Crown accessories are designed to sumptuously dress the bride’s hair. These ornaments, created to sublimate the bridal hairstyle, will accompany it forever thanks to the preservation of our flowers.

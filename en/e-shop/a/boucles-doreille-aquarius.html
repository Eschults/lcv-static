For the bold and blue-loving souls, here are our Aquarius earrings from our magical and enchanting Starry Night collection. Fall for their delicate grayish hue, suitable to wear day and night…

  • Composition : Stabilized gray-blue hydrangea flowers. Gold plated metal structure.

  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity

Our Aquarius earrings, from the new “Starry Night” collection, embody the freshness and purity of stabilized flower petals. These stunning jewels are designed not only for a memorable wedding filled with magic, but for all special occasions such as Valentine’s Day, Mother’s Day…

Venus boutonniere

Boutonniere Eternal Preserved flowers
22€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Declare your love with our Venus boutonniere, an ode to Botticelli’s sublime muse. Offer your witnesses and loved ones this enchanting piece composed of a touch of stabilized ivy and hydrangea.

  • Composition: Stabilized hydrangea, stabilized ivy
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Dedicated to future brides and grooms, our new Venus boutonniere from the Renaissance collection is a true work of art among seasonal flowers. Its pastel tones, purity, and freshness will perfectly complement the wedding decor: floral creations such as the bridal bouquet, groom’s boutonniere, interior decoration such as the centerpiece, reception venue, wedding hall, etc. Sienna is a small accessory for your jacket that will sparkle like dazzling diamonds! Don’t wait any longer to enhance your image and romantic wedding with this floral composition.

Composed of hydrangea petals, baby’s breath, and stabilized ivy, Venus boutonnieres are perfect for your rustic wedding! For a perfect wedding and a successful ceremony, we aim to enhance your wedding theme with the true language of flowers. Regardless of the floral compositions you choose, whether green plants or beautiful flowers like dahlias, lilies, white roses, tulips, hibiscus, whether you choose dried flowers, stabilized flowers, fake flowers, fresh flowers, it is recommended to know how to arrange and choose flowers so that they harmonize well and are compatible.

With the arrangement of our Venus boutonniere, the proposed theme for this special occasion will be vintage-retro, with floral decorations inspired by the beauty of the era. Don’t worry if it’s not your favorite theme; it has been designed to blend well with other floral arrangements! Are you opting for a modern and extravagant wedding? Try playing with different color palettes. Do you prefer a bohemian wedding? Use pastel colors as the main hues. And don’t forget to let your guests know your dress code so they can dress appropriately! In conclusion, mastering the art of floral design is one of the solutions we suggest for creating a dream wedding ceremony!

If you would like to receive more information and advice, feel free to visit us! With Les Couronnes de Victoire, you can learn how to successfully plan a secular ceremony regardless of your budget; we also offer a wide selection of wedding accessories and flowers: flower crowns, personalized boutonnieres, original flower bouquets composed of peonies, hydrangeas, red roses, eucalyptus, etc. And if that’s not what you’re looking for? Other accessory ranges will be available for Mother’s Day, Valentine’s Day, or even for a lovely gift dedicated to newlyweds!”

Sofia scrunchie

Scrunchie Eternal Preserved flowers
48€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our frilly tulle scrunchie, Sofia, is entirely crafted from tulle. Its airy and whimsical effect will appeal to those who dislike dullness and want to add a wow factor to their bridal outfit ! This scrunchie gives off a bohemian vibe in an effortless ponytail but can also structure a more elaborate bun. Either way, it will bring a quirky and fanciful finishing touch to all your bridal looks. This scrunchie was designed in collaboration with Marie, the creator of Scrunchie is Back fancy accessories.

  • Composition : Frilly tulle scrunchie
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Avoid all contact with water.
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

One of the accessories from the new collection between Scrunchies is Back and Couronnes de Victoire is the Sofia scrunchie, combining the chicness of white pleated fabric with the delicacy of Swiss dot tulle. This double-layered scrunchie offers remarkable versatility. Worn as is, it adds volume to a ponytail or bun, thanks to its thick, round structure. When folded over twice, it becomes more discreet while still adding a touch of elegance, perfect for a subtler hairstyle.

Designed to enhance your hairstyle on any occasion, this scrunchie is suitable for both long and short hair. Its soft and pleasant texture is ideal for everyday styling, while its Swiss dot tulle details add a touch of sophistication and glamour, perfect for a special occasion or event such as a wedding.

The two ways of wearing it offer a wide variety of styles and options to enhance your locks. Whether you opt for a casual or more sophisticated look, this scrunchie will adapt to all your desires, adding a touch of chic and refinement to your hairstyle.

If you choose to use the hair elastic to adorn your beautiful wedding hairstyle, we advise you to go for a modern and trendy look. A high ponytail will then be perfect to accompany a satin or lace dress. You can straighten or curl your ponytail depending on your preferences. Then add a beautiful veil for a chic and trendy pin-up look. For brides looking for a more traditional look, the hair elastic can be worn on a small bun tied high, accompanied by a beautiful wedding tiara or a crown of golden flowers. For a larger bun, you can add a doughnut, secured with small clips or hairpins. Don’t hesitate to match your hair elastic with a gold jewelry piece.

A pair of original and colorful earrings or a necklace pair perfectly with our elastics. You can browse our e-shop to discover our selection of gold accessories.

Your hair, tied up with the elastic, will showcase your neck and reveal a pretty pair of golden hoops or one of our flower necklaces adorned with small hydrangea flowers and white pearls. Bracelets, earrings, necklaces. We have many hair accessories, jewelry, and ornaments. We also offer a wide range of hair jewelry with stabilized flowers, such as double combs, bun pins, hats, barrettes, tiaras, headbands, or flower crowns.

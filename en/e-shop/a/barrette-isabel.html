The Isabel bow is more than just a simple hair accessory. It embodies elegance and refinement in its purest form. Crafted in white lace, this XXL bow evokes timeless and delicate charm. With a barrette structure, the Isabel bow easily clips into the hair, adding a touch of sophistication to any hairstyle.

Whether you opt for an elegant bun, a romantic ponytail, or a bohemian braid, this bow is the perfect accessory to enhance your locks.

Made solely of lace, without pearls or rhinestones, the Isabel bow focuses on simplicity and authenticity to highlight your hairstyle. Its immaculate white color harmonizes with all outfits and occasions, making it a versatile and essential accessory for women seeking elegance. Whether for a wedding, a fashion show, or simply to accessorize your everyday attire, the Isabel bow is the ideal accessory to add a touch of glamour to your hairstyle.

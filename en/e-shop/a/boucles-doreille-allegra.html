Discover our Allegra hoop, a subtle touch of flowers to enhance your (most beautiful) day. This delicate pair of creoles, light and all in white, will enhance your bridal hairstyle to make it irresistibly elegant.

  • Composition : white hydrangea, white fern, white stabilized gypsophila, white ceramic flowers, 24k 3 micron gold plated structure
  • Size: 3 cm. Or 4cm and/or on request can be mounted on clips for non-pierced ears
  • Shelf life: eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Our creole earrings are ideal to bring a touch of refinement. This new collection also offers pearl earrings which are the perfect combination for a glamorous effect.

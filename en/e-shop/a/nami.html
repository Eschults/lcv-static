Nami Crown

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
138€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Inspired by our iconic Jeanne crown, the Nami crown plays with soft tones to adorn your hair with a delicate floral halo. Powder pink preserved hydrangea flowers, oats and other small preserved wildflowers adorn this romantic boho crown.

  • Composition : preserved hydrangea, oats, baby’s breath, marcela, lagurus and limonium
  • Size: unisize. Can be adjusted with the ribbon on the back
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Named after the mermaid who inspires it, the Nami crown belongs to L’Odyssée collection. It succeeds an older crown among our wedding accessories, the Jeanne. A new style and a new kind of elegance, always in the form of a floral arrangement from fresh flowers that have become preserved flowers. All the crowns in L’Odyssée collection are hair accessories, designed to sublimate the bride’s hairstyles. These floral creations, inspired by the sea and marine poetry, are the perfect match for a chic bohemian wedding or rural ceremonies. The new collection, L’Odyssée, also includes barrettes, tiaras, combs, bracelets… and other precious ornaments that highlight the floral art.

A crown of flowers such as the Nami is created in one size only but can be easily adjusted to the head circumference thanks to the ribbon at the back that you can turn into a bow. The Nami crown consists of small flowers of hydrangea, oats and gypsophila. Its hydrangeas give it its romantic signature. Created by our artisan florists in our Parisian floral workshop, it is the essential headpiece to acquire in the same way as your wedding dress. It allows you to accessorise your hairstyle, whether you opt for a ponytail, a bun or another hairstyle. Its pastel tones give it charm. The Nami crown shows us that not all flowers will remain in vases, they accompany us in the strongest moments as well as in everyday life.

This hair accessory would therefore be perfectly adapted for a country or bohemian wedding. Granddaughters and grown-ups alike can dream of a wedding with flowers. This crown of pretty flowers can be worn differently. If you don’t yet have an idea for a hairstyle and would like to be inspired, we invite you to visit our Pinterest account where you will find wedding hairstyle inspirations (for loose, tied-up, long hair, short hair, braided hairstyle…). We have thought and continue to add hairstyles for all brides. For the bridesmaids, a hair accessory in the same tones and in the same collection: the Naïa tiara. This sublime pastel jewel will give value to your bridesmaid’s hair, beyond a headdress, a hairband, a clip, or an artificial flower. Every woman can afford pretty flowers, whether they are wedding flowers, a bridal bouquet, a bouquet of flowers or simply for everyday wear. Our creations remain above all hair accessories, and accompany you beyond weddings to bring flowers to your daily life. Flowers for all occasions. If your preference is for white flowers, in the same collection, discover the pretty Sirena crown or the Panarea headband which will seduce you with their sublime white petals. Orchids, peonies, green plants, tulips, lilies … Our greatest satisfaction is to allow you to choose the flowers you like. At Les Couronnes de Victoire, the flowers in our hair jewellery and in all our creations are neither dried nor artificial. They are natural flowers which allow us to create absolutely authentic and original compositions for your union ceremonies. And this for life. How do we do it? All our flowers are preserved. This means that our flower will never fade and neither will your beautiful wreath. The fact that the flowers on the day you pass from Miss to Mrs will last forever, gives you a precious gift and a flowery memory for life.

Our crown with its delicate flowers will allow you to create a sublime bridal hairstyle for your wedding.

Clémentine

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
160€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Bohemian and colourful, discover the bridal crown from our Les Couronnes de Victoire x Louise Misha collection. Hydrangea, gypsophila and chaton bring lightness and volume. Its powdery notes provide the perfect romantic touch for a wedding ceremony. The Clementine crown can be worn both in the front and in the back, with or without a veil. Its flexible structure allows it to perfectly match any hairstyle. For an even more bohemian look, you can combine our Clementine crown with earrings from the same range in a boho-chic spirit.

  • Composition: white, pink and orange hydrangea flowers, ivory, pink and copper mini gypsophila and stabilised beads
  • Size: one size
  • Shelf life: the flowers in this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

The Clementine crown is from our beautiful new LCV x Louise Misha costume jewellery collection. This radiant piece of women’s jewellery will brighten up your face while bringing out your bohemian, joyful, glamorous and romantic personality.

Full of refinement, crowns set with flowers like the Clementine crown can be worn for any occasion. Unlike a crown set with rhinestones, pearls, crystals or other precious stones… Flowers are halfway between jewellery and fantasy. They are therefore easier to wear than fine or semi-precious stones set on the jeweller’s white gold, yellow gold, solid silver or other precious metal setting. This is why it is often a gift idea that one thinks of for the bride-to-be or for the birthday of a loved one. And it is indeed the ideal gift. Proof of infinite love, the eternal flowers that make up this piece of costume jewellery are a true ode to love and joy. It will therefore become a precious good luck charm for the recipient. Hydrangea flowers, gypsophila, preserved catkins… bring novelty to the compositions and give your jewel the rarity of a jewel set with original diamonds. Women’s jewellery such as crowns, necklaces, rings and bracelets always look more cheerful when they are decorated with flowers. Whether it is a rose or a gemstone, the difference is in the value it has for the heart. Because it’s decorated with flowers, you can wear it on the beach in Tahiti, at a picnic with your girlfriends, or for an important occasion in a few months. Just like a gold jewel, it is a timeless piece of jewellery. It will make you shine without excess, through your occasions. And to bring you even more shine, let yourself be tempted by the Clementine earrings, from the same collection.

The beauty of the flower is timeless. Crowns with such beautiful flowers are a delicate and pure accessory that you can pass on through the generations. If you have a daughter, for example, you can give her your wreath when she is older. In the meantime, the LCV x Louise Misha collection offers you the opportunity to match mother and daughter and to sublimate your baby with a halo of flowery finesse with the Rosa crown. The Clementine and Rosa crowns will be the perfect addition to the elegant gold and silver pieces in your precious jewellery box. With your daughter, whether you choose to wear your crowns on a daily basis or for a special occasion, one thing is certain: they will leave an eternal mark on your baby’s heart. The delicate colours of the flowers will allow you to choose your crown for spring or summer. And for your little girl, you can also dress her wrist with the pretty Rosa bracelet.

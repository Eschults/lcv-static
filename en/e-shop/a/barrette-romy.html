Romy Hair clip

Hairpin Eternal Preserved flowers
25€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Fall for our delicate Romy hair clip composed of a small bouquet of preserved white hydrangea, eucalyptus and gypsophila. A delicate floral touch to adorn little girls’ hair, whether it’s to hold a strand in front or to prick into a hairstyle like a bun.

Thanks to its crocodile structure, this hair clip will fit on the fine hair of little girls as well as on taller girls.

  • Composition: white hydrangea, stabilised white eucalyptus and gypsophila, barrette structure in bronze
  • Size: 4cm
  • Shelf-life: the flowers in this hair clip are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

The Romy hair clip belongs to our first and new collection designed to sublimate your princesses. Its preserved Hydrangea, Eucalyptus and Gypsophila flowers give it a glamorous, vegetal and authentic signature.

Adapting itself to all occasions, it is an accessory that can accompany a dressed dress, a magnificent summer dress or even small princess dresses. All the ceremonial outfits are more chic with an accessory. For a wedding ceremony as for a child ceremony, beautiful dresses and beautiful hair clips make the perfect match. A formal dress, whether it is a wedding dress or other, when it is for little girls, can leave room for the creativity of the ladies or their moms. Sequined dresses, rhinestones, prints, flounces, tulle petticoats, sequins, polka dots, long sleeves, embroideries… Cotton dress, satin dress, chiffon, linen… So many particularities and materials that can be found on a pretty children’s dress worn with the Romy hair clip for ceremonies. However, to wear her hair clip, no need for special occasions. In everyday life, it will bring fantasy to your little girl. If she is romantic, her accessory will bring a touch of bohemian and femininity to an outfit, ideally powdered or pastel pink, to contrast in spring-summer. It will make her glow like a little butterfly.

Girls wishing to be original can opt for the hair clip with outfits such as pants, a girl’s tuxedo, a girl’s outfit or other outfits that are both comfortable and elegant. With other accessories from the same collection such as the Romy crown, you can create sublime colorful outfits to make your little darling dress with the most beautiful fairy tale. For a chic bohemian wedding, the Romy range is particularly appreciated. If you are part of the wedding procession as a maid of honor and your daughter is part of the children’s procession among the children of honor, it is ideal. If your daughter brings the wedding rings to the bride and groom on a white satin cushion, there is no doubt that the Romy range worn with a procession dress in white tones will be resplendent. The trick for more effect is to accumulate the hair clips.

In store or on our Eshop, fill your princess with the Romy hair clip and its derived accessories. At the same time, discover the children’s collection and the creations it proposes to match them with all the children’s clothes or girl’s clothes your little fairy has. For a cocktail party, a communion, a baby baptism to come… Let free rein to your desires of ceremonial clothes for your little girl. Les Couronnes de Victoire proposes all the accessories which will come to dress and bring the flowery signature that deserves this party dress or cocktail dress that you will have chosen for your baby girl.

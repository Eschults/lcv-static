Roma Bouquet

Bouquet Eternal Preserved flowers
90€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Just like the eternal city, discover our Rome bouquet that will last a lifetime. It is the perfect representation of a romantic and elegant bridal bouquet. It features foliage with eucalyptus for volume and touches of red with its delicate roses and hydrangea flowers. This is a timeless bouquet that is ideal for any wedding.

  • Composition : mini red gypsophila, pink rice flower, dark fuschia hydrangea, eucalyptus, red princess rose and red English rose stabilized, beige linen ribbon
  • Size : approximately 33 cm in height and 18 cm in diameter
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Discover our new bridal bouquets!

Les Couronnes de Victoire has just launched its collection of flower bouquets for all bohemian and romantic brides-to-be. Just like the eternal city, discover our Rome bouquet that will last a lifetime. It is the perfect representation of a romantic and elegant bridal bouquet in all simplicity. It features foliage with eucalyptus for volume and touches of red with its delicate roses and hydrangea flowers. This is the perfect timeless bouquet for any wedding. It is between softness and bohemian spirit, that our flower bouquet will match perfectly with your wedding theme.

Our floral arrangements are made from natural flowers stabilized to keep your sumptuous bouquet forever. All flowers (roses, hydrangeas, gypsophila, lilies, peonies, tulips, foliage, ranunculus, orchid, lily of the valley, stems, gypsophila, dahlias, eucalyptus, tulip, lavender, small flowers, freesias, ivy, rose flowers, amaryllis, sunflowers, red roses, white roses) are carefully chosen by the designer Victoire, to embellish your wedding gown and wedding theme. Both original and romantic, these flower arrangements are handmade and are of an unparalleled delicacy.

A bouquet for the bride. Our bouquets are perfect for a wedding ceremony or a secular ceremony, a romantic wedding or a bohemian wedding. You can match them to your personality and you can also match them to your favorite flower crown and make it your own.

A bouquet for your tables… If you want a flowery touch on each of your tables, you can choose our wedding bouquets to decorate the centerpieces of your wedding hall. Choose our beautiful and original bouquets in various shades of colors (white, cameo colors, oranges, turquoise, pastels, ivory, colored, purple, fuchsia, green) for the decoration of your room or your place of the ceremony all in a rural, romantic and floral spirit.

Our floral arrangements, whether for our hair accessories or for a bouquet of flowers, are meticulously thought out to never disappoint you. In addition to our original bridal bouquets, we make hair accessories (our sweet flower crowns, flower combs, bun picks and many other accessories) made from stabilized flowers and personalized for you. The bride and groom are honored on this important day and deserve to spend the most beautiful days of their lives under the sign of flowers and love.

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

For pearl enthusiasts, succumb to our Raphaëlle Crown, a true tiara for modern-day princesses! Immerse yourself in the enchanting world of the Renaissance with this dreamy and magical piece. Fall in love with its unique structure that gracefully combines pearls and gold.

  • Composition : Pearls and small flowers gilded with fine gold.
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Inspired by the Renaissance, our Raphaëlle crown is an ode to elegance and delicacy, transporting you to a world where dazzling white freshwater pearls mingle with small golden-tinted artificial flowers, creating an exceptional piece for brides and bridesmaids alike.

This crown is much more than a simple wedding accessory, it’s a real ornament, sublimating all types of hair, whether short or long. Whether you opt for a chignon chouchou, elegant braids, bohemian pigtails, or prefer to let your hair down for a natural look, wearing the Raphaëlle seasonal flower crown will enhance your trendy look and your hippie-bohemian wedding!

To finish off another new style that’s more luxurious, add a sophisticated touch with elastics, small barrettes or hair clips. If simplicity isn’t your thing, don’t hesitate to accessorize your look by combining a variety of wedding accessories. Be creative to create looks that match your hairstyle ideas and shine!

The fusion of romantic, bohemian and sophisticated is expressed in every detail of the Raphaëlle flower crown. Whether you’re getting ready for a wedding, a special occasion or simply to embellish your everyday look, this handmade head jewel will add a delicate, floral touch to your look for a whimsical effect! Discover the softness of little flowers and the sparkle of ornaments, and let our Raphaëlle hair accessory be your accomplice for exceptional moments!

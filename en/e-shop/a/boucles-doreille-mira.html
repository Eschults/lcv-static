Mira earrings

95€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The burgundy Mira earrings are perfect for adding a touch of femininity. With their intense color, they’ll illuminate your bridal or guest outfit. A new wonderful color to bring a classy look !

  • Composition: Burgundy hydrangea preserved flowers. Gold-plated structure.
  • Size: 5 cm or 4 cm and/or on request can be mounted on clips for non-pierced ears on request
  • Shelf life: eternal
  • Storage instructions: keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 days from the order

Our new collection has finally arrived ! Our Indian Summer capsule is an invitation to savor the last moments of the beautiful season…

Let’s journey to the forest of Indian fantasy, where the tree of life, precious gemstones, and crystals like lapis lazuli, rose quartz, emerald, turquoise, amethyst, as well as lovely pinks and mystical creatures reside. This magical world has inspired the Mira hoops, which are also a statement of charm and mystery. These earrings are composed of burgundy hydrangea flowers. A warm and intense color to reveal your femininity…

These earrings will make an ideal gift if you’re looking for a birthday present, a precious gift for a friend (or for yourself, as we always value self-love!). Composed of stabilized hydrangea flowers and gold-plated hoops with a diameter of 5 or 4 cm (according to your preferences), they are the sunny and elegant pair to acquire for any occasion !

The burgundy color of the hydrangeas represents our most discreet feelings, like a Pandora’s box never to be opened. Their petals symbolize our femininity and gentleness perfectly. A bohemian accessory par excellence, true earring jewelry that will enhance all types of clothing: crochet tops, two-tone dresses, silver pants, you just need to choose the right color palette to create the perfect mix & match !

As an ethical jewelry house, we continuously strive to create elegant jewelry to make you look sublime. Whether it’s gold hoops, dangling earrings, rings with semi-precious or fine gemstones, our extensive collection of women’s jewelry will add a touch of glamour to all occasions. And how should you properly care for these costume jewelry pieces? When you go on vacation to an island or on a honeymoon like in Tahiti, for example, don’t forget to protect them from the sun, heat, and humidity. By being mindful during your beach outings, these original earrings will last indefinitely !

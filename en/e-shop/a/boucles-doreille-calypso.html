Calypso earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
70€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Simplicity and delicacy are the watchwords of our delicate Calypso earrings adorned with preserved hydrangea flowers and fine freshwater pearls. Sober and light earrings to be worn alone or combined with one of our crowns or combs from the “L’Odyssée” collection.

Designed with clasps for pierced ears, these earrings can also be clipped on request.

  • Composition: preserved hydrangea and gypsophila, freshwater pearl, gold-plated structure
  • Size: 4 cm
  • Shelf life: the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Let yourself be tempted by a touch of freshness and novelty with our new and extensive collection on the theme of L’Odyssée. You will find plenty to fill your jewellery box with beautiful earrings, bracelets and a wide range of head jewellery such as flower crowns, barrettes and hairpins sets. Something to sublimate your femininity. In L’Odyssée, the Calypso nymph represents bohemian and glamorous life, two adjectives that we have tried to transcribe through our Calypso earrings.

These sober and original earrings made from preserved hydrangeas and gypsophila are ideal as a gift. Its gold-plated structure with matching soft water pearls gives it a bicolour and timeless look to be worn without moderation for all occasions.

For this collection we have made extensive use of precious pearls. These white pearls and coral are two discreet and original materials that we have used a lot for this marine collection to dress up your looks and give you an elegant and feminine look. If you wish to wear earrings with a more fantasy look, we can offer you the Electre or Marina earrings giving off a golden halo that will sublimate your outfit and make you shine. Moreover, these pretty and discreet earrings designed with a clasp for pierced ears can be mounted in clips on request. Indeed, our pairs are original for this aspect, that pierced ears are not an obligation and therefore make the ideal gift. For women, lobe piercing, although very common, is not systematic and this is why our women’s jewellery has this alternative. In this collection we have a wide range of women’s earrings. You will find sleepers such as Anemone earrings, creoles such as Poema, the Naïa with beautiful cultured pearls that hang down and play on asymmetry or the Ezili adorned with sublime Swarovski crystals. We have also tried to play with different geometric shapes, with more oval loops such as the Dana or circles such as the Poseidon. The choice is infinite and you can also find jewellery in the form of a chip, but also in the form of a sleeper, Creole or pendant in the permanent collection. And always with the possibility of putting them under clips and not with clasps. For a nice gift, you can match the earrings with another product from our jewellery shop. Indeed, it is always a good idea to coordinate the colours and theme of your earrings with a pendant such as a necklace, a rush, a solitaire or rings. You will find in our collection the jewellery in golden tones that will enhance your outfit.

The house of Les Couronnes de Victoire advocates Made in France and the quality and know-how that goes with it. All our accessories are made in our Parisian premises by little fairies who use preserved flowers, rhinestones, precious stones, and all sorts of other elements to make the piece all the more precious and eternal.

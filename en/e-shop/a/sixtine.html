Sixtine

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
320€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Absolute favorite, the Sixtine Crown combines the delicacy of pearls and the poetry of flowers within the same sumptuously gilded structure. A bold and powerful piece that complements any bridal hairstyle. You won’t find this piece anywhere else with its undisputed baroque charm.

  • Composition: Stabilized golden hydrangeas and wildflowers, pearls, leaves gilded with fine gold.
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Sixtine, our hair comb from the new Renaissance collection, is a unique piece adorned with hydrangea blossoms, golden stabilized field flowers, white pearls and gilded leaves. Inspired by a magical era and its exquisite beauty, this perfect and sophisticated hair accessory will sublimate your wedding hairstyle, add a bohemian and glamorous touch to your chignon and pair well with your wedding dress.

While the rhinestones and crystals of bridal accessories such as tiaras catch the light, hair accessories such as barrettes and glittering hairpins will ensure perfect hold. Whether you opt for a romantic banana chignon, an elegant braid or a chic ponytail, our comb adorned with pearly pearls, golden flower petals and golden leaves can be worn to suit any hairstyle. This trendy bridal accessory is not only compatible for accessorizing your hair, but also for tying up flyaways, keeping them intact for a haute-couture look and an elegant finishing touch.

Discover our Sixtine golden comb, created for sophisticated brides and their special day, or even for all occasions (especially romantic moments)! Let yourself be tempted by this head jewel, whose refinement will perfect your bridal hairstyle. And if you’d like another selection of accessories, we also offer wedding jewelry such as flower crowns, hair clips, stone barrettes, combs, veils, headbands, earrings, bracelets and more. All the accessories designed by our talented craftsmen are dedicated to you, the bride-to-be, your bridesmaids, and your groomsmen too! Don’t wait any longer and discover our jewelry and accessories for a country wedding or for the holidays at Les Couronnes de Victoire.

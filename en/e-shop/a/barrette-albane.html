To help you make your choice for the big day, here are some tips on what hairstyles are on trend with a wedding barrette. Choosing a hairstyle for a wedding is essential, and barrettes prove to be accessories that sublimate any hair style. Whether you have short, medium or long hair, there are a multitude of romantic and glamorous styles perfect for the occasion. Here are a few ideas for trendy hairstyles that show off a wedding hair clip.

The classic chignon is always a popular choice for brides. You can opt for an elegant, sophisticated low chignon, where bridal barrettes can be integrated to add a touch of sparkle. Whether the barrette is adorned with pearls, rhinestones or even a floral design, it will subtly enhance your hair. For a bohemian effect, consider a loose chignon with loose locks framing the face, where the barrette can accentuate the romantic side.

Braids continue to be fashionable, and wedding barrettes are an integral part of this trend. A delicate herringbone braid or plaited braid can be embellished with a sparkling barrette. If you have long hair, consider a low braided ponytail held in place by a barrette, with wavy lengths for a casual-chic look. Brides with frizzy hair can adopt a braided hairstyle, accessorized with beautiful barrettes to highlight their natural texture.

A voluminous blow-dry with long locks can be enhanced by the addition of barrettes or combs. This style is ideal for a chic, contemporary wedding. Barrettes can be chosen in shiny materials, adding a touch of glamour to this simple yet elegant hairstyle. Alternatively, for an even more personalized look, you can use a barrette decorated with crystals or pearls, as is the case with the Albane barrette.

If you’re looking for a more elaborate hairstyle, the braided chignon is particularly trendy. A twist of hair that becomes a chignon can be accessorized with head jewelry for a sophisticated look. Brides can also opt for a high chignon held in place with a ribboned barrette, such as the Alma barrette, which falls delicately to the back.

For those wishing to let their hair down, a romantic look is within reach with a headband or headband. A simple hairstyle with loose curls, accessorized with a barrette, offering a country-spring spirit. This style works particularly well for outdoor weddings.

For a boho wedding, pairing a barrette with a flower crown can create an enchanting look. Flower crowns work perfectly with wispy locks or natural waves. Consider incorporating floral barrettes into your hairstyle for a harmonious, romantic look.

Bridal hair clips are more than just an accessory. They can transform and sublimate a hairstyle, whether you choose a chignon, loose curls or an elegant braid. With these styles in mind, don’t hesitate to consult a professional hairdresser or your hairdresser to create the perfect hairstyle to match your wedding dress and personality. Trendy hairstyles with barrettes are as varied as weddings themselves, and it’s essential to choose the one that best represents you on your big day.

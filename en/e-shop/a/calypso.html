Calypso Crown

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
138€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Do you dream of a bohemian and country crown ? The Calypso crown is made for you. Decorated with delicate little hydrangea flowers and small wild flowers in creamy white tones, it will delight a loose, slightly wavy hair but can also accompany a bohemian half tail or a bohemian braid. Dare the total bohemian look by matching it with our iconic Maria earrings.

  • Composition : preserved hydrangea, gypsophila, lagurus, marcela and ruscus, gold metal combs
  • Size: unisize. Can be adjusted with the ribbon on the back
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

The Calypso flower crown is the ideal bohemian flower crown. The dazzling white flowers in this sumptuous hair accessory are eternal natural flowers. In fact, all our flowers are fresh flowers that have become preserved. So you can keep them for a lifetime without them being artificial or dried. The floral arrangement of this crown is delicate and romantic. You will find preserved Hydrangea, Gypsophila, Lagurus, Marcela and Ruscus. Your pretty floral crown will be the ideal ornament for your wedding ceremony. The crown comes in one size, and can be adjusted to your head size with the ribbon on the back. This ornament will always enhance your hairstyle and bridal veil. For the bohemian bride-to-be, or hippie as glamour, different hairstyles can be achieved with this jewel. You can opt for a bun or a ponytail, loose or braided hair. In order to make styling easier for you, we offer you several tutorials on our Pinterest account to help you learn how to create a hairstyle from our floral creations and how to match them to your wedding theme. If you need an idea for a hairstyle, to learn how to braid your hair, or how to tame your long hair to make it look beautiful, our Pinterest paintings are the right source of inspiration. All flowers can accessorize your wedding hairstyle as soon as they are transformed into hair accessories. From the centrepiece, through your bouquets of flowers to your sublime hair, you will have a perfectly flowery and bohemian wedding! In the same collection, we invite you to discover earrings, buttonholes, tiaras, bracelets… So many original creations made of hydrangeas, small flowers, pearly pearls, crystals and other noble components to dress and style your hair for your rural ceremonies or your bohemian wedding. At Les Couronnes de Victoire, we offer a new style of floral art for brides and their weddings. Preserved flowers are the originality of the house and our floral arrangements are made with all our heart. Authenticity being a value of the house, we also propose for all our creations to be personalised. Enough to dazzle your guests with a creation that resembles only you. For all these reasons, we propose to our future brides and their friends, perhaps bridesmaids, to privatize the showroom for a floral workshop just for them. Don’t hesitate to book!

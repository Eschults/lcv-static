Amaya mini comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
48€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

With delicate touches of pink and ivory, the Amaya mini comb is the perfect subtle, bohemian touch for brides looking for a simple, floral accessory. You can also combine it with the earrings of the same name for a unique and matching bohemian set.

  • Composition: ruscus, pink and ivory gypsophila, powder pink hydrangea and oats stabilised with gold metal comb
  • Size: around 1,5 inches
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this mini comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

California Dreaming is our new collection of spring-summer wedding accessories inspired by hippie, bohemian and country style. For a romantic, glamorous or more minimalist wedding, fall for our jewellery and accessories that will bring the romantic and bohemian touch to your look.

The Amaya mini comb, in pastel and ivory, is the essential accessory for your wedding, for a discreet, delicate, sophisticated and minimalist look. Composed of small hydrangea, gypsophila and phalaris flowers

How to style your hair when you are invited to an event, when you are the bride or a bridesmaid? This is a common question. The mini comb can be worn on loose hair, with a half-tail. Take two strands of hair on either side of your face, twist them or put them up in a bun, and then put the mini comb in place to attach them to your hair. To create a sophisticated hairstyle, curl the lengths. The comb will then be your flowery, fancy head jewel to enhance your hairstyle. It will adorn your hair tied on a high or low ponytail, simply put it on top of the tail. For bridesmaids or brides who prefer a bun (high or low), place the hair accessory on top of the bun. For braids, place the mini comb on the top of the head or on the bottom of the head.

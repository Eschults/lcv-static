Nami Mini Comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
48€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Have you opted for our Nami crown or earrings with delicate notes of pastel pink and preserved wildflowers ? Match your set to your accessory by offering the Nami mini comb to your witnesses and bridesmaids.

  • Composition : powder pink preserved hydrangea flowers, preserved gypsophila, oats and marcela, bronze metal structure
  • Size: around 4 cm
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this mini comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Our new collection L’Odyssée is a collection that will bring a fresh, sea breeze to your wedding. All our jewellery and accessories are designed to sublimate your wedding outfit and make you the mermaid of the ceremony. Among these elegant accessories, you will find our Nami mini comb, perfect for a wedding. It will become your favourite head jewel.

This discreet and trendy mini comb is made of natural flowers that have been stabilised beforehand. Among the flowers adorning this wedding accessory are pampas, ferns, preserved roses and other pretty flowers. Everything to give your wedding a country and fancy touch and make you a sublime bohemian bride.

The interesting thing about this comb is the way it is worn. Indeed, as a hairstyle for your wedding, you can gather your hair into a pretty low bun and hang the comb on it. You can also make a banana bun. The advantage of choosing to have your hair tied up as a bridal hairstyle is that you can keep the back of your wedding dress free. And if it has a nice neckline, it will allow all the guests to admire your bare back. You can also opt for a pretty half ponytail and accessorise it with the Nami mini comb. In addition to dressing your wedding dresses, it can also adorn your evening gowns. This comb can be reused for all occasions. For an even more romantic and glamorous final touch, you can match your mini Nami comb with the Nami buttonhole for men, to create even more coherence and harmony. If the mini comb is not part of the head jewellery you were thinking of using to dress your hair, we also have tiaras, flower crowns, bun pins, large combs, hair clips and headbands in our selection of accessories. For women who also want to dress their ears, you can find romantic earrings in our Odyssée collection that can be mounted on clips. Like our chic and original calypso earrings, whose soft water pearls can complete the princess and mermaid side of your wedding dress. We also offer you pretty bracelets. For brides who wish to accentuate this bohemian aspect as a wedding theme, you can think of the room decoration in the image of your comb. As wedding decoration ideas, you can place rose petals, candles or put small bouquets of pampas and ferns as table decoration in your wedding room. You can also match the flowers in your bridal bouquet with those of your comb for more refinement.

You can find our wide range of hair and other accessories on our pretty e-shop or by visiting our Parisian showroom. When the bride-to-be comes to our boutique, she will be able to try on the flowers and take advantage of the hairdressing and wedding accessories advice from our consultants. You can also come for your bridal shower with your bridesmaids and create and decorate your bridal jewellery from scratch or from a pre-existing piece of jewellery. All the accessories are original and are made above our showroom, so we can make any alterations you wish to your bridal jewellery and thus match your favourite flowers to each other.

Goldie earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
120€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Goldie earrings are the ultimate romantic earrings. Its delicate flower arrangement with stabilised roses and eucalyptus exudes a wave of elegance and sophistication when worn. They are the perfect pair to finish off a sleek and chic bridal look.

  • Composition: gold-plated frame, fuchsia, white, yellow and pink roses, powder pink eucalyptus and hydrangea and stabilised pink gypsophila
  • Size: 2 inches
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions : keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order

The 70’s, their ethnic and hippie inspirations are in the spotlight at Les Couronnes de Victoire. Find California Dreaming, our great new collection of pieces for women. Inspired by flower power, Californian pop life, and Lana del Ray, we offer you a vast collection of original, sophisticated and bohemian accessories. Let yourself be tempted by our fancy, colorful and elegant pendant earrings with gold frames, flower crowns, flower hats, combs and bracelets. The arrival of summer is the time for sunshine and fantasy. Take your combs and flower picks out of your jewelry box to make you look gorgeous.

Ode to femininity, our glamorous and original Goldies earrings are golden hoop earings, mounted on a gold-plated structure. Real precious jewel, its golden steel perfectly matches the color of the chosen flowers: its hydrangea roses, dressed with ear stems and eucalyptus form a cameo of pink, themselves raised with a bright green and a pale yellow. Its golden rings, round in shape, are enhanced and decorated by golden steel flowers. These women’s earrings are worn only on pierced ears, hence, there won’t be any possibility to change the clasps and mount them in clips.

Wear them as a set with the Camilla crown, set with flowers in the same tones for a very colorful look, or wear them alone to make them more discreet and for a more sober and chic effect. Wearable for any occasion, this pair of earrings is a perfect gift for a birthday or to add to your jewelry collection!

Les Couronnes de Victoire handcrafts your wedding, event and everyday accessories in stabilized flowers. Whether you want to find lever-back earrings, stud earrings, rhinestone and glitter earrings, beaded flower earrings, multicolored earrings, geometric earrings, asymmetrical earrings, openwork earrings or matching earrings… Whether it’s for a birthday gift or to give as a present, you’ve come to the right place, choose according to your personality! Come and meet us in our boutique to see all our models of accessories and jewelry for women at 230 rue Saint-Martin in the 3rd arrondissement of Paris.

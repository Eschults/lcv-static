Maïa earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
100€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our Maïa hoop earrings are the perfect marriage between gold and ivory. They are at the same time bohemian by its stabilized flowers in shades of white, chic and glamorous by its gold touches. Elegance, concentrated in flowery jewelry! Wear it to your ears for a queenly adornment for the most beautiful ball of your life.

  • Composition: ivory hydrangea, gold hydrangea, white russet, white and gold fern, white stabilized gypsophila, white ceramic flowers, 24k 3 micron gold plated structure
  • Taille: 5 cm. Or 4cm and/or on request can be mounted on clips for non-pierced ears on request
  • Shelf life: eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

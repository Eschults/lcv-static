Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our Layla hair clip from our Purple Rain collection is designed in a blue and purple gradation, which will give your hairstyle some sparkle. Its Swarovski crystals will illuminate any hair style. Wear it alone or mix it up with other hair clips from the collection to shine even more !

  • Composition: gold plated metal hair clip, light blue and lilac swarovski crystals
  • Size: unique
  • Shelf life: The crystals in this barrette will stay forever
  • Conservation advice: to keep your jewel sparkling, we advise you to avoid contact with seawater, alcohol, perfume, creams and cosmetics. We invite you to clean your jewel if needed with a soft cloth or brush and clear water
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Our new Purple Rain collection will brighten your days with purple. A new selection of accessories is offered to you so that you can fall in love with our new starry universe decorated with purple. If you are a fan of hair jewelry such as bandanas that we see everywhere on Instagram, headbands, mini clips, scrunchies that follow you everywhere hung with a small scarf for your ponytails that replaces your classic elastics, a hat, a headband, a hat for your winter looks or hair clips, then our collection of accessories is for you. The advantage of purple is that this color stands out on these 2 hair colors. It will bring the final touch you need to enhance your everyday looks and to discipline your beautiful hair.

More and more in vogue in the big Haute-Couture shows, hair clips are part of the must-have fashion accessories. No more hair without an accessory! It is the essential accessory to have on your dressing table. Small rhinestones in your hair = guaranteed sparkle!

Our little Layla barrette, full of shine and softness, will make you fall in love once again. The little extra of this new little hair jewel is that whatever you do, it adapts to all occasions. A great advantage! As a bridesmaid, for the holidays, slipped into an evening hairstyle, to accessorize your outfit of the day? The choice is yours! Moreover, it will blend in with all hair colors, from blond to brunette and whatever your hair thickness, fine hair or thick hair. No questions asked about your lengths! Short hair and long hair will do just fine. To complete your sophisticated look, you can curl your hair with a curling iron and slip this precious jewel onto the front of your head. The two-tone Layla hair clip is made up of 2 Swarovski gem crystals, a pale blue followed by a lilac, on gold metal.

Here, we give you our hair tips to style and wear your Layla hair clip in your pretty hairstyle. First of all, to tie your rebellious locks in the front, and sublimate your face! You can also make a more or less strict bun with the help of pins and slide this accessory how you want to. A high or low ponytail, it can very well come to hold your strands. A braid for the bohemian look is also very popular.

You may have already fallen under the spell of our flagship accessory, the flower crown for your wedding. Make room for your LCV ready-to-wear accessories to complete your jewelry box. You’ll be glamorous and bohemian every day! All LCV accessories are entirely handmade by our little fairies who take great care of your accessories.

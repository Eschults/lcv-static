Calandra earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
95€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our iconic floral creoles come in a sumptuous shade of green: from emerald green to lagoon blue. They will enhance any outfit to give character and depth to any look, no matter how simple! These earrings can be mounted on clips for non-pierced ears on request.

  • Composition: stabilized hydrangea flowers, two-tone emerald green and lagoon blue, gold plated structure
  • Size: 4cm (or 3cm or 5cm on request)
  • Shelf life: eternal
  • Storage instructions: keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 days from the order

Among our new accessories, the Calandra earrings will delight the flower lovers and can be a nice Christmas gift.they are composed of our iconic hydrangea flowers.these original and sophisticated earrings will seduce you with its sublime gradation of emerald green to a lagoon blue. Mounted on gold-plated rings, they will add character to any outfit. Let yourself be tempted by their intense colors to bring a touch of femininity and elegance. These original earrings will complement your extensive collection of precious earrings. To accessorize a party outfit, a special occasion and even your wedding, this pair of earrings will be ideal. This pair can be paired with our new Eden necklace for a refined ensemble that will make you shine endlessly. If you’re looking for a holiday outfit, you can go for our iconic gold earrings.

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a jewelry design house for women. All of our creations and especially our dangling earrings, hoop earrings, clip-on earrings, and barrettes can be worn on a daily basis. Each jewelry collection has its own inspirations and feminine colors. You are sure to find your favorite among our elegant creations to brighten up any outfit.

In addition to women’s earrings, we create flower crowns, barrettes, combs, veils, hats, bracelets and we have a men’s accessory that is the boutonniere. You can match your accessories to your witnesses and bridesmaids and you can even make them a gift by offering them a barrette or a boutonniere matching your accessory. We practice customization which allows you to add or certain elements such as sequins, white pearls, gems, rhinestones, gemstones, crystals, cultured pearl, colored pearls, foliage … For women who are looking for a bohemian side, fantasy, glamour and flowers you are in the right place. We have pieces of ear jewelry that will sublimate you. There are accessories for all tastes: discreet, original, colorful, imposing, minimalist, or even multicolored! All occasions are good to wear earrings and flowers.

Sandro earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
80€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Sandro earrings will give you a delightfully playful look with their long gold chain intricately woven with pretty pearl flowers. An original and timeless design that will charm you with its delicacy and finesse.

  • Composition: Pearls and gold-plated chain, ear stem in gold-plated silver
  • Size : 8 cm
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Welcome to the Renaissance world, where past and present intertwine to enhance the beauty of women on their special day! Our team of artisans has put together a selection of costume jewelry for you to enjoy, so let yourself be tempted by this novelty…

Here’s our pair of Sandro earrings, featuring white pearls and gold-plated chains. Once again, we’ve prioritized simplicity, adding just two main elements to create a discreet, timeless cultured pearl for women. For us, original earrings are sometimes the simplest earrings. Those that aren’t adorned with several precious stones, diamonds, sapphire, turquoise, ruby, quartz, lapis lazuli, emerald, etc., those that aren’t too sophisticated and detailed, but simply discreet.

With the push-back clasp, wearing jewelry will become easier than ever, and these costume jewels will hold up throughout the day. Whether it’s for a rustic wedding, a bohemian affair, or an extravagant birthday, you can wear Sandro earrings for any occasion to enhance your femininity and add an elegant touch to your glamorous dress. Besides special occasions, you can also dress them up for your everyday life. These women’s earrings will also make an ideal gift if you’re looking for something special for your loved ones. Don’t wait any longer to shine brightly with this refined creation!

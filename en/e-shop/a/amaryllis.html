Amaryllis

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
165€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Here is the Amaryllis crown with delicate pastel shades for a wildly bohemian result. Romantic and precious, this women’s accessory blends rose, mauve, and violet for an assembly of infinite softness. Want to create matching looks with your family ? Discover our Coline crown for mischievous little girls !

  • Composition : Stabilized pink and mauve hydrangeas, stabilized mauve and violet gypsophila, dried limonium, gold-plated structure.
  • Size : One size. The crown is mounted on combs and can be worn either in the front or at the back to adorn a bun or a bohemian braid.
  • Shelf life : The flowers composing this crown are eternal.
  • Storage instructions : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity.
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order date.

Amaryllis Crown from Louise Misha’s bucolic collection is much more than a simple hair accessory. It is an exquisite piece that embodies the elegance and refinement of high jewelry. Carefully crafted, it evokes the delicacy of a necklace adorned with precious pearls, with its stabilized pink and mauve hydrangeas, mauve and violet stabilized gypsophilas, and dried limoniums, resembling shimmering pearls.

This crown, mounted on a gold-plated structure, reveals a striking, almost magical brilliance, reminiscent of the most beautiful jewelry creations set with sparkling crystals. The golden details add a touch of glamour and sophistication, while the flowers harmoniously blend together, like a finely coordinated jewelry set.

It is worn with the grace of a delicate bracelet, evoking the finesse and lightness of silver jewelry, but with the nobility of gold-plated gold. Its mounting on double combs ensures a perfect hold, thus ensuring flawless stability on the head, while giving it a bohemian and romantic allure, ideal for enhancing a hairstyle or accessorizing a ceremonial outfit.

Louise Misha’s Amaryllis Crown is much more than a simple hair accessory; it is the embodiment of refinement and elegance, a precious piece that will enhance every moment with its delicacy and timeless charm.

This crown pairs perfectly with other accessories from the same range; the Coline bracelet, or the hoop earrings of the same name, Amaryllis. You can also decide to pair this crown with other accessories from Les Couronnes de Victoire, not necessarily from the same collection, such as dangling earrings, bracelets for little girls, or even a matching boutonniere.

Full of refinement, flower-set crowns like the Amaryllis crown are suitable for all occasions. Unlike a crown set with rhinestones, pearls, crystals, or other gemstones… Flowers are halfway between jewelry and fantasy. They are therefore easier to wear than fine or semi-precious stones set on the mount of white gold, yellow gold, solid silver, or other precious metals from the jeweler. That’s why it’s often a gift idea for the bride-to-be or for the birthday of someone you love. And indeed, it’s the perfect gift. Evidence of infinite love, the everlasting flowers that compose this fantasy jewel are a true ode to love and joy. It will become a precious lucky charm for whoever receives it. Hydrangea flowers, gypsophilas, stabilized catkins… bring novelty to the compositions and offer your jewel the rarity of a jewel set with original diamonds. Women’s jewelry like crowns, necklaces, rings, and bracelets are always more cheerful when they are adorned with flowers.

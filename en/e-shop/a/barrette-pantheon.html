Play with shades of pink, red, and violet to illuminate all your outfits. Panthéon Hair Barrettes is composed of Swarovski crystals of different colors and attached on a gold plated surface. It is perfect to enhance hairstyles like a half ponytail with a side strand or to accessorize a sophisticated hairdo.

  • Composition: Vintage pink Swarovski Crystals, Amethyste, pink fuchsia, red garnet, and Unicorn, Gold plated clip 24 carats 3 microns
  • Size: 6cm
  • Shelf life: Swarvoski crystals used in making this hair barrette are eternal
  • Storage Instructions: To keep your jewel sparkling, we advise you to avoid contact with seawater, alcohol, perfume, creams and cosmetics. We invite you to clean your jewel if needed with a soft cloth or brush and clear water.
  • Delivery time: 5 working days from the date of order

Inspired by this emblematic monument of Paris, the Panthéon hairclip is a small head piece made up of Swarovski sparkling crystals of different colors. Ideal to sublimate all your hairstyles, this wedding hair accessory will bring a feminine and elegant note to your wedding dress as well as your everyday fall outfits.

Composed of subtle shades of pink, red and purple, the Pantheon bridal hair clip is an essential accessory to sublimate your hairdo. On short hair as well as on long hair, this bridal headpiece for women can be worn on the side of your mane or on the back to hold a half ponytail. Do not hesitate to accentuate the glamour of your hairstyle by making curls with the help of a curling iron bringing a real wavy final touch. For women wanting a more sophisticated and romantic hairstyle, they can use this barrette to adorn a low pony tail, leaving a few strands along the face or twist a strand or two and hold them back with this multicoloured barrette.

For a bohemian wedding, the bride-to-be can style her hair up with a low bun or a braid and accessorize her wedding hairstyle with a pretty touch of fantasy. For a country and floral wedding, on the other hand, styling your mane with a fishtail braid embellished with the Pantheon hair pin and other wedding jewellery, will sign your hairstyle with a glamorous touch of originality. Do not hesitate to select a hairdresser who will discipline your lengths and achieve a pretty hairstyle for your wedding-day. A colourful and sophisticated piece of jewelry, our Panthéon barrette can be worn on a satin ivory couture bridal gown for your big day, or with a more sober dress for your everyday outfits. As for the bridesmaid, she can ornate her hair with a sublime half-tail or a braided bun and adorn her hair with bridal jewelry such as an exquisite freshwater pearl necklace, or a bridal hair comb flourished with graceful roses.

The bridal accessories and jewels signed Les Couronnes de Victoires are made by the little fairies of our Parisian workshop. Crafted with a love of well-done work, the beautiful Pantheon hairclip is entirely handmade and adorned with sparkly crystals. Although ideal for a wedding, the Pantheon hairpiece is suitable for all occasions. This accessory can be worn to dress up an evening hairstyle for the holidays, a discreet hairstyle for a communion or to illuminate with a touch of color an outfit to go to work.

Belonging to our new collection “Un coeur à prendre”, the Panthéon hair barrette can be worn alone or as an adornment with a pair of earrings or a bracelet to refine the look.

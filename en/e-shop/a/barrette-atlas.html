Atlas hair clip

Hairpin Eternal Preserved flowers
65€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Atlas hair clip in deep blue tones will be perfect for adding a chic and bold touch to your hair. Clip it on the side or at the back of your head for a truly magical look. You can pair it with the Atlas earrings for a coordinated set.

  • Composition : Stabilized sapphire blue hydrangea flowers, stabilized golden baby’s breath flowers and small sapphire blue crystals. Gold metal plated hair clip.
  • Size : 5 cm
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Our new Autumn/Winter collection, entitled “Starry Night”, is designed to enhance your casual style with all the sophistication required. Featuring a range of fashion accessories such as barrettes, earrings, mini combs, bracelets…, our Atlas barrette is one of our cultured pearls that will add a touch of chic glam to your haircut, whether you have long hair, medium-length hair, short hair, curly hair, fine hair, thick hair or frizzy hair.

The Atlas flower barrette is a work of art in itself. It’s adorned with gorgeous hydrangeas, gypsophila and gemstones in wintry shades that add a touch of color and romance to your hairstyle. These floral elements are subtly arranged to evoke the beauty of bridal hairstyles, whether you prefer a low chignon, braided chignon, banana chignon or dancer’s chignon, a bohemian braid or crown braid, a low ponytail or high ponytail, our barrette will adapt perfectly to your desires. Its subtle setting will keep flyaways in place, guaranteeing optimal support throughout the day or night out.

With this hair clip, you’ll be able to create easy-to-make hairstyles in no time. This hairstyling accessory is ideal for sophisticated fuzzy chignons, bohemian braids or elegant ponytails. Hydrangeas and gypsophila will add volume and lightness to your hair, while sparkling blue crystals will draw all eyes to you, creating a sophisticated and romantic effect. It’s also perfect for any occasion, whether it’s a wedding, gala evening, holiday party or elegant event. It will give you an elegant and original hairstyle, attracting all eyes to you.

For an elegant, sophisticated and resolutely romantic style, opt for the Atlas hair clip from our “Starry Night” collection. Treat yourself to this hair accessory to enhance your trendy hairstyle at any time. Order it today and get ready to shine every day.

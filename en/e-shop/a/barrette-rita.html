Rita hair clip

Hairpin Eternal Preserved flowers
70€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Add some colour to your hair with our Rita hair clip. A real jewel in a gradation of bright and pastel pinks, this hair clip will blend in perfectly with an everyday hairstyle, whether on light or dark hair.

  • Composition: preserved hydrangea flowers, gypsophila and wild flowers, gold-plated hair clip made in France
  • Size: 6cm
  • Shelf life: the flowers that make up that hair clip are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Discover our wide range of fashion accessories for spring-summer with our new Coachella collection. You will find hair jewellery in multicoloured, turquoise, pink and pastel colours. For women with a bohemian and hippie style, this selection of accessories will seduce you.

The very pretty Rita flower hair clip with its preserved hydrangea, gypsophila and field flowers will accessorise your hair wonderfully. With its different shades of pale pink and purple, your hairstyles will shine even more. The hair clip is gold-plated, which perfectly matches the colours of the petals.

This women’s hair accessory is suitable for all lengths and hair types. Whether you have fine or thick hair, long hair or short hair, this clip-on hair clip holds your strands or your lock in your hair very well. It can also be useful for tying up your fringes.

For girls who prefer to have their hair tied up, you can achieve many hairstyles on your hair with this hair accessory. Indeed, you can style it into a ponytail, you can also put it in a banana bun or over low buns. Why not also on top of a nice loose braid?

This romantic hair accessory can be worn for a wedding as a bridesmaid or bride, but also in everyday life or as an evening hairstyle. This is the advantage of hair jewellery, you can wear it for all occasions. This flowery accessory is really an essential accessory. In addition to being discreet and cute, it allows you to tie and discipline your hair. And it can be worn on dark, brown or light hair.

In the same collection, in addition to the flowery hair clips, you will find some with coloured crystals for more fantasy and glitter. The Swarovski crystal will brighten up your hair. We also have earrings on clasps that can be mounted on clips as well as other glamorous head jewellery, a flower crown and tiaras. The structure of our earrings is gold-plated brass.

In our other collections you can find other hair accessories such as combs, precious flower crowns, hairpins, tiaras, hats and headbands. These hair accessories are ideal for a special occasion such as your wedding hairstyle or for a baptism, communion or birthday. Recently we have also released women’s hair accessories and wrists for little girls. These hair accessories are perfect for special occasions. Just like for women, you will find the flower crown, the tiara, the headband as well as flower wrist corsages and hair clips mounted on crocodile clips. This way you can adorn the mane of your little ones with a hair accessory. The crowns for baby girls are adjustable thanks to their satin ribbon.

All the accessories at Les Couronnes de Victoire are handmade in France in our Parisian workshops. They will allow you to sublimate all your outfits on a liberty theme and in all circumstances. You can also customise our floral accessories. You can find us in the marsh in our showroom or on our e-shop.

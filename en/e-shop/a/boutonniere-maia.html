Maïa boutonniere

Boutonniere Eternal Preserved flowers
20€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Composed of delicate gold and white flowers, the Maïa boutonniere will enhance the groom’s or witnesses’ suit with elegance and sparkle. Because these gentlemen also have the right to shine on their big day!

  • Composition : white ruscus, white and gold fern, white and gold stabilized gypsophila
  • Size: one size
  • Shelf life: eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

The Maia wedding boutonniere offers a minimalist and classy composition between gold and ivory. It will bring the final touch to the groom’s suit lapel. Elegant in its simplicity, it is composed of white ruscus flowers and gold gypsophila. The Maia wedding boutonniere dresses up the groom’s suit or that of the witnesses, with a bohemian and romantic touch. This wedding boutonniere is to be matched with the bridal bouquet, your reception venue, the decorations, the bride and groom’s car, to get married with harmonious and unforgettable accessories. Our Maia wedding boutonniere can be worn for special occasions such as weddings, engagements, ceremonies but ideally for a wedding. The bride and groom can match it with a wedding accessory from the same product range, to tie the knot on this beautiful day. This wedding boutonniere is a floral decoration that enhances the groom’s outfit. This ivory and gold coloured boutonniere is very refined and harmonizes wonderfully with the wedding dress of your future wife for your wedding.

The white colour of this wedding accessory makes the Maia boutonniere a very romantic flowery accessory. This wedding accessory brings a touch of freshness that adapts to any wedding theme chosen by the bride and groom: country, bohemian, romantic or flower wedding. Indeed, the wedding bouquet, the wedding decorations, especially in the wedding hall can be matched with the colours of this accessory, for a harmonious wedding. You can put gold and white floral decorations in vases to enhance your wedding hall decoration, using natural flowers, white flowers and dried flowers. For country weddings or a bohemian wedding, these wedding accessories complement your wedding floral arrangements. The wedding flowers selected for each floral arrangement are essential to enhance your reception and ceremony venue. Among the many preparations for the wedding, choosing fresh flowers is one of them. Indeed, flowers are useful to make flower arrangements for your wedding decoration, to decorate a centrepiece, to decorate a hall, to serve as car decoration, to make small bouquets for children or to make wedding bouquets for bridesmaids. These bouquets, flower decorations and floral creations to dress up your decorations can be made by florists or yourself. Your decoration ideas should be personalised, according to your tastes, even the most original ones. The bride and groom’s table can be decorated with roses, orchids or whatever flowers you like. Each table decoration, table runner, vase and other decorative items must be matched and personalised according to the theme of your wedding.

Our floral boutonnieres are handcrafted with care and love by the artisans of the Couronnes de Victoire workshops in the Marais district of Paris. The artisans perpetuate the tradition of French craftsmanship. All Les Couronnes de Victoire flower accessories are made of stabilised flowers. The flowers of the Maia wedding boutonniere have been stabilised, so they will keep their beauty and freshness forever. Each accessory is therefore unique. To keep the Maria wedding boutonniere in good condition, it is important to avoid light, heat and humidity. The Maia wedding boutonniere will be the privileged witness of a beautiful day for a long time

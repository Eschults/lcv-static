Diane

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
205€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Diane crown will adorn your hairstyle with exquisite glimmers of gold, white and mother of pearl. Made of preserved white flowers, pearls and galss beans, the Dine crown is a real jewellery piece. This crown will look lovely on wavy hair or to adorn a messy bun. Combine it with the Elsa or Salome earrings for a most fashion-forward look.

  • Composition: gold plated wire, Preserved ruscus leaves, baby breath and hydrangea flowers, fresh water pearls and glass beans
  • Size: unisize. The soft metal structure of this crown allows it to be tightened or loosened slightly.
  • Shelf life: everlasting
  • Storage instructions: keep away from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 8 working days from the date of order

The Diane flower crown: ike the Roman goddess of hunting, Diana the huntress, you will capture all the hearts wearing this exquisite flower crown, from the latest collection of The Enchanted Forest by Les Couronnes de Victoire. The lightness and the suavity of this flower crown for weddings give it a fiery charm and a rare elegance. Fabricated with preserved flowers the real flower crown holds a unique feature. Undoubtedly, a light brass wire running around the head is threaded with little Ruscus aculeatus leaves, the gypsophila, and hydrangea flowers fused with glass beads and freshwater pearls making it a perfect flower tiara. This indeed, gives an elegance to the flower crown that illuminates the face of the bride and creates an aura of romance making it the finest bridal flower crown on the wedding day for you and your bridesmaids.

The flower crown for brides is conceived in an ideal size which adjusts on the head with a soft brass fastener on the back.

Like all the other products of the workshop at our Flagship Les Couronnes de Victoire, these real flower crowns are created with preserved flowers, which ensures its eternity, just like eternal love. The preservation process secures the color and the gentleness of the plants used to make the bridal flower crown retaining its beauty and its freshness over time. The delicacy of this floral crown still needs some precautions. One must store it in a dry place, away from direct sunlight and heat. These precautions taken, the Diana flower crown will be a witness to the special day of your life like a flower wedding. And will stay with you for a very long time and as a memento of beautiful memories.

The Diane flower crown for brides was prepared with great care and affection by the fairies of the workshop at our Flagship in the Quartier du Marais in Paris. Especially by Franco-french fabrication, which guarantees the quality and expert know-how of the craftsman well reputed for decades all around the world.

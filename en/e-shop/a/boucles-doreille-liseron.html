Liseron earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
95€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Do you feel like floral and minimalist creoles? The Liseron earrings are for you. You will find a new version of our iconic Maria, mounted on a 3 cm structure decorated with baby pink preserved hydrangea flowers. What to give a touch of discreet and elegant color by focusing on the pastel and on a more minimalist volume. These earrings can be mounted on clips for non-pierced ears on request.

  • Composition : stabilised hydrangea flowers baby pink, gold-plated structure
  • Size: 3cm (or 4cm on request)
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions : keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order

Like a hymn to love for women, Victoire was inspired by the feminine and timeless color pink for this capsule “La vie en rose”. An eternally trendy color, this capsule will follow you through all seasons for a girly and bohemian touch. This capsule is composed of a vast collection of rose gold creoles, earrings, hair clips, ribbons, rose gold dangling creole earrings adorned with flowers or precious stones. Les Couronnes de Victoire’s mantra is to make every woman shine and to offer feminine collections to accessorize every outfit.

Floral and minimalist, the Liseron pairs of earrings are gold-plated baby pink hydrangea flower stabilized earrings. These women’s earrings are handmade by our little fairies in our Parisian workshop to find their place in your jewelry box. These earrings for women will bring you a refined outfit and a touch of fantasy to see life in pink.

These discreet and elegant earrings can be worn with a pink gold crystal barrette to sublimate your casual outfit, or your wedding outfit. Our gold earrings jewelry for women are also a nice gift for your bridesmaids. They can be worn with a lacquered bun to bring a sophisticated look. If you have short hair, our gold earrings will hug and bloom the shape of your face. You can also wear them with a high bun, a braid, or a ponytail that will highlight our beautiful girly pink gold earrings.

Les Couronnes de Victoire offers women’s jewelry for all occasions. For a nice gift, your wedding, your engagement outfit, you can wear original and elegant earrings adorned with flowers or fine stones. We offer a wide selection of earrings, dangling earrings, clip-on earrings, (gold or silver plated) to add to your costume jewelry collection. Our costume earrings can also be a beautiful, sophisticated and timeless birthday gift.

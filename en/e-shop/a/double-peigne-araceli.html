Araceli double comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
118€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Ornate with a flower base that is as colorful as bohemian, the Araceli double comb will brighten up any hairstyle on semi-attached or attached hair. With its flexible structure mounted on combs, it will perfectly fit your hair to awaken the sparkling woman that you are.

  • Composition: ruscus, eucalyptus, white and green gypsophila, marcela, pink, purple and light blue hydrangea, stabilised purple limonium and acrolinium
  • Size: 7 inches
  • Shelf life: the flowers in this double comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

California Dreaming is a collection where the pop and colorful Californian universe is mixed with the bohemian, hippie and rural inspirations of the 70s. This collection features soft and warm tones and of course our precious flowers, petals and leaves.

The Araceli double comb is adorned with a base of flowers, composed of ruscus, eucalyptus, gypsophila, hydrangea and other small field flowers. This double comb has the advantage of being very handy and will thus be able to perfectly match your hairstyles. Speaking of hairstyle ideas, this hair accessory, for women, can be worn on several types of hairstyles. Indeed, you can make a ponytail, a semi-attached, a braid, a side hair with your lengths and loose strands, a bun with this accessory in your hair for your wedding. Let’s take the example of a bun. With this hairstyle, the double comb will roll up and adapt to the shape of your bun to fit it perfectly. It will bring the final touch to your bridal hairstyle. You can watch a tutorial on our networks to see the different ways and hairstyles with one of the double combs. Araceli can be worn with different styles of wedding dresses, whether it is satin, tulle, with muslin or lace, it all depends on your tastes and desires.

Les Couronnes de Victoire specializes in hair accessories and hair jewelry for the big occasions of life in stabilized flowers. These hair accessories bring a touch of originality and come to perfect, sublimate, dress, adorn and accessorize your outfit of bride-to-be or every day. In addition to combs, we also create flower crowns, tiaras, earrings, hair clips, hats, headbands, veils, bracelets, a veil and other hair accessories. So that every woman can find the accessory of her dreams we offer customization. Indeed, you can remove and add flowers (be careful not all flowers can be stabilized, like orchid or peony for example), change the color, add purple, silver, gold. You can also add ornaments such as pearls, rhinestones, crystal beads, crystals, sequins, white pearls… So you want to have a discreet accessory or more voluminous, set with a lot of flowers or more ornaments, fancy, romantic or glamorous it is possible. All occasions are good to wear jewelry and accessories made with stabilized flowers.

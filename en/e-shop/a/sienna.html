Sienna

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
195€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Sienna Crown, mounted on wire, is an ode to the Italian Renaissance with its subtle blend of flowers and pearls. It perfectly embodies the seamless fusion of nature and art, giving you the assurance of a graceful and subtle muse. Its delicate shades of pink ensure that this piece exudes a romantic touch par excellence.

  • Composition: Stabilized pale pink and antique pink hydrangeas, stabilized gypsophila, and pearls
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

The “Sienna” hair crown is a delicate floral accessory from the Renaissance collection, carefully crafted to enhance the bride’s look at a romantic wedding. Comprising stabilized hydrangeas in soft shades of pale pink and antique rose, along with stabilized baby’s breath and pearls, this crown embodies bohemian elegance and adds refinement to any hairstyle.

For your wedding, this floral crown is a remarkable piece, perfect for accentuating a bun, a braided hairstyle, or loose hair. Adorned with delicate pearls and gold thread details, it subtly enhances the bride’s hair with an elegant touch.

The pearls add a hint of glamour, while the various stabilized flowers impart sophistication to this accessory. Whether for a rustic bridal hairstyle or a glamorous ceremony, this crown is the perfect adornment to elevate the bride’s hair.

It suits various styles, from loose hairstyles to chignons, ponytails, or romantic braids. Bohemian brides will appreciate its discreet charm, while minimalist brides will find it ideal for a delicate floral touch. This crown, adorned with pearls and ribbons, can also be used to accessorize the hairstyles of bridesmaids. Its floral composition and pastel palette make it a versatile accessory for all wedding-related occasions.

At Couronnes de Victoire, we offer this sophisticated and discreet creation, perfect for those seeking a subtle yet elegant floral touch for their wedding hairstyle. Its delicate tones complement all shades of wedding dresses, adding a touch of color and freshness. It’s the ultimate floral accessory, designed to enhance the bride’s natural beauty. A perfect blend of romance and trend, this crown is a must-have for those aiming to add a floral touch to their wedding look.

At Couronnes de Victoire, all our accessories are handmade, whether they’re women’s hair clips, hair crowns for women and children, flowered earrings for a bohemian touch, wedding veils, flowered bracelets, or boutonnieres for gentlemen. If you have a specific request or questions about the perfect accessory, feel free to schedule an appointment with one of our experts in-store for personalized advice on your upcoming wedding purchase.

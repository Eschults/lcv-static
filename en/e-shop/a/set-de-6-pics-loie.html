Our new Dance with me wedding collection will seduce you with its romantic and elegant magic. All our wedding accessories are suitable for long and short hair and for your wedding hairstyle idea. Whether it’s a pick set, tiara, barrette, or comb, the choice is yours! The hair accessory pick set is perfect if you want to add a discreet finishing touch. This headpiece will accessorise your bridal outfit and hairstyle with a pretty feminine touch. It is also a very trendy accessory if you are a bridesmaid.

For your wedding jewellery set, the Loïe pick set will adorn, sublimate and dress up your hair at your wedding. As its name suggests, the hair pick can be worn in a bun but not only. You can wear this jewel in a ponytail, in a semi-attachment or in a braid. You can even, if you wish, have the set during your wedding ceremony and then remove it to add another more glamorous or fancy wedding accessory. It is also possible to create a jewellery set or personalise your hair accessories to match your guests or bridal party with your pick set. Indeed, the company practices customization which allows to decline a whole range of accessories with the same ornaments.

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a company that makes hair accessories and head jewellery from stabilised flowers. Our iconic piece is the flower crown but we also make earrings, headbands and even a boutonniere. It’s not just the bride-to-be who can be flowered! Our wide range of accessories including a veil, bracelets, tiaras, a bouquet, hats, combs, barrettes and will delight you. Depending on the theme of the wedding, you can change the colours of all the accessories to turquoise, glod pink, gold, silver, ivory etc. In addition, depending on the materials and ornaments that make up your wedding dress (lace, satin, silk, chiffon, with pearls, crystals, sequins, delicate feathers or original rhinestones) you can add to your flower crowns, hairpins, combs or buckles elements to create a reminder and thus perfect your wedding. If the pick set is a hair accessory too discreet for your taste, dare to use crowns for your wedding!

The crown is very trendy for women and there are many different styles: bohemian, chic, sophisticated, elegant, romantic, colourful and original… Whatever you choose, you will be the prettiest bride for a perfect wedding.

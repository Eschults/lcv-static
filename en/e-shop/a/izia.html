Izia

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
52€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The headband, a favourite accessory of little girls (and older ones), is now revisited as a floral accessory with pretty hydrangea and gypsophila petals for the Les Couronnes de Victoire x Louise Misha collection. This bohemian tiara will suit all the most beautiful occasions from christenings to birthdays and weddings. Its very flexible metal structure provides optimal comfort throughout the day.

  • Composition: preserved white, pink and orange hydrangea flowers and mini ivory, pink and copper gypsophila, gold-plated metal structure
  • Size: One size only. The tiara is very flexible and can be adapted to several head sizes
  • Shelf life: the flowers in this tiara are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

The Izia tiara is part of our new LCV x Louise Misha jewellery collection. This wonderful collaboration offers tender and delicate costume jewellery for the bohemian mother and little girls.

The colours of the Izia tiara place it between glamour and romance. It is a delicate head jewel, with refined powdery tones, for the most beautiful of princesses. This pretty piece for little girls, set with hydrangea and gypsophila flowers, will delight your little girl for all occasions. She can choose to wear it for everyday wear, as well as for a princess costume, or for a special ceremony to complete her princess dress. Head jewellery with floral ornaments is often worn for occasions such as a communion, a christening or a wedding. It is the magic of the refinement of this hair accessory that we love at LCV. These flowery accessories have the advantage of sublimating all hair styles, just like a romantic princess crown. Your child can therefore choose this ornament to accessorise a braid, a bun, a ponytail, for long hair, short hair… The hairstyle and haircuts of her choice. If your little princess is not keen on flashy pieces such as those set with rhinestones, crystals, pearls or other elements, the Izia tiara will be the perfect balance between originality and simplicity. It will be her real favourite, both discrete and sublime. It is therefore the ideal gift to give to her or that she can make for the birthday of one of her friends, who will preciously keep her little novelty in her jewellery box, among her other favourite pieces of costume jewellery. Her handmade hair jewel will become the indispensable and rare accessory of her treasure box.

Among the original flowery jewels, if you think she will be more attracted by the flower crown to adorn her hair, you can also be tempted by the Rosa crown from the same collection and other complementary accessories that you will find in L’Enfant by LCV: hair clips, bracelets, other tiaras… Enchant the heart of your princess through the spring-summer season, by offering her a journey through a flowery universe full of sweetness. And for the mother, you too can fall for our flower crown, Clémentine. You will be able to agree with your daughter and inscribe your fusion in the timelessness and infinity of the purity of eternal flowers.

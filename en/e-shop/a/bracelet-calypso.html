Calypso Wrist Corsage

Wrist corsage Eternal Preserved flowers
38€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Give your witnesses a timeless and bohemian accessory with our Calypso wrist corsage and its delicate white stabilized flowers and freshwater pearls.

  • Composition: preserved hydrangea, umbelliferous and gypsophila, freshwater pearls
  • Size: unisize
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this wrist corsage are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

This bracelet is inspired by a beautiful nymph present in the Greek mythology of the Odyssey and named Calypso. Refined and elegant, the Calypso bracelet wonderfully embodies her name. It is adorned with delicate white stabilized flower petals and fresh water pearls. Composed of an adjustable satin ribbon, the Calypso bracelet can be worn in different ways. Simply around the wrist or to bloom a pretty hairstyle, placed around a high bun or a ponytail. The Calypso bracelet will be perfect to bring a touch of freshness to a pretty lace wedding dress.

The pretty hydrangea, umbelliferous and gypsophila flowers that compose this jewel are stabilized natural flowers, they are real flowers treated thanks to a biological process to make them eternal. However, we advise you to keep this ornament away from light, sun and humidity, to keep its brightness forever. Like all of our Les Couronnes de Victoire head jewelry and wedding accessories, our Calypso bracelet is hand-crafted in our Parisian workshop in the heart of the Marais.

The Calypso floral jewelry comes from our “L’Odyssée” collection, a collection for our brides-to-be inspired by the seaside world. It includes various jewels and accessories, such as flower crowns, tiaras, bun pins set with white pearls, flowered hair combs, golden earrings, etc. A pure and bohemian universe, to sublimate women on the most beautiful day of their lives. This bracelet can ideally be worn by bridesmaids, matched with the bride-to-be’s Calypso crown and the groom’s Calypso buttonhole, for a harmonious procession that will amaze your guests. For your wedding, the choice of the floral composition is important, it must be granted to your wedding decoration. Floral arrangements allow you to decorate and bring a warm atmosphere to your reception place: centerpiece, decoration of chairs, arches of beautiful fresh flowers, garland of flowers, bridal bouquet… There are a multitude of flowers and colors: lily of the valley, orchid, lily, tulip, peony, lavender, amaryllis, hibiscus, red rose, white rose, etc… It’s up to you to choose the flowers that best suit the ambiance of your wedding. For a bohemian wedding, choose a composition of white flowers and roses, for a pure and soothing atmosphere. Red roses will be perfect for a glamorous wedding, synonymous with love and romance. For a country theme wedding, choose instead green floral arrangements composed of foliage and green plants.

We invite you to browse through our e-shop, to discover our romantic universe and our many creations: bun picks, flower crowns, hair combs, flower earrings, bracelets, necklaces, hats, veils, headbands set with rhinestones, buttonholes, flower bouquets… A wide choice of original ornaments to sublimate women for small and big occasions.

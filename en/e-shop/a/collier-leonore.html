Léonore necklace

Necklace Eternal Preserved flowers
65€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Léonore necklace is a precious jewel that nestles perfectly in the hollow of your neckline. Thin and discreet, it can be worn for both special occasions and everyday wear thanks to its timeless charm.

  • Composition : Fine gold-plated chain. Freshwater pearls.
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions : keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order

Discover the new piece from Les Couronnes de Victoire’s wedding necklace collection: the magnificent Leonore necklace. This refined jewel embodies elegance and delicacy with its delicate gold-plated chain that highlights a luminous freshwater pearl. From this central pearl, a delicate chain unfolds, adorned with three freshwater pearls, from smallest to largest, forming an exquisite pendant.

The Leonore necklace is designed to enhance your neckline with a touch of glamour and sophistication. Whether you are a bride in search of the perfect jewel for your big day or simply an elegant woman looking to accessorize your outfit, this necklace will capture your heart.

Its timeless design makes it a versatile accessory, perfect for embellishing a wedding dress or adding a touch of refinement to an evening outfit. The freshwater pearls bring a touch of femininity and grace, while the delicate gold-plated chain adds a note of sophistication.

Wear the Leonore necklace alone for a discreet and elegant look, or pair it with other jewels from the collection to create a harmonious set. With its adjustable lobster clasp, it fits perfectly around your neck, allowing you to adjust it according to your preferences.

It pairs perfectly with several of our collections, such as the Renaissance collection, the Dance with me collection, and the L’odyssée collection. In fact, several pieces from these collections would form beautiful sets, thanks to the reminder of the freshwater pearls used for the various earrings, hoops, dangling earrings, but also on the combs, double combs, and crowns.

Look no further, the Leonore necklace is the ideal wedding jewel for all brides in search of finesse and elegance. Treat yourself to this magnificent necklace and let its timeless beauty accompany you on your special day.

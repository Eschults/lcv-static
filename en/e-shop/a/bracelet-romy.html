Romy Wrist Corsage

Wrist corsage Eternal Preserved flowers
22€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Covered with white hydrangea petals, gypsophila flowers and small eucalyptus leaves, the Romy wrist corsage will be ideal to dress the wrists of the little girls in your wedding procession. Thanks to its supple structure, it will adapt to all wrist sizes, and can even surround a bun or a ponytail.

  • Composition: preserved white hydrangea, white eucalyptus and gypsophila, wire structure covered with a brown ribbon
  • Size: 5cm
  • Shelf-life: the flowers in this wrist corsage are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

The Romy wrist corsage belongs to our new collection presenting all hair accessories and small jewellery for children. These accessories, thought and designed especially for little girls will bring a touch of originality for coquette and fanciful girls. When you are a child-girl, all occasions are good to wear our many darling accessories: for parties, to go to school, for a wedding, a baptism, a communion… The ornaments, whether head, neck or wrist jewellery, delight our babies.

The Romy wrist corsage does not aim to accessorise the hair but to dress and sublimate the wrist of your baby-daughter. It is a romantic and flowery jewel that delights the heart and eyes with its softness. Its ribbon, once tied, allows you to adjust the size by forming a pretty knot, which will also adorn the wrist for your little one or baby. The Romy wrist corsage is therefore a very beautiful ornament that you can customise if you or your baby wish to add or modify some floral elements. One thing is for sure, these are original wrist corsages that make their effect. To transform your little girl into a real fairy or princess, it is important to know how she will be able to wear this fashion accessory. You may decide to make a selection of similar accessories such as those of the same name (Romy flowers crown and hair clip). It is also possible to accompany her with different hair jewellery depending on the season. In spring-summer, for example, if your daughter wears prints, you won’t go for a brown or khaki accessory, but rather a bright, light hair accessory like a white headband. Whatever it is, the head jewellery will remain the essential accessory for a little girl’s outfit full of fantasy. It serves a dual purpose, both to beautify the hair and to discipline and style it so that the pretty hairstyle remains sophisticated all day long. The advantage of flowers is that they will always leave pleasant scented notes on the hair, without the need for a spray to scent the hairbrush. Whatever the length, the hair accessories in our children’s collection will enchant little girls and dress up their hair. No need for a wide range but the essentials to adapt to all desires. Whether they decide to tie up their mane or leave it in the wind, styling designed for children will be quick and easy. You won’t need a tutorial to achieve a bohemian or glamorous hairstyle for your princess so that she can wear her hair accessories. As an alternative to flower crowns, wearing barrettes (or a hair clip) alone is also a great idea to simply dress a bun or a ponytail.

If you are still hesitating and don’t know what to choose, the LCV showroom welcomes you by appointment to try on your princess’ hair. This is ideal if it is a wedding hairstyle.

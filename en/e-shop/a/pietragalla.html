Pietragalla

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
300€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

This majestic and singular flower crown is inspired by the famous Prima Ballerina. To make you shine on your wedding day, succumb to our tiara flower crown which is mounted on a delicate cascade of dangling chains for a “wow” effect as in a ballet. Its pendants allow you to dress up your neckline, for a 2 in 1 crown and earrings set. - Composition: hydrangea, fern, preserved white gypsophila, ceramic flowers, freshwater pearls, glass beads, gold-plated chain - Size: one size - Shelf life: eternal - Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity - Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Inspired by the famous dancer Pietragalla, this crown is a strong and charismatic piece from our new Dance with me collection. Victoire offers us an original piece with this tiara crown and its two bright chains on the front embellished by pretty freshwater pearls and glass beads. To bring glamour to your hair with this accessory for women, you can match your pieces to accessorize your bridal bouquet, your bridesmaids’ outfits or match your husband’s boutonniere. You will then be in perfect harmony. For your bridesmaids you can add other hair jewelry such as picks, barrettes, hairpins, headbands or tiaras can be found to perfect your bridesmaids hairstyles and accentuate the romantic side of your wedding. This beautiful hair accessory can also be worn on other special occasions such as an engagement, communion or baptism.

Our Pietragalla hair accessory can be worn on loose and wavy hair. This headpiece lends itself to any bridal hairstyle and can be worn on both short and long hair. It can also be chic to wear it on a half ponytail or a lacquered bun for your wedding. For brides who wish to have their hair tied back, we can offer other hair accessories such as combs or picks that can be used as bun picks. For the bride-to-be, buns are a nice way to show off the back of your wedding dress and to match a nice comb to your wedding veil. You can style your hair in a braid to also reveal your wedding dress and continue with this bohemian wedding idea. It can be lovely to slip in the braid the discreet set of spikes for an even more sublime hairstyle. If you also want to dress up your wrist, the bracelets of the house are perfect for a wedding. If our romantic wedding hair jewelry has seduced you, you can find our permanent collection on our beautiful e-shop. On our website you can find all kinds of hair accessories but not only. The flower crown is there but we also offer other wedding jewelry such as earrings but also pretty veils to accompany your wedding dresses. All the jewelry and accessories of Les Couronnes de Victoire are made by little fairies below our store in Paris. Made in France and the quality that goes with it. Our wedding jewelry is made from stabilized flowers, making it last forever and we offer accessories for all occasions.

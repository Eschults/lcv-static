Rosa

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
58€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Discover our children’s crown from our collaboration Les Couronnes de Victoire x Louise Misha. A bohemian looking crown with powdery notes. This delicate accessory made of preserved hydrangeas and gypsophila will fit perfectly into any little girl’s hair. Ideal for all ceremonies and special occasions or just to wear for fun. The crown is adjustable with a ribbon at the back, making it suitable for all children’s head sizes: from babies to teenagers.

  • Composition: preserved white, pink and orange hydrangea flowers and mini ivory, pink and copper gypsophila, ivory satin ribbon
  • Size: One size only. Can be adjusted with the ribbon at the back
  • Shelf life: the flowers in this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Les Couronnes de Victoire is releasing a new children’s collection for girls in collaboration with Louise Misha, the beautiful brand of formal and everyday clothing for children. We offer a selection of accessories including elegant hair clips, flower crowns, wrist corsages and a pretty headband.

The Rosa flower crown will enhance your little girls’ beautiful dress for special occasions such as weddings, christenings and communions. With its pastel pink and coral petal colours, our Rosa crown for girls will be a breath of fresh air in the bohemian chic style, ideal for spring and summer. For your wedding, thanks to our matchy accessories, you will be able to match the accessories of your ceremony set with those of the little children of honour of the children’s procession. For the little princesses, you can choose a pretty dress, skirt or combishort in ecru, ivory or navy blue with embroidery that will match perfectly with the Rosa crown. For the little bridesmaids or the girl of honour, you need to adapt the formal wear to the season, so that the children are as comfortable as possible. For example, for the warmer season, choose a short-sleeved or strapless linen wedding dress with chic little sandals or ballet flats. You can also add a small cardigan for the evening. For more refinement, you can choose a small romantic ceremony dress with small lace flounces. You will find your happiness in Louise Misha’s collection of dresses. Indeed, the fineness and lightness of the fabrics make it the ideal bridesmaid dress to dress your baby girl for a wedding or special occasion. For ceremonies in colder weather, if you want to go for a beautiful princess dress, go for long sleeves and avoid the short dress without tights.

You can find us in our showroom in Paris. All our accessories are made above our showroom, in our workshops by our manufacturers to offer you original creations.

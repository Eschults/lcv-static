Lou Crown

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
42€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our cute little Lou crown is the perfect way to adorn your little girl’s hair. Made of preserved hydrangea and gypsophila, this delicate white crown will be perfect for a wedding procession, baptism or communion, but it can also be the perfect gift for a birthday or special occasion. Thanks to its white satin ribbon on the back, this crown is adaptable to any child’s head size, from babies to teenagers.

  • Composition: white hydrangea and white gypsoil preserved, white satin ribbon
  • Size: one size only. Can be adjusted with the ribbon on the back
  • Shelf-life: the flowers in this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Les Couronnes de Victoires brought out a little girls line. You will be able to match your hair jewellery with the wedding accessories of the little bridesmaids or the little bridesmaid of honour for your wedding. What could be more romantic?

The arrangements in our delicate Lou flower crown are preserved white small flowers. You’ll find hydrangea and gypsophila in particular to decorate it. Our hydrangeas and gypsophila are fresh flowers that have been stabilised, giving them an eternal life when kept in the right conditions. They are not artificial flowers or dried flowers. This hair accessory adapts to your head circumference with its satin ribbon. This delicate country jewel will make your baby girl look like a princess. This pretty floral crown will allow you to accessorise her hair for a baptism, communion or bohemian wedding procession. To wear the crown on your head, you can make many different hairstyles. First of all, you can decorate both long and short hair with these pretty flowers. Then you can either let your hair down or tie it up. You can style the hair in small braids or in a single braid, or even in a pretty floral bun. For a wedding hairstyle on hair that has been let loose by your little fairy, you can make a half ponytail. This way she won’t have small locks in front. The delicate flower crown will enhance all the hairstyles for ceremonies including weddings. In this same new collection you will find other floral hair accessories for girls for a chic bohemian wedding, hair clips, tiaras, headbands and wrist corsages. You will be able to match the child’s floral composition with the adult’s. In addition to matching the accessory to the bridal hairstyle, you can also match it to the bridesmaids’ or bridesmaid’s small flower bouquets as well as the floral decoration.

All our bridal accessories are hand-made in our Parisian workshops by our little fairies. All the flowers in our floral arrangements are preserved flowers. If you are wondering how to make these and other hair accessories, DIY workshops are organised in our premises. For brides who can’t come to our showroom, you can make an appointment by video. You can then order the accessories you have fallen in love with on our e-shop.

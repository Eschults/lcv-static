Morgane

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
148€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Subtle and delicate, our Morgane crown will compliment any hairstyle with exquisite white and mauve accents. This crown will be lovely with a wedding gown, but it can also be worn all summer with a crisp cotton dress.

  • Composition: Preserved baby breath and limonium, eucalyptus and white hydrangea flowers
  • Size: easy to adjust thanks to the satin ribbon at the back
  • Shelf life: everlasting
  • Storage instructions: keep away from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 8 working days from the date of order

A character from the Arthurian cycle, Morgane is the half-sister of King Arthur and this legend is still alive today in the history of Brittany, the French state of royalty. Owing to this legend, we have designed Morgane flower crowns for weddings and other special moments of your life. This subtle flower crowns speak well for its name and evokes all the Celtic sensitivity with the small white and purple flowers that recall the colors of granite. We can imagine this beautiful bridal flower crown adorning a bride on her wedding day with a flower dress and long loose hair, just as we can imagine it on a romantic date with a floral dress on a summer evening. No wonder, this is a perfect flower crown for a flower girl.

The flower crown Morgane is made with preserved flowers, which means that this flower tiara is eternal and will keep its beautiful colors and softness for the rest of your life. The hallmark of our brand is the everlasting nature of real flowers in our personally designed real flower crowns. Some precautions are important to secure the everlasting beauty of these preserved flowers like avoiding sun, heat, and humidity. This will ensure that this bridal flower crown will be faithful to you for a very long time and remind you of the beautiful memories.

The Morgane bridal flower crown is fabricated with the utmost care and passion for the job carried out in our workshop at our flagship, Quartier du Marais, in Paris. The creation of these unique flower crowns requires patient work that holds the tradition of French craftsmanship, which is as dynamic as always.

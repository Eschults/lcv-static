Sahar earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
95€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Sahar earrings create a true rainbow on your ears, adding a colorful touch for back-to-school. A range of colors from pale pink to raspberry forms a delightful palette, like a beautiful bouquet. Sahar earrings are the floral jewelry to have for your wonderful occasions !

  • Composition: rose raspberry, pale pink, and peach hydrangea preserved flowers. Gold-plated structure.
  • Size: 3 cm or 4 cm and/or on request can be mounted on clips for non-pierced ears on request
  • Shelf life: eternal
  • Storage instructions: to keep your jewel sparkling, we advise you to avoid contact with seawater, alcohol, perfume, creams and cosmetics
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Immerse yourself intensely in the Indian Summer. A capsule full of poetry that celebrates life and everlasting love… A mischievous and unconventional collection where saffron yellow meets delicate pink, where burgundy blends with fuchsia ! Our jewelry house drew inspiration from several timeless cultured pearls for women, such as dangling earrings, original jewelry, and for this novelty, we want to accompany you by immersing you in discreet places where we cannot access, even though we have all the occasions of our lives.

Our timeless Sahar hoops are not just a pretty gift, or costume earrings. They are also the unique expression of our talented creator Victoire, who spends hours thinking about the design of her collections. Here, these hoops are composed of dark fuchsia pink, peach, and pink hydrangea flowers.

A delightful color palette that vividly recalls the sunsets we love so much ! So, these earrings are our pride! The reason why we created all these original earrings ? To honor femininity, to boost your self-confidence. Elegant and precious, they will surely enhance your glamorous look, adding a touch of dreaminess and bohemian flair. Let yourself be tempted !

The Les Couronnes de Victoire house specializes in the jewelry sector; we have launched a variety of jewelry collections. Each collection transports us to a different world and contains various types of accessories such as crowns, combs, and even earring pendants. The flowers we attach to earring jewelry, crowns, boutonnieres, are handcrafted and stabilized through a biological process.

If you desire something new for your jewelry box but your ears are not pierced, we also offer custom clip-on dangling earrings. Need help with gift ideas or engagement ideas? Feel free to make an appointment with us to get additional information.

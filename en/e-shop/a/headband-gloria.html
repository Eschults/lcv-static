Discover Gloria, our white satin headband adorned with an integrated tulle veil. The perfect accessory for brides who want to add a retro and quirky touch to their wedding day look. This accessory easily slips into your hair, whether you choose to wear it down or pulled back in a ponytail. This headband was designed in collaboration with Marie, the creator of Scrunchie is Back fancy accessories.

  • Composition : Headband in satin and delicate tulle veil.
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Discover the Gloria headband, the newest addition to the Couronnes de Victoire. It is the result of a collaboration with the brand Scrunchie is Back. The capsule collaboration between Scrunchie is Back and Couronnes de Victoire has given birth to a collection of hair accessories of incomparable elegance, perfect for enhancing any hairstyle, whether for a wedding, a chic evening, or simply to add a touch of glamour to everyday life, conceived with the expertise, originality, and DNA of both brands.

So here is the Gloria headband, a sophisticated headpiece designed to enhance any hairstyle, whether it’s an elaborate wedding bun or simply loose hair for a bohemian look. Made from high-quality satin, this hair accessory combines modern glamour with retro charm. The satin headband offers a comfortable fit for all hair lengths, whether short or long, and adds a touch of chic to any hairstyle. The delicate veil adds a romantic touch, perfect for weddings, evenings, or any special occasion where you want to add a touch of elegance to your hairstyle. This hair accessory offers subtle sophistication for brides-to-be, bridesmaids, or simply for any woman eager to feel elegant and special.

Whether paired with a sophisticated bun or a simple ponytail, the Gloria headband will add the perfect finishing touch to your look. With its flowing veil and sparkling details, this handmade hair accessory is a true hair jewel, perfect for accessorizing your wedding hairstyles or any elegant outfit. Discreet yet sophisticated, this satin headband will turn heads at any occasion.

Whether you opt for a minimalist style with a discreet elastic from the collection or for a bold look with sequin-adorned headbands and floral brooches, this collection offers a varied range of hair accessories for all occasions. With colors ranging from gold to blush pink to multicolor and ecru, each accessory is designed to complement your outfit and enhance your hairstyle with elegance and refinement.

This collaboration between two iconic brands in the hair universe will allow you to express your unique style with accessories that add a touch of glamour to every moment of your life.

Like all accessories from the brand, this headband was handmade by the little fairies of our Parisian workshop. You are invited to come and see this jewel and many other accessories, from the same collection or even from previous collections, in our two stores, 15 rue du Temple in Bordeaux and at 230 rue Saint Martin in Paris. You can make an appointment with one of our advisors to receive personalized advice and find the perfect accessory for the big day…

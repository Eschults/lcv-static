The new “Renaissance” collection proudly unveils its set of 5 magnificent hairpins named with the tender name of Raphaëlle. These precious accessories, straight out of a fairy tale, embody refined elegance and timeless charm. Each piece in this collection is a masterpiece, merging exquisiteness in its purest form. Comprising six pearl beads, these hairpins captivate with their delicacy and finesse. Their finish gilded with fine gold evokes the splendor of a bygone era while embodying modernity in the world of hairstyling.

The “Renaissance” collection doesn’t limit itself to these exquisite hair jewels. It offers a plethora of enchanting accessories to enhance the bride’s hairstyle and leave an unforgettable impression. From delicate crowns to sophisticated combs, sparkling earrings to sublime chignon pins, each piece is adorned with stabilized flowers in soft, pastel tones. These captivating accessories suit all imaginable hairstyles. Whether it’s a bohemian, romantic, or glamorous hairstyle for a dream wedding, each accessory, whether a headband, an elegant chignon pin, a delicate comb, or a sparkling barrette, harmonizes perfectly with hair, long or short, braided or loose.

The refined details of shimmering crystals, delicate lace, airy feathers, and satin ribbons marry perfectly with the unique pieces of the collection to enhance each bridal hairstyle. Shiny rhinestones, delicate petals, pearl beads, and sparkling sequins elegantly adorn the hair accessories. This collection caters to all brides seeking a sophisticated, rustic, or haute couture touch for their special day. Whether for a sophisticated wedding hairstyle, a relaxed bohemian style, or a romantic and vintage hairstyle, the ‘Renaissance’ collection by Couronnes de Victoire offers an elegant selection of accessories for all tastes and occasions.

These exquisite pieces are the culmination of meticulous attention to detail, designed to complement and adorn the bride’s and her bridesmaids’ hair. They provide the final touch, enhancing each hairstyle with grace and refinement. The little fairies in our Parisian workshop took great care to meticulously create the Raphaëlle hairpin set with their delicate hands. It’s now up to you to preserve your chignon pins by protecting this wedding hair accessory from sunlight, moisture, and heat. All Couronnes de Victoire accessories are handmade, and the flowers in our jewelry and accessories are stabilized through a biological process. The flowers adorning these five headpieces have been stabilized to last forever. Indeed, thanks to this biological process, you can keep the hairpin set forever and perhaps symbolically pass on this wedding hair accessory to your daughter.

Our accessories are not only designed for weddings; it’s very elegant to wear a crown at a communion, or even sport a pretty double comb at a birthday, or our lovely hoop earrings in everyday life!

Lena Hair clip

Hairpin Eternal Preserved flowers
25€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Add a touch of freshness to your little girl’s hair with our delicate Lena hair clip with preserved eucalyptus leaves and gypsophila. This hair clip is mounted on a crocodile structure that fits very well even in the finest little girls’ hair. Ideal for a ceremonial hairstyle, such as a wedding, baptism or communion, this hair clip can also be worn for a festive occasion such as a birthday.

  • Composition : white stabilised eucalyptus and gypsophila, bronze barrette structure
  • Size: 4cm
  • Shelf-life: the flowers in this hair clip are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Les Couronnes de Victoire have released a new collection for little girls. Thanks to this, mothers will be able to match their accessories and formal wear with those of their little princess. These accessories can be worn for special occasions such as a baby’s baptism, a communion or a wedding. At the wedding, with a pretty bridesmaid’s dress, your daughter will be very well dressed.

Our white Lena hair clip is made of preserved eucalyptus and gypsophila. This pretty pastel hair clip for your baby will go very well with a beautiful princess dress. With its white colour all the colours of a little dress or other garment such as overalls for little girls will go with it. Choose fuchsia, beige, ecru or navy blue.

If the christening ceremony or other event is in spring-summer and you’re wondering what dress or outfit to make your baby girl wear, you can opt for a beautiful long or short dress with short sleeves or straps accompanied by sandals or ballerinas. For girls who are a little more nervous, you can dress up the beautiful dress, overalls or combishort with a little cardigan. The most important thing is that the formal wear is comfortable for your elegant and adorable princesses.

All our Les Couronnes de Victoire accessories are made in our Parisian workshops. You will find the quality made in France and original creations to accessorise your wedding dress, ceremonial dress or everyday dress. You can find our collections on our e-shop.

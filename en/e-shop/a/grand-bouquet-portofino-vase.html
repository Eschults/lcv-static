Tall Portofino bouquet with vase

Bouquet Eternal Dried flowers
100€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Portofino bouquet in dried and preserved flowers combines delicate bohemian white pampas and lagurus flowers with small yellow flowers of Sanfordi and Achilea to bring light to the composition. We like the spring and delicate side of this bouquet. With its vase in the vintage spirit, this bouquet will know how to dress your interior with a delicate floral touch.

  • Composition : pampa, dill, white lagurus, yellow sanfordi, yarrow, white oats, gypsophila nature
  • Size : This bouquet measures approximately 35 cm in height and 30 cm in diameter. The matching vase is 19 cm high and 12 cm in diameter.
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

The Portofino bouquet owes its name to a beautiful seaside village located on the Italian coast. Reminiscent of the colorful facades of this unique place, the Portofino bouquet is adorned with small yellow flowers of sanfordi, accompanied by pampas, dill, white lagurus, yarrow, white oats and gypsophila. A beautiful bouquet with pastel shades and springtime allure. It is accompanied by an elegant vintage glass vase, to sublimate your interior with a colorful and romantic note. You’ll love the Portofino bouquet to bring light to your home.

Peonies, orchids, daisies, tulips, roses, lavenders, dahlias, lilies, hydrangeas, hibiscus… There is a wide choice of flower varieties. It’s up to you to choose the floral arrangement that best suits your interior decoration. All occasions are good to buy a nice bouquet of flowers, for you or for the people around you ! Indeed, the Portofino bouquet can be a nice gift for your loved ones, for a birthday, a baptism, a communion, or for Mother’s Day. The large Portofino flower bouquet is also suitable for your wedding. It will bring freshness to your wedding reception place, your table decoration, or for the car decoration of the bride and groom.

At Les Couronnes de Victoire, all of our flower bouquets are composed of stabilized flowers and dried flowers, so that they last forever. We only work with real flowers and not artificial flowers. These bouquets are imagined and designed in our Parisian workshop in the heart of the Marais, with love and passion. It is also possible to make your own floral creations, choosing each flower, each leaf and each color that makes up your bouquet. Les Couronnes de Victoire is not only bouquets of flowers, we also offer you a wide choice of accessories and jewelry decorated with beautiful stabilized flowers, precious stones and crystals. Flower wreaths, flower earrings, bun picks set with petals, gold bars, moose hair combs, straw hats… each jewel and accessory is unique, timeless and eternal.

Daliane

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
52€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Daliane tiara is designed for all little princesses! Perfect for special occasions, it adjusts to fit all head sizes to be worn during festive days. It features hydrangea and gypsophila flowers in soft pastel hues. This bucolic piece was crafted in collaboration with Louise Misha.

  • Composition : Stabilized mauve hydrangea, stabilized mauve, yellow, and violet gypsophila, stabilized eucalyptus, and dried glixia.
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order date

Child’s Diadem Daliane from Louise Misha and Couronnes de Victoire is a beautiful tiara mounted on a delicate structure of gold-plated fine headband. This hair accessory is a true work of art, designed to enhance your little princess’s hairstyle on special occasions such as weddings, baptisms, or special ceremonies.

Carefully crafted, the Daliane crown is adorned with stabilized mauve hydrangeas, gypsophilas, small very rustic flowers, mauve, yellow, and violet stabilized, as well as stabilized eucalyptus leaves and dried glixias. These delicate flowers and foliage bring a touch of enchantment and romance to the ensemble, perfect for a bohemian chic atmosphere or a floral theme.

The gold-plated fine headband adds a final touch of glamour and elegance to the crown, while its structure ensures a perfect hold on your little girl’s head, or even big girl, throughout the day. Whether for a hairstyle with a bun, a braid, loose hair, or even an evening hairstyle, this diadem will be the perfect accessory to complete her look with style.

Give your little girl a true hair jewel with the Daliane crown from Louise Misha and Couronnes de Victoire. With its refined details and golden touch, it will add a touch of magic and sophistication to her outfit, making her the queen of the day.

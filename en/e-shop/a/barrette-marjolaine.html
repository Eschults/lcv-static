Fall for our Marjolaine hair clip, a beautiful dark green velvet bow mounted on a gold-plated hair clip. Marjolaine will enhance your hairstyle in the most beautiful way. This pretty head jewel is part of our new collection imagined for the end of the year and composed of a large selection of hair accessories and earrings. Shades of turquoise, dark green, gold, crystals, rhinestones, glitter and velvet are the key words of this collection thought with ornaments to shine for the holidays. Thanks to our minimalist Amarande comb with golden notes, our Marjolaine bow tie barrette, the headband of the same name adorned with sequins and fine stones, you will be able to shine for the holidays and bring some fantasy!

You can wear this hair clip on loose hair to reveal your lengths and show off your sophisticated mane, or to attach strands of hair and why not braids. Marjolaine will suit all hair types (fine hair, thick hair, blonde or brown) and all hairstyles (ponytails, loose hair, with bangs). It will bring a so romantic style to your looks for all occasions and will accessorize your hair style.

The bows have become real fashion accessories to have in your wardrobe.

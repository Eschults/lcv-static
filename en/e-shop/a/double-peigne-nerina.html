Nerina Double Comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
128€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

This original piece consists of a long gold-plated chain adorned with labradorite stones and decorated with golden shells and pearls. It can be worn either at the back to decorate a half tail or a bohemian braid or in front along the forehead to look like a mermaid. Combine it with our Sirena Tiara to add even more character to your outfit.

  • Composition : gold-plated chain and labradorite stones, gold-plated shell and sunburst, freshwater pearls, gold metal combs, preserved hydrangea flowers
  • Size: around 17 cm
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this double comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

The Nerina double Comb takes its inspiration from the mermaid that bears this name. It belongs to L’Odyssée collection. A collection that draws marine poetry through her jewellery. The particularity of the comb lies in its sublime gold-plated chain, decorated with labradorite stones. Gold-plated shell and sun, freshwater pearls, golden metal combs and preservedhydrangea flowers enhance the whole.

The magnificent gold leaves of the comb’s suns will illuminate your hair during the ceremony. Its style is therefore situated between country, bohemian-chic and glamorous. This accessory with a different design can be worn for a country or bohemian wedding or any occasion with a romantic touch. Indeed, unique by its chain, it is a real head jewel and can dress different hairstyles of the bride. Whether it is a half ponytail, a bridal bun, a bohemian braid or a hairdo full of fantasy, Les Couronnes de Victoire creations will accompany you. Our wedding accessories embellish all types of hairstyles from the bride to the bridesmaid. They can also be combined with other beautiful jewellery such as our tiaras, crowns, earrings, barrettes, buttonholes and other wonders from the L’Odyssée collection.

For the bride, to beautifully create your hairstyle (bun, loose hair, half ponytails, braids…), we have created several Pinterest selections for you to find your inspiration and association tips. For the bridesmaids, we have little flowers and pearls inlaid in unique ornaments, where they will certainly find their happiness. A very trendy creation for the bridesmaids is often the mini comb. It can be worn as the only main accessory or the finishing touch to their hair. Nowadays, barrettes can be used for special occasions and are suitable for curly and frizzy hair as well as straight hair. For a minimalist style, you will find our Electre, Tallulah, Sirena, Calypso and Nami mini combs.

The accessories go from the bride to the woman who takes care of her elegance on a daily basis. Certain accessories such as the earrings in the collection can be worn for all occasions: evening hairstyle, daytime hairstyle, festive hairstyle… Something to add a touch of fantasy to your daily hairstyle. In the same collection, we invite you to discover other sumptuous bridal jewels: bracelets, earrings, flower crowns adjustable with a bow, tiaras… They will perfectly accompany the bohemian wedding dress as well as satin outfits made of feathers and some hats. At Les Couronnes de Victoire, the choice offered to the future bride is adapted to her desires: crystals, pearly pearls, colours… This is why all of our crowns can be personalised. Don’t hesitate to take the pleasure of discovering them. We think and create all the accessories, from bun pins to hair jewellery, in order to accompany the realisation of your dream day. All the accessories are for the women you are and become precious ornaments for your hairstyle, whether you have long hair, short hair, braided or loose hair. From your bouquet, through your wedding dress and bridal veil, to the tip of your jewellery set, everything will be perfect and flowery! For bohemian, original, sophisticated, chic or romantic brides, welcome to Les Couronnes de Victoire.

Clémentine earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
95€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Iconic, the flowered creole is the perfect pair of bohemian and chic earrings to wear for a wedding or any other special occasion. For the Les Couronnes de Victoire x Louise Misha collection, the colours have been revisited to bring a soft and colourful touch thanks to the different shades of colour of preserved hydrangeas and gypsophila. For your wedding, it is the ideal accessory to give your outfit a touch of style. As a guest or in everyday life, it will give you a romantic and feminine touch. These earrings can be mounted on clips for non-pierced ears.

  • Composition: preserved white, pink and orange hydrangea flowers and mini ivory, pink and copper gypsophila, gold plated structure
  • Size: 5 cm. Available in 4 cm on request
  • Shelf life: the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Let yourself be tempted by our new children’s collection in collaboration with Louise Misha. This beautiful collection with a feminine and bohemian theme will fill your women’s jewellery box with timeless earrings and crowns. For the little girls, in addition to the crowns, we also have hair clips, tiaras, wrist corsages and headbands.

Our precious Clementine hoop earrings for women will sublimate your lobe in spring-summer with its white, pink, ivory, copper and coral colours. These original earrings are set with a clasp for pierced ears but they can be mounted on clips on request. They are mounted on a gold-plated brass frame.

Thanks to this collaboration, mother and granddaughter can match their women’s jewellery. Indeed, the crown and earrings for women are matched with the crown, barrette and headband for children. These sober and discreet jewels will dress and illuminate your elegant outfits for this summer. All occasions are good to wear original earrings. These fancy earrings can be combined with other women’s jewellery such as a nicely set pendant, a bracelet, a crown or another piece of jewellery. We also offer other earrings to suit any woman who wears earrings. We have more discreet earrings than creoles made of flowers such as studs or pretty lever-back earrings or pendants. In our new Odyssey collection you will find a wide range of earrings, most of which are adorned with white freshwater pearls. They can bring a glamorous touch to your ears. These earrings can be a lovely gift for a birthday, anniversary or wedding. Most of our earrings are on clasps but some can be mounted on clips.

At Les Couronnes de Victoire, all our accessories are made in our Parisian workshops and are customizable. Indeed, from crowns to pairs of earrings, they can be composed according to your desires. The great novelty of our company is its home-made line, we have brought out pretty dried flower bouquets for your interiors. You can find all our creations on our e-shop.

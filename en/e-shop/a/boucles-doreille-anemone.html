Anemone earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
90€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Sober and elegant, the Anemone earrings are composed of two gold-plated metal cord rings intertwined and finished with a freshwater pearl. These modern earrings will beautifully dress a wedding outfit as an everyday outfit.

  • Composition : gold-plated structure, freshwater pearls
  • Size: 6 cm
  • Shelf life : the freshwater pearls that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

The Anemone earrings are part of our new collection The Odyssey where marine poetry meets Ancient Greece to perfect an imperial look for the bride. Ideal, our earrings can be matched with any hairstyle. This wedding jewel is the final touch to your wedding dress on your wedding day.

This set is ideal for a wedding composed of a delicate golden accessorized with precious freshwater pearls like a treasure from the seabed. This earring is halfway between creoles by its intertwined rings and earrings hanging by its length. This ornament and its refined structure make this jewel, like all our bridal jewels, a unique creation.

As a bride-to-be, bridesmaid or bridesmaids, this wedding accessory can be worn for your wedding as well as for the bridal party. Dare to sublimate your headwear by lifting your hairstyle with hair attached (in a bun or a ponytail). Our dangling curls can be used to dress a décolleté or bustier for example or to replace a back jewel. You can make your jewelry look even better by adding a hairband, crown, hair clip or hair pin without overdoing it. Indeed, these sets are absolutely romantic: hair accessories and gold jewelry will give a bohemian chic tone to your wedding. Dare to mix, match and match our earrings with other head jewelry for a more accentuated side of the “bohemian bride”.

With these Anemone earrings, you can wear a delicate wreath of flowers like Eudora or Calpyso to bring a bohemian side with their white flowers. Or reinforce the “classic-chic” effect with a hair jewel made of fine pearls like the Nerina double comb or the Poseidon tiara. More broadly, in our L’Odyssée jewelry collection, you will find jewelry and accessories, jewelry sets, costume headbands and any other hair accessories adorned with crystals, pearls, rhinestones and stabilized flowers for any romantic occasion (from weddings to engagements without forgetting the occasions to celebrate birthdays and happy events). These accessories will also delight the bride and groom because buttonholes can be placed on their wedding outfit and can also be customized to the gentlemen’s taste.

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a house of wedding accessories for women (and future spouses). All our wedding jewelry is made with the utmost delicacy and handmade according to the floral art of which we have the secret. Are you planning to get married or to organize your best friends’ weddings? Don’t hesitate to make an appointment in our boutique or by videoconference for bridal hairstyling ideas because LCV jewelry is undeniably the glamorous touch for a bohemian wedding.

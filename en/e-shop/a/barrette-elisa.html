Elisa Hair clip

Hairpin Eternal Preserved flowers
25€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

We melt for the delicacy of our Elisa hair clip adorned with preserved powder pink hydrangea and small pink gypsophila flowers and soft green stoebe. This hair clip is mounted on a crocodile structure that fits very well on the fine hair of little girls. Ideal to raise a strand in front, it can also be pricked into a hairstyle such as a bun and will be perfect to give a flowery touch to a wedding, baptism or communion ceremony outfit.

  • Composition : preserved powder pink hydrangea, pink gypsophila and green stoebe, bronze hair clip structure
  • Size: 4cm
  • Shelf-life: the flowers in this hair clip are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

The Elisa hair clip belongs to our new children’s collection, designed and created for your princesses. Because clothes and accessories make the perfect matchy, all these jewels have been designed to dress your baby girl’s outfit more delicately.

Composed of sublime eternal flowers, the Elisa hair clip will be able to accessorise your little princess’ entire wardrobe. Whether it is a baby dress with an English embroidery style, a flying dress or a printed dress, the accessory will adapt to the style of your cute little one. Linen, organic cotton, satin, prints, stripes, rhinestones, sequins, chiffon… You will be able to dare different materials and details to create the bohemian and romantic outfit of your little girl’s dreams. This head jewel will also make a beautiful set with polka dot dresses and long, short or sleeveless sleeveless outfits.

Of course, dresses aren’t the only children’s clothes that make an impression. Today, just like baby fashion, style is evolving. There is therefore a wide variety of clothes and outfits, always more original to form beautiful outfits: striped sailor suit, rompers, velvet pyjamas, dungarees, overalls, overalls, trousers, original jumpers, coats… For example, if your baby is invited to a sleepover, this is also ideal. Pink pyjamas, little booties, pairs of baby socks and hair clips in the same colour make the perfect match. Because little girls like to be pretty and comfortable. Baby accessories are really a must to go with girls’ clothes. They will allow her to express her personality, her tastes and her moods. The hair clip is a hybrid jewel situated between fashion accessories and hair accessories. Full of fantasy, it appeals to mothers for their adorable baby girls. This way, girls will be able to choose to wear their hair clip for all occasions and not only for special occasions. Between femininity, bohemian and glamour… The soft powdered pink and pastel tones of this head jewel will radiate in the spring-summer and autumn-winter seasons. Like a real butterfly, your little rabbit will blossom when she wears her Elisa hair clip.

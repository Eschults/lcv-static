Daphné necklace

Necklace Eternal Preserved flowers
85€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Discover our Daphné necklace, composed of delicate freshwater pearls. This exquisite piece is perfect for complementing a wedding gown or top with a plunging back. This necklace will make a statement with its original composition !

  • Composition : Fine gold-plated chain. Freshwater pearls.
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions : keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order

Immerse yourself in the enchanting universe of Les Couronnes de Victoire jewelry with the magnificent Daphné necklace, a unique piece distinguished by its elegance and sophistication. Designed for brides seeking a refined accessory to enhance their open back or décolletage, the Daphné necklace gracefully combines the timeless beauty of freshwater pearls with the novelty and delicate gleam of the fine gold-plated chain.

This exceptional jewel is worn at the back of the bust, adding a touch of glamour and mystery to any wedding attire. The central freshwater pearl, suspended by a delicate chain, is surrounded by two chains that gracefully extend downward, each adorned with a freshwater pearl, creating a hypnotic and captivating effect.

The Daphné necklace embodies the very essence of femininity and elegance, with its sophisticated design and golden finish subtly capturing the light. Whether you choose to wear it with a backless wedding dress or pair it with an elegant evening outfit, this jewel will make you irresistible.

You have a multitude of choices to coordinate it, whether with a more or less voluminous golden crown, or with a comb, double or mini, or even with dangling earrings with pearls. Do not hesitate to visit our boutique to discover all our accessories, from different collections, and to find the accessory made for you. You can also wear a choker necklace, or a longer pendant necklace in the front, as long as the chain is gold, the necklaces will complement each other.

Treat yourself to the eternal glow of the Daphné necklace and let its timeless beauty add a touch of grace to your look. With its adjustable lobster clasp, this gold-plated necklace set with pearls fits perfectly to your silhouette, allowing you to shine with confidence and elegance on your big day.

Electre Wrist Corsage

Wrist corsage Eternal Preserved flowers
38€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Dress your wrist and the one of your witnesses with subtle golden flowers and pearls with our Electre wrist corsage. As a bride-to-be, don’t hesitate to match your earrings or golden hair accessory with this delicate wrist corsage.

  • Composition: preserved hydrangea and gypsophila gold plated by hand, freshwater pearls
  • Size: unisize
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this wrist corsage are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Electre is a descendant of the Atreides family. She is seen as the saviour of her brother Oreste. And is also the name of one of the wrist corsage in our new collection of wedding jewellery and accessories. This jewellery collection was named after the famous Greek epic, L’Odyssée. You will find in the accessories of this collection the glamour and romance that you can find in this epic.

The pretty handmade Electre wrist corsage is the essential wedding accessory, whether it is for you, the bride or to perfect the wrist of your bridesmaids. Indeed, this discreet wrist corsage will be the final and refined touch to their wedding outfit. This delicate wrist corsage has been created from preserved hydrangea and gypsophila as well as soft water pearls. These white pearls and flowers are eternal. The golden colour of the flowers will enhance your bridal accessories. You can also adorn your hair with hair jewellery to accessorise your bridal hairstyle. In our collection you will find flower crowns, hair pins, tiaras, hair clips, or even refined combs. If you choose a tiara or a flower crown you will look like a real mermaid. Thanks to this jewellery set, you can create many different hairstyles, such as a bun, a delicate braid or keep your hair loose in a bohemian chic spirit. For a wedding, if you decide to lift your hair, it can be nice to dress your ears with pretty earrings. In this bohemian bridal spirit, Nami earrings or Ezili dangling earrings would be a perfect match. On the latter earrings you can find Swarovski crystal and a golden jewellery set, which will give the bride-to-be a more sophisticated but still bohemian look. Most of our earrings are on clasp but they can be mounted on clips on request. The advantage of having your hair tied back is also that it gives a glimpse of the back of your wedding gown, which can be, depending on your wedding gowns, very elaborate and can display a pretty cleavage that deserves to be seen. For women who wish to match their bracelet or head jewellery with their groom’s buttonhole, we have the Electre buttonhole which has the same flowers and delicacy as the Electre wrist corsage. The flowers are also hand gilded and the jewel is set with fresh water pearls. This is the perfect way to have beautiful matching brides and grooms. To continue with the idea of a bohemian and country wedding, brides can add wild flowers to their discreet bridal jewellery. You can think of original and romantic customised sets. All accessories are customisable. It is then the opportunity to marry different assorted flowers, according to your desires and your fantasy. Your hair jewellery can be worn on other occasions, not only for your wedding.

The creation of jewellery is done in our Parisian premises, above our showroom. All our head jewellery and others are made there, thanks to the know-how of our little fairies. Moreover, these accessories have an eternal life span, so you will be able to enjoy the refinement of our jewellery indefinitely. In addition, we organise workshops so that you can create your own jewellery accessories, including your bridal jewellery with your bridesmaids if you participate in one of our bridal shower workshops.

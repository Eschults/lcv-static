Romy Crown

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
43€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Fresh and delicate, the Romy crown combines the delicacy of white hydrangea and gypsophila flowers preserved with subtle eucalyptus leaves. A poetic and airy crown to adorn every little girl’s hair, for a ceremony or a special occasion such as a birthday.

This crown can be perfectly adapted to any child’s head size, from babies to teenagers, thanks to its satin ribbon on the back.

  • Composition: white hydrangea, white gypsophila and stabilised eucalyptus, white satin ribbon
  • Size: one size only. Can be adjusted with the ribbon on the back
  • Shelf-life: The flowers in this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

The pretty flowered Romy crown, belongs to our new and first children’s collection. The children’s collection is the collection dedicated to adorn and accessorize the hair of your princesses. To make a floral composition shaped in the form of jewelry is the result of a real talent held by the artisan florist. Among the hair accessories of our children’s collection, the little stars are our flower crowns. Often noticed on the bride-to-be or on her bridesmaids, they are at the origin of hair jewelry for smaller girls. Little flowers and little girls are therefore the perfect couple, and a crown on the head is always a good idea.

True darling of the collection, this hair accessory is joyful, romantic and delicate. Its hydrangea, gypsophila and white eucalyptus flowers form a set of a new style that will sublimate your little angel. Fresh flowers become eternal because preserved will offer an invaluable accessory to your little darling. Its exceptional composition of preserved flowers will make the perfect ornament. Wearing the Romy crown will give a fairy look to your little flower. For a bohemian wedding, as for ceremonies such as a baptism, the crown on the head is not a disguise, it is a real bohemian jewel. Of course, the little lady will be able to wear it to transform herself into a princess for a day. A wonderful power that crowns and tiaras have. The difference with a tiara is that a crown of flowers makes the complete head circumference, both accessories are however made of sublime floral compositions. To adjust your crown, simply use the ribbon at the back and tie it at the right size. The timeless colors of this crown will allow him to wear it in spring-summer as well as in autumn-winter. She will always be able to bloom her everyday life, like a pretty butterfly. On long or short hair, between colorful and country shades, on loose hair, a ponytail or braided hair, flowery is the trend that delights little girls’ hearts. A few flowers in ornaments, whether on elastics, barrettes or other delicate ornaments such as crowns, will always make a beautiful effect.

At Les Couronnes de Victoire, hand-made is the signature. All the flowers are transformed into true works of art by our florist artisans. With a delicate hand, they transform pretty flowers and small crystals into hair that will be adorned with sumptuous preserved flowers. For your princesses, you will be able to choose from a wide variety of natural flowers to bring the most beautiful hairstyles to life. You can also make an appointment at the showroom for advice on how to realize your hairstyle idea. Between a country style, and bohemian chic, whose white and green flowers convey purity and freshness. Les Couronnes de Victoire are now available for women and children.

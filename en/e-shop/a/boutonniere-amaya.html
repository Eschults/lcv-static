Amaya boutonniere

Boutonniere Eternal Preserved flowers
22€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

In pink and country tones, this is the perfect boutonniere to slip over the groom and his witnesses. Made with delicate powder pink hydrangea flowers and stabilised field flowers, it will add a touch of style to your wedding.

  • Composition: ruscus, pink gypsophila, powder pink hydrangea, phalaris, nigella, peach wild roses, lagurus and stabilised bougainvillea
  • Size: around 2 inches
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this boutonniere are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Here is the new collection of jewelry floral creations from Les Couronnes de Victoire : California Dreaming! If you are dreaming of a romantic or bohemian wedding, our retro style stabilized flower arrangements will be perfect for dressing up for a wedding! All occasions are good to wear beautiful flowers. Find the flower crown, the flowery comb for the bridesmaid, the handmade hair clips, the unique hats and all this in a great number of colors! At Les Couronnes de Victoire, we work with natural flowers that were originally fresh flowers and have become stabilized flowers for the bride and groom to enjoy their accessories for a lifetime.

The Amaya boutonniere is a floral creation that will enhance a groom’s suit, a christening suit, a wedding suit or a Valentine’s Day suit! Amaya boutonnieres are composed of hydrangea, gypsophila and phalaris petals in ecru and pink tones. Come and stitch this pretty floral arrangement into your suit in your own image! Amaya accessories are actually a new line of accessories: the flower crown for brides, the hair clips, the boutonnieres for grooms, the romantic style earrings, the ribbon wristband for bridesmaids… You can all match and wear the same shades.

Wedding preparations for your wedding are not always a breeze : find the reception hall, choose the types of existing ceremonies (secular or religious ceremony), define a wedding theme, your budget, the decoration of the room (garland, candles, vases, personalized dragées … ), choose your guests, your bridesmaids, your caterer, your country florist as well as the wedding flowers while following the language of flowers (country daisies, sunflowers, pretty bouquet of lavender, eucalyptus, lily of the valley, ranunculus, ivory gypsophila, dahlias, tulips, hydrangeas, lilies, red roses, white roses, roses of all colors, orchids, peonies, white flowers, dried flowers, artificial flowers, seasonal flowers, exotic flowers foliage … ), choose your wedding rings, your wedding dress (satin or lace ? ) as well as your bridal bouquet ! For your wedding full of freshness, everything must be perfect and we will help you to sublimate all that!

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a brand of jewelry and floral accessories for the bride and her bridal party. Don’t hesitate to come and see us in our boutique or to make an appointment at our showroom to get precious advice for your upcoming wedding.

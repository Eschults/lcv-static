Colette earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
265€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Celebrate love with our limited edition Valentine’s Day capsule created in collaboration with the Moulin Rouge ! A nod to the most famous Parisian novelists, our Colette earrings are perfect for women with character. Behind the beautiful floral bouquet hides a golden heart ready to keep all your secrets…

  • Composition: Stabilized hydrangea and baby’s breath flowers, gold-plated filigree structure, gold-plated ear stem adorned with transparent and ruby crystals
  • Size : 5 cm in height and 5 cm in length
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Discover the exquisite pair of Colette earrings, a creation crafted with elegance by the prestigious brand Les Couronnes de Victoire, in collaboration with the legendary Moulin Rouge. These earrings embody the quintessence of refinement and femininity, capturing the enchanting spirit of the Parisian cabaret universe. The Colette earrings stand out with their sumptuous design, showcasing delicate hearts crafted from vibrant red stabilized hydrangeas and baby’s breath. These floral elements are meticulously mounted on a filigree structure plated in gold, adding a touch of sophistication to this daring creation. The ear post, also gold-plated, is adorned with transparent crystals and rubies, creating a sparkling brilliance that enhances the natural beauty of the earrings.

Whether you prefer dangling earrings, hoops, or studs, the Colette earrings will enhance your style with their remarkable allure. The gold-plated structure, with impeccable finishes, imparts a sense of opulence and elegance to this piece, making it the ideal birthday, communion, wedding, Valentine’s Day, or any other occasion gift for any woman who appreciates precious and fantasy pieces. This pair of earrings embodies the perfect alliance of yellow gold, sparkling crystals, and zirconium oxides, creating a harmonious jewel of rare beauty. The meticulous details, golden finishes, and quality materials make these Colette earrings an essential accessory for all occasions, whether it’s a special evening at the Moulin Rouge or adding a touch of elegance to your everyday attire.

Indulge in this exquisite novelty, a centerpiece of contemporary jewelry. The Colette earrings transport you to a world of glamour and sophistication, where every detail is designed to enhance femininity and elegance. Adorned with these jewels, you’ll be the one who captures all eyes, revealing the eternal beauty of an exceptional set.

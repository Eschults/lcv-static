Hortense comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our Hortense comb is composed of delicate golden flowers. It’s the perfect accessory to tuck into your bridal hairstyle. Simple and refined, it complements all looks and can easily be worn on a daily basis, in a messy or bohemian bun. Its wide teeth allow you to slide it endlessly according to your desires. This comb was designed in collaboration with Marie, the creator of Scrunchie is Back fancy accessories.

  • Composition : Comb with flowers plated in 24-carat gold, 3 microns
  • Size : 7 cm
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

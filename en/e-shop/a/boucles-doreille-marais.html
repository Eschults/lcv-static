Inspired by our iconic Agatha flower earrings, Marais flower earrings play with shades of pink, deep red, and burgundy red to illuminate your face. Wear these floral earrings with an everyday outfit or to adorn a sophisticated evening outfit with a hair updo.

  • Composition: Preserved Hydrangea in pink, bright red, burgundy red and Gold plated clip and chain of 24 carats 3 microns
  • Size: 8 cm
  • Shelf life: The flowers used in making this hair barrette are eternal.
  • Storage Instructions: Protect from direct sunlight, heat, and humidity.
  • Delivery time: 5 working days from the date of order

Romantic, festive and full of charm, the Marais is a must-see trendy district in the capital city where museums and shopping streets coexist in a typically Parisian atmosphere.The designer thus wished to rediscover this ambiance through the original Marais gold-tone metal flower earrings. Playing with color gradients, wearing precious hoops such as these will add a touch of glamour and sparkle to any outfit. Decorated with preserved hydrangea flowers, these floral earrings are ideal to bring a touch of femininity to your outfit.

Inspired by our Agata dangling earrings, Marais bridal earrings are composed of a perfect blend of pink, bright red and burgundy red hydrangea flowers. These delightful colors co;e together to form these sparklingly extraordinary pair of gold hoop flower earrings. Ideal for women wishing for a bohemian wedding, the Marais gold floral earrings accompanied by a gold-colored chain like a charming necklace or pendant will bring a touch of poise to your outfit and make you shine bright on your wedding day. Perfect to illuminate the face with a delicate multicolored note, this pair of floral earrings with a refined gold-tone frame can be worn with an everyday fall outfit, understated looks or to sublimate an evening outfit with a touch of fancy. Thus, timeless and elegant, they can match any occasion. From a special event like the wedding day, engagement party, prom to a more subtle day like evening dinner, weekend party, these refined pair of shiny hoop earring are superb for all days and evenings. From the collection of fine jewelry, this gold bridal earring can become the ideal gift for a future bride or a nice birthday present for a friend. Adorned with a clasp for pierced ears, the elegant and discreet Marais flower earrings are available as clip-on if requested to suit all women.

The hydrangea flowers in these gold-plated bridal earrings have been preserved through a natural biological process. Handcrafted by the little fairies of our Parisian workshop located in the Marais, these bohemian hoop earrings are made for the love of french craftsmanship are truly handmade and cherished. In order to keep your bohemian earring forever and keep it intact, some guidelines must be followed: avoid exposure to the sun, heat and humidity. Thus, your precious glitter jewel will be kept in its small jewelry box or its delicate case.

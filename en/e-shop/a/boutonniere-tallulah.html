Tallulah Boutonniere

Boutonniere Eternal Preserved flowers
22€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Add a bohemian touch to the groom’s or witnesses’ costume with our delicate Tallulah boutonniere adorned with stabilized wildflowers.

  • Composition : preserved umbelliferous, gypsophila, marcela, limonium, fern, pampas and angel hair
  • Size: around 5 cm
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this boutonniere are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Immerse yourself in our poetic and romantic world of the seashore and seabed by discovering our collection L’Odyssée. A collection that honors weddings to sublimate the future bride and groom. Our know-how is based on floral art to propose original and unique arrangements and jewelry.

Our Tallulah boutonniere has been designed for these gentlemen, future brides or guests such as bridesmaids or groomsmen, to flower your wedding so that it will be the romantic and trendy wedding. This floral creation comes from our Tallulah range which uses the purity of white flowers and soft shades and the ivory hue of the gypsophila to decline its floral composition in a wide choice of floral accessories in your image (flower wreath, combs or even bridal earrings). How to wear boutonnieres for your wedding? To stitch on your groom’s suit, it will be the romantic and country touch par excellence that will match perfectly with accessories such as the bridal bouquet and the wedding dress (in satin or muslin). This bohemian and pastel touch allows the bride and groom to be matched and to mark their wedding day by thinking of the smallest detail. All floral compositions declined in buttonholes are tied with a ribbon for a refined and elegant touch. Small LCV tip to decline the boutonnière for the bridesmaids or the mother of the bride and groom. You can wear it as a brooch, to stitch on a jacket or a suit for example, for floral touches, if the floral arrangements for the hair are not to your style. This will give a flowery reminder to the Queen of the day in addition to her own accessories (bridal bouquet and head jewelry) and general decoration (room decoration, table decoration, table runner, wedding car etc.) for a perfect harmony that will be reflected in the photos of the wine of honour or cocktail. How to choose flowers? Lavender, lily of the valley, buttercups, peonies, white roses, red roses, plant, lyx, eucalyptus, tulip, orchids, hydrangea petals, foliage, exotic flowers, succulents, dahlias, small flowers of all kinds … All these plants have one thing in common: they are seasonal flowers. At Les Couronnes de Victoire we have found the ideal arrangement to marry seasonality and timelessness. That’s why we work with natural and fresh flowers, because like an artisan florist, we refuse to work with artificial flowers. We love the purity of beautiful flowers and therefore we preserve them thanks to stabilization, a 100% natural biological process, which allows us to keep the brightness of our accessories and their composition. This floral arrangement used to make a floral accessory allows the flower to be eternal. We use only stabilized flowers and not dried flowers for our floral creations. A real goldsmith’s work as much for a wedding ceremony as for an engagement or any occasion to get ready and celebrate the beautiful news of life.

You may decide to create unique and fully personalized compositions for a wedding to match your wedding theme. At Les Couronnes de Victoire, everything is handmade and therefore we can make the jewelry floral arrangement that represents you, as well as the bride and groom, as well as their future spouses. And this, for ceremonies ranging from weddings to birthdays, through the secular ceremony, from the bride to the bridesmaids to stick to the bohemian wedding of your dreams. Feel free to browse our e-shop to find the flower wreath or other original creations of floral accessories to give resolutely country tones to your outfit.

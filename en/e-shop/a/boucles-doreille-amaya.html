Amaya earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
95€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Discover our Amaya earrings, a blend of stabilised wild flowers that will add softness and colour in a delicate way. Ideal for your bohemian bridal look, they can be worn with hair slightly raised to reveal all their details and bring you femininity. These earrings can be mounted on clips on request.

  • Composition: gold plated frame, ruscus, pink and ivory gypsophila, powder pink hydrangea and stabilised oats
  • Size: 2 inches (or 1,5 inches on request)
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions : keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order

Come and discover California Dreaming, our latest addition! A collection inspired by Lana del Ray and the bohemian hippie chic, countryside and romantic weddings of the 70s. To be worn for all occasions, these head jewels and fancy earrings are discreet and sophisticated or more colourful and glamorous and will fit perfectly in your jewellery box.

Ode to femininity, the Amaya pairs of earrings are round-shaped hoop earrings. Each hoop earring is set with precious hydrangeas, gypsophila, oat stems which are mounted on gold-plated rings. These fancy hoop earrings are adorned with pastel, coral and powdery flowers and will dress up your look in a timeless way.

The sleeper earrings can be worn as a set with the comb of the same line, and the bridesmaids can wear the matching bracelet which will be a nice gift for them. Finally, we have made the buttonhole for men and for your witnesses. However, this pair cannot be mounted on clips and can therefore only be worn on pierced ears.

Les Couronnes de Victoire offers you handmade wedding jewellery and jewellery for all occasions, for women and men. Whether you want a hat, a veil, a pair of earrings, clips, bun picks, necklaces/ pendants, bracelets/sleeves or flower crowns, everything can be personalised according to your wishes and your personality so that you can shine. We offer you a vast collection of fancy, original, sober or discreet earrings, from hoop earrings or dangling earrings (gold and silver plated), to earrings set with rhinestones and sequins, sublimated with mother-of-pearl, decorated with flowers, with pearls, or multicoloured, geometrical, asymmetrical, with crystal, twisted with elegant flowers, openwork or matching, everything is made to allow you to find the ideal gift for a birthday present or for a reception. Our precious pairs of feminine and original earrings include various shapes (cubic, oval/losange, round) and are adjustable: their clasps can be mounted as clips for non-pierced ears, the colours can be changed (turquoise, blue, white, pink etc.).

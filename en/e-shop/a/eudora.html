Eudora Crown

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
180€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

With his pretty plumetis veil, the Eudora crown will give a look a bit retro to your figure. This crown is perfect to dress your hairstyle, as well as bent in half to be worn as a tiara on the front.

  • Composition : white plumetis veil, preserved hydrangea and baby’s breath
  • Size: unisize
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

For our collection L’Odyssée, we sought to give a Greek reference to our products. Give a magical, divine and delicate touch to your wedding, whether it is summer or winter. To do this, you can use our hair accessories and in particular our pretty Eudora flower crown whose name reminds us of nymphs, those Greek deities. Eudora was one of the Hyades, the rain nymphs. These nymphs are feminine and are associated with nature, just like the wreaths of flowers. Succumb then to the charm of the crowns for a bohemian wedding.

Our Eudora crown is ideal to sublimate your wedding, but not only, it can be worn for any occasion such as a baptism, an engagement or a communion. This crown is made from natural flowers that have been stabilized. Thanks to this stabilization, the white hydrangea and gypsophila flowers that make up this floral creation will last forever. It is a great reminder of this important event. Combined with a white feathered veil, this crown will give you a retro look. Eudora’s special feature is its wearing. It can be worn as a headband or folded in half to make a beautiful tiara. Everything you need to be the queen of your ceremony. Who can wear it? All women looking for bucolic and country inspiration for their wedding. But also, the bridesmaid or bridesmaids for a bohemian chic and a touch of freshness. You can also match your bouquet of flowers and the little flower bouquets of your bridesmaids to your crown. The advantage of the crown is that you can wear it on all hair types and there are many different hairstyles. For styling ideas, you can place it on your loose hair, your hair left in the wind in the image of the Greek goddesses, or on your hair gathered in a braid, bun or half ponytail to free your neck. Whether you have long hair, short hair or medium-length hair, you can adorn it with a flower crown. The flower crown will be your ally on the day you’ll say yes. The crown can also give you little ideas for floral decorations, for decorating the hall, the composition of the bridal bouquet or the centrepiece in the image of your crown for your bohemian wedding. And why not match your future husband’s buttonhole to your wedding flowers? You can make all sorts of floral arrangements that remind you of this pretty crown. If our collection inspired by the sea and women seduces you for your wedding, you can accompany your hair jewellery with earrings or a bracelet always inspired by flowers. Do you like this pretty Eudora crown but would you like to add more flowers or remove some? This is possible. All our crowns are customizable, you can prick in pink flowers, red roses for a more romantic touch or eucalyptus for colourful touches. We offer a floral workshop for you and your bridesmaids to make floral arrangements. You can choose the preserved flowers that make you fall in love with to create the crown of your dreams. But if the flower crown is not the right accessory for you and with which you want to create your wedding hairstyle, we can also offer you combs, double combs, hairpins or hair clips. You have a wide choice of hair accessories for your bridal hairstyle, all based on fresh flowers that have been stabilised afterwards. If the white flowers of the Eudora crown have inspired you, we can offer you the Rochelle comb. But if you want more golden tones, we can offer you the large Poseidon comb with gold-plated metal shells and freshwater pearls in addition to hydrangeas and gypsophila. For a new style, opt for the Panarea headband. Your wedding dress with one of these products will make you shine and attract all eyes. Become one of the enchanted goddesses of L’Odyssée… To find out more about Les Couronnes de Victoire accessories, you can visit the e-shop or the boutique to benefit from our advice. All our compositions are made from delicate stabilised flowers and are made in France, in our workshops in Paris. You can find French savoir-faire and chic in each of our creations. Each accessory is designed by the founder Victoire, who seeks to create everyday jewellery, in addition to bridal accessories. Our creations must be protected from the sun, heat, humidity and humidity for a better conservation.

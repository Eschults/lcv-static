Our Amarande flower barrette is made of stabilized hydrangea flowers combined with crystals for a romantic and chic look. With this hair clip, you can wear flowers on a daily basis or for a special occasion with the principle of our everlasting flower being preserved as a precious jewel. The Amarande hair clip can be worn with our Eden crystal earrings or our Calandra flower earrings.

  • Composition: emerald green hydrangea two-tone gradient of emerald green and bleu lagon, cristal royal green, gypsophile et hortensia or stabilisés, fleur en plaqué or avec pistil cristal émeraude, barrette en métal doré
  • Size: 6cm
  • Shelf life: eternal
  • Storage instructions: keep out of the sun, heat and humidity

The end of year celebrations are coming, you want to shine like the Christmas lights, it’s time to make a pretty glamorous hairstyle. And who says festive hairstyle, says accessories and hair jewelry! Sequins, glitters, crystals and rhinestones to accessorize your hairstyle and shine brightly! Discover our new Christmas collection of head jewels and earrings in shades of turquoise, dark green and gold. The accessories of this collection will bring the bohemian and shiny touch to your look.

The Amarande hair clip is a flowery jewel set on a golden metal structure and composed of stabilized hydrangea flowers combined with crystals for a romantic touch while remaining in a country spirit. With this barrette, you can wear flowers for all occasions or for a winter wedding for example. You can wear it with a dark green velvet dress, trendy color of the season or with a nice satin blouse and why not with lace.

How to wear our Amarande hair clip ? There are many ways to wear this precious jewel. If you want to highlight your detached hair, you can simply attach the hair clip to the side of your head to raise a strand, or arrange it above a ponytail for example. A true fashion accessory, the hair clip will become your favorite hair accessory, and you can put it back in your daily routine for a hairstyle with a bohemian touch.

The great thing about barrettes is that they are perfect for all hair types and styles! Whether you have long hair or short hair, thick hair or thin hair, all you have to do is style your mane with a hairbrush and arrange your floral hair jewelry. It doesn’t take a special occasion to wear fresh flowers! Brides, guests, bridesmaids, for women and girls who want to sublimate themselves with small pearls, golden touches, precious stones, bows in the hair, Les Couronnes de Victoire has what you need!

We make handmade hair accessories from natural flowers that have been stabilized with an all-organic plant process to make them last forever. Say goodbye to artificial flowers and wear our beautiful creations adorned with sophisticated bohemian floral arrangements. Comb, flower crown, fancy headband, tiara, bun picks, hats, decorative flower bouquet or for a bride, these women’s hair accessories will surely seduce you!

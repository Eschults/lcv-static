Flamingo Bouquet

Bouquet Eternal Preserved flowers
165€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Embrace a truly modern bridal look with our Flamingo bouquet and its bold pink color-block design. We love the contemporary touch of Anthurium flowers and its deconstructed style, perfect for a bride with a confident and fashion-forward vision.

  • Composition : stabilized pink and cherry red hydrangea, stabilized anthurium, white satin ribbon
  • Size : approximately 35 cm in height and 35 cm in diameter
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Discover the bold elegance of the Flamingo bouquet, a unique creation in vibrant shades of pink and burgundy. Designed for brides seeking a modern, refined, and deconstructed wedding bouquet, this preserved flower arrangement combines the opulence of hydrangeas with the exotic charm of anthuriums, creating a look that is both sophisticated and distinctive. Its interplay of textures and volumes adds character to your bridal ensemble while maintaining a romantic and timeless touch.

Handcrafted in our ateliers, the Flamingo bridal bouquet is made to stand the test of time, allowing you to keep an everlasting memory of your bohemian, romantic, or contemporary wedding. Its blend of deep and delicate hues complements both a minimalist wedding dress and a bolder bridal look.

More than just a statement accessory for the bride, this preserved wedding bouquet is perfect for enhancing your table décor, wedding arch, or ceremony aisle, adding an elegant and long-lasting floral touch. Thanks to a natural preservation process, these flowers maintain their beauty and softness without wilting, providing an eco-friendly and timeless alternative to fresh flower bouquets.

Complete your bridal look by pairing it with a matching flower crown or floral hair accessory, creating the perfect harmony between your bouquet and attire. At Les Couronnes de Victoire, each creation is crafted with passion and expertise to ensure an exceptional bouquet for your special day. Let yourself be enchanted by the Flamingo bouquet, a unique floral masterpiece that will elevate your wedding and remain a cherished keepsake for years to come.

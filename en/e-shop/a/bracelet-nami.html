Softness and delicacy are the definition of our Nami wrist corsage made of powder pink hydrangea and small preserved wild flowers. To offer to your witnesses for a bohemian and romantic procession.

  • Composition : powder pink preserved hydrangea flowers, preserved gypsophila, oat and marcela
  • Size: unisize
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this wrist corsage are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity

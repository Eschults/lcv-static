Fiorella double comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
145€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The double comb Fiorella is inspired by the opulence of the Renaissance. Adorned with ivy leaves and stabilized roses, this accessory embodies timeless elegance. It is worn to secure a sophisticated bun and can be paired with pearl earrings to create a refined ensemble.

  • Composition : Stabilized hydrangeas and roses, stabilized ivy.
  • Size : 16 cm
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

In the Renaissance collection, among the jewels that enhance the hair, lies the double comb Fiorella, a true symbol of timeless elegance. Evoking the poetry of ancient times, this hair accessory embodies delicacy and sophistication. Whether for a bride wanting to adorn her locks for the big day or for a special occasion, the Fiorella comb proves to be the ultimate accessory.

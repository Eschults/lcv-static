Sirena Double Comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
200€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Decorated with delicate preserved roses and wildflowers in white and powder pink tones, the Sirena double comb will match to your bridal bun beautifully. Very supple and light, it will easily take the shape of your bridal bun and can be accompanied by a bridal veil if you wish.

  • Composition: preserved roses, gypsophila, ruscus, nigelle, pampa and hair angel, gold metal combs
  • Size: around 17 cm
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this double comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Welcome to our magical marine world thanks to our new collection “L’Odyssée”. The idea of this wedding accessory is to find the marine imprint that will transform you like a queen thanks to your jewellery set. Our double comb name is Sirena, evoking the grace of a marine goddess and her bewitchment, so that our brides will charm their guests and futur grooms in their wedding outfit. If you are a bride-to-be, this double Sirena comb will be the perfect romantic touch to your wedding. Composed of floral ornaments such as fine and precious stones, the wild and sophisticated flowers in soft pink and ivory tones will sublimate your bohemian bridal look. Delicate rose petals are the perfect complement to a total white wedding. This hair jewel is ideal for adding the finishing touch to your hairstyle. Its supple structure allows you to wear this bridal hair accessory with refinement no matter what your hairstyle is (loose hair or tied-up).

Our hairstyle ideas for wearing our accessory. Who says getting married says a lot of preparation and among the priority tasks is “how to style your hair”. The groom-to-be doesn’t (or less) need to worry about this hair concern. But whether it is the bride or the bridesmaid, it is the question that makes us all hesitate. The most important first step is to determine the theme of your wedding. Our hairstyle ideas and more generally our hair accessories are made to accessorize and sublimate your hairstyle to give a bohemian side while refinement. Romantic hair accessories such as the comb or the bun pick have the advantage of being able to slip into any hairstyle thanks to their format. Braid, ponytail, banana bun or even more boho (with a few strands of hair sticking out). All options are possible for a wedding. The current trend is to match the bridal hairstyle to those of the bridesmaids. For example, if the bride opts for loose hair, why not tie her bridemaids’ hair with an elastic band? The glamorous effect is guaranteed: the bride will be all the more in the limelight because she will be the only one wearing a very different hairstyle from the other bridesmaids.

The advantage of our head jewel in double comb format is that it can be worn on the back of the head to adorn chic hairstyles (elegant braids, structured chignons etc), for a rather discreet look. This option also allows you to use your double comb as a tie for your wedding veil. Or you can opt for more fantasy and wear it at the front like a tiara to dress a fringe or cascading strands. All these hair wedding accessories for women from our L’Odyssée collection are designed for weddings and finely sublimate our brides to transform the young lady into a queen. Discover also the whole range of Sirena bridal jewellery: crowns and earrings for matching bridal accessories. If you want bridal accessories even more anchored in the spirit of the sea goddess, other selections of accessories with pearls have been designed through our Poseïdon, Ezili and Nerina ranges.

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a house specialising in wedding accessories and offers hair jewellery for a wedding that is at once country, natural and refined. You will find all types of bride accessories: from the flower wreath to the hair clip, from the elegant pin to the bun pins. All these head jewels are designed and made in Paris in the Marais with stabilised flowers which, by a 100% natural process, will preserve the flower and ensure that it will never wither or get damaged. Everything is done by hand, this is the heart of the house. Still hesitating about your wedding hairstyle? We will advise you and think with you about the ideal hairstyle that will go with your wedding dress and your style. Do not hesitate to make an appointment (in the shop or by videoconference).

Garance Mini comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
48€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Orned with a little thistle and a preserved rose, the Garance comb will brighten up a bun or a half updo.

  • Composition: Preserved eucalyptus, thistle, bears’ear, stoebe, roses, gold plated structure
  • Size: one size only (approximately 1.5 inches)
  • Shelf life: the flowers that compose this comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions: keep away from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 8 working days from the date of order

Garance, the mini bridal hair comb offers the same beauty as Garance, the double bridal hair comb: an unusual use of two contradictory flowers thistle and rose. Yet, unparalleled beauty is induced by this rapprochement! A blue thistle, a rose, leaves of lamb’s ear, stoebe, eucalyptus, all fixed on a golden mini hair comb for wedding and voilà the most romantic and charming flower hair accessory ready to attach on a low bun or half-ponytail.

If you chose Garance, the double bridal hair comb for your wedding day, you must know that the mini comb will delight your bridesmaids to their irresistible romantic side. Similar to Garance, the double bridal hair comb for wedding, the leaves, and flowers that make up this decorative mini comb have been preserved by a bio-organic procedure. This natural and biological process allows plants to keep their quality of color and tenderness while escaping the ravages of time, an eternal youth…. for your floral bridal comb! There are a few precautions to keep in mind to prosper from your wedding hair comb for years to follow: avoid prolonged exposure to sun, humidity, and heat.

Like all the flower hair accessories of the brand, Garance, the mini hair comb for wedding was designed and manufactured at the atelier of Les Couronnes de Victoire, Quartier du Marais in Paris. Each and every piece is handcrafted by the little fairies of atelier with care and love for perfection. The flower comb for hair testifies to the quality and vitality of French craftsmanship.

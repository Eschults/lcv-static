Whitney earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
85€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Adorn yourself in glitter and gold for your parties and special occasions! Our Whitney flower earrings are adorned with delicate hydrangea flowers stabilized in gold and then sprinkled with a fine glittery film to keep you glowing all night long. These earrings can be mounted on clips for non-pierced ears on request.

  • Composition : hydrangea flowers stabilized gold glitter, gold plated structure
  • Size: 1.1 inchs (or 1.5 inches on request)
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions : keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order

Let yourself be tempted by les Couronnes de Victoire extensive Christmas collection, “Tout ce qui brille”! You want to shine for the holidays and dress in femininity and glamour: this collection full of glitter, rhinestones, crystals, silver and gold is for you! The choice will be difficult between flower and crystal barrettes, clip-on earrings if you don’t have pierced ears, original xxl charm earrings, feminine dangling earrings, timeless twisted women’s earrings, precious sleeper earrings, a headband set with Swarovski crystals to wear for any occasion and lots of other jewelry! If you need to make a gift like a birthday or Christmas present for example, this is where to look for fantasy and innovation!

Introducing the Whitney gold plated earrings! These pretty gold sleeper earrings will accompany you on all your wildest nights. These gold earrings are adorned with gold and glitter hydrangea petals to illuminate and enhance your look. These earrings for women will match all your most refined outfits, elegant, original or discreet and sober if it is rather your type! Wearing glitter earrings will bring a rock’n’roll touch to your look with a nice low lacquered bun ! Add these fancy earrings to your precious jewelry box. Creole earrings are always a great idea. The creole can be 3 or 4cm in diameter on request and can also be mounted on a clip-on clasp so you can wear this jewelry on non-pierced ears, no discrimination at LCV!

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a French brand that offers women’s jewelry with a bohemian style such as crowns made of stabilized flowers for your most beautiful events, but also combs, earrings of many different styles ( geometrical, flowered, with crystals, asymmetrical earrings…), original bracelets, boutonnieres for men… All our collection of jewelry is handmade in the heart of Paris by our little fairies. Come and visit us at 230 rue Saint-Martin in the 3rd arrondissement of Paris to discover our treasures for women and find the perfect gift or piece to wear for an engagement or a wedding!

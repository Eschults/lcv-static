Calypso Double Comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
118€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Orned with a base of stabilised white hydrangea flowers and sprinkled with a multitude of small white preserved wild flowers, the Calypso double comb will give a bohemian look to your bridal hairstyle. Very flexible, it will easily adapt to your hairstyle and can be completed with a veil.

  • Composition : preserved hydrangea, fern, gypsophila, marcela, lagurus and angel hair, golden metal combs
  • Size: around 17 cm
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this double comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

For our new collection, our creator and founder has sought to take you on a journey to Ancient Greece with the theme of L’Odyssée by creating jewellery and accessories for your wedding. The nymph Calypso is a sea nymph who inspires us love and passion. The Calypso comb for women will make your wedding all the more romantic, it will allow you to accessorise your hair. For women looking for a bohemian and glamorous wedding, this pretty comb is a hair accessory that will allow you to perfect your outfit. This hair accessory is composed of a panel of delicate white hydrangea flowers and small stabilised white wild flowers adorning a golden metal comb, ideal for a wedding. As wedding accessories, combs are ideal for a bridal hairstyle and are a wedding accessory easy to reuse. You can reuse this hair jewel as a fashion accessory for any occasion and enhance your evening hairstyle for the holidays. As a bride, if you don’t know which hairstyle to choose, your hairstylist can play with this accessory and the hairstyles. The comb can be worn on either attached or loose hair and can give a more sophisticated look to a bun or half ponytail. For girls who have opted for the bun, we can also offer you the pretty set of Poseidon hair pins, in the Calypso comb theme. And for those who have decided to let your hair down, the Calypso hair pins set will be able to style your hair and tame your hair. This bridal jewellery set can also complement a veil and your pretty wedding dress. You can find a selection of veils on our e-shop and benefit from our advice to find the perfect accessory to complete it.

For women wondering how to wear this discreet comb, know that it can be worn on both long and short hair as well as on smooth and curly hair. And with its white colours, it’s perfect for both blonde and brown hair and all the types of hair colour in between those two. This styling accessory will be your best ally as a bride-to-be or for women attending special occasions. Moreover, for a harmonious wedding, you can match your head jewellery with your bridesmaids’ hair accessories. Without forgetting art-deco, this indispensable accessory made of flowers can be used as inspiration for your bouquets of flowers and room decoration. To give you the look of an ancient Greek goddess, we can also offer you pretty flower crowns, bun pins, hair pins, bracelets, tiaras and earrings. All these accessories are real fashion accessories that will allow you to dress, tie and knot your hair according to your desires. You can thus adorn a pretty hippie braid with hair jewellery such as a hair clip or hair pins. All hair accessories can be used on different hair types.

All the accessories have been created and made in our workshops in Paris. We have little fairies with great expertise who make flower crowns and all types of jewellery and hair jewellery from flowers that have been stabilised. This stabilization allows you to keep these fashion accessories used at weddings or other occasions forever. The colouring and delicate shine of our flowers are preserved over time. However, for this preservation to be optimal, our accessories must be kept away from sunlight, humidity and heat.

Designed with the pretty bohemian brand Louise Misha, the Adelaide hair clip is made of ruscus leaves, hydrangea flowers and gypsophila handmade. An elegantly detailed hair accessory that makes a difference! Placed on the side or behind to hold the strands, it adorns the hair with a light golden touch. Designed for little girls and their moms, it promises festive moments and laughter.

  • Composition: preserved gold hydrangea flowers, gypsophila and ruscus, gold-plated structure
  • Size: about 8cm
  • Shelf life: eternal
  • Storage instructions: keep away from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

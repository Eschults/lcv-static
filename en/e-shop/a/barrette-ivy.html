Ivy hair clip

Hairpin Eternal Preserved flowers
38€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

This bohemian flower-shaped hair clip, entirely made of Swarovski crystals, will give a poetic touch to your outfit. It will delicately slip into your hair like a precious ornament. It can be worn alone or combined with other barrettes from the Purple Rain collection such as the Faye or Amber barrette.

  • Composition: gold plated metal barrette, purple and parma swarovski crystals
  • Size: unique
  • Shelf life: the crystals that make up this barrette are eternal
  • Conservation advice: to keep your jewel sparkling, we advise you to avoid contact with seawater, alcohol, perfume, creams and cosmetics. We invite you to clean your jewel if needed with a soft cloth or brush and clear water
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

This hair accessory is part of the new Purple Rain collection where purple is the star. A new selection of accessories is offered to seduce you. This hair clip is suitable for all hair types, whether you have fine hair or thick hair, the purple stands out as well on blondes as on brunettes.

The little bonus of this hair clip is that it follows you everywhere, in all occasions. The end of year parties or simply in your evening hairstyle, for a wedding, in your pretty everyday hairstyle… It will bring a touch of fantasy and freshness and come to discipline your rebellious strands. To know the different ways to tie them, you can go on our Instagram to see some tutorials or on our blog with our many articles.

It’s impossible to resist a little hair jewel to perfect your star look. The Ivy hair clip with its flower shape is well representative of our pretty flowery brand. So it’s impossible to pass up this timeless hair accessory. Put away your classic mini clips or barrettes and make the glamorous and romantic barrette the must-have to remember. Accessorizing your look is not something to be left out!

The Ivy barrette is composed of a flower adorned with Swarovski crystals of fine and delicate stones mounted on a golden metal structure. It will sublimate you day and night, and for any season, for your spring-summer and fall-winter outfits. But you are probably wondering how to wear this delicate barrette? If you have long hair, you can braid your hair into a ponytail and add this barrette to hold your strands in place. If you have short hair, you can simply slip it in the front so you don’t get annoyed with your hair. As you can see, it is always possible to slide this accessory according to your lengths. Many hairstyles are therefore possible to sublimate your beautiful silky hair. For women who are fans of hair accessories such as bandanas, hats, fancy hair pins in your buns and ponytails, a headband, the Ivy barrette must be part of your wide range of fashion accessories.

All accessories have the possibility to be fully customizable to satisfy all your desires and are handmade. You can customize the bohemian accessory that you dream of, from flower crowns and double combs, to earrings and hair accessories. So go to our showroom in the Marais to flash on the must-have accessory!

Isadora

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
148€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

This fine and delicate wedding flower crown is inspired by one of our iconic models. Isadora is a revisit between softness and pink tones for a poetic and romantic touch to celebrate Love.

  • Composition: white and pink hydrangeas, white and pale pink gypsophila, white fern and pale pink rice preserved flowers
  • Size: unisize. Can be adjusted with the ribbon on the back
  • Shelf life: eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

“Dance with me” embodies a vision specific to the LCV house, revealing femininity to sublimate each woman. Inspired by the world of dance, each accessory is an ode to softness. Do you dream of a chic wedding with pretty flower crowns on your loose hair? Dance with me offers accessories combining delicate stone ornaments and eternal flowers: romantic and country flower crowns for your mane, flowery combs to slip into a lacquered bun like an opera dancer, or into a glamorous half-tail. Let yourself be transported by the refined universe of our elegant pieces. Any occasion is good to wear pretty flowers!

To celebrate love and this special day, the pretty Isadora crown is a romantic piece composed of a mix of white and pink colors that will sprinkle some flowers in your hair. The soft tones of this flower crown will bring you a romantic style. How to wear the crown? Isadora is suitable for all hair types: long hair, short hair, loose hair, tied hair, brown hair or blonde hair, thin hair or thick hair: this head jewel will fit your mane and give it a sophisticated look.Dreaming of a hippie wedding or a bohemian chic wedding? Come and put the Isadora flower crown on your loose hair, or with a braid.

The Isadora flower crown is one size fits all and is attached at the back with a ribbon. It is composed of white and pink hydrangea flowers, white and light pink gypsophila, white fern and light pink rice flowers stabilized. Whether you choose a ponytail, braided hair, or a low bun for your wedding hairstyle, the Isadora crown will adorn the lengths of your hair with fantasy and glamour and will perfectly match your delicate wedding dress You can find our pin-up hairstyle ideas on our social networks or on our journal to find inspiration for a hairstyle idea with our wedding accessories.

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a French brand of head jewelry and jewelry for women to wear for special occasions. Among our stabilized flower hair accessories, you can find our flower crowns, headbands, jewel bun picks, pins, combs, tiaras, iconic barrettes (bow, sequins, rhinestones, crystals…), flower hats, bracelets for bridesmaids or granddaughters… All Les Couronnes de Victoire’s boho accessories can be worn for spring-summer or fall-winter celebrations to create the perfect alliance. Our little fairies create your delicate pieces in the heart of Paris where you can come and meet us at 230 rue Saint Martin.

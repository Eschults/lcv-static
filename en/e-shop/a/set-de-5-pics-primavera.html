Les Couronnes de Victoire unveiled their latest marvel, the Primavera set, a dazzling creation crafted to enhance the trendiest hairstyles. Their fine finishing in 24k gold brings an ultimate touch of glamour, captivating gazes and infusing an aura of nobility into every mane.

These Primavera pins adapt to all hair lengths, from short to long, offering unparalleled versatility for every envisioned style. Be it for a sophisticated wedding hairstyle, with skillfully placed pins framing the nape, or for a low ponytail on hot summer days, these pins become the essential accessory to elegantly adorn each hairstyle. During weddings or even soirées, these pieces become staples, adding a touch of glamour to every hairstyle. They adapt to every style, from casual hairstyles to sophisticated buns, from low ponytails to more intricate, voluminous braids.

Tucked discreetly at the back of the head, these accessories enhance the beauty of curly, straight, or wavy hair.

Hairpins in the form of bows are once again the trendy hairstyling accessory! Discover our Théia hair clip from the “Starry Night” collection. This hair accessory is designed to elegantly illuminate your haircut for any special occasion. Its delicate design, combining a navy blue velvet bow tie attached to a gold frame, makes it a must-have handmade accessory for a modern wedding, for the festive season, or simply for a night out with friends.

Our Théia hair clip is the perfect hair accessory to enhance your look, whether you have long, medium or short hair. Its velvet bow tie and gold-plated clasp add a glamorous touch to your hairstyle.

With its navy blue color adding a touch of originality, Théia lets you achieve your most creative hairstyle ideas. You can create an elegant chignon, a festive ponytail or a sophisticated braid. This real hair jewel will also accessorize your classic, romantic bridal hairstyle look: loose hair, adding a touch of color and sparkle to your style.

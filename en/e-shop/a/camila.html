Camila

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
225€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Romanticism and vintage spirit are the keywords that best describe this crown. Its colourful flowers and stabilised roses will bring you all the charm that is contained in this flower of Love. Its composition and foliage will give you a retro look like an icon of the 70s. Its ribbon allows you to adjust the crown on all types of hairstyles, to give way to your desires!

  • Composition: yellow and peach roses, pink wild roses, eucalyptus, purple limonium, red carnations and pink bougainvillea stabilised
  • Size: unisize. Can be adjusted with the ribbon on the back
  • Shelf life: the flowers in this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Les Couronnes de Victoire releases its new collection of bohemian hair accessories for women : California Dreaming. This collection is inspired by California, pretty flower crowns and has the iconic singer Lana Del Rey as its muse. California Dreaming offers different types of accessories for a wedding: flowery jewel combs, colorful flower crowns, the delicate flower hair clip and a pretty beige hat to accessorize your bridal hairstyle. These fancy accessories will bring freshness to your bridal look. Adding some flowers to your hair is very trendy whether it’s a spring-summer wedding or a winter wedding : flower petals adorn your favorite hairstyles and match with all our other floral jewelry.

The Camila crown is a handmade bohemian chic flower crown that will look great in your hair at your wedding whether you have short hair or long hair. The delicate Camila hair accessory features stabilized flowers (not artificial flowers) to be worn like a modern day hippie : large yellow and peach roses, pink wild roses, eucalyptus, purple limonium, red carnations and stabilized pink bougainvillea. The natural flowers of this romantic bohemian ornament accessory will hold your hair loose or tied up. Wondering how to wear this hair accessory to make you look great for your wedding? There are many hairstyle ideas that work with a crown and are very simple to achieve: a low bun, a braid, a ponytail, a classic bun or even just loose hair in country style do the job!

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a brand of jewelry and accessories made of fresh flowers that have become stabilized flowers. Our favorite pieces are obviously the colored or unicolor flower crowns, then the wedding combs, the earrings… We even make accessories for the groom too! Our glamorous pieces can be worn for a wedding, a christening and for all occasions really! Our creations are handmade in the heart of Paris by our little fairies who have at heart to satisfy your floral desires. If you want to try our jewelry or have a little bridal hairstyle tutorial, feel free to drop by our Parisian showroom at 230 rue Saint Martin.

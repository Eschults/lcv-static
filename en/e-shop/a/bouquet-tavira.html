Tavira Bouquet

Bouquet Eternal Preserved flowers
118€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Fall for the Tavira bouquet with its soft, powdery floral notes. Composed of hydrangea, gypsophila and touches of eucalyptus, it will seduce you with its poetic composition that will enchant all lovers of the great classics. This timeless bouquet has the particularity of matching with all styles of wedding dresses, the sure value in terms of bridal bouquet. Give it a second life by keeping it after your wedding day for a lifetime memory.

  • Composition : natural and pink gypsophila, coral broom, tatarica, eucalyptus, stabilized white and powder pink hydrangeas, beige linen ribbon
  • Size : approximately 26 cm in height and 28 cm in diameter
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Les Couronnes de Victoire presents its new collection of wedding bouquets. Inspired by the island of Tavira, this bouquet offers a beautiful composition. Delicate and feminine, it embodies romance par excellence. Like all our creations, the Tavira bouquet has been handmade in our Parisian workshop. To bring a touch of freshness and to add flowers to your wedding dress, let yourself be enchanted by our new collection of bouquets. The choice of a bridal bouquet should be tailored to you. To best meet your desires, it is possible to personalise your floral composition according to your tastes. All our bouquets are handmade in our Parisian workshop. If you have already placed an order with your florists for your fresh flower arrangements (wedding car, table centrepiece, decoration of the reception venue), let yourself be tempted by a bouquet of stabilised flowers that you can keep for life.

Unlike artificial flowers, stabilised flowers are real bouquets of flowers. The romantic bouquet is ideal if you want a romantic wedding. This floral arrangement can be in harmony with the rest of your accessories (boutonnieres, crowns) and your decoration elements (reception hall, ceremony venue, home decoration). This wide choice of collections will allow you to choose the bouquet that suits your image and personality.

Les Couronnes de Victoire puts all its know-how into its compositions and its floral art. The house puts all its passion into proposing harmonious compositions to be able to offer you a beautiful bouquet for your romantic wedding. Choosing flowers and a beautiful floral creation for a wedding is important, a pretty floral composition always makes its effect with your wedding dress. It is possible to match your bouquet to your hair accessory. You can also offer yourself a bouquet of fresh flowers during the bouquet toss and keep your bouquet in stabilised flowers for a nice souvenir of your wedding. Les Couronnes de Victoire handcraft your floral arrangements to offer you a vast collection of bridal bouquets and wedding flowers, each set with stabilised flowers to dress up and enhance your wedding theme.

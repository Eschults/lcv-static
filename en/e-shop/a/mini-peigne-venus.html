Venus mini comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
48€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The mini Venus comb is distinguished by its stabilized rose and delightful gradient of pastel hues. Feel free to pair it with the Venus bracelet for an elegant ensemble. You can also wear it with the boutonniere from the same range for a 100% coordinated ensemble, including both women and men.

  • Composition : Stabilized hydrangeas and roses, stabilized ivy, gold-plated comb.
  • Size : 4 cm
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Elevate your bridal hairstyle to new heights of grace with our Venus mini comb, an exceptional piece from the new Renaissance collection crafted from stabilized hydrangea, stabilized roses, stabilized ivy, and a gold-plated comb. This floral accessory is designed to enhance your bridal hairstyle, adding a romantic, bohemian finishing touch to your sophisticated look.

This minimalist ornament is much more than a simple hair accessory, it’s a delicate head jewel that enhances your chignon or elaborate hairstyle with incomparable elegance. Hydrangeas, roses, stabilized ivy and their sublime details merge harmoniously to create an essential accessory that evokes romance and delicacy.

Adorned with a gilded metal comb, this hair jewel for women is easily set into your locks, adding a touch of golden glamour to your hair. Whatever your hairstyle ideas, whether you opt for a sophisticated chignon, a bohemian braid or a casual ponytail, the Venus comb adapts to every bride-to-be’s hairstyle. Pair this haute-couture creation with gold earrings, a silver necklace, or other bridal jewelry to create a harmonious ensemble that will capture the essence of your special day, sublimate your darling hair and your wedding dress to perfection.

Treat yourself to the luxury of a timeless hair accessory with the Venus comb. And if that’s not enough for you? Find what you need for a country wedding at Les Couronnes de Victoire!

Sirena Boutonniere

Boutonniere Eternal Preserved flowers
22€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Be seduced by our bohemian Sirena boutonniere and dress the groom and his witnesses in a bohemian and country style.

  • Composition: preserved rose, umbelliferous, gypsophila and nigella
  • Size: around 5 cm
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this boutonniere are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

For your wedding, we have brought out a new collection of jewellery on the bohemian and romantic theme of L’Odyssée. The bride and groom will find flowery pieces to accessorise their outfits. For the groom and his groomsmen, we propose buttonholes including the pretty Sirena buttonhole. The name of this buttonhole takes the groom to the waves to join his mermaid, the future bride. The buttonhole gives a more bucolic look to the groom’s outfit.

The flower arrangements for this accessory are made from fresh flowers that have been stabilised afterwards and not from artificial flowers or dried flowers, giving them a fresh flower look and eternal durability. More specifically, you can find white roses, gypsophila, angel hair, pampas and other small flowers in this buttonhole’s floral arrangement. Everything you need to marry a romantic wedding to a country wedding.

For a wedding, it can be a good idea to match the bride and groom’s accessories. Indeed, for the bride, it is possible to match her original wedding jewellery to the buttonhole of her future husband. She can opt for the Sirena flower crown, which has a similar flower arrangement to the buttonhole, meaning mainly white flowers. If the flower crowns are not for you, we also have the Sirena double comb and the Sirena mini comb whose flower arrangement also remains the same. So it all depends on what you want and which beautiful flowers you want to dress your hair with. You can add hydrangeas, eucalyptus or red roses if you wish. To continue in this harmony, you can choose a flowery wedding theme for a bohemian wedding and create floral arrangements using similar flowers. Your guests and bridesmaids can then sublimate their floral arrangements with beautiful bouquets and accessories. As a bride, you can also choose a magnificent bouquet whose floral creation takes up the composition of your accessories. The beautiful Nami bouquet from our new collection is a bridal bouquet with stabilised roses and small wild flowers, such as the wedding flowers from the Sirena buttonhole. It is possible to make a bouquet from an original bouquet from our collection to modify it according to your wishes and to choose the flowers you like. Our other pieces can also be personalised to your image. For decoration, you can decorate the wedding ceremony and reception hall with preserved flowers. For table decoration, you can create a table runner with a few flowers and rose petals. As a centrepiece, it can also be a good idea to put a floral creation in the form of a beautiful bouquet. To go further than just decorating the room for the wedding, have flower decorations made for the bride and groom’s car. Car decorations can also add romantic touches to your wedding. The advantage of these everlasting flowers is that you can reuse the centrepieces for home decoration.

All flowers are natural flowers that have been stabilised. All our original floral creations are handmade in our Parisian premises from beautiful flowers to offer you the most beautiful and eternal accessories possible. In our Parisian showroom, we also organise a floral workshop for bridal showers and birthdays. You will thus be able to express your floral desires.

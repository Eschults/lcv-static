Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Discover our Zeta hair clip, created in collaboration with the talented designer Justine from Les Noeuds de Maman. Dare to shine with this hair clip subtly adorned with glitter. An easy-to-wear piece that fits well in both thick and fine hair !

  • Composition : UV-resistant resin hair clip with midnight blue-colored glitter. Each hair clip being unique, Justine arranges the flowers with care, which may slightly shift their location compared to the photos on the site
  • Size : 6,5 cm
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions : none
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order

Welcome to the enchanted world of the “Starry Night” collection, where the magic of the festive season comes to life. Are you planning a big party with friends? Or, bigger than that, a modern fairytale wedding, and you’re looking for hair accessories for your bridesmaids? Our Zeta hair clip, embodying the elegance of this special, romantic time, is just what you’re looking for.

Adorned with a sapphire background and glitter, this golden hair clip is much more than a minimalist accessory for your hair. It’s a piece of fancy jewelry that will sublimate your hairstyles. Whether you opt for a sophisticated chignon, a classic ponytail or a bohemian braid, whether your hair is long, medium-length or short, its glittering details are designed to suit every hairstyle and are sure to add a glamorous twist to your final look.

For all women in search of originality and elegance, this subtle head jewel is a must-have accessory that will accessorize and complete your hair look. For a wedding full of magic, a special night out, an extravagant fashion show or the holidays, Zeta is one of our hair accessories that goes perfectly with every style and occasion, enhancing your spirit at every moment. Moreover, thanks to its practicality, it will be very easy to manage your flyaways while preserving your original look and your sophisticated haute-couture look. No need to watch a tutorial, straighten your hair, attach several bobby pins or use extensions when you’ve got our hair jewel in the spotlight!

Don’t wait any longer to shine at every event, come and see us to discover our new Zeta hair accessory and other head jewels and add a touch of magic to your party hairstyles. You deserve to stand out and dazzle those around you!

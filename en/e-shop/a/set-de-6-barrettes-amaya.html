Set of 6 Amaya hair clips

Hairpin Eternal Preserved flowers
128€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Inspired by our Amaya crown, this set of bohemian hair clips will be the bohemian touch that will sprinkle in your hairstyle. Ideal to add a floral touch to your hairstyles such as a low bun or a braid. For an even more bohemian effect, you can also wear the hair clips on loose hair.

  • Composition: stabilised old pink and peach wild roses and powder pink hydrangea, brass structure
  • Size: around 1 inch
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up these hair clip are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Looking for multicoloured accessories in pop and pastel colours inspired by spring and summer? The California Dreaming collection is for you with its hair accessories in different colours and jewellery in colourful shades inspired by the hippie and bohemian movements of the 70s. It is ideal for spring-summer weddings and for all upcoming occasions. With hair pins, hair clips, combs, bun picks, hats, crowns and tiara headbands for women, as well as veils and earrings, we offer you the complete set for the romantic, bohemian bride.

The set is perfect for a bridal hairstyle and consists of three different golden hair clips, decorated with small flowers in beige, pink and fuchsia colours. These precious mini clips are ideal for a minimalist and discreet effect on your hairstyle and will become your new fashion accessory. The barrette set is also the perfect hair accessory for little girls’ hair: small and thin, their crab clips will hold them all day long and can be placed in a zig-zag pattern on the child’s head. It is suitable for both thick and fine hair.

How to style the hair? Styling is quite simple, and different hairstyles are possible with these accessories. Simply place the hair clips on loose hair around the edge of your head to enhance your look. Each hair clip will be used to discipline unruly strands to create a lace of flowers on your hair. More than one clip can also be placed on one length of your hair and used as a fancy ornament. Another possibility would be to have your hairdresser curl your hair (or do it yourself with a tutorial), twist two strands on either side of your head and tie them in the middle with a clip and scatter the other clips as you see fit. This hairstyle can be done on short or long hair and the twist can be replaced by a braid. For brides, bridesmaids or guests who want to show off their shoulders, first tie your hair in a tail, braid it, and then put these head jewels along the braid. You can also tie them into a bun, either loose or donut, and then spread them over the wedding bun.

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a company that makes hair jewellery for women to accessorise your looks. All the accessories are handmade by us to offer you the best quality possible. From flower crowns, elastics, bun picks, hairpins, clip-on barrettes, ribbons/knots, hats, veils and earrings with pearls or flowers, bouquet of flowers and even scented candles, all types of hair jewellery and ornaments for a bohemian, glamorous and romantic wedding are offered. Come and see us in our Parisian boutique or on our social networks.

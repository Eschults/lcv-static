Giovanna child crown

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
45€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

This crown will be sublime on the hair of little girls with its botanical composition ! The mix of stabilized ivy and hydrangea forms a bucolic and playful accessory for your adorable fairies. You can match it with the bracelet from the same range for a most divine set.

  • Composition : Stabilized hydrangea, gypsophila, and ivy
  • Size : One size. Can be adjusted using the ribbon at the back
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

The Giovanna children’s crown, part of the brand new Renaissance collection by Les Couronnes de Victoire, radiates incomparable elegance. This delicate crown is crafted with stabilized hydrangeas in delicate shades of white and pink, complemented by stabilized pink gypsophila and stabilized ivy, creating an absolute floral enchantment. Featuring an adjustable satin tie at the back of the head, this crown is an exceptional jewel to adorn the little ones. It perfectly marries the beauty of eternal flowers with adjustable comfort for children’s hairstyles.

Within the range of accessories for little girls offered by the brand, the Giovanna crown stands out for its refinement and its ability to elegantly accessorize. The brand offers a variety of hair accessories for little girls or women: delicate floral barrettes, rhinestone-adorned clips, charismatic crowns, exquisite headbands, delicate hairpins, and many other headpieces. Hair accessories, from bandanas to ribbons, even down to scrunchies, allow for styling and taming the rebellious strands of younger heads with style and imagination. Elastic bands, hairpins for buns, and hair combs add a chic and practical touch to every hairstyle. Styling little heads can sometimes be a challenge, but with shiny, floral accessories they love, it becomes child’s play!

Whether for a wedding, communion, or any special occasion, Les Couronnes de Victoire offers a multitude of colorful, feminine, and varied accessories to accompany little girls in their hair styling journey. Naturally, these accessories for little girls can be personalized and expanded to suit mothers as well. We particularly enjoy creating sets, whether it’s bracelets to match the crown or earrings, or even coordinating the groom with the honor children through boutonnieres.

Thus, the Giovanna crown presents itself as the precious adornment for all little girls, perfectly combining the delicacy of flowers with the fantasy of hair accessories. Each creation from the brand becomes an exquisite finishing touch to enhance hairstyles and perfect their allure, from everyday looks to the most special events.

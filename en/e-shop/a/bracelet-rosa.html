Rosa wrist corsage

Wrist corsage Eternal Preserved flowers
22€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

This delicate wrist corsage will poetically dress up any wrist with its preserved hydrangea flowers and touches of copper-coloured gypsophila. The ideal accessory designed for ceremonies from our Les Couronnes de Victoire x Louise Misha collection. Its flexible structure allows it to be adapted to all wrist sizes and even to slip over a bun or ponytail thanks to its satin ribbon.

  • Composition: preserved white, pink and orange hydrangea flowers and mini ivory, pink and copper gypsophila, wire structure covered with a brown ribbon, ivory satin ribbon
  • Size: 5cm
  • Shelf life: the flowers that make up this wrist corsage are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

The Rosa wrist corsage is a delicately bohemian children’s wrist corsage from our new LCV x Louise Misha collection. This jewel is one of the wrist corsages that we at LCV love for its fantasy. Indeed, it perfectly dresses up the wrist with a touch of gaiety that provides the same magic as a diamond, a wrist corsage made of pearls, yellow gold, rose gold or solid silver.

Preserved flowers are precious and represent the engraving of an eternal love. When made into a piece of jewellery, they are therefore a great idea for an original gift as a baby gift, christening gift or communion gift. They are a unique gift for little girls. If the pretty wrist corsage is intended for your child, it will be a precious ornament and an ideal gift for her costume jewellery box. Moreover, it will fit perfectly on different wrists because it can be adjusted like a cord wrist corsage, with a ribbon that you can tie in a knot. In order to change the rhinestone jewellery for children, or the silver wrist corsage or the traditional gold-plated wrist corsage, opt for the costume jewellery set with flowers. This fabulous gift for your little angel will delight his heart by joining his collection of favourite jewellery and original personalised jewellery. You don’t need precious stones to enchant a real princess, give her flowers to cheer her up. If you dream of matching your baby, we recommend you choose a woman’s wrist corsage. All our women’s jewellery is customisable, so you can choose a wrist corsage that is infinitely customisable, so that your wrist corsage jewellery matches. In the same collection, for the mother, you will find the Clementine earrings, which form the perfect match with the crown of the same name. Just like the Rosa wrist corsage, these are not gold or semi-precious stone earrings. It is a bohemian floral jewellery set that will make you glow like a multicoloured butterfly under a rainbow of love.

The creation of floral jewellery is a true floral art that lies between that of the jeweller and that of the florist. It is the combination of these two arts, which makes it possible to create a combination of jewellery and fantasy.

With its delicate white hydrangea flowers stabilized on the front of the head, the Pietragalla veil offers a truly sophisticated touch to the bride. This veil is sewn onto a tiara and fits all face shapes.

  • Composition : French made veil, stabilized white hydrangea flowers, gold metal tiara
  • Size: This veil measures approximately 20 cm in height
  • Shelf life: eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Dance with me is our new wedding collection inspired by the world of classical dance. Between pearls and flowers let yourself be transported by this chic and refined collection. The bridal veil is a delicate accessory to adorn your hair during a wedding. For the bride herself or for her bridesmaids, the Pietragalla veil will be your sophisticated touch.

Sewn onto a tiara, the Pietragalla veil is a French made veil made of white hydrangea flowers stabilised on a gold metal tiara structure. The Pietragalla wedding veil can be worn on different types of bridal hairstyles such as a bun, a ponytail and even on loose hair with a semi-tie. It will offer you a chic and bohemian style whether you have a long wedding dress, a short wedding dress, a bohemian wedding dress, a dress with a back neckline, a vintage wedding dress, a cocktail dress with ruffles, a lace wedding dress, a trapeze dress, a custom-made wedding dress, a princess dress, a strapless dress with a train. You can find lace, chiffon, satin, tulle, dresses with plumetis, small pearls, crystals, pearls and rhinestones, pearly pearls, etc. The Pietragalla veil is delicately attached to your bridal hairstyle. The Pietragalla veil is ideal with a bun or loose hair slightly raised by a half-tail. Discreet and delicate, this wedding accessory goes perfectly with all styles of wedding dresses and will offer you a romantic and vaporous look. Lightweight, this bridal accessory will be very pleasant to wear throughout the day.

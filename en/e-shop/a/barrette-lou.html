Lou Hair clip

Hairpin Eternal Preserved flowers
25€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

This delicate little barrette decorated with hydrangea flowers and preserved white gypsophila will be ideal to lift a lock of hair or to prick into a small bun or a pretty ceremonial hairstyle. Very light and pleasant to wear, this hair clip is designed to hold on very fine little girl’s hair. Don’t hesitate to combine the Lou hair clip with our small Lou wrist corsage from the same range for a special occasion such as a baptism, a ceremony or a birthday.

  • Composition : white hydrangea and white preserved gypsophila, bronze hair clip structure
  • Size: 4cm
  • Shelf-life: the flowers in this hair clip are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Les Couronnes de Victoire have released a new bohemian chic collection of ready-to-wear accessories for little girls. This way, at your wedding, in addition to matching your accessories to your wedding dress, you will also be able to match them to your little bridesmaid or bridesmaids. These pretty bridesmaid accessories can also be for a communion, a baptism, they will go well with any ceremony outfit.

The pretty Lou flower hair clip is adorned with hydrangea flowers and white gypsophila. For big occasions or small ceremonies, you will be able to sublimate the pretty princess dress or skirt of your baby girl. You will thus give a touch of femininity to your maid of honour.

These pretty white hair clips will go perfectly with small ceremonial dresses or skirts in pastel tones. So a powdered pink, coral or even turquoise girl’s outfit will make your baby girls look elegant. To be more original and change from the little ceremony dress, for a wedding or other, you can dress the little princesses in a pretty strapless combishort in viscose or linen with embroidery and little lace flounces. This outfit will be perfect for a wedding ceremony in spring-summer but also in autumn-winter, you can simply add a little knitted cardigan and put on children’s ballerinas instead of sandals. The most important thing is that their formal wear is comfortable.

So whether it’s for a beautiful wedding dress or a baptismal gown, the Lou hair clip will be the perfect fancy accessory. White, it will look great with a white dress, a pink dress or even a black dress. You can also wear it in a bun or with loose hair for chic hairstyles. For more glamorous and romantic touches, the mother can match her wedding dress or her beautiful girl’s ceremonial dress with her daughter’s wedding gown.

Afterwards, you can reuse this hair clip for all occasions in everyday life. The same goes for formal wear, depending on the girl’s outfit you have chosen, you can put it back on every day. You will never have enough beautiful dresses for the girls at home. A large collection of princess dresses will satisfy both mother and child.

All the accessories for Les Couronnes de Victoire are handmade in our Parisian workshops. You can find them in our showroom but also on our e-shop. Our floral accessories can be personalised, so you can be creative and make the jewellery your own.

Donatella double comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
170€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Do you dream of a unique accessory that would give you a wildly original bridal look? Say no more, the Donatella double comb was obviously made for you. It can be worn asymmetrically towards the front of the head, to refine a side-swept hairdo, chignon or braid, or make for an eye-catching low bun.

  • Composition: preserved flowers and greenery (eucalyptus, David Austin English red roses, wild red roses, red Princess roses, fuchsia-pink rice flowers, fuchsia-pink hydrangeas).
  • Size: approximately 10 inches.
  • Shelf life: the flowers that make up this comb are eternal.
  • Storage instructions: keep away from direct sunlight, heat and humidity.
  • Delivery: 8 working days from order date.

Dana earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
105€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Decorated with a hammered gold-plated ring and delicate preserved flowers intertwined with dangling chains, Dana earrings will give a bohemian and elegant touch to your wedding outfit. Discreet and lightweight, you can easily combine our Dana earrings with a hair accessory from our collections, such as the Ezili comb or the Naïa double comb from our “L’Odyssée” collection.

These earrings can be mounted on clips on request.

  • Composition: gold plated ring and chain, preserved fern and gypsophila
  • Size: around 4 cm
  • Shelf life: the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

L’Odyssée is the new collection of wedding jewellery and accessories. This bohemian and romantic theme will transport you, the bride-to-be, your groom-to-be and your guests to the sandy beaches of Greece. All our bridal accessories will allow you to accessorise your hair, wrist and ears. For the latter, the Dana earrings are the ideal jewel to sublimate your wedding outfit and make you look like a true modern-day mermaid. If, like us, you have fallen in love with the Dana earrings but your ears are not pierced, these earrings can be clipped on request. So you can dress your lobes for the ceremony.

Decorated with a hammered gold-plated ring and pretty preserved flowers, this head jewel will make you a bohemian bride. To accentuate this bohemian and country wedding side, this trendy wedding jewel can be worn with one of our hair accessories, such as the Ezili comb or the Naïa double comb from our Odyssée collection. The discreet Naïa hair accessory is the ultimate hair accessory. Discreet and glamorous for your wedding, it will add a romantic touch to your bridal gown.

The curls combined with one of our combs will give you some styling ideas. In fact, if you want to make your curls visible, you can choose a hairstyle in which your hair is attached. Why not a low bun or banana bun, a braid or a half ponytail for a semi-stitched, semi-loose effect and to let the back of your wedding dress show off. To achieve such a wedding hairstyle, no particular haircut is necessary, all hair types are ideal. So whether you have long hair or short hair, you will be able to tie your hair and perfect it with one of our hair jewellery. For a wedding, the comb can also be worn with a veil. You can find our selection of bridal veils on our pretty e-shop. Don’t limit the use of our elegant Dana curls to weddings, you can wear them in everyday life and for special occasions. Combined with a cocktail dress or for a communion, baptism or engagement, you will be the Queen of the event. Our wedding accessories can be declined for different occasions and give you a little finishing touch. As little ideas for the wedding decoration, you can also get inspired by these accessories for women and men who appreciate this flowery side and would like to make it their wedding theme. For the table decoration of your wedding room, you can match it with our flower crowns, tiaras, hairpins and other romantic accessories. Why not put petals on the table, to remind the flowers of the Dana earrings, with golden sequins and pretty pearls. Your bridal bouquet and bridesmaids’ bouquets can also be inspired by our original accessories. You may find it easier to plan your wedding day by choosing a harmonious wedding inspired by existing head jewellery. The bride and groom will be delighted.

All the accessories are designed and created in our Parisian workshops. You will find in our shop under our premises our collections with pretty decorations presenting them. You can also make an appointment to take advantage of our advice to find the wedding accessory that will make you a mermaid. You can come with your wedding dress and bridal veil so that we can help you find the hair jewellery and other jewellery that best suits you.

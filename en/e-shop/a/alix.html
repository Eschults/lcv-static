Alix Crown

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
43€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

With its delicate touches of dark pink hydrangea and small, preserved gypsophila and eucalyptus flowers, the Alix crown will be adorable to flower little girls’ heads. Ideal for a wedding procession, this crown can also be worn for a special occasion such as a baptism or communion or even a birthday.

This crown will fit any child’s head size, from babies to teenagers, thanks to its thin satin ribbon at the back.

  • Composition: dark pink hydrangea, preserved pink gypsophila and eucalyptus, white satin ribbon
  • Size: one size only. Can be adjusted with the ribbon on the back
  • Shelf-life: the flowers in this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Our new collection for girls at Les Couronnes de Victoire will allow mothers to match their child daughter and brides to match their accessories and wedding outfits to those of the little bridesmaids. This children’s collection can also be worn for all occasions, big occasions such as baptism, communion or wedding ceremonies but also in everyday life.

The Alix flower crown for girls will make a lovely ceremonial ensemble. The petals of its dark pink flowers will sublimate all the colours of a girl’s ceremonial dress. The hydrangeas, gypsophila and eucalyptus in the crown have been preserved. It adapts to all head sizes and can be worn from the baby girl to the teenager thanks to its satin ribbon to be tied with a small bow.

This crown for girls will complete a pretty wedding outfit for a little girl of honour. In the wedding procession, whether the bridesmaids hold the train or just walk in front of the bride, they will be watched by all the guests. This is why a pretty dress or combishort in linen or viscose with little ruffles and embroidery dressed with a flower crown will make your little girls all the more adorable and elegant. If the wedding is in Spring-Summer, you can opt in children’s formal wear for a little bridesmaid dress with short sleeves or sleeveless with straps and small sandals or ballerinas. Short dress or long dress according to your preferences. The little crown will add a chic and romantic bohemian touch to the little princesses.

For your little girl, the most important thing is that her clothes are comfortable and that she looks good in her ceremonial outfit. You can add a little knitted cardigan to the beautiful dress to keep her warm. You have several possibilities for the choice of colour, with the pink crown you can put her in a powder pink, ecru, fuchsia, navy blue or a white dress.

All our chic accessories for special occasions such as your wedding or everyday life are made in our Parisian workshops. You can make an appointment in our showroom or by video to be advised and accompanied by a sales consultant totally dedicated to you.

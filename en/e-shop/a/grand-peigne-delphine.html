Delphine Wide Comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
95€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Decorated with stabilized wildflowers, Delphine’s wide comb will bring a bohemian and country touch to a pretty bridal bun or a half-tail. Light and airy, it will be ideal to bring a floral touch to your outfit with discretion.

  • Composition : preserved umbelliferous, ruscus, gypsophila and nigella, gold metal comb
  • Size: around 13 cm
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this wide comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Delphine comes from the Greek word “delfis” which means “dolphin”, beauty of the seas and oceans. This is how we named one of the pieces in our new collection “L’Odyssée” : the large Delphine comb. A collection that honors the world of the ocean to sublimate our brides. Dressed with stabilized wildflowers such as umbelliferous, ruscus, gypsophila and nigella, Delphine’s large gold-colored flowered comb is the perfect hair jewel to adorn your bridal hairstyle, for your country or bohemian wedding. Our Delphine ornament is a handmade creation created in our Parisian workshop in the heart of the Marais, just like all our LCV pieces. Minimalist and glamorous, this fancy accessory will bring a flowery touch to your wedding dress, with discretion and delicacy. The large Delphine comb is an elegant headpiece that fits all bridal hairstyles and all hair types, whether you have short or long hair, thick or fine hair. Here are some inspirations made in Les Couronnes de Victoire to know which hairstyle to match with our large Delphine comb.

There’s nothing better than a bohemian braid for a hippie chic style, then attach the Delphine large comb to the back of your head and get a look that’s both poetic and trendy. This hair ornament also goes perfectly with a voluminous bun. There are different types of bun: high bun, low bun, bun with braids, banana bun… It’s up to you to find your favorite bun, more or less sophisticated or discreet. Then place the jewel above the bun for a real bohemian princess look for a day. For a fresher, more natural look, you can let your hair down and tie it into a half ponytail, attached with two small braids on either side of your hair. Let two thin strands of hair frame your face to add a romantic touch to your bridal hairstyle, then attach the pretty Delphine jewel to the back of your head. If you don’t have curly hair, don’t hesitate to wave your lengths to add volume to your mane as well. All of our flower accessories are not exclusively reserved for brides-to-be! These accessories can also be worn by bridesmaids or guests. Flower wreaths, bun pins, combs, tiaras, hairbands, flowery earrings… We offer a wide range of hair jewelry and ornaments to accessorize your outfit on this important day. You will also find different buttonholes that can be attached to your future husband’s bow tie to bring a floral touch to his suit.

We invite you to visit our e-shop to discover all of our hair ornaments and jewelry, a multitude of elegant and unique creations, to make you the most beautiful bride: flowered hair combs, hair clips, hair pins decorated with Swarovski crystals or fine stones, headbands with sequins, bun pins set with pearly pearls, ear clips, hair clips, rush bracelets, elastic bands, golden earrings, veils, straw hats, etc.

Discover our new capsule collection Golden Hour ! Still pleasant for their heat, their luminosity and the beautiful colors that begin to appear in nature. Golden Hour is an ode to the Indian summer, where nature is adorned with subtle gold.

This country and bohemian capsule has natural and nude tones such as different shades of brown, gold and white and is composed of 7 jewelry and accessories for your wedding.

The hair accessory presented here is a hair pins set consisting of 5 hair pins. The ornaments in this set are hydrangea, gypsophila, roses, ruscus, lagurus and other small flowers of the field.

For a bride-to-be who wants to have a nature theme with nude colors and a minimalist hair accessory, this pin set is the PERFECT accessory. In fact, no matter what pretty hairstyle you want to achieve, hair pins are wedding accessories that can be worn on different hairstyles. First of all, you can make a bun, the traditional bridal hairstyle, and arrange the set as you like around it, on the sides of your head or in the bun. You can also make a romantic, fuzzy, bohemian braid, and stick the pins like a flowery waterfall in your braided mane. The bun picks can also be worn on a ponytail or a semi tie. As for your hair, long and medium length hair can wear this accessory, while short hair will have more difficulty in maintaining the pins. It all depends on the hairstyle you want and the skills of your hairdresser.

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a wedding jewelry and hair accessory company that makes bridal jewelry and hair accessories to enhance and decorate your bridal hairstyle and to complement and dress up your wedding dress or bridal gown. Many of our fashion accessories are for a wedding or special occasion but we also have accessories that will enhance an evening hairstyle or even an everyday look. Any occasion is good to wear flowers ! In addition to bun picks, we make flower crowns, earrings, hats, bracelets, headbands, combs, barrettes and other hair jewelry.

These brilliant accessories are handmade in our Parisian workshop. The choice of an accessory is made according to your wedding outfit. For women who have a dress or an elaborate outfit, discreet head jewelry will be perfect to accessorize the outfit. On the other hand, for brides with a relatively simple outfit, a fancy and sophisticated flower crown will be the perfect complement to your outfit.

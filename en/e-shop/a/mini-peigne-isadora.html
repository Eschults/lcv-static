Isadora mini comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
48€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Ode to poetry and romance with this mini Isadora comb made of real flowers and an eternal “princess” rose. A discreet touch to slip into your hair or your wedding hairstyle. We recommend this “mini” format for the bridal party, if you want matching looks for even more beautiful memories.

  • Composition : ruscus, pink hydrangea, pale pink gypsophila, stabilized fern and princess pink, gold metal comb
  • Size: 4 cm
  • Shelf life: eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

The Amandine mini hair comb is a refined wedding flower comb. This flowery hair accessory will be perfect on all types of hairstyles with its small white and pink flowers it will sublimate your bridal hairstyle with elegance.

On short or long hair, on loose hair or on tied hair, this head jewel can be worn on all wedding hairstyles. For a bohemian, country wedding, a pretty low bun with a floral hair accessory is ideal. As well as showing off your wedding dress, a bun accessorised with a mini Amandine comb will be perfect. If you’re leaning more towards a chic, bohemian formal hairstyle, you can tie your hair into a loose braid or a half ponytail with waves. Add a piece of jewellery and your hair will be both sophisticated and glamorous. For the final romantic touch, if you’re not wearing fringes, you can curl a few strands and let them gently frame your face.Our Amandine mini flower comb comes in several other wedding jewels: a double flower comb, a bracelet and a boutonniere. These flowery accessories can sublimate the outfits of your bridesmaids for example. The bracelet can be worn by your bridesmaids to tie their buns or ponytails with some flowers. The future groom and his witnesses of honour can stitch the lapel of their suit with Amandine buttonholes. These fashion accessories will harmonise your procession and will be a nice reminder of the colours of your hair accessory.

This delicate floral comb was handmade by our little fairies in our Parisian workshop in the Marais district. Made of stabilized flowers, this hair jewel will be an eternal souvenir of the most beautiful day of your life. To do this, you must avoid the sun, heat and humidity to prolong the life of your mini comb.To discover the rest of our Dance with me wedding jewellery and accessories, we invite you to browse our e-shop. Flower crowns, real flower tiaras, earrings and bracelets, combs and barrettes set with fine stones and coloured crystals, bun picks decorated with flowers and small pearly pearls, hats and veils… You will find your happiness for head jewellery and flower accessories to perfect your hair and make you the prettiest of brides

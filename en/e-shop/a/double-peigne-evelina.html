Evelina double comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
145€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

This double comb expresses the enchanting fusion between flowers and art through its sublime composition. It forms a true lush bouquet with its pastel pink tones and delicate pearls. An exceptional piece to slide into your bridal bun for a nymph-like hairstyle.

  • Composition : Stabilized pale pink and golden hydrangeas, stabilized roses and gardenias, pearls.
  • Size : 13 cm
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Discover the Evelina double comb, the hair accessory from our new Renaissance collection. Created with stabilized hydrangeas in two shades of pink: pale pink and golden pink, stabilized gardenias, stabilized roses and white pearls, this hair comb is much more than just a pleasing accessory to use, it’s a work of art.

The Evelina is meticulously crafted by our team of artisans to sublimate your hairstyle, adding a soft elegance to your hair. The delicate petals of stabilized flowers add natural shine, while freshwater pearls add timeless sophistication to your style. The combination of pale pink and golden pink creates a captivating aesthetic, perfect for all hair types - thick hair, fine hair, curly hair, short hair, long hair, curly hair and more. The comb’s teeth are ideal for detangling hair and adding a touch of glamour to your daily styling routine.

This unique wide-tooth comb is a blend of timelessness and class. Its stainless steel structure ensures a comfortable grip, stimulating your intrinsic femininity and styling your hair type to perfection. Our fine comb is equally suited to a variety of hairstyles, whether for a sophisticated chignon, elegant curls, or simply to tame a few rebellious strands. Its elegant allure makes it one of the most wonderful hair accessories for any occasion.

For smooth, strong hair and perfect application, don’t hesitate to use hair care to promote blood circulation, moisturize and add volume at the roots! You can also straighten, curl or simply brush your hair for a more sophisticated look. If you’re interested in other creations from our jewelry house, such as flower combs, gem-studded barrettes and stainless steel curls, find them on our official website or in one of our boutiques !

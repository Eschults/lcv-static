Lorelei earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
110€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

This delicate cascade of flowers, a wink to our Elsa earrings, will offer an elegant and refined headpiece. The association of lilac hydrangeas with delicate pink and purple transparent pearls will enchant you. We advise you to wear your earrings by raising a few strands of hair or to tie them up completely to free your neck and thus highlight the earrings.

  • Composition: stabilised hydrangea flowers, gold-plated chain, pink and purple glass beads and freshwater pearls
  • Size: 9 cm
  • Shelf life: the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions: keep away from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Les Couronnes de Victoire is launching its brand new Purple Rain collection. Purple has become a very trendy color in the last few years and that’s why we wanted to create a collection only composed of pieces declined in different shades of purple. With a bohemian and Parisian inspiration, Purple Rain is the refined collection that brings a touch of femininity to your outfits. Let yourself be tempted by new and original dangling earrings! The Lorelei pair of pearl earrings is a latest addition totally inspired by our Elsa earrings. This jewel is a cascade of poetry that comes to adorn your ears with flowers and pearls with delicacy. These earrings are a pair of 9cm dangling earrings adorned with stabilized hydrangea flower petals as well as pink and purple glass beads and freshwater pearls. The petals and pearls are mounted on a gold-plated chain. This pair of earrings is so precious it could be worn by a fairy. This cascade of flowers and poetic pearls softens your face by adding whimsy.

How to wear the Lorelei dangling earrings? This accessory is so delicate and graceful that it can be worn for all occasions: weddings (even your own!), ceremonies, events, in your daily life… Depending on the occasion for which you want to wear the Lorelei bohemian earrings, your outfit will be different! To wear this romantic and glamorous pair of earrings in the evening, we advise you to wear a simple dress in light tones (white, ecru, pale pink, pastel yellow) and to make a ponytail to highlight your new original and sophisticated earrings decorated with pearls and flowers. For everyday life, these earrings can be matched with a very simple white total look. So, they will be the essential accessory that changes everything! Match your Lorelei earrings with a nice thin gold metal barrette or a nice thin gold necklace. If your ears are not pierced but you love the Lorelei bohemian earrings, we can transform the clasp of these sleeper earrings into a clip-on clasp. At les Couronnes de Victoire, we don’t just make women’s ear jewelry. We have a vast collection of jewelry for women to make you shine in all occasions: flower crowns, tiaras, bracelets, headbands, earrings, berets, crystal barrettes… This jewelry can also be a beautiful birthday gift sophisticated and timeless. All our refined flower pieces are handmade by our little fairies in the Marais.

Beaubourg clips

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
118€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Like a waterfall of pearls adorning your ears, Beaubourg Clip-on earrings with their retro charm gives a sophisticated and elegant note to all your outfits. These fancy earrings are assembled on a clip-on structure.

  • Composition: Gold plated clip of 24 carats 3 microns, freshwater pearls
  • Size: 7 cm
  • Shelf life: These earrings are eternal.
  • Storage Instructions: To keep your jewel sparkling, we advise you to avoid contact with seawater, alcohol, perfume, creams and cosmetics. We invite you to clean your jewel if needed with a soft cloth or brush and clear water.
  • Delivery time: 5 working days from the date of order

A central and emblematic place in Paris, the Beaubourg centre, also known as the Centre Pompidou, is full of modern and contemporary art. It’s after this cultural place with the atypical architecture of Renzo Piano that we named our new pair of clip-on earrings. The gold plating of this earring gives it a retro touch, that will brighten up all of your outfits.

This fall, stud earrings are out and drop earrings are in ! This is why for our new collection we have imagined different crystal earrings and freshwater pearl pendants to sparkle your fall outfits !

These original earrings are composed of a yellow gold 24-karat 3 microns setting and small white pearls. They are mounted on clips for women who would not have pierced ears. This gold-plated jewel will dress and complete any chic bohemian outfits in your wardrobe. These fashion earrings being pendants, they will look dazzling worn with your hair up in a bun or a ponytail for instance !

This pretty pair of dangling earrings is a novelty that belongs to our new collection of jewellery for women Un Coeur à Prendre, inspired by the latest trends of Paris and the elegance of parisian women. Necklaces, bracelet, hoops, heart-shaped earrings… This collection is composed of many jewels and accessories for women that can be worn on all occasions : with your everyday outfit, at a party, as a bridesmaid at a wedding….

The freshwater pearls of these clip on earrings will ornate and sublimate your hair, for a glamorous and sophisticated look. Our gold earrings can be matched with other jewels from the new collection, such as a gold necklace encrusted with Swarovski elements or our gemstone cuff, to dress your neckline or wrist and complete your outfit with a precious and dazzling ornament.

You can also definitely wear these beaded dangle earrings as a bride ! They will give a boho touch to your bridal look. And if you wish for your bridesmaids to wear glamour jewellery as well, you can perfectly get them a glittering clear crystal hair clip or a pair of large hoop earrings adorned with beads to wear.

Like all our accessories, these trendy pearl vintage earrings for women were handmade by the little fairies of our Parisian workshop. These precious gold earrings adorned with tassels will make a nice birthday gift that will surely please.

To discover the rest of our jewelry collection composed of gold earrings, you can visit our pretty online shop. Elegant flower crowns and tiaras, coloured crystal pendant necklaces, gold hoop earrings, barrettes set with semi-precious gemstones and swarovski crystals, bracelets adorned with precious cultured pearls… Let yourself be tempted to fill your jewelry box with marvels that are both refined and feminine.

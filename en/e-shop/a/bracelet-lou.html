Lou Wrist Corsage

Wrist corsage Eternal Preserved flowers
22€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

This delicate wrist corsage covered with preserved white hydrangea petals and white gypsophila will be perfect to adorn the wrists of the little girls in your wedding procession. With its supple structure, this wrist corsage will fit easily on every little girl’s wrist and can also be worn in the hair to adorn a bun or ponytail.

  • Composition: white hydrangea and white gypsophila preserved, wire structure covered with brown ribbon
  • Size: 5cm
  • Shelf-life: the flowers in this wrist corsage are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Les Couronnes de Victoire are launching a new and wide range of baby girl and child girl accessories so that the mother can match the accessories of her bridal hairstyle with those of her little girl or little girls of honour. We offer you a whole selection of hair accessories, from the flower crown to the hair clip and the tiara. We also have wrist corsage to dress little girls’ wrists.

Our Lou Flower Fantasy wrist corsage is a must-have accessory made of white little flowers. It is perfect to tie on the wrist for a bohemian wedding but it can also be tied as a hair accessory, like a scrunchy. It can be used to style hair in a bun, ponytail or braid. In this way, you can use it to discipline rebellious little locks by making a pretty hairstyle. Buns are very chic and glamorous hairstyles for girls at weddings, christenings and communions. Its supple structure allows it to be used as a head jewel in the hair and to discipline a mane.

In this Lou accessory set, you will also find a pretty flower crown for a girl that is tied with a satin ribbon and a hair clip. We also have other wrist corsages in different colours such as the Alix, Elisa or Lena.

These flowery and original fashion accessories will enhance the outfits for a spring-summer wedding and give you a romantic touch. You can customise them with the colours you want. We also have hair jewellery and other jewellery for women to accessorise your outfits and tie your hair. A hair accessory is what will enhance any type of hair for any occasion. Indeed, head jewellery can be tied up in an evening hairstyle or for everyday use whether you have long hair or short hair.

All the accessories of Les Couronnes de Victoire are made in our Parisian workshops. For women you will find hair clips, combs, crowns, headbands, earrings and other accessories. For more advice on our many accessories you can make an appointment in our boutique. Our sales consultant will guide you in your choices for your event.

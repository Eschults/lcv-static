Rochelle Mini Comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
85€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

With its delicate golden coral branch adorned with pearls and its small preserved gypsophila and hydrangea flowers, the Rochelle mini comb will subtly dress up your hairstyle. Ideal to prick on a half hairstyle, a bun or a bohemian braid.

  • Composition : gold-plated coral branches, freshwater pearls, preserved gypsophila and hydrangea, gold metal comb
  • Size: around 4 cm
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this mini comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

For this winter season 2021, a new collection of wedding accessories under the theme of L’Odyssée will complete your wedding outfit. For this collection we have chosen a glamorous and romantic theme, the theme of the sea, Greece, goddesses and mermaids. Let yourself be captivated by these fashion accessories which will allow you to sublimate your hairstyles for special occasions but also for all those occasions for which you wish to adorn your hair with jewellery and accessories and have the sophisticated look and presence of a mermaid. So whether it is for your wedding or for parties to accessorise an evening hairstyle, this indispensable accessory will embellish any hairstyle.

Our pretty Rochelle mini comb is composed of a delicate golden coral branch adorned with precious white pearls, pretty preserved flowers and a golden metal comb. This wedding accessory is discrete and will allow you to dress your hair in a bohemian and country spirit with golden colour tones. You can combine it with one of our veils and why not be inspired by its floral, white and golden side for art-deco?

Ideal for accessorising your bridal hairstyle and giving it a sophisticated look, this delicate comb will allow you to tie your loose hair into a pretty half ponytail and tame your mane. For women who prefer to tie their hair up to reveal the back of their wedding dress this is also possible. You can put it on a low or normal bun or on a braid to accentuate the bohemian and mermaid side. The bride-to-be who is looking for the greatest harmony for her wedding day will be able to use the Poseidon hairpins set as a styling accessory for her bridesmaids. This discreet hair accessory can be used as a bun pick or in a braid to style their hair. It can also be inserted into flower girls’ braids or buns. This hair jewel lends itself to all lengths of hair, both short and long, and curly and straight hair. All the accessories can be worn on blonde or brown hair and are sure to give you a sophisticated look. For curious girls, you can visit our pretty e-shop to discover all the accessories and hair jewellery for a flowery wedding. You can find flower crowns, combs, clips, hair pins, bracelets, tiaras. The tiara is an ideal accessory to give your hairstyle the look of a goddess.

These accessories are all designed in our Parisian workshops. Our little fairies use their know-how to create jewellery that will make you the most beautiful bride. Our women’s wedding jewellery is made from flowers that have been stabilised. Enough to make your hair accessories or other wedding accessories last forever. Head jewellery is very trendy today and these hair accessories can be used and reused as you wish.

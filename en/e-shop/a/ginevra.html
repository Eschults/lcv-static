Ginevra

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
170€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Introducing the Ginevra Crown for eternal romantics seeking a design with a subtle twist. Its delicate gradient of pink forms a beautiful bouquet reminiscent of the budding spring. Easy to wear, this piece with golden accents is placed at the front of the head, allowing you to experience a princess-like wedding.

  • Composition : Stabilized hydrangea with a gradient of pink and leaves gilded with fine gold.
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Ginevra, a genuine glittering crystal from our new Renaissance collection, is inspired by the opulence of the period and draped in hydrangea petals in three shades of pink, accompanied by fine gold leaves. This flower crown, with its elegant details and stable frame, embodies the ideal bohemian-chic refinement to sublimate your hair and also your wedding dress on the big day.

Delicate shades, from pale pink to eggshell pink and peachy pink, evoke the sophistication of paintings by Onorio Marinari, Dante Gabriel Rossetti and Bartolomeo Veneto, adding a romantic, haute-couture touch to your hairstyle. Ginevra can be worn to harmoniously match any accessory, be it white pearl earrings, clips, small hair clips with sequins, or rhinestone bracelets, for a complete look that reflects your personality.

Whether you opt for a sophisticated chignon, chic braids or loose hair, this crown is designed to perfect any hairstyle. The golden leaves will add a glamorous, understated touch, while the delicacy of hydrangea petals captures the essence of romance. Ginevra transcends the hair accessory to become a work of art that you, the bride-to-be, can wear with pride.

Find the Ginevra crown for timeless elegance that evokes the essence of the Renaissance. Perfect for a country wedding or even any special occasion, this trendy accessory will add the finishing touch to your look. Focus on a pastel color palette and let our head jewel accessorize your bohemian chic style with its floral delicacy and golden accents.

Want to explore more wedding accessories? Come see us at our jewelry house to find a wide range of jewelry and accessories such as combs, flower crowns, crystal-embellished barrettes, headbands, hairpins and more. With Les Couronnes de Victoire, nothing is impossible ;)

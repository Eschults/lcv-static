Gina earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Celebrate love with our limited edition Valentine’s Day capsule created in collaboration with the Moulin Rouge ! The Gina earrings adorned with luminous crystals and stabilized roses declare your passion in a spirited manner. Be the queen of the evening with this precious accessory that will undoubtedly make a lasting impression !

  • Composition : Stabilized roses, gold-plated ear stem adorned with transparent and ruby crystals
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Les Couronnes de Victoire, renowned for their elegant and refined creations, have collaborated with the iconic Moulin Rouge to present a sublime collection in shades of red. Among the eight creations is the Gina earrings, an exceptional piece that blends the daring spirit of the Moulin Rouge with the timeless elegance of the brand. These earrings are a true jewelry masterpiece.

The pair of dangling hoop earrings highlights three distinct roses. A stabilized wild red rose adds a touch of passion, accompanied by a lighter rose known as the princess rose, and a delicate red rose called the button rose. These flowers evoke femininity and delicacy, adding a romantic touch to every outfit. The Gina earrings feature a gold-plated ear wire adorned with transparent crystals and rubies, adding a touch of glamour and sophistication. The sparkling crystals capture the light, while the rubies introduce a note of passion to the ensemble. The quality of the gold plating and the precision of the details ensure exceptional durability.

These fantasy jewelry pieces for women embody elegance with carefully set diamonds, bringing a touch of luxury to each ear. The dangling hoops with delicate motifs make a bold statement of style and refinement. The variety of gemstones combined with the floral colors adds an extra dimension to these exquisite creations. The Gina earrings offer a versatile option for all occasions. Whether for a casual look or an elegant evening, these gold-plated earrings will enhance your femininity with a touch of brilliance and originality.

These earrings can be paired with various bridal accessories for women, such as a crown, a comb (double or mini), a bracelet, a hat, a veil, or even hairpins. Les Couronnes de Victoire offers an endless array of customizable accessories, including earrings such as sleeper earrings, dangling earrings, hoops, studs, and studs with a variety of styles to suit every preference. Gold earrings adorned with zirconium oxides, cultured pearls, or stabilized flowers add timeless sophistication to your ears for events or daily life.

In partnership with the Moulin Rouge, Les Couronnes de Victoire has created a true masterpiece with the Gina earrings. These feminine jewels, crafted with care and attention to detail, by hand, are more than just accessories – they embody the very essence of high-end jewelry. Treat yourself or your loved ones to these unique pieces to celebrate femininity and timeless elegance. The Gina earrings from the collaboration between Les Couronnes de Victoire and the Moulin Rouge are a true statement of style and sophistication.

Nami Boutonniere

Boutonniere Eternal Preserved flowers
22€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our Nami boutonniere with its delicate powder pink preserved flowers is a must have. A bohemian accessory that will be perfect to dress the suit of the groom-to-be and his witnesses.

  • Composition : powder pink preserved hydrangea flowers, preserved gypsophila, oats and marcela
  • Size: around 5 cm
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this boutonniere are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

The Nami boutonniere belongs to the poetic collection of L’Odyssée, created especially for your wedding. Hydrangea, gypsophila, oats and preserved marcela form the small bouquets of the Nami boutonnieres.

This floral arrangement made of preserved powder pink flowers will be ideal to dress the suit of the future groom and his witnesses at your wedding. The freshness of this flowery jewel is transmitted by its natural flowers. Unlike artificial flowers or dried flowers, the plants that make up this wedding decoration are fresh and natural flowers that have become eternal. Our secret ? The preservation of your beautiful wedding flowers by Les Couronnes de Victoire. The speciality of the house floral art. White and pink preserved flowers will no longer be the only ones for your bouquets. The current trend at a flowered wedding is to create continuity between all the flowers that make up your beautiful bridal bouquet and those that dress the future bride and groom. The foliage of these floral creations, makes them original.

Thus, you will be able to match the suit of the future husband to the wedding dress, as well as to the round bouquet that will accompany you to the altar or to those that will be in the vases at the reception venue. Flowers and plants will sublimate everything. A floral arrangement dresses people as well as the places that wear them.

Having the choice, and being able to opt for a personalised way of decorating is always the ideal way to get married in a setting that reflects your image. Depending on your wedding theme (bohemian, country, pastel, purple…) and the place of the ceremony, you can associate these floral creations with the environment as well as with other pieces of the collection. Thus, apart from the newlyweds, the bridesmaids and the witnesses can also have pieces made to complete the elegance of the bride and groom. They will be able to choose flowers from the entire collection, or from others. To do this, you will find other matching pieces in L’Odyssée such as : flower crowns and all other floral creations that are hand-made. A floral creation is more than a simple wedding flower, it is a full part of the preparations as it is thought for those who will wear it. Just like bridal bouquets.

At Les Couronnes de Victoire, we offer more than just a creation, we offer a whole experience around original arrangements made for your flower crown, or your other romantic creations. In our showroom, come and experience your first floral workshop designed and conceived for your wedding. Immerse yourself in a world of beautiful flowers that will not only dress up the bride and groom’s car or the bride and groom’s table. Little-flowers, pink flowers, original bouquets… Everything has been thought out for your rural ceremonies, but not only that. Exotic floral arrangements, personalised compositions … We are waiting for you at 230 Rue Saint-Martin, 75003 Paris. A promise: to bring flowers to the bride-to-be’s dreams and thoughts by inviting her to the language of flowers. Our artisan florists will transform petals, hydrangeas, red roses, white roses, green plants, orchids, foliage, stems or bouquets of flowers into a true work of art… To dazzle all your guests. Between crystal and cocktail, pastel and colourful tones, in the showroom, you will be our guest.

Candice hair clip

Hairpin Eternal Preserved flowers
27€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our Candice hair clip is the perfect accessory between delicacy and bohemian spirit to wear flowers everyday. It takes the traditional shape of a hair clip, composed of hydrangeas and small pink flowers stabilized. This creation was handmade in collaboration with the talented Justine from her workshop where she works with resin in all its forms.

  • Composition : UV resistant resin hair clip, white hydrangea and pink flowers stabilized, silver and pink crystals. Each clip being unique, Justine arranges the flowers with care, which can slightly shift their location compared to the photos on the site
  • Size : 6,5 cm
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions: none
  • Delivery: 5 days from the order

Candy Flowers is our new collection of hair clips. Feminine and romantic, the Candice hair clip will add sparkle to your hair. With its spring colours, the Candice barrette offers a touch of glam to your dream mane. Our resin barrettes made with flowers and glitter perfectly match with floral patterns to enhance your hair with elegance.

The Candice hair clip, decorated with flowers and sequins, is the essential hair accessory for all occasions. Flower hair clips are essential accessories for all women who want to add a touch of femininity to their hair. They are not only a fashion accessory, but also a practical way to fix your hair. The little crocodile or crab clips are perfect for granddaughters and young girls. So let yourself be tempted by a favorite flower clip to create trendy hairstyles.

Our new barrettes are available in a variety of colours and styles and are a perfect choice for weddings, holiday parties, special events or just to add a touch of whimsy to your everyday outfit. This multi-coloured combination will enhance your lengths. Say goodbye to the classic scrunchies, the hair barrettes are true fashion accessories and will adorn your hair with pretty floral barrettes and style your hair for a very pretty touch of glitter and flowers to make you look like a pin up. Hair accessories are perfect for adding the perfect finishing touch to your outfit.

There are many ways to wear a flower barrette. For women with long hair, flower clips can be used to hold back flyaways or to enhance and discipline a bun or ponytail. If you have short hair, hair clips can be used to add a touch of sparkle to your hairstyle or to give a retro feel to your look. Another important factor to consider when choosing a flower barrette is the colour. Flowers in pastel shades such as pink, mauve or light blue are perfect for a romantic and soft look.

Pasadena earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
100€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Inspired by our iconic Salome earrings, this new version is straight out of the colours and sweet life of California. Airy with its glass beads, they are light and will be the colourful bohemian touch to your bridal or guest look.

  • Composition: gold plated chain and structure and stabilised pink gypsophila
  • Size: 1,5 inches
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions : keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order

California Dreaming is our new wedding collection! It is colourful, bright and roses are in the spotlight as well as wild flowers. A perfect blend of California pop and colour with bohemian and 70’s hippie inspirations for a new and more elegant look.

The Pasadena earrings are inspired by our iconic Salome earrings. This new version is adorned with stabilised pink gypsophila and features a gold-plated chain setting. Discreet and sober, they will accompany you in all the occasions of your life whether it is in day or evening. Elegant, original and glamorous, this woman’s earring can be a perfect gift for a birthday present. They will bring a feminine touche, a bohemian touch and fantasy to your looks. These earrings can only be worn by women with pierced ears as they have stems connected to a flower, if we modify the clasps to put a clip, the gold flower would have to be removed.

If you like hoop earrings, original earrings you can look through all our old collections, we are sure you will find the precious pair that will join your vast collection in the jewellery box. Wearing earrings is a great way to finish off an outfit and add femininity. Moreover, you can match our accessories and pairs of earrings with other accessories such as bracelets or crowns for a sophisticated result down to the last detail.

To enable you to find earrings to your ears and your personality; we offer you the possibility to personalize your accessories and jewels, you can thus add or remove elements such as spangles, pearly gems, white pearls, cultured pearls, precious stones, mother-of-pearl, crystals, diamonds, rhinestones, crystal pearls… The colour can also be modified, you can carry out mixtures of multicoloured flowers, put coral, turquoise, gilded, silver plated and other colours. Our accessories for women are original and will accessorize, sublimate and dress all your outfits whatever they are. All you have to do is let yourself be tempted.

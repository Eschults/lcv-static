Florentine

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
195€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Choose the Florentine Crown for your wedding to embody the essence of a sublime nymph. This piece is an invitation to reinvent yourself as a muse and transform into an icon, straight out of a painting, for a day. This wired crown can seamlessly blend into long, flowing, untied hair.

  • Composition : Stabilized golden hydrangeas and gypsophila, pearls, flowers, and leaves gilded with fine gold.
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Does the combination of stabilized hydrangea and gilded gypsophila flowers, freshwater pearls and gold leaves have anything in common? Introducing our Florentine flower crown, from our new Renaissance collection! With its elegant details, this headpiece will become the finishing touch to your look, can be worn to match other fashion accessory pieces and sublimate your bohemian, country wedding.

If you’re looking for a hair accessory that will bring a touch of femininity and delicacy to your hair, Florentine is one of our original floral accessories that can meet your needs. Dried flowers and white pearls are adorned on a frame of fine gold threads. Simple yet sophisticated, this pretty crown will become the perfect wedding accessory if you don’t like high-end head jewelry such as a tiara or headband, but opt for a more discreet haute couture accessory. Its refinement can accessorize your long or short hair, accentuating it with glamour and whimsy.

A flowery hippie wedding, an extravagant ceremony, a cozy cocktail party, or even a darling outing for two, Florentine is one of the most versatile hair accessories, to be worn for all occasions, not just your wedding. This bridal jewel is also perfect for all types of hair, whether it’s a ponytail, a crown braid…, this trendy accessory will add a romantic touch and sublimate your discreetly elegant look.

If you’d like to explore a different selection of accessories, Les Couronnes de Victoire offers jewelry and accessories such as combs, gem-studded barrettes, bun pins, flower crowns, rings, hair clips, earrings and even hair clips. Don’t wait any longer and come and see us to discover a wide range of wedding jewelry, or even find your new style for an original wedding!

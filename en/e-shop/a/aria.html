Aria

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
155€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Fall in love with our nature-inspired bohemian crown made of wild flowers. Its terracotta tones will give you the delicate touch of colour that goes perfectly with your bohemian-chic bridal look. The Aria crown can easily be worn on loose hair with some locks intertwined in the crown or on a pretty wheat braid.

  • Composition: ivory and pink gypsophila, old pink phalaris, powder pink and burgundy hydrangeas, stabilised oats
  • Size: unisize. Can be adjusted with the ribbon on the back
  • Shelf life: the flowers in this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

California Dreaming is our brand new collection for brides-to-be. For those who love 70’s weddings, for those who love the pop and colorful world of California and for fans of the bohemian, hippie and rustic universe, this collection will delight you. Colorful, bright, romantic and glamorous are the key words of this collection where roses and small flowers of the fields are at the honor. Discover the Ava flower crown, which is one of the romantic pieces of this collection! Adorned with delicate stabilized wild roses, it will sublimate your hair no matter what wedding hairstyle you have chosen. The crown is the essential accessory for a bohemian, floral and sophisticated wedding. This hair accessory is delicate and will adorn your hair with elegance, poetry and flowers. Ava is one of the flower crowns that can be adapted to different hairstyles and whether you have long or short hair. In fact, you can wear it with any hairstyle such as the traditional low bun, braids, a sophisticated ponytail, a semi attached. The semi-attached allows you to keep your lengths and not have your hair tied up and you can come and wrap and tie strands of your mane around your crown. This hair accessory will bring a little fantasy and will sublimate and accessorize your wedding dress whether it is in satin, lace, muslin or with tulle. If you don’t know how to wear our crowns, we offer bridal hairstyle tutorials on our social networks or you can come and meet us in our store and make an appointment with one of our consultants to try on our head jewelry and hair accessories. All the accessories of Les Couronnes de Victoire are handmade in our Parisian workshop. We also create veils, veils, tiaras, bracelets, hair clips, combs, hats, crowns of course but also bun picks and earrings. Our wedding accessories are customizable so you can remove or add elements and ornaments such as gold stones, white pearls, mother of pearl, sequins, crystals, rhinestones ect. You can change the colors of some flowers, for example put purple hydrangea, silver, gold, red. All occasions are good to wear a hair accessory: your wedding, a baptism, a birthday, a party.

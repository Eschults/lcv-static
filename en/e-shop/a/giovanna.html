Giovanna

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
180€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Giovanna Crown will captivate you if you appreciate bold and elegant pieces. It perfectly embodies the chic and baroque spirit with a distinctly modern twist for those daring souls who crave originality. With its pastel and powdered tones, it allows for all kinds of eccentricities !

  • Composition : White, blue, powdered pink, and raspberry pink stabilized hydrangeas, stabilized eucalyptus, and stabilized limonium.
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Are you overwhelmed by hundreds of hair accessories and don’t know what to choose? Do you have a bohemian wedding theme and want to know how to create floral creations for the reception venue, centerpieces, bridesmaids and, of course, for you, the bride? Don’t panic, you’ve come to the right place!

Our new Renaissance collection will evoke the nostalgia of years gone by, of an iconic era with architectural beauty, historical events, …, and hence our Giovanna floral crown is a reminder of those times. Made with our signature stabilized hydrangea flowers, with an accumulation of white, blue, powder pink and raspberry pink hues, stabilized eucalyptus and limonium, this handmade hair accessory will sublimate your sophisticated image and harmonize perfectly with your choice of wedding gown.

These seasonal flowers are mounted on a rigid frame, the two toothed elements on each side of the flower crown making it stable. So, while it looks grand, there’s no need to carefully wear it at all times. This original creation will not only brighten up your wedding hairstyle, it will also make a statement and become a star in the eyes of your guests… Don’t wait any longer and get ready with our pretty Giovanna wreath to be the prettiest flower of all during this special occasion…

Looking for wedding accessories that fit your budget? Would you like the cultured pearls that will add a touch of purity to your country wedding, the most romantic and memorable day of your life?

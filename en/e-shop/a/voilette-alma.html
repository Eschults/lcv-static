Alma voilette

Voile Eternal Preserved flowers

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Say goodbye to boring veils and hello to magic with our Alma model. This delightfully vintage design will add an air of mystery to all the brides who dare to wear it. What’s the special touch ? The pearls, hand-sewn one by one, adding a whimsical flair.

  • Composition : White veil made in France, fine gold-plated metal combs, Swarovski crystals, and freshwater pearls.
  • Size : This veil is approximately 20 cm in height.
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions : keep away from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from order date

The veil is a fascinating accessory that adds a touch of glamour and elegance, making it perfect for many occasions, including weddings. The Alma veil pairs beautifully with various dress styles, whether they are made of lace, satin, or chiffon.

For weddings, a veil is often the ideal choice to complement a sophisticated wedding gown. Whether your dress is a stunning ball gown with ruffles or an elegant short dress for a bohemian bride, the veil can elevate your look. Brides of all body types can choose silhouettes that enhance their figure, such as a mermaid gown, an A-line dress, or a princess-style gown. Coordinate your veil with elements of your attire, like a delicate bustier or a romantic neckline, for a chic and harmonious look.

For Formal Events and Evening Receptions The veil is also a refined accessory for formal ceremonies and receptions. Evening gowns, especially those in velvet or adorned with pearls, can be enhanced by a delicate veil. Brides may opt for long dresses with exquisite embroidery or floral motifs, adding a touch of femininity to their ensemble. Whether bicolor, printed, or lace, the dress should harmonize with the veil’s style for an elegant overall look.

For a civil wedding, our Alma veil is a perfect addition, especially if you’re wearing a sexy cocktail dress or a strapless gown. Whether you choose a short lace dress or a vintage-inspired gown embellished with sequins or glitter, the veil can be a charming accessory. Pair it with elegant heels or refined sandals to complete your look.

A veil can add a touch of freshness and originality to daytime weddings. Opt for lighter, more airy styles, such as the Pietragalla veil, which pairs beautifully with floral or printed dresses. A beautiful ceremony dress combined with a delicate veil creates a stunning effect.

The veil is a versatile accessory that enhances any outfit, particularly for weddings and special celebrations. Whether you’re a bride, bridesmaid, or wedding guest, it complements a range of classic and contemporary dress styles. Don’t forget to accessorize with key elements like a chic clutch, elegant heels, and delicate jewelry to achieve a perfectly polished look. Ultimately, choosing a dress that makes you feel beautiful and confident is essential—whether it’s a satin wedding gown, a sleek white dress for brides, or an elegant black dress for evening events. Never underestimate the power of a veil to perfectly complete your stunning ensemble !

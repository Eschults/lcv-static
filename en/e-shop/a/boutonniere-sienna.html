Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Sienna boutonniere will charm you with its enchanting shades. With its pale rose and delicate hydrangeas, it will perfectly adorn the groom’s suit as well as all your guests ! - Composition : Stabilized pale pink and antique pink hydrangeas, stabilized gypsophila, stabilized rose - Size : One size - Shelf life : eternal - Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity - Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Discover our Sienna boutonniere from the new Renaissance collection, a true work of art among seasonal flowers. Its pastel pink hues, purity and freshness are a perfect match for wedding decorations in general: floral creations such as the bridal bouquet, the groom’s boutonniere, interior decorations such as the centerpiece, the reception venue, the wedding hall, and so on. Sienna is a little accessory for your jacket that will shine like sparkling diamonds! Don’t wait any longer to sublimate your image and your romantic wedding with this floral arrangement.

Composed of stabilized hydrangea petals in two shades of pink: pale pink and antique pink, and stabilized gypsophila flowers, Sienna boutonnieres are perfect for your country wedding! For a perfect wedding and a successful ceremony, we want to enhance your wedding theme with the true language of flowers. Whatever floral arrangements you choose, green plants or beautiful flowers such as dahlias, lilies, white roses, tulips, hibiscus, whatever your choice between dried flowers, stabilized flowers, faux flowers, fresh flowers, it’s advisable to know how to pick and choose flowers so that they harmonize well and are compatible.

With the arrangement of our Sienna bracelet from the Renaissance collection, the theme proposed for this special occasion will be vintage-retro, with floral decorations inspired by beauty at the time. Don’t worry if this isn’t your favorite theme, it’s designed to blend well with other floral arrangements! Opting for a modern, extravagant wedding? Try playing with different color palettes. Prefer a bohemian wedding? Use pastels as your main colors. And of course, don’t forget to communicate a dress code so your guests know what to wear! In conclusion, mastering floral art is one of the solutions we recommend for creating a dream wedding ceremony!

If you’d like more information and advice, please don’t hesitate to visit us!

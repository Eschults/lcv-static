Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our crystal barrette is making a comeback, succumb to the new pink swarovski to add sparkle to any hairstyle. This real head jewel will give you an elegant look while taming your locks. An accessory that will make you see life in pink.

  • Composition: pink transparent Swarovski crystals, 24 carats 3 microns gold plated base
  • Size: about 6cm
  • Shelf life: the crystals that make up that hair clip are eternal
  • Preservation advice: in order for your jewel to keep its brilliance, we advise you to avoid any contact with sea water, alcohol, perfume, creams and cosmetic products. We invite you to clean your jewel if necessary with a soft cloth or brush and clear water
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Want to find the perfect women’s head jewelry for any occasion? The La vie en Rose collection is made to seduce you with its colors, bewitching your days. Discover among a large collection of hair and costume accessories to brighten up your hair or your outfit with a pretty lace blouse for example. The Jaipur barrette, a real head jewel, is made of a brass structure with gold-plated metal - 3 microns. Adorned with fine stones - pink swarovski crystals, you will be able to opt for all hairstyles on long hair or any type of hair for a resolutely romantic effect. This high quality jewel barrette allows you to wear it as a real colored must have in the category “hair accessory”. So, how to wear this ornamental barrette? The hair accessory for spring-summer or winter time is the Jaipur hair clip. For everyday wear, go for a minimalist hairstyle, trendy hairstyle like strict braids, high ponytail hairstyle. For an even more sophisticated touch how to style? You can pull your hair with your hairbrush (a must-have accessory) to wear a pinned-up bun. You will have a hairstyle without any strands, a trendy hairstyle with a hair accessory to adorn your hairstyle and give it an effect both discreet and sparkling.

Also opt for low bohemian buns for a wedding hairstyle with our wonderful Jaipur barrette. You can ask your hairdresser to create a beautiful hairstyle for the holidays or your party. The Jaipur hair clip can be worn just like other hair accessories for a special occasion or not. For brides, if you want a wedding hairstyle with your pink hair clip accessory, it is possible! You can choose any type of braid (bohemian braid, wheat braid, side braid etc. …) leaving some rebellious strands or hide any strand with the help of hair pick and elastics. If you have fine hair, our hair clips adhere to all types of hair: thin or thick, straight or curly, blonde or brown!

All the accessories of the Crowns of Victory, the collection La vie en Rose or other jewelry for hair are to be found in our Parisian store or on our website.

Giuseppina

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
175€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Succumb to the light with our tiara crown made of white stabilized flowers and natural freshwater pearls. Like a ballerina, the Giuseppina crown will give you the confidence to shine on your big day.

  • Composition: preserved white ruscus, freshwater pearls and glass pearls, golden metal structure
  • Size: one size
  • Shelf life: eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Inspired by the dancer with the same name, the Giuseppina crown belongs to the Dance with me collection. This new collection brings a new style and a new form of elegance, always with a floral composition from stabilized flowers. The tiara crowns of the Dance with me collection are hair accessories, thought to sublimate the hairstyles of the bride. These floral creations, inspired by dance, poetry and the elegance of the greatest ballets, are perfect for a chic wedding or a country ceremony. This new collection offers precious ornaments highlighting the floral art.

The Giuseppina Crown is made of white ruscus stabilized with freshwater pearls and glass beads on a gold metal frame. The tiara shape allows you to accessorize your hairstyle, whether you choose a ponytail, a bun or any other hairstyle, so this hair accessory would be perfectly suited for a country or romantic wedding. If you don’t have any ideas for a wedding hairstyle yet and would like to get inspired, we invite you to visit our instagram where you will find wedding hairstyle inspirations (for loose hair, tied up hair, long hair, short hair, braided hair…).

Come and discover our poetic universe through our different collections. You will find different hair accessories and head jewels: tiaras, flower crowns, double combs, bun picks set with petals or small flowers, flowered buttonholes, ribbon bracelets, hats, headbands, veils, etc. We also offer jewelry and hair accessories for all occasions: colorful and original earrings, ear clips, hair elastics, barrettes set with Swarovski rhinestones and necklaces. Our Victory Wreaths ornaments are created from real natural flowers and not artificial ones. We work exclusively with flowers through an organic and 100% natural process, to make the flowers last forever and sublimate women and brides-to-be. It is also possible to make your own flower ornament or wreath among all the flowers and colors we offer.

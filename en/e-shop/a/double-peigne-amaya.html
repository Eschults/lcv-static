Amaya double comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
148€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Amaya double comb will be your best ally for your big day. Made of delicate field flowers stabilised in terracotta tones, it will subtly bring colour and elegance to your hairstyle. You can wear it on a bun or on a half-tail for a simple bohemian-chic look.

  • Composition: ruscus, pink and white gypsophila, powdered pink hydrangea, phalaris, nigella, wild peach roses, stabilised lagurus
  • Size: 7 inches
  • Shelf life: the flowers in this double comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

California Dreaming is finally here! Our new collection of jewelry and accessories for special occasions (or everyday life if you feel like it) will bring you on a journey. This collection is inspired by the bohemian, hippie chic weddings of the 70’s and the pop and colorful Californian universe. The colors of these new accessories are soft and warm. The Amaya double comb is composed of a cameo of pink and orange tones for an infinite softness. This hair accessory can be described as rural, flowery and romantic in view of its floral composition: hydrangea, ruscus, gypsophila and other tiny stabilized field flowers. The advantage of the double combs lies in their manageability. Thus it adapts very easily to any type of hairstyle : a low bun, a ponytail, a braid, a semi attached or a side hair. Whatever your choice of hairstyle, Amaya will adapt and bring a sophisticated, country touch to your mane and your wedding dress (whether it is in sequins, satin, lace, muslin or with tulle). Amaya is sure to bring the final touch to your bridal outfit and will impress your partner and guests for sure. Les Couronnes de Victoire is a house that handcrafts hair accessories and head jewelry for brides-to-be but also for all occasions in life that need to be celebrated. In addition to double combs we create flower crowns, tiaras, headbands, hair clips, hairpins for buns, veils, headbands, earrings, bracelets and boutonnieres for the bride and groom. For a bohemian wedding, you will find the jewel that will adorn, perfect, sublimate, accessorize and dress up your wedding hairstyle. Our fashion accessories are customizable so that each person can find their happiness and have the head jewel of their dreams. You can change the colors of some flowers, put purple, ecru, brown, pink gold, ivory, gold, silver and even make a multicolored accessory for your wedding. You can also add some ornaments such as small pearls, mother of pearl, crystals, pearls, rhinestones, sequins, pearly beads, fine stones, white beads … Come and meet us at 230 rue Saint Martin, 75003, Paris to create together the perfect accessory.

Constance necklace

Necklace Eternal Preserved flowers
80€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Constance necklace is a back jewelry piece that will be perfect for brides seeking a delicate and contemporary accessory. This two-strand jewel gracefully slides to the back of your dress to enhance a plunging neckline.

  • Composition : Fine gold-plated chain. Freshwater pearls.
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions : keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order

Looking for necklaces for your wedding or engagement? Now is the time to fall for our Constance necklace to enhance your neckline ! Our latest addition, with its delicate chain, will suit you perfectly! This pearl necklace will be perfect for brides seeking elegance. Back jewelry is a real trendy accessory to wear on your big day. Today, there are all kinds: long necklaces, with small pearls, coral-colored, lapis lazuli, bangles, rose gold, oxides, quartz, with or without a lobster clasp, minimalist, romantic…

To make sure you don’t make a mistake, it’s best to choose a timeless, adjustable, and stainless model that you can wear after your wedding! Our Constance necklace fits all body types and can be worn in summer or winter, on a neckline or a turtleneck. Its delicate pearls are chic and refined, bringing the elegant touch we love so much.

Our women’s necklace falls between a long necklace and a choker and can be adjusted to your liking (you can wear it at the front even though this model was designed to delicately enhance beautiful backs). Discover all our necklaces and long necklaces in the necklace category and fall for our fine jewelry models for women of all ages and styles! We also have two choker models for those who want an easier-to-wear and feminine piece, namely our Aline and Léonore models.

Prefer a hair accessory ? A pretty ribbon, for example? Ruby, garnet, purple, orange, yellow: our crowns, combs, and hair clips come in all colors to satisfy all your desires. Our jewelry and accessories are designed to last. Want a bracelet, earrings, brooches, hoops, a chic set, or a bohemian piece ? We have what you need! All our costume jewelry is designed for beautiful ceremonies! Find our gems in our two boutiques in Paris and Bordeaux as well as on our website. See you soon

Adelina hair clip

Hairpin Eternal Preserved flowers
95€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our hair clip will follow you everywhere you go… Discover our new Adelina hair clip, its delicate pastel blue colour will allow it to fit easily into your hair for a refined and sparkling effect. Wear it on a half-tail or with a few strands at the back.

  • Composition: pale blue swarovski crystals, 24 carats 3 microns gold plated base
  • Size: 5.5 cm
  • Shelf life: the crystals that make up this clip are eternal
  • Conservation advice: to keep your jewel sparkling, we advise you to avoid contact with seawater, alcohol, perfume, creams and cosmetics. We invite you to clean your jewel if needed with a soft cloth or brush and clear water
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

The new collection entitled “Purple Rain” is composed of a selection of accessories to make you beautiful. As the name suggests, a collection of hair jewelry, a small tooth comb, the famous Lorelei, mini-pins … This time, no flower crowns but a multitude of fashion accessories is offered. You must have seen that in many major Haute-Couture runways, the purple and the barrette was very trendy for the fall. These colorful barrettes are perfect for making your comeback at work like a queen. At les Couronnes de Victoire, we offer the perfect 2-in-1 accessory, with the trends of the moment.

The Adelina hair clip will bring a touch of shine to your beautiful hair. It will sublimate and discipline your pretty bohemian hairstyle for all occasions, whether your hair is blonde or brown (even for brunettes). A great way to accessorize your romantic outfit for every day. A pretty hair clip is the essential accessory that will give the little extra touch of fantasy. The advantage of the hair clip is that it goes to all hair lengths, long hair or short hair and to any thickness, thick hair as fine hair. With a fringe, retro look guaranteed! Equally good in spring-summer and fall-winter. For the end of the year parties or parties with friends, it will also be perfect, slipped into a nice blow dry! For a bridesmaid for a wedding, it can be very well suited. You can choose between our Violette headband or a beautiful Swaroski rhinestone barrette! The choice is yours! It is made of pale blue Swarovski crystals on a 24 carat gold plated 3 microns structure.

Very simple to slip into your hair, it will be glamorous to the highest point. No need to style your hair afterwards and make “sophisticated” hairstyles like a braid, a ponytail or a bun and have hair elastics and hair clips at your disposal. Simply take your two front strands and pull them into a half-tail. Or, you can tie your hair in a low ponytail and put the Adelina hair clip instead of your scrunchie.

All accessories are fully customizable and handmade with lots of love. Put some glitters in your life with these beautiful crystals! So visit our boutique in the 3rd arrondissement of Paris to receive personalized advice on how to wear your floral accessories or you can watch a hairstyle tutorial on our networks to get inspired! This flowery accessory will adorn your precious hairstyles with the help of these numerous hair jewelry for women.

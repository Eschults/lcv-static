Dare to be original with our Venus wrist corsage ! With its pastel hues, it adds color and infinite poetry to your ceremonies. The Venus bracelet, a precious gem from the latest collection by Les Couronnes de Victoire, the Renaissance, embodies the very essence of elegance. Comprising stabilized hydrangeas in enchanting shades of blue, pale pink, and antique rose, complemented by stabilized ivy, this captivating piece of jewelry is a true ode to floral beauty.

This refined bracelet, with its adjustable satin link, celebrates the delicacy of eternal petals intertwining with infinite grace. Every detail speaks of the craftsmanship and meticulousness deployed in its creation. Adorning your wrist, this adjustable bracelet, with its turquoise and pink accents, captivates with its brilliance and finely crafted design. Each color, each detail, is meant to enhance the wrist and illuminate your style. Gifting this piece would be an ideal gesture, a timeless token of affection. Whether for the delicate wrists of women or the distinguished elegance of young girls, this lovely bracelet becomes a symbol of refinement and glamour.

In the Renaissance collection, many antique and vintage colors have been used, as is the case with the Venus set. Indeed, you’ll find a crown, a mini comb, earrings, a boutonniere, and finally the bracelet. Each of these accessories shines with its own aura and entices with its unique allure as this collection invites you to shine on every occasion. Thus, the Venus bracelet stands as a timeless jewel, a perfect blend of contemporary elegance and timeless charm, an exceptional craft legacy transcending trends to illuminate every moment of your life.

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a brand of customizable jewelry for the romantic bride and her entourage (we aim for the perfect alloy for your fantasy jewelry!) but can be worn for all occasions. The color is customizable for your order: turquoise, silver, gold, yellow, emerald, black and white… You’ll have the adorned wedding jewel (with flowers, rhinestones, pearls, or crystals!) perfectly matching your precious personality and cherished endlessly. You can create dream sets with our original personalized jewelry for your wedding entourage. For big events, celebrations, communions, weddings, baptisms, or any special occasions, we offer a wide range of delicate, fancy, original, and colorful accessories. From bracelets to boutonnieres, earrings, bouquets, and crowns, we offer sets to adorn your guests at your secular or religious ceremony. We aim to create head jewelry tailored to your image for the big day.

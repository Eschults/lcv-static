Liza earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
195€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Celebrate love with our limited edition Valentine’s Day capsule created in collaboration with the Moulin Rouge ! The Liza earrings promise to capture the heart of your loved one with their wild roses. An ode to femininity par excellence, this piece can be worn for all your special occasions.

  • Composition: Stabilized wild roses, gold-plated ear stem adorned with transparent and ruby crystals
  • Size : 6 cm
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

Les Couronnes de Victoire, in an exclusive partnership with the prestigious Moulin Rouge, proudly present their latest creation: the Liza earrings. These exquisite jewels embody the perfect alliance between the glamour of the Moulin Rouge and the exceptional craftsmanship of Les Couronnes de Victoire. The Liza earrings stand out with their unique design, blending the boldness of hoops and the timeless elegance of dangly earrings. Each pair is composed of three stabilized wild roses in a vibrant red, symbolizing the passion and energy of the Moulin Rouge. Delicate petals are accentuated by a gold-plated ear post, adorned with transparent crystals and rubies, adding a touch of luxury to these exquisite creations.

The Liza earrings captivate with the diversity of their high-quality materials. The gold-plated ear post provides a warm and opulent glow, while transparent crystals and rubies bring unparalleled brilliance. These earrings are a true work of art, evoking the refinement of yellow gold jewelry and the sophistication of carats. Each earring is meticulously set with zirconium oxides, reminiscent of the sparkle of diamonds. This perfect combination of zirconium and rubies creates a captivating play of light, adding a touch of glamour and fantasy. These earrings symbolize dazzling and precious femininity.

Whether you prefer rose gold, white gold, or classic gold, the Liza earrings offer exceptional versatility. They can be worn for any occasion, whether it’s an evening at the Moulin Rouge, a glamorous night out with friends, or a special day. The Liza earrings from Les Couronnes de Victoire in collaboration with the Moulin Rouge are more than just an accessory. They are a statement of bold style, a celebration of timeless elegance, and a manifestation of radiant femininity. Treat yourself to this unique piece that will enhance your beauty and add a touch of glamour to every moment of your life. The Liza earrings are more than just a pair of earrings; they are exceptional jewels to cherish for years to come.

Les Couronnes de Victoire handcrafts your wedding accessories, event accessories, and everyday accessories using stabilized flowers. Whether you’re looking for sleeper earrings, ear studs, or earrings with rhinestones and glitter, decorated with pearls, multicolored, geometric, asymmetrical, perforated, or matching… whether for a birthday gift or to treat yourself, you’re in the right place—choose according to your personality!

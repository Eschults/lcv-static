Cosmo hair clip

Hairpin Eternal Preserved flowers
70€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

This pretty hair clip with purple crystals will be the perfect accessory to wear every day. A basic to have in your jewelry box. Its “deep purple” color will give a touch of character to your look. Feel free to combine this hair clip with other crystal hair clip for more fantasy.

  • Composition: amethyst swarovski crystals, 24 carats 3 microns gold plated base
  • Size: 6 cm
  • Shelf life: the crystals that make up this barrette are eternal
  • Conservation advice: to keep your jewel sparkling, we advise you to avoid contact with seawater, alcohol, perfume, creams and cosmetics. We invite you to clean your jewel if needed with a soft cloth or brush and clear water
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Les Couronnes de Victoire is releasing its new ready-to-wear collection named Purple Rain. All the new creations in this collection are variations of the color purple: parma, lilac, lavender, deep purple… Purple Rain offers a multitude of pair of earrings, hair clips, headbands and berets for women. Let yourself be enchanted by our floral and rhinestone accessories for women!

The hair clip has become the must-have accessory for women in recent years. Its strong point is that it suits everyone and comes in many shapes and shades to sublimate all women’s hair! It is suitable for women with short hair and long hair. The hair clip can be worn during the day as well as in the evening (even for Christmas and NYE parties)! It’s your feminine and discreet finishing touch.

The Cosmo hair clip is a hair clip with a 3 micron 24 carat gold plated structure enhanced with 5 oval Swarosvki crystals in a deep purple color. This color is perfect for fall and winter and will complement your fall and winter wardrobe. It is a very feminine and fancy hair accessory.

The extra fantasy is the mix and match: you can wear several head jewels for hair at the same time! You just have to know how to match them. For example, you can choose a deep purple hair clip with crystals like the Cosmo hair clip and match it with a thinner gold butterfly hair clip like the Faye hair jewelry clip. You can also pair it with a crystal hair accessory from the Purple Rain collection or our barrette capsule like the Juliette green hair barrette. We have many types of barrettes to accessorize you: rhinestone barrettes, sequins, precious pearls, crystals, flowers… We also have many colors: pink, green, blue, yellow, purple…

Tutorial on how to wear the hair clip: several solutions are offered to you and it will depend on your clothing style. You have a rather preppy and girly style? To be girly, no need for a tiara, satin or ribbons. A rhinestone hair clip is enough! - The half-tail: take two strands of hair from the lengths on either side of your face and pull them back into a half-ponytail. Place your hair clip on the junction of the two strands and voilà! You’ve got a trendy hairstyle with a cute barrette. - The low ponytail without hair elastic: For a very elegant and sophisticated style, inspired by Blair Waldorf’s style, make a low ponytail and use your hair accessory instead of a hair elastic. It’s a very haute-couture and runway spirit. More rock’n’roll and glamorous? Choose the most colorful hair accessories and pair them together to decorate your hair! You can also combine a rhinestone hair clip with a more discreet gold hair clip. These accessories will add personality to your look. Here’s our hairstyle idea: - The side-hair: you can curl your hair or have it straight. After parting your hair low on the side, pull it back to the opposite shoulder. The trick: place it behind your ear and hold it in place with the Cosmo barrette. Very rock’n’roll and groovy, very Purple Rain!

Lookwise, the Cosmo hair clip and its deep purple color will go great with an off-white outfit. Take a pair of jeans and a white blouse and add the Cosmo hair clip in your hair: you are ready for a beautiful day accessorized in LCV.

At les Couronnes de Victoire, we ensure that everything is handmade. Our makers create your floral and rhinestone headpieces in the heart of Paris so that you have a pretty hairstyle and a romantic and bohemian look. We also make stabilized flower crowns, flower earrings, bracelets, boutonnieres, women’s barrettes, hats, bun picks and headbands.

Malibu earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
132€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Welcome to California with our airy and delicate earrings to wear without moderation. Surf on the wave of love for your most beautiful day with our earrings made of stabilised wild roses and dangling chains, which will delicately dress up your head. These earrings can be mounted on clips for non-pierced ears on request.

  • Composition: gold plated structure and pink pearl chains, wild roses, powder pink fern and stabilised ivory hydrangeas
  • Size: 3 inches
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions : keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order

Our new wedding collection has arrived and it is called California Dreaming. Discover colourful accessories and jewellery, inspired by Californian pop, bohemian and 70’s hippies. Let yourself be tempted by our new Malibu earrings and their wild roses that will bring character to your look. Elegant and feminine, the Malibu earrings are made of gold plated pink beaded chains, wild roses, powder pink fern and stabilised ivory hydrangeas. This women’s earring is set in gold. If you don’t have pierced ears and don’t want to go through the piercing process, we can mount this pair of earrings on clips with ear tag. Original and glamorous, the Malibu could be an ideal gift for a friend’s birthday, the pair is sure to please and will make a lovely present. Why don’t you gift yourself? It is also important to treat yourself as a woman can never have enough earrings in her jewellery box. Malibu will be the perfect addition to your extensive collection and may be your thousandth pair of earrings. Wearing these earrings on a daily basis will bring THE colourful touch to your outfit. If you prefer discreet jewels, small sober earrings, discover the previous collections of Les Couronnes de Victoire so that you can find your happiness. There is something for everyone, with rhinestones and sequins to shine, crystals, a precious cultured pearl, discreet gemstones, a precious stone, adorned with pearls, set with diamonds, mother-of-pearl, black pearls … We practice customization which allows you to add or remove ornaments but also to change the colour of some precious flowers to put coral, silver, turquoise, gold, ruby red, emerald green hydrangeas, pink, two-tone for example. If you prefer to wear hoop earrings rather than dangling earrings, our new collection includes several such as the Melrose, which is mounted on a gold frame and is discreet. Les Couronnes de Victoire offers many other accessories for women such as combs, bracelets, crowns and tiaras made of stabilised flowers to accompany you in the beautiful moments of your lives. Our accessories will sublimate, dress, bring femininity, bohemia and a country side to your outfits. All occasions are good to wear original earrings for women.

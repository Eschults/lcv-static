Introducing the new Hortense L barrette, a 5 cm round hair clip from the Scrunchies is Back collection in collaboration with Les Couronnes de Victoire. This elegant and sophisticated hair accessory will add a touch of glamour to your hairstyle, whether it’s for a special occasion or for an ordinary day when you want to shine bright.

Crafted with high-quality materials, this barrette is adorned with 24-carat gold-plated flowers, with a thickness of 3 microns, giving it an incomparable shine. Its round shape gives it a delicate and feminine appearance, perfect for enhancing all types of hairstyles.

Whether you opt for a sophisticated bun, an elegant ponytail, or delicately pinned-up strands, this barrette will add an elegant touch to your hair. Its refined design makes it the perfect accessory for a wedding, a chic evening out, or simply to accessorize your daily outfit. The sturdy structure of the barrette ensures a perfect hold throughout the day.

Whether you have long, short, fine, or thick hair, this barrette adapts to all hairstyles and hair types. Its versatile design makes it an essential accessory in your hair jewelry collection.

Ideal for accessorizing your bridal hairstyle and giving it a sophisticated look, this delicate barrette allows you to gather your loose hair into a beautiful half ponytail and tame your mane. For women who prefer to tie their hair up to reveal the back of their wedding dress, this is also possible. You can pin it on a low or normal bun or on a braid to accentuate the bohemian side. This hair accessory can be used as bun pins or in a braid to style your hair. It can also be pinned into the braids of little flower girls or into their buns. This hair jewel suits all lengths, from short to long hair, and both curly and straight hair.

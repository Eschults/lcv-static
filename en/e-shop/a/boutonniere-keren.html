Keren boutonniere

Boutonniere Eternal Preserved flowers
22€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Made of preserved roses, ruscus, gypsophila, lagurus and pampas flowers, the Keren boutonniere is a must-have to brighten up the groom’s suite with a delicate golden touch

  • Composition: princess roses, ruscus, gypsophila, lagurus and stabilized pampas flowers
  • Size: around 5 cm
  • Shelf life: the flowers that compose these boutonniere are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Golden Hour is our new capsule collection ! Placed under the sign of the Indian summer and sunny days, this capsule takes soft, warm and nude shades.

You already have your florist to decorate the table runner and centerpieces with seasonal flowers, and you’re already well into the other preparations for your wedding, but have you thought about matching the bride’s accessory to the groom’s ?

The Keren boutonniere is matched with the Keren double comb or the Keren pick set but also with the Keren hairclip. For this special occasion that is your wedding every detail counts and it is in these details that you will reach perfection.

The boutonnieres are in fact small bouquets of stabilized and dried flowers. The stabilized flowers are not artificial flowers but natural flowers that have been injected with glycerin to make them immortal. The Keren boutonniere has the following floral composition : wild roses, hydrangea, gypsophila, ruscus and other small flowers of the fields. For a bohemian wedding with country touches, this boutonniere will be perfect. It’s a relatively imposing boutonniere that should be stitched on the left lapel buttonhole of your jacket to give it a romantic touch.

Choosing flowers for the creation of your boutonniere can be done according to several things : your tastes, the wedding theme, the wedding decoration, the wedding flowers, the bridal bouquet.

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a brand where we speak the language of flowers. Our core target is brides and grooms and we love to help you during your transition from future bride and groom to young bride and groom. In order to create accessories to your image, we practice customization. You can create your own floral arrangements by choosing the flowers that will compose your jewelry and accessories. Be careful however, not all fresh flowers stabilize : sunflowers, ranunculus, lily of the valley, tulips, lilies, amaryllis, chrysanthemums, ivy, hibiscus, dahlias, peonies or orchids do not stabilize. On the other hand, daisies, lavender, red roses, hydrangeas, white roses and other colors, gypsophila, eucalyptus, immortals, ferns and other foliage are stabilizing.

All wedding themes have their accessories: romantic, folk, country, bohemian, natural, and even with a color theme: ivory, turquoise, pastel, red, fuchsia pink, purple etc. We don’t only make boutonnieres, our favorite accessory is the flower crown. We also make combs, earrings, barrettes etc. Our wreaths also have buttonholes, if you customize your wreaths you can have matching personalized buttonholes.

All occasions are good to flower your daily outfits and even your wedding dress. To impress your guests at your wedding, match your bridal party ! Even the bridesmaids can have their accessories. For a wedding filled with purity and freshness, bet on beautiful original flowers, foliage and large compositions in your reception place but also on you.

Adriana Wide Comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
160€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Result of our collaboration with the Belgian fashion group of wedding dresses Marylise & Rembo Styling, our Adriana wide flower comb will be the perfect accessory to adorn a bohemian chignon. With its preserved tiny hydrangea, ruscus and other little white flowers, the large flower comb Adriana plays on the subtle bond of these pretty flowers, pearls and gold plated flowers to create an ideal bohemian and romantic accessory.

  • Composition: Preserved leaves of white ruscus, munni leaves, fern and hydrangea flowers along with nacre, pearls and gold plated comb
  • Size: 13 cm approx
  • Shelf life: The flowers that compose these hairpins are eternal.
  • Storage advice: Protect from direct sun, heat and humidity.
  • Delivery: 5 days from the placement of order.

Derived from the name of the city Adria, located by the sea in Veneto, the name Adriana is full of meaning and history. This is how we named the large comb of our new collection. Adorned with white hydrangea flowers, ruscus leaves, munni and ferns, fresh-water pearls and a few delicate touches of gold leaves, this large white flower comb will be sublime to enhance your bridal hairstyle. You may have recognized it, this novelty was inspired by our great comb Wendy which you liked so much when it came out.

On long hair as on short hair, on tied hair as on loose hair, a hair jewel will always be elegant and will adapt to all wedding hairstyles. Here are some ideas if you lack inspiration on how to tie your hair on your wedding day. If you prefer chic hippie hairstyles, the braid is the best way to tie your hair up. Add a hair sparkling accessory, and your hairstyle will be sophisticated and elegant.

For a minimalist and elegant wedding, a simple half ponytail is the ideal ceremonial hairstyle. You can accessorize it with our white and gold flower Adriana comb. Its discreet petals and fine touches of gold will bring freshness and glamour to your mane. For bohemian and retro brides, the bun is the best way to tie your hair. Low bun, high bun, banana bun, braided bun… There are many ways to style the bun, and all will be dainty with a wedding jewel like our Adriana large comb. This hairstyle will also enhance your wedding gown and allow you to easily attach a wedding veil. To bring a touch of elegance to your updo, you can curl a few strands of your hair for a nice wavy effect.

This graceful bridal hair comb will bring elegance to any wedding dress, a silk or satin one, whether it is ivory or cream white. This white flower and gold leaf adornment will be the perfect piece of hair jewelry for a winter wedding.

All the floral wedding hair accessories from Les Couronnes de Victoire can also ornate the hair of your bridesmaids.

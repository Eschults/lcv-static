Aria mini comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
48€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

With its powdery notes, the Aria mini comb will enchant you with its softness. You will be transported to the Californian beaches in no time and will only have to slip it into your hair to make you be the perfect bohemian bride.

  • Composition: stabilised pink gypsophila and powder pink hydrangea, gold metal comb
  • Size: around 1,5 inches
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this mini comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

California Dreaming is our new collection of jewellery and accessories for life’s big and beautiful occasions. With colourful, pop Californian inspirations mixed with a bohemian and hippie spirit of the 70’s weddings, this trendy collection has been imagined. The Aria mini comb is set with hydrangea and gypsophila in shades of powder pink. It is minimalist and will be perfect for a bohemian bride to bring a romantic touch and a bit of fantasy to her pretty hairstyle. This accessory can be worn on different types of hairstyles such as a bun, ponytail, braid, or with the hair loose in a semi-tie. It will bring the final sophisticated touch to your bridal outfit. For your wedding, dare to add a touch of colour with a sophisticated floral accessory. The mini comb for women is a discreet accessory that will sublimate, adorn, dress, perfect and accessorize your hair. You can wear two or even three to make it more imposing. Aria will bring a touch of country glamour to your bridal hairstyle. Les Couronnes de Victoire is a company that makes hair accessories from stabilised flowers. All accessories are long-lasting because the stabilised flowers never fade. We make a wide range of accessories such as veils, flower crowns, bun picks, hats, tiaras, earrings, veils and barrettes. Our hair accessories are often part of a product range which allows your future husband, your bridesmaids, to have an accessory with the same ornaments as yours. For the Aria mini comb there are for example, the large comb, the bracelet, the buttonhole and the flower crown of the same name and line. We can personalise the accessory so that you can wear the accessory of your dreams to match your wedding dress perfectly. It is therefore possible to change the colour of the flowers but also to add or remove certain ornaments such as pearls and stress, precious little flowers, pearly pearls, fine stones, pearls, sequins, crystals etc. Do not hesitate to make an appointment at our showroom at 230 rue Saint Martin, 75003 Paris to benefit from the advice of our saleswomen.

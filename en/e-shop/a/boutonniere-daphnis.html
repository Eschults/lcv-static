Daphnis boutonniere

Boutonniere Eternal Preserved flowers
29€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Here is the Daphnis boutonniere designed to adorn the clothing of both young and old ! With its composition of flowers blending hydrangea, gypsophila, and eucalyptus, it brings a touch of softness. This accessory was crafted in collaboration with Louise Misha. - Composition : Stabilized mauve hydrangea, stabilized mauve, yellow, and violet gypsophila, stabilized eucalyptus, and dried glixia. - Size : 4 cm - Shelf life : eternal - Storage instructions : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity - Delivery : 5 days from the order date

Daphnis boutonniere by Louise Misha is a delicate floral composition that will bring a romantic and rustic touch to your wedding. Composed of stabilized mauve hydrangeas, mauve, yellow, and purple stabilized gypsophilas, as well as stabilized eucalyptus leaves, this boutonniere is a true miniature bouquet to be worn with elegance.

Designed to be worn by the groom, bridesmaids, or guests, the Daphnis boutonniere will perfectly complement the crown and earrings from the same collection, creating a lovely floral motif throughout your event.

Its original design and pastel colors make it a floral accessory perfectly suited for a romantic and bucolic ambiance. Both discreet and elegant, the Daphnis boutonniere will enhance your outfit and that of your loved ones for this special day.

At Les Couronnes de Victoire, we have found the ideal arrangement to marry seasonality and timelessness. And for that, we work with natural and fresh flowers because, like a florist artisan, we refuse to work with artificial flowers. We love the purity of beautiful flowers, so we preserve them through stabilization, a 100% natural biological process that allows us to maintain the brightness of our accessories and their composition. This floral arrangement used for the making of a flowery accessory allows the flower to be eternal. We use only stabilized flowers and not dried flowers for our floral creations. A true work of craftsmanship for a wedding ceremony, engagement, or any occasion to prepare and celebrate the beautiful news of life.

You can decide to create unique and fully personalized compositions for a wedding to perfectly match your wedding theme. At Les Couronnes de Victoire, everything is handmade, so we can create floral arrangements in jewelry form that represent you, whether for brides or their future spouses. This applies to ceremonies ranging from weddings to anniversaries, including secular ceremonies, from the bride to the bridesmaids, to match the bohemian wedding of your dreams. Feel free to browse our e-shop to find the flower crown or other original floral accessory creations to give a decidedly rustic tone to your outfit.

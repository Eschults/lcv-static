The starry night is the moment when your wildest and deepest dreams can come true. For our new “Starry Night” jewelry collection, we dive into the world of cultured pearls where you can shine brightly with one of our timeless accessories - our glamorous Neven bracelet. This piece of fantasy jewelry, specially designed for winter celebrations, is the perfect embodiment of the season’s elegance and beauty.

This gold-plated bracelet for women is a true work of art. It features delicate chains adorned with blue pearls, perfectly evoking the freshness and splendor of endless winter. The contrast between luminous gold and sapphire pearls creates a remarkable visual harmony.

The minimalist look of the Neven costume bracelet is enhanced by the deep blue of the gemstones, adding a touch of refinement. The pearls, both precious and delicate, merge harmoniously with the chains, creating a cultured pearl of rare beauty.

The Neven chain bracelet embodies elegance, finesse, femininity and timeless beauty. Wear this timeless bracelet to light up your wrist for an elegant evening, a party, or give it as a gift to someone special. Thanks to its discreet clasp and adjustable chain, it will fit perfectly on your wrist for any occasion.

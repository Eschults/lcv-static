Pina wide comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
100€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Discover our romantic accessory, the large Pina comb, inspired the poetry found in the world of dance and fairy tale ballets. Pina is a delicate blend of stabilized white hydrangeas and a sumptuous Gardenia flower to top it all off. A perfect wedding hair accessory to slip into your bridal hair.

  • Composition: ruscus, stabilized pristine white hydrangea and Gardenia, gold metal frame
  • Size: 10 cm
  • Shelf life: eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

The large pina comb is as poetic as its name. From the Dance me Collection, a tribute to the sumptuous beauty of dance mixed with the evanescence of flowers. The Pina large wedding comb is elegance and refinement. This large comb will particularly enhance any bride’s hairstyle. This hair accessory will match perfectly with a beautiful satin or lace wedding dress. The middle of the comb forms a discreet yet sophisticated floral bow, perfect for a chic country wedding! All the accessories in this new collection will add a romantic touch to your wedding outfit. How to wear our hair accessories? You can give your hair a chic inspiration with a braided ponytail, or with a loose bun. Enhance your hairstyle with a pretty floral headpiece and you’re done. You can also let two strands of hair escape from your hairstyle and frame your face for a romantic look. Using the Pina Double Comb is also a sophisticated way to tie your bridal veil and add glamour to your hair. This wedding hair comb will look great with any wedding dress, long or short. Romantic as can be, it will be the essential and noticed element of your outfit.

This large wedding hair comb is made entirely of: ruscus, white hydrangea and gardenia stabilised, mounted on a golden metal structure. The whole thing forms a delightful floral bow that you can accessorise with either a low pulled bun or a half-tail for a more bohemian effect. You can pair it with another bridal accessory, such as earrings, to give your bridal hairstyle extra character. The precious flowers of this hair jewel are a must for styling your bridal mane. Whether it’s short hair, long hair, medium-length hair, curly hair, frizzy hair, tied-up hair or semi-tied hair, the large Pina comb is ideal for enhancing your ceremony hairstyle. A true fashion accessory worthy of the fashion houses, this double comb set with white flowers will perfectly match your wedding hairstyle or the hair of your bridesmaids. In the same range as this wedding comb, you will find other jewels and accessories such as the delicious Margot comb which will be able to decorate the hairstyle of your bridesmaids, while the pretty irresistible bracelets will seduce your bridesmaids. For the final touch to your outfit, it is trendy to match the colours of your head jewellery to the groom with our irresistible buttonholes. The little fairies of the LCV workshop make your precious jewellery in Paris in our workshop in the Marais. At Les Couronnes de Victoire, we offer a new style of floral art for brides and their weddings. Stabilized flowers are the originality of the house and our floral compositions are made with all our love. Authenticity being a value of the house, we also propose for all our creations to be personalized. What to dazzle your guests with a creation that looks like nobody else but you. We propose to our future brides and their friends, perhaps bridesmaids, to come to our showroom for a personalized and unique meeting!

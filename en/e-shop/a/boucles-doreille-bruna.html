Bruna earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
100€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Result of our collaboration with the Belgian fashion group of wedding dresses Marylise & Rembo Styling, our Bruna flower earrings bring an elegant and portic touch to your wedding dress. Set on a gold plated and hammered structure, the Bruna floral earrings unify flowers and gilded leaves along with preserved ruscus and limonium flowers. Our Bruna flower earrings are perfect to accessorize a bohemian dress.

  • Composition : Pearls, hammered and gold plated hoops, gold plated leaves and flowers along with preserved ruscus leaves and limonium flowers
  • Size: 6 cm
  • Shelf life: The flowers that compose these hairpins are eternal.
  • Storage advice: Protect from direct sun, heat and humidity.
  • Delivery: 5 days from the placement of order.

Our elegant Bruna teardrop earrings are composed of a flower and a thin sheet of gold-plated metal, mounted on a hammered gold-plated structure. They are also decorated with a delicate mixture of small stabilized ruscus and limonium flowers that form this pretty oval earring. Finally, a small freshwater pearl is meticulously deposited to bring a poetic and elegant touch. A perfect harmony between the elements makes this yellow gold hoop earring unique. The Bruna white and gold flower earrings are suitable for pierced ears but it is possible to mount them as clip-on on request.

Hymn to romanticism and finesse, these shiny gold hoops are perfect to accessorize a bohemian wedding dress. The golden and white tones blend perfectly and discreetly with a long satin dress. This novelty comes from our collaboration with Rembo Styling and Marylise, a Belgian wedding dress designer that we love. She creates dresses with a refined and bohemian inspiration, thanks to delicate materials such as silk, satin and lace.

Our capsule collection in collaboration with the Belgian brand offers several pieces in a bohemian style, always chic and glamour to sublimate your wedding outfit. Flower crowns, discrete gold-coloured large hoop earrings, gold pendants set with flowers and semi-precious stones, sets of flowering hair picks and combs. These beautiful sets will be perfect to bring light to your wedding outfit and make your face shine on your big day. Mainly composed of pearls and golden and white flowers, these jewels will remain timeless and can be worn at other special events or to dress up a bridesmaid’s outfit. Indeed, these jewels are not only dedicated to brides, all women can obviously enjoy them. If you want your bridesmaids to have assorted jewellery, you can ornate their outfits with a sparkling beaded gold necklace, a cultured-pearl and gems cuff bracelet or a pair of beautiful earrings.

These pearl drop earrings are perfect to make your wedding gown sparkle, but can also be worn as fashion earrings to add a touch of elegance to your everyday-wardrobe. You can pair it with stackable 24 carat bangles to create cuffs set with gemstones.

These dazzling 24 karat gold plated earrings were handmade by the little fairies of our parisian workshop. You can store your jewellery in a small jewelry box to protect it from moisture, heat and the sun, to preserve it as well as possible. If you want to accompany our pair of Bruna gold drop earrings with another set of Les Couronnes de Victoire, we recommend the big comb Adriana. The alliance of white flowers, gold-plated metal flowers and freshwater beads will no doubt match the Bruna yellow gold earrings. A dainty duo, perfect for a soft and feminine wedding outfit.

Do not hesitate to browse our website Les Couronnes de Victoire and discover our bridal jewelry collection. Swarovski crystal barrettes, coloured flower hair combs, chignon pins decorated with coloured fine stones, gold hoop earrings, pairs of dangle earrings set with flowers, clip earrings, necklaces set with gemstone, gold bracelets… We offer a wide choice of gold jewelry and hair accessories to bring femininity and glitter to your look in every occasion. Let yourself be tempted by our original creations for a wedding, an engagement party or to give a nice gift to your entourage.

Poséïdon earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
265€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Poseidon Earrings are the flagship piece of our “L’Odyssée” collection. Decorated with a fine gold shell set with freshwater pearls, these earrings reinvent our iconic Maria earrings to sublimate your bridal gown and make you look like a modern-day mermaid. Wear alone or in duo with one of the accessories from our collections such as the Poseidon comb from the “L’Odyssée” collection.

  • Composition : shell and hammered gold-plated ring, freshwater pearls, preserved hydrangea and gypsophila flowers
  • Size: around 5 cm
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

To bring a touch of marine and enchanting novelty to your outfit, our designer Victoire has composed a vast collection of discreet jewellery including glamorous and original Poseidon earrings. This pair of feminine earrings are ideal for a wedding and will make you the mermaid of the event. Let yourself be tempted by these timeless earrings adorned with a fine gold shell and set with soft water pearls. In addition to these pretty white pearls, you will find our trademark, preserved flowers to sublimate the pair of earrings. For women who wish to dress and sublimate their lobe, these earrings are ideal because they are very discreet and glamorous at the same time. Moreover, you can wear them for all occasions. They will give you a little bohemian touch and accentuate your femininity. If you don’t have pierced ears, don’t worry, our Poseïdon earrings are clasp earrings but they can be mounted on clips on request. The pierced state of your ears will no longer prevent you from wearing earrings normally with clasps on which you have had a crush.

This discreet jewel set with white pearls is the ideal gift and will make the person you are considering giving it to shine. This lovely gift can be accompanied by other of our women’s jewellery. In the same collection we have the lovely Eudora flower wreath, which goes perfectly with these Creole earrings. With these beautiful women’s jewellery, finding a gift has never been so simple. If the Creole style is not quite your style, we also have pairs of sleepers, the Anemone as well as pendants, such as the Marina for example, whose gold and precious Swarovski crystals will make your ears look even more elegant. Moreover, if you have rings, a rush, a choker or a necklace with gold ornaments in your jewellery box, you can combine them with these beautiful earrings. Our Poseidon earrings have a circle shape, but we can offer you other geometric shapes such as the Dana earrings which are more oval and can also be matched with the precious Eudora crown. We also have other head jewellery such as the timeless Rochelle tiara whose coral structure will remind you of the cultured pearls in the earrings.

All our jewellery pieces made of stabilised flowers are created in our Parisian workshop. Below this workshop you will find our showroom where our jewellery is displayed. There is something for all tastes, from more fancy jewellery to more discreet jewellery, the choice is infinite.

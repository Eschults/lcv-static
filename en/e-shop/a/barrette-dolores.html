The Dolores bow is made of delicate guipure lace. Worn at the back of the head, this lovely bohemian ribbon falls with elegance for a beautiful romance. Its pretty floral lace pairs well with both a wedding dress for a romantic look and a fitted suit for a more edgy look. This bow was designed in collaboration with Marie, the creator of Scrunchie is Back fancy accessories.

  • Composition : White floral guipure lace bow
  • Size : One size
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instruction : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

The Dolores bow is much more than just a simple hair accessory. It is the very essence of elegance and femininity. Crafted in white floral guipure lace, this XXL bow adds a touch of charm and romance to any hairstyle.

Thanks to its barrette structure, the Dolores bow easily clips into the hair, offering a perfect hold throughout the day. Whether to enhance a sophisticated bun, embellish an elegant ponytail, or simply add a touch of whimsy to a casual hairstyle, this bow is the ideal accessory.

Without pearls or rhinestones, the Dolores bow relies on the purity of its lace to captivate all eyes. Its simplicity makes it a versatile accessory, perfect for all occasions, from weddings to fashion shows to evenings out with friends. Worn as a headband, a hairband, or simply clipped into the hair, the Dolores bow will elegantly enhance your locks with elegance and sophistication.

Its white floral guipure lace brings a touch of freshness and softness to your look, while highlighting your femininity. In essence, the Dolores bow is much more than just a simple hair accessory; it is a true jewel that will enhance your hairstyle and make you shine on all occasions.

Whether you opt for a bohemian, romantic, or chic style, this bow is the perfect accessory to accessorize your look with style and elegance.

In the realm of weddings, every detail counts to enhance the beauty of the bride and groom, and accessories play a crucial role in this endeavor. At Les Couronnes de Victoire, artisanal craftsmanship is at the heart of creating unique accessories to style the bridal couple.

Hair accessories come in a multitude of styles, from delicate headbands adorned with sequins and rhinestones to elegantly embroidered hairpins with pearls, flowers, and crystals. Barrettes and hair clips, in gold, silver, or multicolors, add a touch of glamour to all hairstyles, whether sophisticated buns or bohemian braids.

For brides with long hair, crowns or barrettes are perfect for elegantly securing rebellious strands, while flower and pearl-adorned headbands bring a romantic touch to any wedding hairstyle. Hair accessories for grooms are not forgotten; satin headbands are highly appreciated for their discreet elegance.

As for earrings, they delicately complement the brides’ look. From golden designs adorned with sparkling crystals to more delicate silver designs, each pair is designed to enhance the bride’s natural beauty.

At Les Couronnes de Victoire, each accessory is carefully crafted with attention to detail, in accordance with traditional craftsmanship. Each piece is unique, created to add a touch of refinement and elegance to the bridal couple’s attire and to allow them to shine brightly on the most important day of their lives…

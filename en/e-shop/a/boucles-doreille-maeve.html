Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our iconic Maria earrings got a makeover and now see life in purple! Discover our floral earrings in a dark purple to give character to your outfits. Purple is the color that will follow you everywhere. These earrings can be mounted on clips for non-pierced ears on request.

  • Composition : purple preserved hydrangeas, gold-plated base
  • Size: approximately 4 cm (or 5 cm upon request)
  • Shelf life: the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions: keep away from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from order date

Our extensive new “Purple Rain” collection will once again enchant you and make you shine.

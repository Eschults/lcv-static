Lorelei mini comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
80€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

With a delicate golden branch covered with small glass flowers, this mini comb will make you see life in… purple ! Its pastel colors will take you into a world full of poetry. This romantic accessory is ideal for a wedding or a pretty occasion, to fit into a hairstyle to sublimate it.

  • Composition : gold plated branch, fresh water and glass pearls, purple gypsophila and stabilized hydrangeas, gold metal comb
  • Size: unique. The mini comb measures approximately 4 cm
  • Shelf life: the flowers that make up this comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions: keep away from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Discover the new hair accessory collection of les Couronnes de Victoire, whose star is the color purple which comes in many shades : mauve, lilac, parma, lavender… Purple is a delicate tone which is also Victoire’s favorite color and this mini comb will brighten up your hairstyle for all occasions. Purple Rain is a floral collection in reference to the famous song by Prince. It comes as an ode to love and passion.

The Lorelei mini gold comb is a head piece made of freshwater pearls and pearly pearls, stabilized small flowers of purple gypsophila, mauve and parma hydrangea assembled on a gold metal comb. Purple is a trendy and sophisticated color. This pastel hair accessory is great for a romantic bridal hairstyle. The Lorelei comb and its flower petals will give a fresh bohemian and bucolic style to your bridal look. The flower comb is therefore perfect for weddings, ceremonies, Valentine’s Day or other happy celebrations. However, for a refreshingly bohemian look, you can totally wear this flower comb in your daily life.

Flower combs are delicate hair pieces that have been very fashionable in recent years because they add a touch of flowers when paired with a wedding dress without being as imposing as a flower crown. Hair combs are best suited for women who want a discreet hair accessory. However, its purple color allows you to accessorize and decorate your hair with flowers and fantasy. You can match these hair accessories to your bridal bouquet and/or groom’s boutonniere as well as to your floral decoration.

To wear a floral arrangement of this style in your hair for a wedding, we suggest that you opt for a wedding bun. This romantic and bohemian bridal hairstyle will perfectly give way to the Lorelei floral comb. You can also put your floral accessory in a ponytail, a half-tail or a braid.

At Les Couronnes de Victoire, we make floral arrangements of all forms : flower crowns (for your hippie or bohemian wedding), bracelets, flower earrings, combs for flowery hair, headbands, hats … Our pieces are handmade in Paris by our designers who love to make real works of art in the form of floral creations to adorn your most beautiful moments. This jewel is a pastel-colored model that will suit all hair colors: blondies, brunettes… but also long and medium-length hair. You will find all your favorite flowers at les Couronnes de Victoire : pink (red roses, pink roses, white roses …), gypsophila, eucalyptus, greenery …

Our original and delicate floral creations are made with stabilized flowers which means that all the natural flowers on our hair accessories are everlasting. They are not fresh flowers, artificial flowers nor simple dried flowers that have a shorter life cycle than stabilized flowers. Each of our hair jewelry pieces will enhance your wedding hairstyle or your everyday look.

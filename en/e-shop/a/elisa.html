Elisa Crown

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
45€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

We melt for the delicacy and poetry of our little crown Elisa. With its soft shades of pale pink and soft green, the poetic Elisa crown will delicately bloom the heads of little girls, whether for a wedding procession or a special occasion such as a communion, a baptism or a birthday.

This crown is perfectly adaptable thanks to its satin ribbon at the back, from the baby’s head circumference to the teenager’s head circumference.

  • Composition : powder pink hydrangea, pink gypsophila and green stoebe preserved, white satin ribbon
  • Size: one size only. Can be adjusted with the ribbon on the back
  • Shelf-life: the flowers in this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

The Elisa flowers crown is one of the original pieces of the LCV children’s collection. A collection that will sublimate the hair of your princesses and delicately adorn their wrists. You will find sumptuous head jewellery and wrist jewellery to accessorise the outfit of your little-flowers. All the hair jewellery offered by this collection are made from pretty floral compositions that will dress the soft mane of your little fairies. The petals of the flower crowns will then be placed on the head like real ornaments.

When they are lucky enough to attend a bohemian wedding, little girls often envy the bridal hairstyle or that of the bridesmaids for all their softness and enchantment. The romantic Elisa crown with its colourful flowers in soft, powdery tones is a remarkably sublime ornament. Glamorous and sophisticated, it is composed not of artificial flowers but of beautiful natural flowers that have become eternal through their preservation. The hair accessory therefore retains all its delicacy through its fresh flowers and will bring all the magic it desires to your little girl. It will fit perfectly to your baby’s head thanks to its ribbon which, when tightened and tied at the right size, allows the crown to fit all heads. All you have to do is tie it in a pretty knot. Wearing a head jewel offers a real transformation of the state of mind. These hair accessories quickly become the darlings of little ones. For the most bohemian girls, Elisa crown will certainly become the one and only darling. If you’re wondering what pretty hairstyle to choose and how to wear this hair accessory, the easiest way is to check out our hairstyle ideas, and discover a tutorial that you can find on our Pinterest account or Instagram pins to stay inspired for beautiful, sophisticated hairstyles. For long hair for example, the ideal would be to make a low bun, a braid, pretty braids or a half ponytail. For short hair, loose hair dressed with a flowery accessory always makes a beautiful bridal (or everyday) hairstyle by creating a casual yet poetic style for these cuts. This ornament will turn your little butterfly into a true light. If she wears it for a wedding (or for your wedding), a baptism, a communion, or for parties in general, we advise you to optimise the sophisticated look of this hair accessory by accompanying it with a few extra flowers in the form of bracelets for example. The children’s collection offers colourful hair accessories as well as bracelets to accompany the hair jewellery. The Elisa range consists of the Elisa flowers crown, as well as a matching Elisa hair clip and bracelet. Between floral fantasy and jewellery art, the Elisa crown and its complementary hair and wrist accessories are the perfect jewellery for the spring-summer seasons. For all occasions and hairstyling ideas, they will bring cheerfulness and freshness to your fairy.

Before going to the hairdresser, the LCV showroom is waiting for you with your little lady to help her discover and try on the accessories that will go best with her haircut, whatever the length.

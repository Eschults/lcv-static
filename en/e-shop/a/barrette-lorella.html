Lorella hair clip

Hairpin Eternal Preserved flowers
70€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Accessorize your hair with our romantic flower hair clip. Its purple color will make you see life in pink ! Girly and chic, it will delicately adorn your hair : wear it on the side or at the back if you have longer hair.

  • Composition: stabilize hydrangea flowers in mauve, pink and purple gypsophila, glass and freshwater pearls
  • Size: about 6 cm
  • Shelf life: the flowers that make up this hair clip are eternal
  • Storage instructions: keep away from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Jewelry and hair accessories are pieces not to be neglected. Seen and seen again and again in the great Haute-Couture fashion shows, a pretty glamorous hairstyle is always paired with a hair clip or other hair accessories. But the question that all fashionistas ask themselves is: “How to wear a hair clip? How to style your hair? And for what occasion?” We have the answers for you. Any occasion is appropriate to wear hair clips. For the holiday season? A sophisticated evening hairstyle? A bridal hairstyle ? For a bridesmaid? Anywhere and anytime, these accessories will accessorize your queenly hair whatever the hairstyle. It will become the essential accessory of your little barrette basket. Brown hair ? Blonde? The advantage of purple is that this blazing color adapts to all colors and also, all lengths and all thicknesses! Thick hair and thin hair; long hair and short hair. And whatever the season, spring-summer or fall-winter, it will follow the course of your life. Visit our social networks to get inspired by some pictures and hairstyle tutorials.

At les Couronnes de Victoire, we have a new collection, with new barrettes! And yes… You’ll fall for a selection of accessories again. This time you won’t fall for our fabulous flower crowns but for our flowered barrettes. A great way to tame your flyaways. If you’re used to LCV flowered hair clips, the Lorella hair clip will complete your collection. Planning a party? You can curl your hair with your curling iron and add this accessory, or even better! Ask your hairdresser for a star blowout. Hairbands, hair clips, hair pins, headbands, hair elastics… In short, make room for the Lorella fancy barrette. This mini clip is composed of stabilized flowers of mauve hydrangea, pink and purple gypsophila and an ornament of glass beads and fresh water.

Leave your hair down and slip on the Lorella hair clip that will come to sublimate you and give you that bohemian look you imagine in your sweetest dreams. All LCV accessories are entirely handmade in our showroom in the 3rd arrondissement of Paris. Romantic and girly atmosphere !

Venus veil

Voile Eternal Preserved flowers
370€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Dare to embody the true goddess of love with this airy and divine veil. Make a statement with this exceptional piece that you won’t find anywhere else! The Venus veil consists of a floral comb and a tulle embroidered with flowers one by one by hand. A complete accessory that stands on its own, giving you the aura of a muse.

  • Composition : French-made polyamide tulle, covered with stabilized hydrangeas and roses. The comb, gilded with fine gold, is integrated and composed of stabilized roses, hydrangeas, and roses
  • Size : 170 cm.
  • Shelf life : To ensure your veil retains its brilliance, we advise avoiding contact with seawater, alcohol, perfume, creams, and cosmetic products. We recommend cleaning your jewel if needed with a soft cloth or brush and clear water
  • Storage instruction : Keep away from sunlight, heat, and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the date of order

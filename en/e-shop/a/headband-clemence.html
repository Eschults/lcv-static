Clémence Headband

Eternal Preserved flowers
48€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

This delicate satin ribbon embroidered with small preserved white hydrangea flowers will be ideal to crown a baby’s head for a christening or other special occasion. Very soft and adaptable to the head size with its elastic embroidered with a golden edging, this headband will also be suitable for an older little girl for a communion, a wedding or a birthday.

  • Composition: white hydrangea and white gypsophila preserved, satin ribbon and white elastic decorated with a golden edging, golden metal clips
  • Size: the headband adapts to the size of the head with the elastic band attached at the back. Thank you to indicate the indicative headband size of the child by email to support@lescouronnesdevictoire.com indicating the reference of your order
  • Shelf-life: the flowers in this headband are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

The Clémence headband is a head jewel belonging to the Couronnes de Victoire children’s collection. All made of white, embroidered satin and white hydrangea flowers, it is the ideal bohemian jewel for your little princesses.

Its delicacy, more supple than a metal head, will suit little girls and will transform them into real bohemian and romantic fairies. The preserved flowers of this head jewel give it an eternal value that will never leave your princess. White hydrangea, gypsophila, satin ribbon, golden metal clips and white elastic… So much softness and flowers for your little-flower.

Unlike a hair clip, crown or other hair accessory, the headband is perfect for all occasions and all children. For baby, for example, it’s best to opt for this type of soft headband, which is the flowery headband. These accessories, which are also often used for children’s disguises, delight mothers.

It doesn’t matter how long and what kind of hair they have. Long hair, short hair, fine hair, thick hair, colour-treated hair… This white headband will make every little girl look her best. It will become the assured darling. The baby headband is an essential accessory that can be adjusted over the years. It will go perfectly with many accessories whether they are coloured or pastel. For a wedding, a baptism or a communion, it is the hair accessory perfectly situated between fantasy and jewellery.

In the same range, you will find the Clemence tiara for older girls, and in the same collection, flower crowns, hairbands and bracelets. All the accessories in this wide range for children, offering different colours, are both hair accessories and fashion accessories. For their preservation, in spring-summer, make sure to keep the accessories out of the sun. In autumn-winter, protect the new darlings from humidity. Hair and wrist jewellery is part of our gorgeous children’s collection.

For women who want to match their princesses, choose a flower crown from our women’s bridal crowns. The perfect ornament, glamorous and trendy, without even having to go to the hairdresser.

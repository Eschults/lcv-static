Silvana

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
160€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Based on our iconic Lucia crown, Silvana is adorned with delicate hand gilded dazzling eucalyptus leaves resting on greenery and white baby’s breath. Secure it effortlessly, either at the front or back of your head, to enhance a low bun or a bohemian braid, thanks to its headband structure with combs.

You can pair this crown with our range of Lucia or Océane accessories to dress up your wedding party. - Composition: preserved eucalyptus and white gypsophila, preserved hand gilded eucalyptus. - Size: one size only. - Shelf life: the flowers and leaves that make up this crown are eternal. - Storage instructions: keep away from direct sunlight, heat and humidity. - Delivery: 8 working days from order date.

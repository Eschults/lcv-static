Sierra earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
95€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Inspired by our iconic Monica earrings, these earrings combine a stabilised hydrangea flower in a pop of colour with stabilised eucalyptus, so you’ll look great in your wedding outfit. This is the colourful and original touch you can allow yourself to wear. These earrings can also be mounted on clips for non-pierced ears, on request.

  • Composition: gold-plated structure, yellow, ivory, fushia, peach, light blue, red and stabilised eucalyptus hydrangeas
  • Size: 2 inches (or 1,5 inches on request)
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up these earrings are eternal
  • Storage instructions : keep out of the sun, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 days from the order

Let yourself be tempted by Les Couronnes de Victoire’s new women’s jewelry collection, California Dreaming. Any occasion is good to take your earrings out of your jewelry box and wear them proudly! Crowns adorned with flowers, original and precious earrings, bracelets full of femininity, discreet boutonnieres, flowery hats… These women’s jewelry pieces can be worn for a wedding, for a festive evening or in everyday life if you like fantasy for women. If you are used to wearing hoop earrings, twisted earrings, geometric earrings, asymmetrical earrings, dangling earrings, stud earrings, flower / pearl / gemstone / crystal / rhinestone rings: you are in the right place! We make pieces that can be sober, feminine, elegant, bohemian, timeless and glamorous. If your ears are not pierced, we can make clip-on earrings upon request.

The new Sierra earrings are gold creoles made of hydrangea flower petals, eucalyptus, and a gold plated ear post and setting. The fancy earrings are sleepers but can be mounted on clips on request if you do not have pierced ears. This precious novelty will come to sublimate you, make you shine and dress you up. Wear this pair of jewel earrings as a set with the mini comb for Sierra women and pair them with a ponytail or a beautiful lacquered bun : it’s the perfect combination!

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a brand of accessories and flowery jewelry for the bride and her bridal party. All our costume jewelry is handmade from stabilized flowers. You can come and see our jewelry in our store at 230 rue Saint-Martin in the 3rd arrondissement of Paris.

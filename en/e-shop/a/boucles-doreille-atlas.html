Atlas earrings

Earrings Eternal Preserved flowers
90€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our Atlas earrings will bring you an instant glow with their subtle color. They are made of stabilized blue and gold hydrangea flowers to shine in the Starry Night ! A pair that will enchant you…

  • Composition: Stabilized Majorelle blue hydrangea flowers, baby’s breath, and stabilized golden hydrangea. Gold plated metal structure.
  • Size : 5 cm. Or 4cm and/or on request can be mounted on clips for non-pierced ears on request
  • Shelf life : eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

Our new Starry Night collection offers various types of women’s jewelry to accompany you during your end-of-year festivities and beyond. This collection consists of timeless accessories in midnight blue and gold, giving you a wide range of choices to treat yourself. Designed to add a touch of color and glamour, our new creations are here to shine on you. Explore our pair of Atlas creole earrings and elevate your style with these stunning blue and gold accessories. These dangling earrings are true costume jewelry with a 24-carat gold-plated structure, adorned with precious stabilized flowers. Their original and elegant design highlights the two colors of the hoop, creating a unique pair of earrings and adding a touch of glamour to your looks. Their gold-plated clasp ensures a perfect fit, ideal for pierced ears. The Atlas earrings can be personalized. If you don’t have pierced earlobes, you can convert them into clip-on earrings. The clips don’t weigh down the earrings, and the jewelry remains very comfortable and lightweight to wear. If you want a discreet, timeless piece of jewelry that will make you shine all night, these original earrings are made for you.

You can easily match your creole earrings with everyday outfits or dressier ensembles for ceremonies, weddings, or other events. They are a safe bet if you want to give them as a gift. The blue and gold colors will perfectly complement your finest holiday outfits. The added stabilized flowers provide a bohemian and ethnic earring style, perfectly complementing flowered hairstyles and other feminine jewelry. Jewelry has always had the power to enhance femininity and add a touch of elegance to all occasions. Among the many options available to women in search of style and originality, earrings hold a special place.

Whether it’s for an elegant evening, a birthday gift, engagement, or simply to add a touch of femininity to your outfit, earrings are the perfect ear jewelry for all women. So, indulge in this new jewelry collection and shine on every occasion with earrings that reflect your style and personality. You can complete your jewelry box with other highly feminine accessories, such as our gold rings, flower crowns, combs and hairpins, colorful flower bracelets, and more. You can also schedule an appointment on our website for a more personalized experience at one of your chosen Les Couronnes de Victoire boutiques.

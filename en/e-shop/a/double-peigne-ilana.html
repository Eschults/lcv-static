Ilana Double Comb

Comb Eternal Preserved flowers
95€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Our Ilana comb has such an original feel. Pair it with the Stella tiara for a fashion forward look. This comb can also be worn on the back to adorn a bun.

  • Composition: Preserved baby breath and hydrangea, gold plated leaves, flowers and chain
  • Size: one size only. The chain length is approximately 15 inches
  • Shelf life: the flowers that compose this comb are eternal
  • Storage instructions: keep away from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 8 working days from the date of order

A superbly original composition, Ilana the bridal hair comb was designed to be worn in two ways. Either at the top of the forehead to emphasize the purity of this part of the face or at back to adorn a low bun on the nape of the neck. In either case, you have an original design made to reflect a strong and independent woman, with free and appealing beauty. The decorative hair comb if worn on the forehead can be paired with Stella the flower tiara The composition of Stella the flower tiara has a lot of affinity with Ilana hair comb for wedding. You will have a stylish bohemian hairstyle that will sway everyone’s attention and impress the spectators with Ilana bridal hair comb’s classy appearance.

Fashioned on a pretty golden chain with fine gold, the bridal comb aligns an immaculate hydrangea flower in the center and small white gypsophila flowers on each side, interspersed with tiny little pearls. All of this blended together in a decorative hair comb offers to the eye a flower hair accessory of beauty.

The real flowers that are used to make the hair comb for wedding are preserved by an organic procedure. This guarantees an unlimited lifespan of this decorative hair comb. Certainly, there are a few simple precautions to pay attention to. Do not let your bridal hair comb stagnate in full sun, avoid heat and humidity. These simple precautions observed, the flower comb for hair will offer you an enduring span of purity and beauty. The decorative hair comb will stay in its best for another special occasion like an evening paired with the classic black dress or why not with a casual denim suit, its naturally classy nature allows it to ally with any outfit.

Ilana, the wedding hair comb was created for our latest collection of 2020 “The Enchanted Forest” by Les Couronnes de Victoire. Especially, handcrafted by the little fairies of the atelier of our brand in Paris. This flower hair accessory continues the perennial tradition of French craftsmanship with its grace and quality.

Nora

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
205€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Like a muse, the Nora crown will bring you radiance and dress you in delicate flowers for an irresistibly romantic look. Composed of a soft shade of pink with hydrangeas, gypsophila and wild roses in stabilised blooms, this crown is a real head jewel. You can choose to let your hair down or give it more flair by opting for a loose bun.

  • Composition: golden brass wire, pink and ivory gypsophila, stabilised wild roses and powder pink hydrangeas
  • Size: unisize. The soft metal structure of this crown allows it to be tightened or loosened slightly
  • Shelf life: the flowers in this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

Your bohemian wedding is coming up and you don’t know what to choose between stabilized flower crowns made of natural flowers, a pretty floral comb to accessorize a bun, a hair clip and many other accessories. You’ve come to the right place because Les Couronnes de Victoire is releasing its brand new collection of jewelry and accessories for your wedding. This collection inspired by the 70’s hippie style and California is called California Dreaming and offers many accessories to sublimate you for a romantic and bohemian wedding. The soft tones of these accessories ornaments for brides will adorn your hair for your wedding hairstyle or your evening hairstyle with its pink, orange, yellow, blue …

Here is the Aria flower crown that suits all hair types : long hair, short hair, loose hair, tied hair, brown or blonde hair, thin or thick hair: your mane will inevitably go with this pretty sophisticated ornament crown for women. This delicate headpiece is one size fits all and fastens at the back with a ribbon. The Aria flower crown is made with ivory and pink gypsophila, old rose phalaris, powder pink and burgundy hydrangea and stabilized oats. Whether you choose a ponytail, braid, multiple braids or a low bun for your wedding hairstyle, the Aria crown will adorn the lengths of your mane with fantasy and glamor and will match perfectly with your delicate wedding dress.

Les Couronnes de Victoire is a French brand of head jewelry and jewelry for women to be worn among our hair accessories, you can find flower crowns, jewel hairpins for buns, hair clips of all kinds (bow, sequins, rhinestones, crystals…), flowered hats, bracelets for bridesmaids… All the accessories of Les Couronnes de Victoire can be worn for spring-summer or autumn-winter celebrations to create the perfect match. Our little fairies design your delicate treasures in the heart of Paris where you can come and meet us at 230 rue Saint Martin.

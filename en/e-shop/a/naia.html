Naïa

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
220€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

Decorate your hair with delicate pearls and preserved white flowers. Fine and delicate, the Naïa tiara will be perfect to adorn loose, slightly wavy hair but you can also wear it with half a tail or a low bohemian bun. Match it with one of our pairs of pearl earrings from “L’Odyssée” collection, to add charm to your outfit.

  • Composition: golden metal structure, freshwater pearls, preserved gysophila and hydrangea
  • Size: unisize
  • Shelf life : the flowers that make up this tiara are eternal
  • Storage instructions : protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery : 5 working days from the date of order

The Naïa tiara is an accessory belonging to the wonderful collection L’Odyssée. The bridal accessories in this collection are inspired by the ocean and marine poetry. This hair jewel is named after the mermaid who inspires it.

This bohemian ornament with its touch of fantasy will sumptuously dress up your bridal hairstyle. Its gold-plated metal structure creates the basis of its look, which the freshwater pearls, gypsophila and preserved hydrangea will perfect. Not made of rhinestones, sequins or crystals, but of delicate flowers, this type of wedding accessory is ideal for a discreet, country effect. For your wedding, it will make you feel more confident by lending even more of its beauty and elegance to your hair. To create a glamorous and romantic style with your tiaras, we recommend that you wear them on a low bridal bun or ponytail. However, you may as well wear them with a braid or another look. Before you get them done at a hairdresser, our Pinterest account contains bridal hairstyle ideas for all brides. We advise you on how to accessorise the bride-to-be’s hairstyle as well as those of the bridesmaids. Our proposal ranges from autumn-winter looks to spring-summer styles to delight you. Contrary to popular belief, a comb, a ribbon, a headdress, a bow, a hair clip, a crown, a headband, and any hair accessory can dress up any hairstyle. Long hair, short hair, in chignons, in braids or loose… Jewellery will be your best hair accessories to go with your wedding dress.

For a wedding, jewellery and accessories complete the confidence and sophisticated look of the bride who wears them. They are real ornaments to style your queen’s hair at weddings. In order to sublimate your bridal hairstyle, the head jewellery can be worn alone or accompanied by our combs, barrettes, bracelets, flower crowns, buttonholes… If you have already bought your wedding jewellery set from Les Couronnes de Victoire, you can then expand your jewellery collection with one of the sublime pieces from L’Odyssée. Some other head jewels, although not exclusively reserved for weddings, match the aesthetics of the Naïa tiara. We invite you to discover the Nami earrings. If you wish to brighten up the jewellery with brighter accessories, we recommend the Electre jewellery, these golden ornaments will bring the right light to your look. The floral accessory has the advantage of being able to be matched depending on whether your outfit is made of satin, lace or feathers. The materials are precious and the result will be sophisticated.

At Les Couronnes de Victoire, tiaras such as flower crowns and all the accessories can be completely personalised. This is why you can easily decide to match your wedding accessories with a bridesmaid of honour. These sophisticated trends are becoming more and more common, just like matching wedding dresses to your wedding theme.

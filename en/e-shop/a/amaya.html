Amaya

Crown Eternal Preserved flowers
190€

Accessory custom-made by hand in our Parisian workshop 🇫🇷

The Amaya crown will captivate you with its elegance and unique structure. It will perfectly adorn your hairstyle for your big day, composed of delicate wild flowers in a resolutely modern spirit. The crown is worn at the back and lets the flowers scatter all along your hair, intertwined with your hairstyle.

  • Composition : ruscus, pink gypsophila, powder pink hydrangea, phalaris, nigella, wild peach roses, lagurus and bougainvillea stabilised
  • Size: unisize
  • Shelf life: the flowers in this crown are eternal
  • Storage instructions: protect from direct sunlight, heat and humidity
  • Delivery: 5 working days from the date of order

You’ve been waiting for it, here is our new collection to accessorize your wedding dress with a handmade headpiece. California Dreaming, is a collection that connects the pop and colorful world of California with the bohemian chic, hippie and country inspirations of the 70s.

The Amaya flower crown has a unique structure never seen before at Les Couronnes de Victoire. This flower crown will sprinkle your hair with delicate little field flowers. Composed of ruscus, gypsophila, hydrangea and other stabilized flowers, this hair accessory will bring a floral, whimsical, bohemian and glamorous touch to your wedding hair. This crown is worn at the back of your head and requires a suitable wedding hairstyle. A low bun, a low ponytail can be appropriate as well as a semi-tied, braided or simply by keeping your lengths and strands free. It can be worn on short or long hair.

For your wedding, dare to wear flower crowns that will bring freshness, foliage, some flowers and petals to your outfit. It’s your day, you are the bride, you wear your delicate dress in satin, muslin, sequins or lace, you are the most beautiful!

Our hair accessories will be with you for all the occasions in your life. We make them by hand in our Parisian workshop. We work with stabilized flowers, not artificial ones, which allows all these accessories to age with you. Stabilization keeps the look and color of fresh flowers. With this technique your accessory whether it is a comb, a crown, a hair clip will never fade, you will not keep your bouquet of fresh flowers but your hair jewelry, you will.

In order to create hair accessories that match each of our clients and brides we offer customization. We can change the color of some flowers, put purple flowers, ivory, white, parma, ecru, purple, pastel, bronze, silver, gold …. You can also remove some flowers and add others, but be careful, not all flowers are stabilized like orchids or peonies for example. Finally, we can add a lot of ornaments like white pearls, precious stones, crystals, sequins, rhinestones, mother of pearl….

We make a lot of wedding accessories and head jewelry, like tiaras, bracelets, veils, headbands, hats, buns, combs, earrings and of course crowns. If you don’t know how to wear our hair jewelry, if you don’t have any hairstyle ideas you can watch a tutorial on our networks or come and meet us in our store at 230 rue Saint Martin, 75003.

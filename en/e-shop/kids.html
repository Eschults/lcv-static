Floral accessories for children

Sophisticated, the flower crown has given birth to complementary hair and wrist accessories such as barrettes, headbands, or bracelets. These delicate hair jewels and ornaments will enhance your little angel with a country and bohemian style to create a neat outfit and a trendy hairstyle. Beautiful hairstyles do not depend on the type of hair or its length, but on the head jewel that will add magic, even to a simple hairstyle. These hair accessories will allow you to opt for an easy hairstyle like, loose hair, buns or ponytails and turn these easy hairstyles into sophisticated ones. All you need is an easy do-it-yourself hairstyle and a beautiful flowery accessory to top it off.

Adding a flower accessory to highlight your wedding fashion is an awesome idea for sure. What better way to spice things up than a flower hat mirroring the victorian era. Hats have sealed themselves as a high-fashion statement accessory for centuries. Generations have managed to keep up with prevailing fashion trends by sprucing up their looks with fashion hats. Hatspiration from our favourite celebrities like Katy Perry Yellow Cube Hat or Lady Gaga lobster hat have always created trends and buzz. Wait no more, get ready to experiment with fashion and create buzz. The floral wedding hats offered by Les Couronnes de Victoire will give you allure and enhance your beauty more than ever for this special day of your life, your wedding day. It is the perfect accessory for all brides who want to be both romantic and modern at the same time. It is not just a simple hat, far from it, a rare style vintage hat. The wedding hats created by our brand are decorated with natural flowers, preserved and beautifully arranged for a very unique boho result. The fashion hats of our brand are fabricated from natural straw and preserved flowers that you can easily wear with your pretty summer dress. Depending on the fancy hat model you choose, you will have different flowers, layouts, and therefore different colors. What remains unchanged is the elite quality that is the hallmark of our brand for all the wedding accessories on our website. The natural flowers used for the embroidery of these hats are eternal flowers, so you can keep them indefinitely. You just have to keep them away from humidity to avoid damaging them.Also, a flower girl doesn’t need a reason to shine, it will be very easy for you to find a summer outfit that will allow you to wear your favorite flower hat from Les Couronnes de Victoire.

Wearing a flower crown for a ceremony

For parties and ceremonies of a higher order, the flower crown is timeless and iconic. Whatever the little girl's hairstyle, it will be dressed in a sublime floral halo. The Romy crown and the Lou flower crown for example, can be worn by your daughter for everyday as well as to create an original hairstyle for a bohemian wedding or other important occasion to make her hair look pretty. No need for a tutorial, an LCV flower crown is easy to put up, whatever the hairstyle idea in question. Its ribbon at the back allows it to be adjusted by forming a pretty little bow.

Wearing the wrist corsages

For a romantic little girl who, like in a fairy tale, wants to be the most beautiful, beyond a beautiful hairstyle, the details make the difference. Dress your baby's wrist with a pretty bracelet, which like the Lena and Elisa bracelets, are a true ode to nature in bloom. Bohemian and colorful, these accessories for dreamy girls will add a note of poetry to the outfit they have chosen for the day, regardless of the hairstyle.

Wear a hair clip for any event

The barrette, a favorite accessory for everyday wear, is the darling of little princesses. A small accessory, it adapts to all hairstyle ideas. Spike braid, African braid, bun hairstyle, short cut, bangs... It is the hair accessory that sublimates curly hair as well as frizzy or straight hair.

With the flowery accessories "Little Princess" by Les Couronnes de Victoire, dare your ideas of hairstyles and have fun, to please your baby.

The history of bridal sash and its evolution

The wedding belt is an exquisite vintage accoutrement. Bridal sash is an ancient wedding accessory that dates back to antiquity. The history of bridal sash is intriguing. During the ancient times, the bride's dress used to be formed with a large piece of rectangular fabric, called "Peplos Kore", and was held at bride’s waist with the support of a belt or a sash. The bridal sash is also found in the Roman Empire. The bride's outfit was codified and the day before her wedding the woman had to wear a white tunic dress, the "stola", with a belt as a symbol of chastity. In past, most European royal families wore sashes, as a part of their royal regalia.The wedding belt has gone through the ages and centuries and today it has evolved to be employed as a dazzling wedding accessory that enhance your wedding dress.

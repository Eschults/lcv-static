Pic Andrée

Pic à chignon Eternelles Fleurs stabilisées
48€

Accessoire confectionné à la commande et à la main dans notre atelier parisien 🇫🇷

Le pic Andrée est issu de notre collection avec Olive + Piper, la marque canadienne de bijoux de mariage. Il allie la douceur du rose poudré qu’apporte l’hortensia avec l’esprit bohème des petites fleurs stabilisées réunies en petit bouquet fleuri. Il se glisse très aisément dans toute coiffure de mariée et permet d’ajouter poésie et romantisme.

  • Composition : structure plaqué or, hortensia rose poudré, tatarica, avoine, gypsophile mini ivoire stabilisés
  • Taille : 8,5 cm
  • Durée de conservation : les fleurs qui composent ces pics sont éternelles
  • Conseils de conservation : préserver du soleil, de la chaleur et de l’humidité
  • Livraison : 5 jours à compter de la commande

A new capsule collection is born in collaboration with the brand Olive & Pepper! This capsule features delicate, sober and elegant hair accessories to accessorize your wedding outfit. The Andrée hair pin is composed of a gold plated structure, powder pink hydrangea, oats, tatarica and mini ivory gypsophila. All the flowers that composed this pick are stabilized which will allow your hair accessory to stay over time while keeping all its brilliance. This pick will be able to sublimate a bridal bun, a semi-attached or a ponytail, all hairstyles will be suitable. Whatever your bridal hairstyle, it will dress up and match your wedding dress (cocktail dress, theme of your wedding, bow tie or tie of Mr., bridesmaids dresses or the procession). You can even complete this accessory with a barrette, a clip or mini combs for women (you can see the collection in the tab barrette and comb). All the accessories in this new capsule are chic and fit perfectly into the wedding world. According to the style of your wedding dress, bohemian, halter, with a neckline, stripes, mesh, strapless, bows, country, princess and according to the materials that compose it, lace, silk, ruffled, chiffon, tulle, your hair jewelry will have a corresponding style. You can come and choose and try on wedding accessories in our showroom at 230 rue Saint Martin 75003 Paris. To do so, it is best to make an appointment with one of our consultants and you can be accompanied by your bridesmaids or witnesses for your fitting. If there is a crucial step, it is the one of walking through the bridal store to choose your dress and its style, its cut, its color, its ornaments, a simple wedding dress, a princess dress, a long dress, a short wedding dress, a chiffon dress, with a neckline, feathers, with a train, an underskirt, a trapeze dress, a lace dress, an evening dress with crystals, a custom-made wedding dress, a mermaid satin dress… All dresses will have a headpiece that will match them. Then comes the step of pumps or small heels or even elegant sneakers, then the clutch, the stole, the bolero, the jewelry and finally the hair accessories. (we have a wide range) It is recommended to come and try on your wedding accessories once you have found the perfect dress, whether it is glamorous, romantic, cocktail, fancy, with sequins, rhinestones or sequins to create harmony. Les Couronnes de Victoire is a house that makes many flowery accessories such as original barrettes, bohemian earrings, crowns, picks, hats, a bouquet, a headband, veils, a tiara, a veil, a comb, and for men we create boutonnieres ect. You will surely find THE finishing touch to your bridal outfit. The flower crown is our favorite accessory whether you are a bohemian bride or a vintage bride, our crowns reveal and emphasize the femininity. It is difficult to project yourself with these accessories so you will be reassured if you make an appointment for a first fitting. Stay tuned to discover our new romantic collections for a successful wedding.
